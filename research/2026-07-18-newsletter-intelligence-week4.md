## Approved knowledge deltas

### Context trajectory and staged compaction

Context quality depends on relevance, sufficiency, size and the trajectory by which a session arrived at its current state. Repeated corrections followed by superficial agreement can indicate that prior assumptions continue to shape later reasoning. A practical response is to write a curated handoff containing the goal, accepted evidence, constraints, rejected assumptions and next step, then restart from clean context. Long work can compact state between research, design, planning and execution. Model-specific token thresholds and reported productivity gains are excluded.

Source: [Context engineering with Dex Horthy](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/context-engineering-with-dex-horthy) (practitioner interview).

### Harness deprecation and first-divergence verification

Model upgrades should trigger both regression testing and a check for scaffolding that is no longer needed. A harness built around the limitations of an older model may constrain a stronger replacement. Where a reference implementation exposes intermediate artifacts, the first divergence between reference and candidate can localize missing or incorrect implementation details more precisely than an end-state score alone. Technical causal claims remain qualified.

Source: [LLM Architecture in 2026](https://hugobowne.substack.com/p/llm-architecture-in-2026-agent-harnesses) (expert synthesis).

### Inner execution and outer direction

Enterprise AI benefits from separating an inner execution loop from an outer loop that sets priorities, supplies business context, evaluates outcomes, changes permissions and preserves human decision rights. Embedded implementation roles connect workflows, systems, teams and measurable outcomes. Conference adoption and ROI claims are excluded.

Source: [Five Trends That Defined AI Engineering at World's Fair 2026](https://www.latent.space/p/aiewf26trends) (conference synthesis with vendor incentives).

### Loop-readiness gate

Autonomous repetition is suitable when progress is observable and the workflow has a target state, feedback, one bounded item per pass, persisted progress, a fresh-context or compaction rule, retry/time/cost/concurrency limits and human escalation. A schedule or trigger without feedback and a stop condition is recurring automation, not a reliable improvement loop.

Source: [What is loop engineering?](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/what-is-loop-engineering) (practitioner method).

### Review-surface minimization

In high-stakes workflows, generation speed is not enough if a qualified human must re-check the complete artifact. Visible evidence paths, claim-to-source mappings and targeted diffs or redlines reduce the review surface. Professional rules, individual preferences and organizational precedent should remain distinguishable context layers. Vendor performance, accuracy, context-capacity and adoption claims are excluded.

Source: [How to Make Legal AI Trustworthy](https://www.artificialintelligencemadesimple.com/p/how-to-make-legal-ai-truthworthy) (vendor interview).

### Specialized harness and economical model routing

A specialized harness is justified when a repeated workflow needs stable setup, narrow tool adapters, permissions, checkpoints and artifact bundles while retaining a bounded judgment step. High-volume screening and aggregation can be routed to cheaper or local models, with stronger models or humans reserved for consequential judgment. Hardware equivalence, cost estimates and general model rankings are excluded.

Source: [How I AI: model review, local AI operation, and agent harness building](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-i-ai-gpt-56-review-how-a-solo) (practitioner interview bundle).

### Targeted retrieval and visual verification

Recurring monitoring is useful for discovery on confirmed priority topics, not as evidence by itself. Domain and document-type constraints can surface authoritative primary material when generic search favors summaries. Reverse-image checks across multiple systems can help reconstruct prior publication and context for images or video frames, but a match is only a provenance lead and does not prove authenticity.

Source: [Search Smarter, Verify Faster](https://wondertools.substack.com/p/search-smarter-verify-faster) (practitioner method).

### Build-versus-buy gate

Before building a custom AI tool, test existing solutions against the actual workflow. Compare fit, total ownership and maintenance cost, integration burden, data sensitivity, switching cost, required control and strategic differentiation. Faster AI-assisted implementation does not remove maintenance or opportunity cost. This is a decision heuristic, not an empirical rule.

Source: bounded newsletter argument, “Before you build that tool, ask this first,” Tina Huang newsletter (no external article target).

- More autonomous repetition does not guarantee improvement without feedback and stop conditions.
- Higher nominal accuracy does not guarantee adoption when outputs are costly to verify.
- A stronger model does not imply that all existing harness scaffolding remains useful.
