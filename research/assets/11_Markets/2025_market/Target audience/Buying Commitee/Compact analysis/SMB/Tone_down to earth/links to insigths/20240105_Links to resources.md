# Web Resources Supporting Supply Chain Analysis by Customer Segment

## Small and Medium Businesses (SMBs)

1. **Global Trade Challenges**
   - [U.S. Small Business Administration: Exporting](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/grow-your-business/export-products) - Provides basic guidance for SMBs looking to export.
   - [World Bank: SME Competitiveness](https://www.worldbank.org/en/topic/smefinance) - Offers insights into challenges faced by SMBs in global trade.

2. **Unpredictable Demand**
   - [Inventory Management Techniques for Small Businesses](https://www.netsuite.com/portal/resource/articles/inventory-management/inventory-management-techniques.shtml) - Discusses practical inventory management strategies for SMBs.

3. **Technology Use**
   - [Supply Chain Digital: Top 10 Supply Chain Management Software for SMBs](https://supplychaindigital.com/top10/top-10-supply-chain-management-software-solutions-smbs) - Lists accessible tech solutions for SMBs.

4. **Environmental Concerns**
   - [EPA: Small Business Resources](https://www.epa.gov/smallbusiness) - Provides resources for SMBs to improve their environmental practices.

5. **Supply Chain Disruptions**
   - [Harvard Business Review: A More Resilient Supply Chain](https://hbr.org/2020/06/building-a-more-resilient-supply-chain) - Offers strategies applicable to businesses of all sizes, including SMBs.

2. **Unpredictable Demand**
   - [McKinsey: Supply Chain 4.0 for Mid-Market Companies](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/retail/our-insights/supply-chain-4-0-in-consumer-goods) - Discusses how mid-market companies can use technology to manage demand.

3. **Technology Use**
   - [Gartner: Supply Chain Technology](https://www.gartner.com/en/supply-chain/insights/supply-chain-technology) - Offers insights into supply chain technologies suitable for mid-market companies.

4. **Environmental Concerns**
   - [MIT Sloan: Sustainability in Mid-Sized Companies](https://mitsloan.mit.edu/ideas-made-to-matter/sustainability-conundrum-mid-sized-companies) - Discusses how mid-sized companies approach sustainability.

5. **Supply Chain Disruptions**
   - [BDO: Building a Resilient Supply Chain](https://www.bdo.com/insights/business-financial-advisory/building-a-resilient-supply-chain) - Provides strategies for mid-market companies to build resilience.

## Large Enterprises

1. **Global Trade Challenges**
   - [World Economic Forum: Global Value Chains](https://www.weforum.org/agenda/2020/05/global-value-chain-risk-resilience/) - Discusses challenges and strategies for large enterprises in global trade.

2. **Unpredictable Demand**
   - [IBM: AI in Supply Chain](https://www.ibm.com/supply-chain/artificial-intelligence) - Explores how large enterprises use AI for demand forecasting.

3. **Technology Use**
   - [PwC: Digital Supply Chain](https://www.pwc.com/gx/en/industries/industrial-manufacturing/digital-supply-chain.html) - Provides insights into digital technologies used by large enterprises in supply chain management.

4. **Environmental Concerns**
   - [UN Global Compact: Supply Chain Sustainability](https://www.unglobalcompact.org/what-is-gc/our-work/supply-chain) - Offers guidance on sustainable supply chain practices for large enterprises.

5. **Supply Chain Disruptions**
   - [McKinsey: Risk and Resilience in Supply Chains](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/operations/our-insights/risk-resilience-and-rebalancing-in-global-value-chains) - Provides strategies for large enterprises to manage supply chain risks.

These resources provide additional context and validation for the points made in our simplified analyses. They offer real-world examples, research findings, and expert opinions that support our understanding of how different-sized businesses approach supply chain management.
