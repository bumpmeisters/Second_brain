# A Week in the Life of a Mid-Market Company Supply Chain Manager

Hello, I'm Alex, the supply chain manager for a growing sportswear company. We've got about 200 employees and are expanding our presence both nationally and in a few international markets. My role is becoming increasingly complex as we grow, but it's an exciting challenge!

## Monday: Strategic Planning and Forecasting

The week kicks off with our monthly S&OP (Sales and Operations Planning) meeting. I've spent the weekend reviewing last month's sales data and our current inventory levels.

"Team, our new running shoe line is outperforming expectations in the Southeast, but we're seeing slower uptake in the Midwest," I report. We discuss adjusting our regional distribution strategy and I make a note to update our demand forecasting model.

After the meeting, I catch up with our newly hired logistics coordinator. We're finally at a size where we can have dedicated roles, and it's a relief to have support in managing our day-to-day operations.

## Tuesday: Supplier Management and Sustainability Initiatives

Today, I'm meeting with our main fabric supplier. We're looking to increase our use of recycled materials, but I'm concerned about the impact on costs and quality.

"We're aiming for 30% recycled content in our next collection," I explain. "Can you help us achieve this without a significant price increase?"

It's a tough negotiation, but we manage to find a middle ground. I'm excited about the progress but know we'll need to monitor the quality closely.

## Wednesday: Technology Implementation

We're in the middle of implementing a new Transportation Management System (TMS). It's a significant investment for us, but as our distribution network grows more complex, we need better visibility and control.

I spend most of the day with the IT team, ironing out integration issues with our existing ERP system. It's a headache now, but I know it'll pay off in the long run with better efficiency and data for decision-making.

## Thursday: Risk Management and Compliance

News breaks about political unrest in one of our sourcing countries. I quickly pull together a meeting with our production team to assess the potential impact.

"We need to look at our options," I tell the team. "Let's map out scenarios for shifting production to other facilities if needed."

Later, I review our compliance documentation for a new European market we're entering. The regulations are complex, and I make a note to consult with our legal team to ensure we're not missing anything.

## Friday: Performance Review and Future Planning

I end the week by reviewing our key performance indicators. Our on-time delivery rate has improved, but our inventory turnover is slower than I'd like.

Looking ahead, I start drafting a proposal for expanding our warehouse capacity. We're outgrowing our current space, and I need to make a case for the investment to the executive team.

As I wrap up, I reflect on the week. We're at an interesting stage – big enough to need more sophisticated systems and strategies, but still small enough that I can have a hand in many aspects of the business. It's challenging, but I wouldn't have it any other way.
