## A closer look atIntent data

What is buyer intent data?

Buyer intent is a signal that predicts when a company is ‘in market’ for a product or solution. There are myriad sources of intent signals derived from first-party and third-party sources.

How does Bombora create Intent data?

Bombora’s Company Surge® Intent data is derived from our Data Cooperative using proprietary AI-driven machine learning and natural language processing models. Bombora continuously monitors company research activity against 21,600+ topics. To identify elevated Intent, Bombora evaluates behavioral changes across every account-topic pair, via relative, account-specific scoring that compares the most recent three weeks of activity against a 12-week historical baseline.

How is Bombora different from other Intent data providers?

Company Surge® is the only Intent data derived from a diversified Data Co-op with thousands of B2B publishers and brand sites. Research consumption is collected through a proprietary, consent-driven tag on every site in our Co-op. Bombora’s proprietary models, fueled by rich, direct engagement data, eliminate signal noise and identify true intent.

How does Company Surge® Intent data help sales and marketing teams?

Bombora’s Intent data helps sales and marketing teams identify “in-market” accounts with high propensity to buy. Marketers can build Intent-based digital ABM campaigns to engage these accounts. Sales teams use Intent data to prioritize sales motions and customize outreach. Intent data is an effective tool for unifying strategies across marketing and sales.
