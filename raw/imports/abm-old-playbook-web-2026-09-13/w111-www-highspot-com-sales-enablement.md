Highspot’s adaptive learning doesn’t just personalise training. It also prioritises what actually impacts performance. By dynamically adjusting to each rep’s real-world skills, and knowledge gaps, reps skip what they’ve mastered and focus only on what drives performance. The result? Faster ramp, sharper execution, and learning that fuels revenue, not checkboxes.
