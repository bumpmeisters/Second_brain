FAQs about Digital Sales Rooms

How do Seismic's Digital sales rooms accelerate deals?

Seismic's Digital Sales Rooms streamline the buyer journey by providing a centralized, personalized digital workspace for sellers and buyers to collaborate, engage with content, and make decisions faster — all within one secure virtual deal room.

Can I personalize the content and branding within a DSR?

Yes! Seismic’s digital sales rooms let you customize branding, messaging, and content to create more engaging, trustworthy buyer experiences online.

How does Seismic track engagement within a Digital Sales Room?

With Seismic’s Digital Sales Rooms, sellers can see how buyers engage with content in real time—so they can follow up smarter and close deals faster.

Are Seismic’s Digital Sales Rooms secure?

Yes, Seismic’s Digital Sales Rooms offer secure document sharing with access controls like password protection—buyers don’t need a Seismic account to view content, making the experience seamless and secure.

What makes Seismic’s DSRs different from other virtual sales room platforms?

Seismic’s Digital Sales Rooms are built on a leading sales enablement platform — bringing together personalized content, collaboration, and analytics for a more effective selling experience.
