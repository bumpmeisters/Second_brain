# The data-backed guide to multi-threading and team selling

#### Close Bigger Deals. Faster. With More Confidence.

Single-threaded deals are where healthy pipelines go to die. And the data proves it.

After analyzing millions of sales interactions, **Gong found that closed-won deals include 67% more contacts than closed-lost deals.**

And that’s just the beginning.

When sellers actively multithread and team sell the _right way,_ the impact is massive. Higher win rates, shorter deal cycles, and bigger deals.

This guide breaks down the exact system top sellers use to win multi-stakeholder deals and the internal moves that separate elite reps from everyone else.

A proven playbook for turning multi-stakeholder chaos into deal-winning clarity, including:

- How high performers map champions, decision makers, and influencers in their deals
- When and where to match the buying team with your selling team
- A step-by-step playbook for flawless internal alignment

All based on Gong Labs data.
