# About Advantage+ audience

Meta Advantage+ audience (or Audience with Advantage+ on) lets advertisers use Meta’s advanced AI to find their Meta ad campaign audience.

Advantage+ audience campaigns can achieve the following results:

- **The Awareness objective** could get 14.8% lower cost per result. ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/rsrc.php/yX/r/-N6UxtC4nZh.webp)

- **The Traffic, Engagement and Leads objectives** could get 9.7% lower cost per result. ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/rsrc.php/yX/r/-N6UxtC4nZh.webp)

- **The Sales and App promotion objectives** could get 7.2% lower cost per result.![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/rsrc.php/yX/r/-N6UxtC4nZh.webp)


## How Advantage+ audience works

Meta’s AI uses numerous pieces of information to find your audience, evolving constantly as it learns. For example:

- Past conversions

- [Meta Pixel](https://www.facebook.com/help/742478679120153) data

- Interactions with previous ads


Our latest model innovations analyze millions of signals to find the right message, for the right audience, at the right time.

## When to use Advantage+ audience

Meta recommends [A/B testing](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1738164643098669) with Advantage+ audience for almost all campaign types, except [retargeting campaigns](https://www.facebook.com/business/goals/retargeting).

For campaigns using the sales, leads or app promotion objectives, we recommend our more complete campaign solution [Advantage+ campaigns](https://www.facebook.com/help/1292656978738967) which applies AI across many steps. If you want to explore using AI to find your audience while still sharing knowledge about your customers but don't want to automate all steps in your campaign, Advantage+ audience is an effective alternative.
