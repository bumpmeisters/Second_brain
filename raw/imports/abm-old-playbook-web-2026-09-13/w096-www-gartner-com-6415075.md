# Case Study: Digital Sales Rooms for Faster Sales Cycles

Published: 02 May 2025


### Summary

Sellers struggle to engage effectively with digitally driven buyers, resulting in missed opportunities. CSOs can deploy digital sales rooms early in the sales process to streamline discovery and facilitate buying group consensus, leading to faster sales cycles and higher win rates.
