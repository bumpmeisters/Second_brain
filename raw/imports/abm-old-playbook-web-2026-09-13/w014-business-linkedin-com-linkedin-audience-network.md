## Quality and brand safety with the LinkedIn Audience Network

We're committed to protecting your brand from fraud and objectionable content on site and on the LinkedIn Audience Network. We help customers uphold their brand safety standards — on top of our own — so customers can feel confident that their message is delivered in brand-safe placements when advertising with LinkedIn.

Learn more about [brand safetyopens in a new tab](https://business.linkedin.com/content/dam/lem/business/en/advertise/ads/linkedin-audience-network/brand-safety-overview-july2020.pdf "brand safety") on the LinkedIn Audience Network.

## How to get started with the LinkedIn Audience Network

## Create your campaign

Sign in to [Campaign Manageropens in a new tab](https://www.linkedin.com/ad/accounts?src=or-search&veh=www.google.com&trk=lmsvideoadlp "Campaign Manager") and create a Sponsored Content campaign.

LinkedIn Audience Network is default enabled for your image, carousel and video format campaigns.

Follow [these stepsopens in a new tab](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/83628?src=or-search&veh=www.google.com&trk=lmsvideoadlp "these steps") to adjust your Audience Network preferences.

![Screenshot of Sponsored Content LAN enablement using Campaign Manager](https://delivery-p143253-e1476319.adobeaemcloud.com/adobe/assets/urn:aaid:aem:bfcead67-6955-4964-b75d-f13c15e84866/as/bg01-scale-v02-banner-dskv2.jpg?width=640&preferwebp=true)

## Uphold your brand safety standards

During campaign set up, you can [exclude categoriesopens in a new tab](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/83630?src=or-search&veh=www.google.com&trk=lmsvideoadlp "exclude categories") of publishers to make sure your message is customized for each campaign and in alignment with your brand safety standards. Categories are based on Interactive Advertising Bureau (IAB) definitions.

![Screenshot of Campaign Manager LAN enablement category options](https://delivery-p143253-e1476319.adobeaemcloud.com/adobe/assets/urn:aaid:aem:b5fec7c5-02b3-4102-9bce-c287a07293fe/as/position2-dsk.png?width=640&preferwebp=true)

## Measure the ad performance impact

In the Campaign Manager reporting dashboard, enable LinkedIn Audience Network to view [the On/Off Network breakdownopens in a new tab](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/83629?src=or-search&veh=www.google.com&trk=lmsvideoadlp "the On/Off Network breakdown") of your ad campaign performance, with metrics like clicks, impressions and views.

![Screenshot of Performance tab in Campaign Manager using the breakdown filter](https://delivery-p143253-e1476319.adobeaemcloud.com/adobe/assets/urn:aaid:aem:2102d7b6-5f40-4e16-bbbd-e2a2f3aeb03c/as/position3-dsk.png?width=640&preferwebp=true)
