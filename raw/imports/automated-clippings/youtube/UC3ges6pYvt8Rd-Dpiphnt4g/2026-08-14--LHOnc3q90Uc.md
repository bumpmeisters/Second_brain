# 15-Min Pork Blanket Dumplings | No Folding Skills Needed & Here’s Why They Work!

## Creator description

Love dumplings but hate spending forever folding them? These Pork Blanket Dumplings are the perfect shortcut! In just 15 minutes, you'll make juicy pork dumplings with tender wrappers and crispy golden bottoms—no folding skills required.

For the filling:
1 pound ground pork
2 tablespoons oyster sauce
1 tablespoon dark soy sauce
1 tablespoon sesame oil
1 large egg
1 tablespoon cornstarch
3/4 cup carrots, minced
1 cup cabbage
5 stalks scallions, minced

For the dumplings:
9 sheets wonton wrappers, or dumpling wrappers
1 tablespoon cooking oil
3/4 cup water

For the sauce:
1 stalk scallions, minced
2 tablespoons soy sauce
2 tablespoons chili oil
