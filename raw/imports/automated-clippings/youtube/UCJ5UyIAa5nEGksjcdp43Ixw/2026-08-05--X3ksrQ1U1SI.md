# Google Meridian | Scenario Planner Overview

## Creator description

Ready to turn your Marketing Mix Modeling (MMM) data into a highly visual, actionable media plan? In this hands-on tutorial, we dive deep into the Google Meridian Scenario Planner.

Learn how to connect your trained Meridian model to an interactive Looker Studio dashboard so you can run powerful "what-if" scenarios, justify your marketing budget to the C-suite, and ensure every dollar is optimized for maximum impact.

What You'll Learn in This Video:
- Generating the Dashboard: How to use the Meridian Colab notebook to automatically generate your Looker Studio link.
- Analyzing the Past: Validating your model’s accuracy (R^2) and understanding the critical difference between historical ROI and Marginal ROI (mROI).
- Planning the Future: Setting up Fixed vs. Flexible budget scenarios and applying realistic channel constraints.
- Optimizing Spend: Reading response curves to see exactly why the optimization engine recommends shifting dollars away from tapped-out channels and into high-growth opportunities.

Try out the Scenario Planner - https://developers.google.com/meridian/docs/scenario-planning/meridian-scenario-planner

Check out the Google Meridian community - https://discord.gg/googlemeridian
