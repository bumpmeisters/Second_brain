The marketing industry is facing a growing crisis. Teams are cutting entry-level roles while demanding that the remaining practitioners master AI tools but produce undetectably human work.

Teams can produce content but can't evaluate whether it’s any good. Rather than automating away critical competitive advantages, he recommends deliberately slowing down processes to create learning opportunities for junior team members.
