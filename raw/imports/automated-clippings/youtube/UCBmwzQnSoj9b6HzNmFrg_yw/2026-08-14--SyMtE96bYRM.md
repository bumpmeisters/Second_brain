# How Indeed Recruit Technologies helps job seekers feel understood with Gemini

## Creator description

Featured in this video: hiyingxue Li, SVP of Engineering, Indeed Recruit Technologies

Executive summary: Indeed Recruit Technologies helps job seekers find meaningful opportunities while using technology to make the hiring journey faster, more personalized, and less reliant on manual processes. Building on a long-standing partnership with Google, Indeed Recruit Technologies is using Gemini and multimodal AI systems to move beyond traditional keyword-based job matching. Its AI products can ask dynamic, personalized questions in real time and create practice interview experiences where job seekers can express themselves naturally, helping the system better understand their skills and preferences.

Internally, teams are also using Gemini, NotebookLM and BigQuery to accelerate work and make organizational knowledge more accessible. NotebookLM helps employees uncover insights from existing documents and presentations, while Gemini enables teams to create editable presentations through natural-language instructions and move from ideation to delivery without spending days on early-stage development. The engineering team has also built an always-available BigQuery agent that allows employees to retrieve customer and performance data directly, reducing repetitive requests and enabling engineers to focus on higher-value technical work.

Challenge: Indeed wanted to create a more personalized job-search experience that could understand candidates beyond resumes and keyword matches. At the same time, internal teams were spending significant time searching through existing knowledge, developing presentations, responding to routine data requests, and completing repetitive tasks that limited their ability to focus on more strategic work.

Solution: Indeed introduced Gemini and multimodal AI systems to create more conversational and personalized experiences for job seekers, including dynamic questioning and AI-powered practice interviews. Internally, the company uses NotebookLM to surface insights from its existing Google Docs and Slides, while Gemini helps teams rapidly turn ideas into presentations and other deliverables. The engineering organization also built a BigQuery agent that gives employees 24/7, self-service access to business and customer data without requiring support from the engineering team.

Results: With Google AI, Indeed is creating more personalized experiences for job seekers while accelerating work across internal teams. Employees can move from ideation to delivery faster, extract more value from existing organizational knowledge, and access critical data on demand. By automating routine data requests, the BigQuery agent also frees engineers to focus on building stronger systems and infrastructure rather than answering repetitive questions. Together, these tools are helping Indeed Recruit Technologies improve how people discover opportunities while enabling its own teams to work at a higher level.
