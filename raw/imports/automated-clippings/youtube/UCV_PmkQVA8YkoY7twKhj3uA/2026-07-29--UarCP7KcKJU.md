# Webinar: Making AI transformation stick - The leadership playbook

## Creator description

AI transformation stalls at the leadership level, not the technology level. The pull of the present is real: stretched teams, competing priorities, governance that hasn’t caught up, and pilots that never scale beyond the team that ran them.

In this webinar, we’ll cover what it actually takes to move past it.
