# We Got Open Source Palantir Before GTA 6

## Creator description

You asked, so it’s happening. God’s Eye View (formerly known as WorldView) is now open source. Get the code for V1 → https://github.com/bilawalsidhu/gods-eye-view
Live open source spatial intelligence on a photorealistic 3D globe. Half the magic is that it feels like a forbidden cockpit -- then you realize the sources are public and the code is inspectable.

It started as a slightly ridiculous question: how much of the planet can you make sense of from public data and a browser tab? Once aircraft, vessels, satellites, active fires, earthquakes, traffic, public cameras and physical infrastructure live on the same photorealistic 3D globe, it stops feeling like a pile of dashboards. It feels like a command center.

In this video I walk through the whole thing: ask the planet what’s happening over a cluster of military flights; jump into cockpit mode; voice-fly to points of interest, draw real boundaries and routes; move from city traffic into street cameras; inspect the physical internet; tune into radio around the world; and replay a recent Falcon 9 launch.

V1 is the live open playground. It’s a fast, hackable foundation for exploration and learning -- not a complete or authoritative picture of the world. Break it, extend it, add the layers you want, and show me what you cook! 

WHAT’S INSIDE:

• Photorealistic 3D globe: Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles + CesiumJS
• Live and near-live aircraft: OpenSky + adsb.lol, including a separate military layer
• Cockpit mode: flight paths, nearby context, AR labels and visual presets
• Live vessels: AISStream
• Propagated satellite orbits: CelesTrak TLEs
• Active fires: NASA FIRMS
• Earthquakes: USGS
• Traffic and public cameras: OSM road geometry, optional TomTom congestion, and supported city camera sources
• Physical infrastructure: submarine cables, data centers and dams
• Internet radio: Radio Browser + individual broadcasters
• Recent space missions: Launch Library 2 metadata 
• Voice navigation, layer control, annotations, measurement and spatial queries: OpenAI Realtime API
