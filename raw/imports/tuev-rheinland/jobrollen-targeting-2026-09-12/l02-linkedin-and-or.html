Use AND-OR targeting to set up your LinkedIn Ads audience

Last updated: 1 month ago

LinkedIn’s targeting allows you to customize your audience size. Using AND-OR logic helps further refine your targeting criteria by leveraging audience segments or audience attributes to select or exclude member accounts from your audience.

For example, you can target the job title of software engineer OR the software engineer skill, AND have five or more years of experience. This means that your audience will include:  

  • Member accounts that have software engineer as a job title and five or more years of experience. 
  • Member accounts that have software engineer listed as a skill and five or more years of experience.  

While creating your target audience, if you select: 

  • Narrow - Customizes your audience to those who meet your selections.  
    • To increase your audience to member accounts who meet multiple criteria, add more audience segments or attributes and select AND. 
    • To decrease your audience using multiple criteria, add more audience segments or attributes and select OR. 
  • Exclude - Reduces your audience to those who meet any previous selections and don’t meet the exclusion criteria.

To use LinkedIn’s AND-OR functionality for an ad set:

  1. Go to the Audience section of the ad set you want to edit or create.

  2. Choose to build your audience from an existing audience segment or create your own. Learn more about setting up ad set targeting.

  3. In the Who is your target audience? section, you can:

    • Click Narrow or the ﻿﻿﻿ icon to specify the attributes you want included in your audience.
    • Click Exclude to specify the attributes you want excluded from your audience. 
    • Keep in mind, you can click Reset audience at the bottom of the section to reset your audience.

  4. Choose the attributes you’d like to use to filter your audience. You can choose from Audience segments, like predictive or Retargeting, or Audience attributes, like Company or Job Experience.

  5. After you select your audience segments and attributes, you can click AND in the upper-left corner to change to OR. 

  6. Click View audience summary to review your audience criteria. You can also leverage Forecasted Results on the right of your ad set to gain insights about the size of your target audience based on your audience attribute selections. 

Here's a tip

If you have a self-service account, we’ll also suggest a target audience for your ad set. You can use these suggestions, edit the suggested audience, or reset the audience if you’d like to choose your audience without suggestions.
