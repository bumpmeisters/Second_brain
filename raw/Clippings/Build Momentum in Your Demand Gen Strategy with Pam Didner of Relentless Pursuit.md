How can B2B marketers keep their demand generation strategy relevant in a fast-changing world?  
  
In this episode of the Demand Bites podcast, we’re joined by @PamDidner —author, speaker, consultant, and fractional CMO with over 20 years of B2B marketing experience—to explore how continuous learning fuels long-term demand generation success.  
  
In just 15 minutes, Pam and host Ross Howard share practical strategies for building a sustainable demand gen plan, from using generative AI effectively to strengthening sales and marketing alignment. They also unpack a commonly overlooked growth lever: continuous buyer engagement and the power of a thoughtful follow-up after the first touchpoint.
