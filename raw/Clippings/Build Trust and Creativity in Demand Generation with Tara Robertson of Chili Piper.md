How can B2B marketers build trust while still driving real pipeline impact?  
  
In this episode of the Demand Bites podcast, we’re joined by Tara Robertson, Head of Demand Generation at Chili Piper, to explore how empathy, creativity, and sales alignment power modern demand generation.  
  
In just 15 minutes, Tara and host Ross Howard break down how to build trust through transparency, scale personalised campaigns without losing authenticity, and balance creative experimentation with measurable results. Tara also shares how tools like GoodFit can support smarter account scoring.
