JAGGAER, a global leader in procurement solutions, partnered with [2X Marketing](https://6sense.com/partner/2x/) – a premier B2B growth marketing agency- to revolutionize its go-to-market strategy using [6sense Intelligent Workflows](https://6sense.com/platform/marketing-intelligent-workflows/). Facing classic operational hurdles, 2X Marketing orchestrated a multi-channel 6sense Intelligent Workflow (IWF) focused on high-intent accounts in decision and purchase stages, integrating 6sense AI for precise targeting and engagement. This game-changing approach, delivered on a tight budget, minimized silos and maximized pipeline impact, with early results already tackling pain points and setting the stage for a Breakthrough’25 showcase.

## The Challenge

Like many B2B organizations, JAGGAER encountered persistent inefficiencies in its sales and marketing motion when adopting 6sense, with SDR teams overwhelmed by manual processes and fragmented data.

**Key Challenges Included:**

- Excessive backlog of SDR tasks and account ownership misalignment, creating siloed heroics and delaying outreach.
- Stale and incomplete contact data, hindering effective prioritization of warm leads.
- No centralized reporting for hot 6sense qualified accounts (6QA), complicating real-time decision-making.
- Limited budgets for ad campaigns, restricting scalable targeting and engagement efforts.

These issues translated to more headaches than pipeline wins, underscoring the need for a unified, automated strategy to engage buyers at the right time with resonant messaging.

## The Solution

2X Marketing teamed up with JAGGAER to deploy 6sense powered audience workflows, emphasizing automation and AI to streamline the buyer journey from ad interactions to sales handoffs.

Key solutions included:

- **[6sense Intelligent Workflows](https://6sense.com/platform/marketing-intelligent-workflows/) and [AI-Driven Emails](https://6sense.com/platform/email-agent/):** Started with [6sense 6QA](https://6sense.com/glossary/6sense-qualified-account/) accounts from the last 30 days, using 6sense AI Email Agents to filter high-intent ones in decision and purchase states; routed qualifying contacts to Salesforce with criteria for valid names, emails, marketing ownership, and no active opportunities, while timing non-qualifiers for future engagement- no cost leads.
- **Ad Interaction Triggers:** Monitored clicks on JAGGAER’s targeted campaigns (e.g., Riddle-optimized digital procurement case study and transformed direct spend infographics), triggering personalized, pain-point specific AI emails that felt human and adaptive based on buyer actions.
- **Dynamic Automation and Insights:** Applied IF/THEN logic for chaos-free engagement, reducing manual tasks, ensuring sales alignment, and generating actionable next steps- fun, scalable across products, industries, and regions, all on a tight budget!

This plug-and-play setup and far from crystal-ball guesswork, produced real-time insights that 2X Marketing optimized for JAGGAER’s needs, turning static campaigns into a living system.
