How do you build an ABM Center of Excellence inside a 90,000-person “startup”?  
Avishek Chakrobarty reveals how Kyndryl runs ABM for some of the world’s biggest enterprises.  
  
In this episode of Let’s talk ABM, we go inside one of the most advanced ABM operating models in enterprise tech: Kyndryl’s Global ABM Center of Excellence.  
  
Avishek Chakrobarty explains how the IBM spin-off built a “startup mindset” around relationships + growth, why ABM sits on top of functions as a strategic bridge, and how his team supports field marketers across the world.  
  
We unpack Kyndryl’s RRS model (Reputation, Relationships, Signings), how they choose which accounts enter ABM, and the two engines at the heart of their program: the Social Eminence team (elevating internal leaders) and the Customer Eminence team (creating “customer moments” that go far beyond case studies).  
  
Avishek also shares how they compare ABM vs non-ABM accounts, the dashboards they use to get to a “single source of truth”, and why he tells leaders to focus on the journey, not just the deal size, when pitching ABM.  
  
Avishek Chakrobarty is Head of the Global ABM Center of Excellence at Kyndryl, the world’s largest IT infrastructure services provider and a recent spin-off from IBM. With a background in Sales and Enterprise Marketing, he specializes in building ABM as an internal consulting function—aligning field marketing, sales, data, and brand teams around strategic accounts, relationships and long-term growth.
