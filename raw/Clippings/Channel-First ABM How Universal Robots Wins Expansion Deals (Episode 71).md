Customer Expansion ABM—what actually works when you sell through partners?  
Universal Robots’ Katarzyna Pawlik shares the channel-first playbook that turns one plant win into multi-site growth.  
  
In this Let’s talk ABM, Katarzyna Pawlik breaks down how to run ABM in a channel-first world. You’ll hear why UR focuses on customer expansion over net-new, how “one plant” can operate like a new logo, and how to earn direct executive access while respecting the partner ecosystem.  
  
We dig into account selection (fit + collaboration readiness), tiering (1:1, 1:few, industry 1:many), and the actual experience customers get—from targeted digital to high-touch “appreciation” dinners around flagship events. Katarzyna explains how to orchestrate cadence across sales, partners, and regions so buyers see one consistent story (not five conflicting emails).  
  
Measurement gets real: manual trackers + intent + ABM tools where they help, but “insight translation” is the hard part. Katarzyna shares the unspoken alignment challenges (rep changes mid-program, KPI shifts, regional ownership), and the fixes: joint planning, RACI, shared trackers, and relentless transparency.  
  
We also cover pursuit marketing for RFPs, visual storytelling that actually stands out (her “ham book” and Vogue-style team intros), a pragmatic view on AI (analysis + intent synthesis beats copy), and hiring with the “Five Cs” (Curiosity, Creativity, Courage, Consistency, Composure)—plus a sixth: Clarity.
