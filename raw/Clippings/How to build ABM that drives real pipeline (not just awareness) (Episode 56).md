Most account-based marketing fails long before the ads ever run.  
Not because of tools — but because teams confuse awareness with impact.  
  
ABM is often sold as a silver bullet, but in practice it breaks down fast when goals, strategy, and teams aren’t aligned. In this episode, we unpack why so many ABM programs stall — even with great technology in place.  
  
Taylor Young shares what she’s learned as both a former ABM practitioner and the internal “voice of the customer” at Terminus. From goal misalignment to creative bottlenecks, she explains the real blockers teams don’t see coming.  
  
You’ll hear why ABM is not advertising, why technology can scale bad strategy just as fast as good strategy, and why understanding your customer matters more than any platform.  
  
This is a grounded, practitioner-led conversation for marketers who want ABM to actually move revenue — not just impressions.  
  
Taylor Young is a Principal of ABM Strategy and Customer Experience at Terminus. A former Terminus power user, Taylor now represents the voice of the customer internally, helping teams translate ABM strategy into real-world execution.
