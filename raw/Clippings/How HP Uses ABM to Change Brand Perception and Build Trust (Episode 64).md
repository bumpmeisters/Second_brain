Can ABM make customers feel something?  
HP’s Kathryn Nimmo shows how storytelling, empathy, and focus turn campaigns into meaningful relationships.  
  
In this episode of Let’s talk ABM, host Declan Mulkeen sits down with Kathryn Nimmo, to explore how she’s building an ABM program rooted in empathy and credibility — not just campaigns.  
  
Kathryn reveals how HP is repositioning itself beyond hardware by using ABM to change customer perceptions around security, services, and collaboration. She shares how her sales background helps bridge the marketing-sales divide, why “always-on activation” keeps HP front of mind, and how focusing on a few accounts deeply can drive long-term impact.  
  
From a year-long financial-sector campaign that opened 50 new stakeholder relationships to her belief in “meaningful, magical moments,” Kathryn proves that ABM is as much about emotion and trust as it is about pipeline.  
  
Kathryn Nimmo is the ABM Marketing Manager at HP, specializing in enterprise account engagement within the Workforce Solutions Business Unit. With two decades in sales at IBM before moving into marketing, she champions sales alignment, customer empathy, and ABM storytelling that delivers human impact.
