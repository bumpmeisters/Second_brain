# On-Demand Webinar: "Cutting Through the Digital Noise: Real Engagement in the AI Era" with Sendoso

> Quelle: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kn_OUkXZnVQ
> Veröffentlicht: 
> Geclippt am: 2026-07-22

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kn_OUkXZnVQ)

Digital fatigue is real. Buyers are overwhelmed by automated emails, AI-generated content, and endless “personalization” that doesn’t feel sincerely personal. At the same time, marketing teams are under pressure to scale engagement faster than ever with fewer resources and more tools.   
  
So how do you use AI without sounding robotic? And how do you re-introduce human connection without sacrificing scale?   
  
In this conversation-style webinar, now on-demand, Matt Heinz and Patty DuChene explore how modern B2B teams are rethinking engagement in an AI-driven world. They unpack why digital-only strategies are stalling, where human touchpoints matter most, and how better orchestration—not more automation—is the key to making AI feel assistive instead of inhuman.   
  
In this webinar, you’ll learn:   
  
Why “more AI” often creates less connection   
Where human touchpoints deliver the highest impact in modern buying journeys   
How orchestration helps teams scale personalization without sounding fake   
What leading teams are doing to balance efficiency, empathy, and trust   
  
Leave with frameworks and ideas you can apply immediately to balance digital efficiency with more human, high-impact engagement.  
  
Check in soon for our co-branded guide, built with Sendoso, that walks you step-by-step on when to best use AI and automation versus real human touchpoints in your marketing and sales cadences. We'll link in here!
