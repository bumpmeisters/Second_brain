Right now, YouTube Shorts is underpriced relative to other channel. The reason is simple: creators have flooded the platform with content faster than advertisers have built workflows to match. You need a playbook to close that gap before the window shuts.

## Step 3: Amplify — produce the ad and publish

**What you're doing:** Building the actual video with AI tools, then getting it live.

**Recommended toolchain:**

- **HeyGen** — AI video generation with spokesperson or avatar if you don't want to be on camera
- **Captions.ai** — Auto-captions, editing, and text overlays for short-form video
- **Canva** — Static elements, thumbnails, and any branded overlays

## Production Checklist:

- Hook is visible in the first frame — don't bury the lead
- Captions are on — most YouTube Shorts play without sound
- Your CTA appears both verbally and as on-screen text
- Video length is under 60 seconds; 20–35 seconds is the sweet spot for response
- File is formatted vertically (9:16)

## Targeting setup:

- Budget: Start with $20–$30/day minimum to generate statistically useful data
- Bidding: Use Target CPM to control costs while YouTube's algorithm learns
- Placement: YouTube Shorts only (exclude in-stream and other placements for a clean test)
- Geography: US or your primary market
- Run for at least 7 days before drawing conclusions

## Step 4: Evolve — feed the data back into Claude and improve

**What you're doing:** Using performance data to sharpen the next iteration, not just eyeball what worked.

**Metrics to pull after 7–14 days:**

- View rate
- Click-through rate
- Cost per click
- Conversion rate

## The LinkedIn Format Fix

If you're on LinkedIn and frustrated by cost, the problem almost certainly isn't the platform. It's the format.

![05_linkedin_format_efficiency_gap](https://53.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hub/53/hubfs/05_linkedin_format_efficiency_gap.png?width=3240&height=1314&name=05_linkedin_format_efficiency_gap.png)

05\_linkedin\_format\_efficiency\_gap

The single most actionable finding in the benchmark data: Thought Leader Ads deliver roughly 6x better efficiency than Single Image Ads on the same platform. If your LinkedIn mix is concentrated in Single Image, this is the immediate lever to pull.

**What Thought Leader Ads require:**

- A personal LinkedIn profile from someone in your company (founder, CMO, relevant exec)
- A post that already has organic engagement — or one you're willing to boost from zero
- Sponsored amplification of that post to a defined audience

They perform because they look like a real person's content in the feed, not an ad. That trust signal changes the engagement equation entirely.
