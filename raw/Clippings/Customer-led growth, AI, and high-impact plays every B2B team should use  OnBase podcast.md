Why does ABM fall short in large enterprises, and what can teams do to actually fix it? In this episode, Tejal Patel breaks down the real reasons ABM struggles inside big tech and reveals the practical steps any B2B organization can take to improve alignment, data quality, and intent-driven execution.  
  
With 20+ years across Cisco, Microsoft, Nokia, retail, and e-commerce, Tejal brings a rare B2C-meets-B2B perspective on customer centricity, brand building, and scalable ABM. She shares what smaller companies get right, why data quality is often the silent killer behind ABM failures, and how AI is finally enabling true 1:1 personalization at scale.  
  
Whether you work in enterprise marketing, revenue operations, or sales, this conversation offers clear, actionable insights to transform your go-to-market approach.
