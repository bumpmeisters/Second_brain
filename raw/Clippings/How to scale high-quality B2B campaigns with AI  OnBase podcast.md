What does it take to build a winning account-based marketing (ABM) strategy at scale?  
  
In this episode of the OnBase Podcast, host Chris Moody sits down with Craig Abramson, Senior Marketing Leader at Workday, to uncover how data-driven precision targeting fuels smarter, faster, and more personalized go-to-market strategies.  
  
Craig shares his journey from startup scrappiness to enterprise excellence—revealing how clean data, timing, and human intuition drive real ABM success. From turning intent data into deals to balancing personalization with automation, this episode is packed with actionable insights for every B2B marketer.  
  
💡 In this episode, you’ll learn:  
  
Why data quality is the foundation of every successful ABM strategy.  
  
How to use intent signals and predictive scoring to strike at the perfect moment.  
  
The secret to balancing ABX and demand gen for maximum impact.  
  
Why messaging alignment between sales and marketing makes or breaks deals.  
  
How AI amplifies performance—but can’t replace human instinct.  
  
🧠 Key takeaway:  
  
ABM isn’t just about technology, it’s about precision, timing, and the power of clean, actionable data.
