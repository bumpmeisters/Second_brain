Has ABM really “won”—and what happens to Demand Gen now?  
Forrester principal analyst Nora Conklin lifts the lid on convergence, buyer-led growth, and what top teams are doing differently.  
  
In this special Let’s talk ABM, we flip the script and go analyst-side. Nora Conklin shares what she’s seeing across hundreds of B2B teams—from org design to operating models—plus where ABM and Demand are truly converging. Spoiler: the best of ABM is seeping into everything.  
  
We unpack “ABM has won” (a.k.a. “ABM ate Demand”) and why the core question isn’t 1:1 vs. 1:few vs. 1:many—it’s how proactive and how personal your approach is for specific accounts and buying groups. Nora contrasts demand capture with demand seeding, reframes metrics around signals and engagement, and explains why your revenue engine must orient around “helping buyers buy,” not just “helping sales sell.”  
  
We dig into maturity traps (thinking ABM = programmatic lists), the skills every marketer needs next (strategic thinking, collaboration, ops fluency), and change management: bringing Sales, RevOps, Field, and Product into the same planning room so ABM isn’t “a marketing project.”  
  
Finally, Nora looks ahead: self-serve preferences, e-commerce motions for enterprise, and a rising cohort of younger buyers who identify as early deciders—meaning your narrative and signals work must start before a deal even exists.
