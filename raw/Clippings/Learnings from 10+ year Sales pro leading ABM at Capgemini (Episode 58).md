Sales thinks marketing wastes time. Marketing thinks sales doesn’t listen.  
Both are wrong and the real problem is deeper. A former salesperson turned ABM leader explains what actually fixes the disconnect.  
  
Most B2B teams say sales and marketing are “aligned.”  
But in reality, they’re measured differently, speak different languages, and optimize for different wins.  
  
In this episode, Mujesira Dudic shares what changed when she moved from a decade in sales into account-based marketing — and why structure, not effort, is what’s really broken.  
  
We unpack why ABM works best when it’s treated as a go-to-market motion, not a marketing program — and why honesty, patience, and human ownership matter more than tech stacks.  
  
Mujesira Dudic is Director of Account-Based Marketing for Financial Services at Capgemini. With 10 years in enterprise sales before moving into ABM, she brings rare credibility on what sales teams actually trust — and why most marketing fails to earn it.
