SAP reinvented ABM—again.  
Global ABM VP Marcelo Fernandes reveals how a 400K-customer giant built a global playbook that keeps personalization human at scale.  
  
In this episode of Let’s talk ABM, host Declan Mulkeen sits down with Marcelo Fernandes to explore how one of the world’s largest enterprise brands redefined account-based marketing.  
  
Marcelo shares the journey from fragmented regional pilots to a unified global framework and the launch of SAP’s “Strategic Account Marketing” (SAM) program. He explains how the playbook standardizes ABM worldwide while allowing regional creativity, and why SAP’s focus has shifted from deal acceleration to customer lifecycle growth and adoption.  
  
You’ll hear how Marcelo and his 40-person global team support 138 strategic accounts, what measurement looks like in a cloud-first world, and why he believes true ABM must stay account-based—not audience-based.  
  
Marcelo Fernandes is Global Head of Adoption & ABM Vice President at SAP, responsible for developing and scaling the company’s global ABM and Strategic Account Marketing program. With nearly a decade at SAP and a career spanning Latin America, he leads cross-regional teams driving account growth, adoption, and customer lifetime value.
