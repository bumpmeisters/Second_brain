ABM or RAM? Tanium’s Lindsay Baggett explains the difference—and why “random acts of marketing” stall pipeline.  
Discover her ABM-as-a-Service playbook, from listening tours to first-down wins and vertical-led motions.  
  
In this episode of Let’s talk ABM, Lindsay Baggett shares how she built ABM from zero—starting with a company-wide listening tour, defining what ABM is not, and aligning to the only metric that matters: how the business wins. She reframes ABM as a service, not a silo, and breaks down the “first downs over touchdowns” model that keeps teams focused on progress, not perfection.  
  
We unpack her pyramid approach: vertical-centric 1:many as the foundation, insight-driven 1:few as accounts heat up, and targeted 1:1 to de-risk, expand and accelerate late-stage deals. Lindsay details how she mobilizes subject-matter experts for peer-to-peer moments, feedback loops, and executive sponsorships that actually move opportunities.  
  
You’ll hear practical tactics for driving sales adoption (social proof, anecdotes, curiosity), setting expectations (“what ABM is not”), and keeping a true North Star: know your forest before you work the trees.  
  
Lindsay Baggett leads ABM at Tanium, the converged endpoint management platform used by many of the world’s largest enterprises. She specializes in building ABM programs that sales teams adopt and that turn strategic plays into revenue.
