# What’s ahead for virtual events in 2026 — and what marketers should do about it

> Quelle: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GE5y9cTCVx0
> Veröffentlicht: 
> Geclippt am: 2026-07-22

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GE5y9cTCVx0)

Virtual events are becoming increasingly important for B2B marketers, but the rules are changing rapidly. Attendee expectations are rising, engagement is becoming harder to earn, and leadership wants to see a real impact on the pipeline, not just registration counts.  
  
Join analyst Matt Heinz and Zoom experts for a practical conversation on where B2B events are headed in 2026 and what marketers should prioritize to stay ahead.  
  
You’ll learn how to:  
  
\-- Connect events to pipeline, and what top teams do differently  
\-- Shift from lead volume to pipeline influence when measuring success  
\-- Extend the life of webinar content instead of letting it end at “live”  
\-- Reduce friction caused by fragmented tools and handoffs in the event workflow  
  
This session is focused on practical insight, so you’ll leave with ideas you can apply immediately.
