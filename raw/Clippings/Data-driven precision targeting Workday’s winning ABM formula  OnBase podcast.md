What happens when one of the brightest minds in marketing takes on the AI revolution?  
In this episode of OnBase, Jon Miller — co-founder of Marketo and Engagio, former CMO at Demandbase, and now founder of a new AI venture — breaks down how artificial intelligence is reshaping B2B marketing from the inside out.  
  
Jon and host Chris Moody dive into why traditional marketing playbooks are failing, how AI is exposing the “broken math” of MQLs, and what real one-to-one personalization might finally look like. They explore the cultural shifts required for AI success, why leadership matters more than tools, and the mindset every marketer needs to thrive on the jagged frontier of AI.  
  
If you care about the future of growth, personalization, and modern go-to-market strategy, this conversation will challenge how you think about marketing — and what comes next.
