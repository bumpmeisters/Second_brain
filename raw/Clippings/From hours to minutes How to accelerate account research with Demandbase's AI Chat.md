# From hours to minutes: How to accelerate account research with Demandbase's AI Chat

> Quelle: https://www.demandbase.com/resources/case-study/ai-chat-account-research/
> Veröffentlicht: 2026-07-13T16:38:49+00:00

## Demandbase's AI Chat reduces account research time, surfaces deeper insights, and makes account intelligence easier for stakeholders to understand and act on.

- ##### Industry
	Corporate Services
- ##### Company Size
	10,000+
- ##### Solution
	Demandbase AI

#### Executive summary

A global enterprise in the professional services sector relies on timely account intelligence to support business development. However, building a complete picture of an account often required manually pulling information from multiple Demandbase reports, a process that could take hours for a single request.

By adopting Demandbase’s AI Chat, the organization streamlined account research into a simple conversational experience. Teams can now access account insights, visualizations, and recommendations in one place, helping them uncover opportunities faster, have more productive stakeholder conversations, and make more informed go-to-market decisions.

“Demandbase's AI Chat surfaces more information than I could uncover manually, and it does it in a fraction of the time.”

#### Introduction

As account-based strategies became increasingly important, the marketing team needed a faster way to surface account intelligence and share actionable insights across the business.

A Digital Advertising Specialist who led Demandbase adoption across the team recognized both the value and challenge of working with large volumes of account data. While Demandbase provided rich account insights, extracting, synthesizing, and sharing that information often required significant manual effort.

#### The problem

Demandbase provided rich account insights, but finding the right information often meant navigating multiple reports and data sources.

When stakeholders requested a deeper understanding of a specific account, Lizanne gathered information from intent data, engagement analytics, and other Demandbase reports before manually synthesizing the findings into clear recommendations. As teams across the business found value in these reports, requests became increasingly frequent.

The challenge extended beyond collecting data. Once the analysis was complete, they still needed to translate marketing insights into language that business stakeholders could easily understand and apply.

As demand for account intelligence increased, the manual approach became increasingly difficult to scale.

“Demandbase's AI Chat understands the context behind my questions and helps me get to the right insights faster. It's like having an experienced analyst available whenever I need one.”

#### The solution

Demandbase’s AI Chat changed how the organization accesses and acts on account intelligence.

Instead of manually assembling reports, users can ask Demandbase’s AI Chat a question in plain language and receive a response that combines relevant data, visualizations, tables, insights, and recommended actions.

According to Lizanne, one of Demandbase’s AI Chat’s greatest strengths is its simplicity. Users can interact conversationally without needing highly engineered prompts, while seeing how responses are generated and which data sources inform them.

Teams can also save and revisit conversations, making it easier to build on previous analyses and respond to recurring business questions.

Most importantly, Demandbase’s AI Chat helps turn account intelligence into action by presenting insights, recommendations, and supporting data in a format stakeholders across the organization can quickly understand and use.

#### The results

For a recent series of stakeholder meetings, preparation that would have previously required nearly an entire day of manual research and reporting was completed in approximately one hour using Demandbase’s AI Chat.

In addition to reducing research time, Demandbase’s AI Chat helped stakeholders understand and act on account insights more effectively by:

- Consolidating account intelligence into a single conversational experience
- Generating tables, visualizations, and summaries automatically
- Providing recommendations alongside supporting data
- Translating marketing insights into business language stakeholders can readily use
- Reducing the effort required to prepare in-depth account analyses and stakeholder presentations

Stakeholder feedback has been overwhelmingly positive, with team members describing the experience as “mind-blowing” and highlighting how Demandbase’s AI Chat reinforces the value of the data already available within Demandbase.

“An account insight report that used to take an hour now takes 15 minutes with Demandbase's AI Chat.”

#### Key takeaways

- Reduced time spent on account research and meeting preparation from nearly an entire day to one hour.
- Eliminated the need to manually gather information across multiple reports.
- Improved collaboration with sales teams by presenting insights in clear, actionable business language.
- Increased the accessibility and business impact of account intelligence across the organization.

#### Conclusion

For this organization, the value of Demandbase’s AI Chat extends beyond efficiency. By helping teams access account intelligence faster, identify opportunities sooner, and communicate findings more clearly, Demandbase’s AI Chat enables stakeholders to make more informed decisions and focus on the opportunities that matter most.

Demandbase’s AI Chat allows teams to spend less time searching for answers and more time uncovering insights, making informed decisions, and driving business outcomes.
