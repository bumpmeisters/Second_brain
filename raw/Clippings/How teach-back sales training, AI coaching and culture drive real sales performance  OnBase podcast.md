Sales teams have more tools, more training, and more AI than ever — yet performance, confidence, and execution continue to break down in the field.  
  
In this episode of the OnBase podcast, Barry Flaherty and David Thomson explain why traditional sales training fails, and how teach-back sales training and AI coaching help teams build real capability without burning people out.  
  
Drawing on decades of experience across enterprise sales, scale-ups, behavioural science, and enablement, this conversation explores why information alone never creates transformation, how onboarding can be fixed, and why AI should act as a coach, not a cop.  
  
If you lead sales, enablement, revenue operations, or go-to-market teams, this episode offers practical insight you can apply immediately.  
  
In this episode, you’ll learn:  
  
\-why most sales training doesn’t translate into performance  
  
\-how teach-back sales training improves retention and confidence  
  
\-the difference between AI as a coach vs AI as a control mechanism  
  
\-how to reduce ramp time and onboarding failure  
  
\-how to scale sales teams without losing culture or empathy
