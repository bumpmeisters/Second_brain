Running a high-performing, cost-effective demand generation program is becoming more challenging than ever. With persistent economic pressures, securing funds to fuel growth is increasingly difficult, compelling marketers to closely examine their strategies and budgets for greater operational efficiency.  
  
On top of that, outbound engagement rates are dropping as AI saturates the market, making it tougher to stand out without a strong brand presence. In this webinar, experts from agencies and publishers will share insider tips on executing efficient campaigns with speed. Attendees will gain insights into:  
  
\- Step-by-step guidance on market sizing and defining ideal customer profiles (ICPs);  
\- Using data to drive planning across multiple channels;  
\- Strategizing for international markets and adapting to local nuances; and  
\- Leveraging AI and intent data to enhance planning.
