# When AI scales what's already broken. Featuring Lisa Gately, Principal Analyst, Forrester

> Quelle: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DeYk6L4EASc
> Veröffentlicht: 
> Geclippt am: 2026-08-03

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DeYk6L4EASc)

Our guest speaker, Lisa Gately, spent more than 20 years building content systems inside tech companies before she crossed over to study those systems as a Principal Analyst at Forrester.  
  
After hundreds of conversations with marketing leaders, she found something uncomfortable: content problems are rarely about content. They're organizational problems that show up in the content first.  
  
In this episode, Diego Lomanto, CMO at WRITER, sits down with Lisa to explore what actually separates teams thriving in the AI era from those spiraling into scatter. The answer isn't more tools or bigger budgets. It's three things marketers have known for years (topical authority, expert-driven content, and independent validation) and the hard organizational work of prioritizing them.  
  
They also dig into the visibility vacuum: a world where buyers form opinions through AI-generated answers without ever visiting your site. And the Ferrari problem: what happens when your CIO hands every employee the same general-purpose AI and expects marketing to maintain brand differentiation with it.  
  
Alaura Weaver, host of the Humans of AI podcast, joins to introduce the episode and discuss the biggest takeaways with Diego after the interview.  
  
Watch to find out:  
\- Why the keyword-first content model was already broken before AI arrived  
\- What "the visibility vacuum" means for your brand strategy  
\- The single most important relationship every marketing leader needs to maintain right now  
\- Your Monday Morning Action: one thing you can do this week to start building content that only you could create
