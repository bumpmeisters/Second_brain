What happens when you turn ABM into a sprint instead of a marathon?  
ServiceNow’s Celia Slack explains how Pursuit Marketing delivers results — fast.  
  
In this episode of Let’s talk ABM, host Declan Mulkeen sits down with Celia Slack, to explore how one of the world’s most advanced ABM organizations has evolved. Celia unpacks how ServiceNow turned deal-based and opportunity-based marketing into a repeatable “pursuit” model — bringing speed, agility, and focus to high-stakes deals.  
  
You’ll hear how her team scales personalized experiences across global enterprise accounts, the difference between ABM and PBM, and why collaboration, customization, and C-suite buy-in are the true fuel for growth.  
  
Celia also shares ServiceNow’s fourth R (retention!), the company’s unique approach to measurement and dashboards, and her strong views on why one-to-many isn’t “real ABM.”  
  
Celia Slack is EMEA Pursuit Marketing Lead at ServiceNow, where she helps sales teams accelerate key opportunities through strategic, high-impact ABM and deal-based programs. Working closely with Elise Miller and ServiceNow’s global ABM leadership, Celia blends creativity, precision, and pragmatism to drive meaningful results and measurable relationships.
