# AI SDRs explained: How AI agents are redefining inbound pipeline | OnBase podcast

> Quelle: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qHpUCdkP00Y
> Veröffentlicht: 
> Geclippt am: 2026-07-23

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qHpUCdkP00Y)

AI SDR agents are no longer a future concept. They are actively reshaping how B2B teams generate pipeline. In this episode of the OnBase podcast, Chris Moody is joined by Maura Rivera, CMO at Qualified, to break down how AISDRs work, why inbound is the best starting point, and what CMOs need to know to deploy agents without hurting brand trust or sales alignment.  
  
Key insights from this episode:  
\-AI agents represent a fundamental go-to-market shift, not another short-term marketing trend.  
\-AI SDRs work best when positioned as teammates with defined responsibilities, not just software features.  
\-A crawl, walk, run, fly framework reduces risk and accelerates adoption of AI SDRs.  
\-Inbound SDR roles are especially suited for agent automation due to speed, scale, and consistency demands.  
\-Brand voice and buyer trust require ongoing coaching and feedback, even with AI agents.  
\-Agent orchestration across marketing, sales, and revenue operations is critical for success.  
\-CMOs can regain control of pipeline by deploying agents at the most painful bottlenecks.  
\-Standing out in a crowded AI market requires real customer results, not generic “AI-powered” messaging.
