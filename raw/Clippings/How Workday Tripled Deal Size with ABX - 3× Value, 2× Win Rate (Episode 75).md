ABM vs ABX — what’s the real difference?  
Evett Baranov from Workday shares how a dual ABM + ABX model drives 3× deal size and 2× win rates across global markets.  
  
In this Let’s Talk ABM, Evett Baranov explains how Workday runs two account-based engines side by side — a 1:1 ABM team driving Stage 1 opportunities and deal support, and a digital-first ABX team overlaying regional demand gen with targeted account experiences.  
  
Evett breaks down Workday’s data-driven approach to account selection through its FIRE Model (Fit, Intent, Relationship, Engagement) and its proprietary Workday Addressable Market (WAM) — focusing on the top 15 percent of accounts that deliver half of Workday’s revenue. You’ll learn how her team refreshes account lists every six to eight weeks, visualizes journeys in Demandbase, and measures lift beyond MQLs.  
  
We dive into the difference between ABM and ABX in practice — from programmatic ads, content syndication, and personalized landing pages to a test-and-learn mindset that even includes an Out-of-Home experiment in Canberra targeting public-sector leaders. Evett also shares why Workday is embracing ungated experiences to reach “hidden buyers” and why ABMers deserve far more recognition for building trust in complex buying journeys.  
  
Finally, Evett offers clear guidance for ABM leaders on stakeholder alignment, internal education, and positioning ABM as a strategic long-term play — not a quick win.  
  
Evett Baranov is Global Digital ABM Lead (EMEA & APAC) at Workday. She has led Workday’s evolution from traditional ABM to a scalable ABX model built on data, intent, and account experience design. Her work has delivered 3× deal sizes, 2× win rates, and 44 percent faster conversions for priority accounts.
