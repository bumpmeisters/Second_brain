In this episode of the OnBase podcast, host Paul Gibson sits down with Joel Harrison, founder of B2B Marketing, for a deep dive into one of the most important topics shaping modern go-to-market strategy: trust.  
  
Joel explains why trust is now the foundation of every successful B2B relationship and how ABX, thought leadership, and AI can work together to build authentic influence in an era crowded with average content.  
  
Together, Paul and Joel explore what real thought leadership looks like, why AI has created both opportunity and risk for marketers, and how advocacy and emerging “TrustTech” platforms are reshaping how brands build credibility. If you’re looking to elevate your B2B strategy, stand out in the age of AI, and create deeper alignment between marketing, sales, and customer success, this episode is packed with practical insights you can use right away.  
  
In this episode you’ll learn:  
\-Why trust is the true currency of modern B2B  
\-How to build authentic influence with ABX and thought leadership  
\-The biggest trust challenges created by AI  
\-Why average content is everywhere—and how to rise above it  
\-How advocacy and “TrustTech” are transforming customer relationships  
\-What it takes to measure trust beyond vanity metrics
