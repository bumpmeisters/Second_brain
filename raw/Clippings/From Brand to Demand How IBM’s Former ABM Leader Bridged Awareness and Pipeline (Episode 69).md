How do you connect brand, demand, and pipeline in one seamless motion?  
Sophia Agustina reveals how she scaled ABM at IBM—and why true customer centricity takes more than tech.  
  
In this episode of Let’s talk ABM, we dive deep into one of enterprise marketing’s biggest challenges—bridging brand to demand. Sophia Agustina, shares how she built a framework that aligns awareness, engagement, and revenue impact.  
  
From her early ABM experiments in 2012 using Demandbase and Eloqua, to scaling IBM’s global programs with intent data, AI, and deep personalization, Sophia breaks down how to operationalize “brand drives demand” at scale. She explains how IBM transformed its flagship brand moments (Think, Grammys, US Open) into true customer engagement engines—and how she tiered accounts across ABM, Demand Gen, and PLG.  
  
We unpack intent vs. interest, human vs. digital personalization, and her 9Cs framework that puts the customer at the center of every go-to-market motion. Whether you’re an ABM veteran or scaling a new program, this is a masterclass in turning marketing from a department into a growth strategy.  
  
Sophia Agustina is North America Advisory Board Lead for the CMO Council and formerly led IBM’s global Brand-to-Demand strategy. With 20+ years in enterprise marketing, she’s known for bridging brand storytelling with data-driven demand, developing frameworks for personalization, and advancing ABM as a company-wide GTM strategy.
