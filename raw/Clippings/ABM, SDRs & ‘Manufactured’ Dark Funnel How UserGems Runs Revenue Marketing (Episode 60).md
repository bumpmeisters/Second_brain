How do you build pipeline when you can’t afford spray-and-pray demand gen?  
Trinity Nguyen breaks down how UserGems used ABM, SDR alignment, and “manufactured dark social” to fill their funnel.  
  
In this episode of Let’s talk ABM, Declan speaks with Trinity Nguyen about how a bootstrapped go-to-market forced them to think differently: one-to-one ABM, SDRs reporting into marketing, prioritising accounts by job-change triggers, and multi-threading during open opportunities using ads like “Someone on your team is talking to us…”.  
  
Trinity explains how UserGems tracks champions when they change jobs, surfaces that intel in CRM, and then turns it into orchestrated ABX plays across sales, marketing and RevOps. She shares the exact experiment behind their multi-threading ad strategy—resulting in a 33% higher win rate—and why most marketers are standing on the dark funnel instead of actively creating it.  
  
If you care about account-based everything, SDR alignment, or using job-change signals to win faster (and bigger) deals, this one’s packed with practical ideas you can steal tomorrow.  
  
Trinity Nguyen is VP of Marketing at UserGems, a revenue platform that tracks your key champions when they change jobs and turns those moves into pipeline. With a background in product marketing and a remit that includes both marketing and SDRs, she’s known for building highly orchestrated ABM/ABX motions, experimenting aggressively, and making “I keep seeing UserGems everywhere” an actual company OKR.
