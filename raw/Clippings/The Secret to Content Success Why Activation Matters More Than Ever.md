## Key Elements of an Effective Content Activation Strategy

A solid Content [Activation strategy](https://www.pathfactory.com/blog/content-activation-strategy/) includes 3 key elements:

1. Adaptive content journeys: In order for your strategy to be activated, you must be able to recommend what’s next based on behavior, engagement and firmographic data. It gives your buyers flexibility to quickly and easily change their course, knowing you’ll be there for every level.
2. Closed loop approach: Here’s where you’ll identify the most engaged prospects and guide their journey with your content and strategy. This creates a cycle of improvement where deeper engagement with content leads to richer insights into their interests and intentions.
3. Ability to spot engaged intent: This level help you gain access to a new class of data such as assets consumed in a single session to the amount of time spent on that asset. These deeper metrics allow you to spot engaged intent: the leading indicator of marketing performance and sales readiness.
