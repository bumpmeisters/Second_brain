## What is 1-1 account planning in ABM

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_xsL!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fc0e5aff2-4583-494e-94ef-f808cbc7177f_800x499.png)

1-1 account planning in ABM is a continuous nurturing playbook to generate a sales opportunity with a strategic account designed for 12-18 month sales cycles. It requires a full personalization to different stakeholders based on:

- In-depth account research
- Captured 1st party and 3rd party signals
- Engagement and insights captured from interactions with the different stakeholders

Think about it as a puzzle. You create a final fully personalized solution matching account top priorities and challenges tailored to every stakeholder based on different bits of insights and signals.

It’s based on two types of playbooks you run in parallel:

**Time-based touchpoints:** Planned activities throughout the quarter. This month we’re running a webinar, next month we’re launching market research, the following month we’re hosting a workshop.

**Signal-based touchpoints:** React to buying signals as they happen. Hiring announcements. Management changes. High-intent page visits. Content engagement.

Time-based playbooks keep you top-of-mind and help to uncover new insights about account priorities. Signal-based playbooks let you spark the conversation when timing is right.

## The 6A Framework for creating ABM playbooks

We use the 6A framework to orchestrate the ABM playbook. Here are the 6 elements.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Zyzq!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff34ba635-1797-44f8-9b05-5b2cc6060199_1200x966.jpeg)

### 1\. Account Qualification.

How do you decide which accounts to include?

Define account qualification criteria across 4 areas:

- **Firmographics**: Size, location, revenue. Look at patterns from your best accounts.
- **Qualification criteria and product need evidence signals**: What makes an account a good fit? For us at Fullfunnel.io, we prioritize companies with distributed marketing and sales teams that lack alignment. ACV should be >$30k to see positive ROI from ABM.
- **Disqualification criteria**: What makes an account a bad fit? Analyze lost deals to spot patterns. For example, software development companies with <1,000 employees typically want high-volume lead generation, not what we do.
- **Buying committee structure**: Who exactly are your champions, decision-makers, influencers, and blockers? The buying committee ≠ target department + VP/Chief title. Enterprise deals have 10+ people involved.

**[\*Learn more here](https://fullfunnel.io/ideal-customer-profile/)**: [https://fullfunnel.io/ideal-customer-profile/](https://fullfunnel.io/ideal-customer-profile/)

### 2\. Account Segmentation.

Segment all accounts by Tiers, Vendor Awareness, Relationship and Product-Need Evidence signals.

We use 3 segments:

**Cluster ICP accounts**: Not aware of us & product need is unknown. Opportunity likelihood <5%. Focus: create awareness.

**Future Pipeline accounts**: Aware of us & product need is unknown. Opportunity likelihood <30%. Focus: validate need, build relationships.

**Active Focus accounts**: Aware of us and the product need is known. Relationship established. Focus: 1:1 personalization, book discovery calls.

This prioritization is based on: revenue potential, vendor awareness level, relationship strength, product-need evidence.

**[\*Learn more](https://fullfunnel.substack.com/p/selecting-accounts-for-the-abm-program)**: [https://fullfunnel.substack.com/p/selecting-accounts-for-the-abm-program](https://fullfunnel.substack.com/p/selecting-accounts-for-the-abm-program)

### 3\. Account Research

Define information you absolutely must know about an account to create a personalized offer:

- Strategic initiatives and priorities
- Challenges
- Jobs-to-be-done & KPIs of target buyer persona, etc

Split it into desks (what you can find online through publicly available data) and 1-1 research through conversations with the buying committee group.

### 4\. Account Awareness.

Define core activities and content to create awareness among buying committee members.

**[\*Learn more](https://fullfunnel.io/abm-content/)**: [https://fullfunnel.io/abm-content/](https://fullfunnel.io/abm-content/)

### 5\. Account Development and Nurturing.

Define key activities to engage buying committee members and build a relationship with them.

Account development is not “sending nurturing emails.” It’s continuous engagement across time-based and signal-based playbooks.

### 6\. Account Activation.

Personalize the offer. Define bridge activities to book discovery calls.

For Active Focus accounts, this means:

- Project memos outlining how the program could work
- Free strategy sessions with SMEs or clients
- 1:1 expert sessions
- Personalized content hubs

The key principle: every activation play should feel like a natural next step in the relationship, not a pushy sales touchpoint.

## Real example: How we built an account plan for one of the accounts.

The playbook I’ve shared is not rocket science. But it requires a joint marketing and sales analysis of available account data, and the account planning. Here is an example (I’ll simplify it without going into too much detail).

I’ll skip the account account qualification step and start immediately with account prioritization.

**Company:** Eloomi (employee performance management software, Copenhagen).

### 1.Account prioritization.

**Awareness and engagement signals:**

- Downloaded our GTM strategy guide, ICP guide, ABM tactics
- Signed up for Full-Funnel Summit
- Checked ABM and LinkedIn guide
- Newsletter signup
- Website activity: 4 visitors, 64 page views, repetitive engagement over 10+ hours total

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!xtnB!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8a621434-1965-4d2d-86cd-970e592a0223_1600x1147.png)

**Relationship:**

- Connections with the buying committee group: 1 common connection.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!kzSX!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ffa6fa98b-5766-4a1e-a0a7-287e8868b9a4_1236x646.png)

We put this account into a Future Pipeline list (hit our engagement threshold, we have a relationship, but product need is unknown).

Our key goal is to validate their interest in ABM.

### 2\. Account research.

Eloomi raised $55M and are reinvesting in growth. Proof: opened UK and USA offices, hired VP of Sales in each region.

They’re actively hiring SDRs and AEs (3 open AE positions: UK, Nordics, USA remote).

They’re selling an enterprise solution (no pricing on website, demo requests routed to sales).

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Qysx!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fc31b7aaf-c3c1-4ef5-b12c-626110c1956b_1600x779.png)

**Our assumption about current buyer journey stage:**

Eloomi is re-evaluating GTM strategy, aligning with enterprise sales and ABM ([I’m looking for a solution](https://fullfunnel.substack.com/p/b2b-demand-generation-playbook)). Marketing strategy was mentioned in the champion’s job responsibilities.

The champion was engaging with Justin Rowe (Impactable) and Peep Laja (Wynter) content on LinkedIn. They signed up for the ABM webinar we were presenting.

**Here’s the signal-based touchpoint in action:** When we saw Eloomi hiring 3 AEs across different regions, we knew they were scaling sales teams. We immediately shared our content on how to create a central ABM playbook and adjust it to regional teams—directly matching their current challenge.

**Assumed challenges:**

- Not having experience of developing effective ABM programs
- Regional teams are sales-led and are left without marketing support
- Lack of understanding what changes should happen in their GTM

**Draft solution:**

Based on this research, here’s what we thought they needed: developing a cohesive ABM & demand generation strategy enabling sales to talk to warmed-up accounts already aware of the product, increasing marketing contribution to revenue.

Key points:

- How to build ABM proof of concept without organizational changes and budget ramp up
- How to scale it to regions
- How to create awareness among strategic accounts in new regions

### 3\. Account development.

I skipped account awareness because the account was already engaged with our [cluster ABM program](https://fullfunnel.substack.com/p/how-to-target-accounts-in-2026). Here is the plan we mapped out for the next 90 days.

- Manual engagement on LinkedIn with our Champion and VP of Sales in UK and USA to uncover their plans about ABM and validate our hypothesis.
	- Ask our Champion what he is willing to learn at the ABM webinar he signed up for and if they are planning or already running ABM
- Invite to our private invite-only event in Copenhagen
- Nurturing content
	- 11 Common mistakes when launching ABM
		- Marketing and sales executional alignment
		- How to implement account-based marketing from scratch
		- Lead generation is not synonym for ABM
		- 4 stages of ABM
		- 8 pillars of ABM strategy
		- 10 things to succeed with ABM

### 4\. Account activation.

- Suggest a free strategy session to plan their ABM program
- Prepare content hub with ABM case studies and step-by-step pilot planning
- Retargeting content:
	- First inhouse ABM campaign: 2 clients, 6 opps, 76% reply rate (detailed breakdown)
		- How we landed opportunity in 50 minutes (campaign breakdown)
		- Simple ABM campaign: 1 SDR, 1 BDM, generated $300k revenue, 37% reply rate
		- ABM campaign: 11 opps, 3 won deals from 30-account pilot

Notice what we’re NOT doing: sending a cold sequence asking to book a demo. We’re creating value at every touchpoint.

### The outcome.

We didn’t create an immediate sales opportunity with Eloomi. But we built a real relationship.

Our Champion, when we reached out on LinkedIn, said that he’d been following us and likes a lot of [full-funnel ABM](https://fullfunnel.io/account-based-marketing-playbook/). We stayed in touch. I shared the webinar and asked about our hypothesis. He confirmed our hypothesis was “pretty damn close to the truth.”

Most teams stop here and move to the next account. And this is the biggest mistake teams make with ABM if they have a long sales cycle.

The fact that you are working on an account right now doesn’t mean they are ready to buy. You need to adjust your playbook to the length of the buyer journey.

Our next step was inviting him to our in-person event in Copenhagen. He came. When he left eloomi and joined another ICP account, we had a great champion there. Not because we were pushing him all the way long, but because of the great relationship we have established.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!yBA8!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff79a667d-90e9-4717-8713-233c648071ff_876x958.png)

If it’s your strategic account, create and update quarter account plans:

- Track the engagement and signals. Plan together (marketing and sales) the best next steps.
- Use time-based playbooks for continuous nurturing.
- Create and distribute relevant content based on the newly captured insights.

This is what 1-1 account planning and continuous nurturing actually looks like. Not a 3-week cadence. An 18-month relationship built on value, not product pitches.
