How can B2B marketing leaders balance category creation with day-to-day execution?  
  
In this episode of the Demand Bites podcast, host Ross Howard sits down with Mike Hicks, CMO of AppSpace, to explore how modern marketing leaders can think strategically while still delivering short-term results.  
  
In just 15 minutes, Mike shares lessons from his journey from agency life to CMO, explains why category creation matters more than ever, and breaks down how to balance long-term brand building with immediate demand generation goals. He also offers practical advice for marketing leaders navigating the strategic vs. operational divide—and for those looking to take the next step in their careers.
