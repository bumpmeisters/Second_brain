How can B2B marketers stay efficient and resilient during economic uncertainty?  
  
In this episode of the Demand Bites podcast, host Ross Howard sits down with demand generation expert Jana Pelchen to unpack practical strategies for navigating economic downturns, improving marketing efficiency, and aligning sales and marketing for long-term impact.  
  
In just 15 minutes, Jana shares actionable advice on avoiding panic-driven decisions, using first-party data to improve targeting, and optimising cost efficiency to protect CAC and pipeline. The conversation also explores how AI, organic channels, and smarter measurement can help recession-proof your demand generation strategy.
