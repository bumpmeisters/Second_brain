### Hot Tips For Getting a Jump on Smarter Content Distribution

**Pair with human input**

Language and your brand’s lexicon are nuanced, specific and unique. Plus, many times, your content will be more powerful with a link to cultural insights and emotional stories. By inputting these valuable content themes into datasets, you get supercharged ideas that can take on even the most villainous B2B subset.

Leverage data-driven, user-centric content strategies and insights to double-down on what you’ll create next: and what you’ll do with that content. Review. Optimize. Change. Repeat.

There are human instincts AI can’t touch or recognize. When it comes to content, fuelling AI-generated pieces with more emotion and expression can helps your pieces soar. The same goes for leveraging data: use your gut to uncover opportunities identified by intelligence to really go the distance.
