What happens when you combine fashion, AI, and ABM?  
Pixelz CMO Katrine Rasmussen shares how she turned enterprise sales into an experience-led, community-powered ABM engine.  
  
IIn this episode of Let’s talk ABM, Declan sits down with Katrine Rasmussen, about the company retouching 40–50,000 e-commerce fashion images per day for brands like Zalando, Mango, and Bestseller.  
  
Katrine explains how Pixelz made a strategic decision to stop playing the volume game and instead go all-in on big “whale” accounts—the top 1% of customers generating 60% of revenue. She walks through how they defined a razor-sharp ICP, narrowed their positioning around fashion and high-volume studios, and built an ABM motion designed for large, complex, enterprise deals.  
  
At the heart of their strategy is FLOW, Pixelz’s flagship event series turned experience and community platform. From LA to Berlin, New York, and London, FLOW brings together studio directors, photographers, and decision-makers from top fashion brands—creating an environment where relationships, trust, and community drive pipeline.  
  
We talk account selection, experience marketing, attribution, Sales–Marketing alignment, Slack communities, and why Katrine believes ABM is not a marketing strategy—it’s a business strategy.  
  
Katrine Rasmussen is CMO at Pixelz, an AI-powered post-production partner for leading fashion e-commerce brands. With a background in B2B marketing and enterprise go-to-market, she’s known for building experience-led ABM programs, sharpening ICP and positioning, and turning events into high-impact growth engines.
