# Customer-led growth, AI, and high-impact plays every B2B team should use | OnBase podcast

> Quelle: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WCvA7-hCFsc

In this episode of OnBase, Corrina Owens breaks down what most B2B teams are missing when it comes to account-based marketing (ABM), customer expansion, and AI-powered go-to-market strategy.  
  
Corrina shares her non-traditional path into B2B SaaS, why generalists thrive in modern marketing, and how ABM has evolved far beyond “tactics” like direct mail and targeted ads. She dives deep into first-party data, ICP clarity, and the biggest blind spot in B2B: companies only land ~30% of potential ARR on first purchase, but stop all coordination afterward.  
  
You’ll learn how to build customer-led growth, create meaningful brand moments, run high-impact ABM plays, and use AI to democratize data and accelerate alignment across teams.  
  
If you’re in B2B marketing, sales, RevOps, or go-to-market leadership, this conversation is packed with insight you can apply immediately.  
  
What you’ll learn:  
  
\-Why ABM is a full go-to-market operating model  
  
\-How to uncover your real ICP using first-party data  
  
\-Why customer expansion is the #1 missed revenue opportunity  
  
\-High-impact “give-first” ABM plays any team can run  
  
\-How AI is transforming GTM strategy  
  
\-The human skills sellers must master in a digital-first world
