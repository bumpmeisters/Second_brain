How can B2B marketers turn thought leadership and lead nurturing into real pipeline revenue?  
  
In this episode of the Demand Bites podcast, host Ross Howard is joined by Julie Smutko, Head of Demand Generation at Verse.ai, to unpack how modern B2B teams can build trust, qualify leads effectively, and nurture prospects all the way to revenue.  
  
In just 15 minutes, Julie shares her demand generation journey and the practical frameworks Verse.ai uses to align marketing and sales, improve lead scoring, and turn content-driven strategies—like thought leadership and ROI calculators—into measurable pipeline impact.
