### Executive summary

Founded in 2024 and based in San Francisco, [Breakout](https://getbreakout.ai/) is a leader in AI-driven B2B engagement, empowering companies to identify, engage effectively with, and convert anonymous website visitors into high-value pipeline.

By integrating the Demandbase world-class account intelligence directly into its platform via the **Powered By** partnership, Breakout provides its customers with the high-fidelity firmographic and intent data they need to prioritize outreach and personalize engagement in real-time.

### Introduction

B2B SaaS companies spend millions of dollars on targeted campaigns across ads, e-mail, and social media only to route every visitor to its website, a static page that too often becomes a dead-end road when it comes to prospect engagement. Considering that B2B organizations typically spend 8% of their annual revenues on marketing, according to [Forrester](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/the-average-b2b-firm-invests-8-of-revenue-in-marketing-but-thats-not-the-whole-story/), these organizations clearly expect a large and predictable return on their massive investments.

Breakout is dedicated to helping B2B SaaS organizations bridge the gap between web traffic and revenue generation. The Breakout platform engages website visitors in real-time and automates follow-ups for visitors who bounce. Unlike traditional rule-based chatbots that require complex, lengthy setups, Breakout *offers a faster, more agile inbound AI SDR* designed for the modern inbound marketing motion.

Breakout provides precision and identifies exactly who is on a website, providing its users with the tools to effectively and immediately engage those visitors. The platform thus ensures that organic and paid traffic investment translates directly into measurable business opportunities for its customers.

### The problem

Many B2B SaaS companies face a significant conversion hurdle. While they invest massive resources into attracting traffic to their website, they lack the visibility necessary to see which high-value accounts are behind those website visits. Without this “account-level” lens, marketing and sales teams are flying blind and cannot:

- Identify anonymous businesses browsing their site.
- Prioritize leads based on account fit or buying stage.
- Deliver personalized messaging that resonates with specific industries or geographies.

To solve this visibility problem, Breakout needed a data foundation that was not only accurate, but also carried the industry reputation required to build trust with its base of enterprise customers.

### The solution

By leveraging the Demandbase API, Breakout integrated premium account intelligence directly into its core product offering. This “Powered By” partnership allows Breakout to deliver:

- **Instant account identification:** Breakout identifies the specific accounts visiting a customer’s site in real-time, using Demandbase’s proprietary IP-to-company mapping.
- **Rich firmographic context:** For every identified visitor, Breakout surfaces Demandbase data points such as industry, company size, and location to inform better targeting.
- **AI-Driven lead scoring:** Combining visitor behavior with Demandbase’s account data, Breakout provides a lead score that helps sales teams prioritize the most active and relevant accounts.
- **Rapid time-to-value:** Because the data is integrated seamlessly, Breakout customers experience faster setup times compared to legacy rule-based platforms.

### The results

The partnership has enabled Breakout to deliver superior value to its B2B SaaS clients, including:

- **Higher conversion:** Breakout customers can now turn formerly anonymous inbound traffic into actionable opportunities: now, Breakout customers know exactly who to target and what those targets want.
- **Improved sales efficiency:** With AI-driven scoring and Demandbase-backed prioritization, sales teams focus their energy on the highest-potential accounts.
- **Enhanced personalization:** Users can dynamically tailor their website engagement and messaging based on the rich industry and geographic data provided by Demandbase.

High-quality data is foundational for any successful AI-driven marketing motion. Key factors in Breakout’s decision to partner with Demandbase included:

- **Data superiority:** Demandbase is recognized as a market leader for high-quality B2B data, including deep firmographic, geographic, and industry insights.
- **AI leadership:** Demandbase’s strong presence and innovation in AI products aligned perfectly with Breakout’s technology-first mission.
- **Market reputation:** Leveraging Demandbase data provides Breakout’s customers with the confidence that their engagement strategies are built upon a “gold standard” source of truth.

### Key takeaways

What can B2B organizations learn from Breakout’s successful partnership with the **Powered By** program? Here are a few:

- **Data as a foundation:** For AI products like Breakout, the quality of the underlying data determines the quality of the output and the ultimate ROI for the customer.
- **Seamless integration:** The Powered By model allows partners to focus on their unique AI innovation while relying on Demandbase for the heavy lifting of data sourcing and maintenance.
- **Trust and reliability:** Partnering with a recognized leader like Demandbase accelerates market adoption and builds immediate credibility for emerging tech platforms.

### Looking ahead

As Breakout continues to scale, its partnership with Demandbase will evolve to include deeper intent signals and expanded datasets. Together, Breakout and Demandbase are redefining how B2B companies leverage AI to transform website visitors into a more responsive, data-driven, and high-velocity pipeline.

### Conclusion

Visibility matters for turning hard-won website visitors into personalized engagement and ROI. Breakout wanted to give its enterprise customers the precise, data-fueled visibility necessary to fuel smarter and more revenue-generating engagement. Demandbase is proud to help them do exactly that.
