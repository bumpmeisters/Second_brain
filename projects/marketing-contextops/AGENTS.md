# Marketing ContextOps Agent

This directory is an inactive control shell for the future Marketing ContextOps sibling system. The root `AGENTS.md` remains fully controlling. The active ContextOps capability remains in `projects/No and low code_1st Marketing Agent/` until a separately approved cutover.

## Current authority

- This shell has no operational or canonical authority.
- Do not route ContextOps work or resolve root skill wrappers to this directory.
- Do not create or copy frameworks, skills, workflows, templates, tools, wiki pages, project assets, sources, evaluations, integrations, agents, or runtime records here.
- Do not move, rename, edit, retire, or repoint any legacy component or external reference under this checkpoint.
- Decisions still held at later gates, including shared-capability ownership, must not be inferred from this shell.

## Target responsibility after activation

If later activated, Marketing ContextOps may own reusable, evidence-aware methods and contracts for:

- company evidence intake and claim governance;
- market context and buying contexts;
- segmentation and audience understanding;
- framework fit and proof-led positioning;
- GTM context mapping and downstream handoffs;
- producer-validator orchestration and ContextOps evaluation.

This target role is descriptive, not executable authority.

## Output and custody boundary

- Root Second Brain owns source custody and durable vault knowledge.
- Marketing ContextOps may own reusable methods and contracts only after activation.
- The initiating company or thematic source project owns every concrete analysis, decision, Brand Core, positioning, GTM, campaign, approval, run, and episodic-learning artifact.
- ABM Operating System owns specialized account-based GTM execution methods.
- Content Operating System owns expression, channel, content-object, publication-state, and bounded editorial-learning contracts.
- Never create a second raw library, company-asset repository, Brand Core, or downstream content store here.

## Stage discipline after activation

Each stage must consume an explicit upstream packet, perform one bounded decision job, label unsupported assumptions, and hand off without silently making downstream decisions. This principle does not activate any current workflow.

## Review and maintenance

- Keep this file concise; procedures belong in reviewed skills or workflows after cutover.
- Record architecture or authority decisions in `decisions/log.md`; the log cannot grant its own authority.
- Activation requires exact component ownership, wrapper and reference transition manifests, regression checks, and a tested rollback path.
