## Contradiction And Rejection Register

| Claim or tension | Treatment | Reason | Canonical consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| “ABM works 100% of the time” | Reject literal transfer | NTT source reports losses, failures, demotion, and measurement uncertainty | None |
| One-to-many cannot be ABM | Do not promote | One ServiceNow practitioner’s taxonomy does not override defined motions | None |
| Mapped contacts are already qualified | Reject | Identity and plausible role do not prove need, authority, timing, consent, or readiness | Existing qualification gates stand |
| Personal identifiers and person-level tracking should be default scaling tactics | Reject default | Necessity, lawful basis, transparency, proportionality, platform terms, security, and objection remain unresolved | Existing privacy/human governance stands |
| Omnichannel ubiquity proves success | Reject | Recall may indicate awareness or fatigue; incrementality is unknown | Selective, measured execution stands |
| Correlated media spend and pipeline establish causality | Reject | Selection effects and alternative mechanisms remain | Contribution and evidence limits stand |
| Reported capacity and engagement figures are reusable benchmarks | Contextualize only | Definitions, cohorts, methods, and denominators are absent | No benchmark added |
| A new plant inside an existing logo is automatically net-new ABM | Reject automatic routing | The installed relationship changes access and evidence even when the local buying group is new | Route by customer state, then adapt service and route |
| A partner or integrator is only an activation channel | Reject simplification | It may control access, carry commercial rights, co-sell, or require separate relationship management | Observe an ecosystem-route object |
| "AI-native" or "white box" establishes AI quality and governance | Reject | Neither label supplies evals, error rates, lawful basis, permissions, monitoring, or change control | Existing AI and human-governance controls stand |
| Account progression stages are Growth Motions | Reject | Stages describe account state or workflow; motions describe the managed commercial/customer state and service logic | Existing two-axis architecture stands |
| Replacing MQLs with engagement proves revenue impact | Reject | Engagement is a leading indicator and can become another proxy without opportunity, customer, comparison, and definition evidence | Layered measurement stands |
| Pilot success with champions is sufficient for scale | Reject | Champion selection and consulting support can mask adoption, capacity, and operating-system weaknesses | Require a non-champion adoption and scale gate |

No case currently contradicts the canonical five Growth Motions or six Growth Execution Loop phases. The new cases expose sharper implementation boundaries, but the evidence still mainly **confirms and contextualizes** the blueprint. Candidate extensions belong below the canonical level until independent evidence accumulates.
