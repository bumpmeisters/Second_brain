# ABM Practice

**Summary:** Private, evidence-bounded diagnostics and evaluation assets derived from the Enterprise Growth System. The current practice layer is limited to synthetic and public-evidence work.

## Current boundary

- The first six-case Blueprint Challenge cycle is complete and Gate G5-approved.
- Real-life application evidence is not expected in the foreseeable future.
- Synthetic cases can test internal consistency, discrimination, failure handling, and context sensitivity.
- Synthetic results cannot establish effectiveness, business impact, transferability, or real-world validation.
- No client or company data may enter this practice layer until a client-data contract and Gate G6 approval are in force.
- No artifact in this directory is approved for external publication or private consulting use.

## Practice components

- [ABM Situation Diagnostic](abm-situation-diagnostic.md) - routes an organization to its dominant intervention need, appropriate Growth Motions, resource configuration, first validation step, and stop rules.

## Evaluations

- [Synthetic Scenario Evaluation](evaluations/abm-situation-diagnostic-synthetic-evaluation.md) - contrasts an advanced enterprise, an immature enterprise, and a resource-constrained mid-market scenario.
- [Enterprise Growth System Decision Test Suite](evaluations/enterprise-growth-system-decision-test-suite.md) - adversarial routing and governance cases for framework regression testing.

## Governance

- **Current review gate:** G6 pending.
- **Permitted now:** private design, synthetic evaluation, public-evidence comparison, and internal refinement.
- **Prohibited now:** real client input, client recommendations, claims of validation, canonical promotion, or publication.
- **Next review decision:** whether the diagnostic recommendations differ materially across the synthetic scenarios and the control boundaries are sufficient for continued private research use.

## Deferred G6a path

- Build a relevant LinkedIn community before opening a research-only G6a review.
- Use LinkedIn posts to develop authority and recruit qualified reviewers, not as the blind-test response surface.
- Freeze the diagnostic and scoring contract before recruitment, then use private randomized case packs so reviewers cannot see prior answers.
- Evaluate reviewer agreement, unsafe decisions, acceptable alternatives, confidence, and usability without claiming business effectiveness.
- See the [decision log](../decisions/log.md) for the readiness signals, publication boundary, and separation between G6a research review and later Gate G6 consulting use.
