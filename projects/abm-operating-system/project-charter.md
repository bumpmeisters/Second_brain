# ABM Operating System Project Charter

**Summary**: The ABM Operating System is Rolf's private, evidence-driven research, practice, and authority environment for designing and operationalizing adaptive account-based growth systems. The [[projects/abm-operating-system/frameworks/enterprise-growth-system|Enterprise Growth System]] is its strategic kernel; real cases continuously test, bound, and improve that kernel.

---

## 1. Executive intent

ABM is a core professional field for Rolf and a long-term career asset. This project must turn accumulated knowledge into a coherent capability that can:

- strengthen Rolf's authority as an enterprise ABM strategic architect;
- demonstrate hands-on program, campaign, and experience execution;
- support future consulting opportunities and employment responsibilities;
- transform evidence into reusable intellectual capital rather than disconnected notes; and
- improve the Enterprise Growth System through disciplined challenge and real application.

The project is not merely an ABM library. It is intended to become an **ABM Operating System** spanning research, theory, practice, authority, and learning.

## 2. Professional positioning

### Primary identity

**Enterprise ABM strategic architect with execution credibility.**

### Capability promise

Rolf can diagnose the commercial and organizational context, design an appropriate ABM system, mobilize cross-functional stakeholders, translate strategy into programs and campaigns, lead implementation, measure progression, and help the organization operate the capability sustainably.

### Intervention scope

The system must eventually support four context-dependent intervention paths:

1. **Foundation** — assess readiness and establish an ABM capability from the ground up.
2. **Transformation** — turn fragmented, technology-led, or campaign-only activity into a coordinated growth system.
3. **Activation** — translate account, cluster, opportunity, or customer strategy into executable campaigns and experiences.
4. **Scaling** — make a working ABM model repeatable, measurable, and operationally sustainable.

These are client states, not competing specializations. Diagnosis determines which path is appropriate.

## 3. Design centre and adaptability

The primary design centre is **enterprise and mid-market B2B**, especially environments with meaningful account value, complex buying groups, long decisions, relationship dependency, and cross-functional delivery.

The system must not equate company size with the appropriate ABM model. It should configure its recommendations using four dimensions:

`commercial complexity × operating readiness × company archetype × available resources`

Relevant resources include people, budget, data, technology, content modularity, sales commitment, executive support, analytical capability, and delivery capacity.

The canonical core should remain industry-agnostic. Business-model, industry, maturity, and resource adaptations should be explicit modules with documented applicability boundaries. Enterprise technology, SaaS, traditional enterprise, professional services, industrial, logistics, channel-led, and resource-constrained organizations may therefore use different implementations without requiring different definitions of ABM.

## 4. System architecture

```text
ABM Operating System
│
├── Evidence
│   └── Sources, research, experience and observations
│
├── Blueprint Challenges
│   └── Stand-alone cases tested against the strategic kernel
│
├── Pattern Intelligence
│   └── Recurring patterns, conditions, exceptions and contradictions
│
├── Enterprise Growth System
│   └── Canonical motions, principles and decision logic
│
├── Practice
│   └── Diagnostics, programs, campaigns, workshops and tools
│
├── Authority
│   └── Website, LinkedIn, articles, presentations and public tools
│
└── Learning
    └── Application results returned through governed promotion
```

### 4.1 Evidence

Evidence may include public cases, practitioner interviews, vendor material, analyst or academic work, user experience, and future application observations. Every item retains provenance, date, context, ownership, potential bias, confidentiality, publication status, and evidentiary limitations.

An evidence item is not automatically a fact, principle, or recommendation.

### 4.2 Blueprint Challenges

Each meaningful case is reconstructed as a **Blueprint Challenge Record**. It must explain the business problem, context, method, sequence, reported outcomes, relevant Growth Motion, relevant Growth Execution Loop phase, blueprint implications, applicability conditions, risks, and practical adaptation.

The record must distinguish:

- what the source reports;
- what is inferred;
- what remains unknown or unverified;
- what is adapted through the Enterprise Growth System; and
- what another organization can legitimately reuse.

### 4.3 Pattern Intelligence

Pattern Intelligence connects individual cases to durable principles. It records where a pattern appears, where it fails, which contexts modify it, whether the sources are independent, and how strong the evidence is.

Case counts alone do not determine confidence. Independence, documentation quality, proximity to execution, commercial bias, contextual diversity, contradictory evidence, and repeated application all matter.

### 4.4 Enterprise Growth System

The Enterprise Growth System is the strategic decision kernel and current North Star. Its five Growth Motions and six-phase Growth Execution Loop organize interpretation and action without forcing every organization into one funnel, tier model, technology stack, or campaign design.

The framework is a living reference architecture, not immutable doctrine. It changes only through explicit evidence review, bounded interpretation, and human approval.

### 4.5 Practice

The private practice layer will eventually contain consulting and operating intellectual property such as:

- readiness and maturity diagnostics;
- resource and capability assessments;
- motion and portfolio routing;
- operating-model and governance design;
- program architecture and roadmaps;
- account, cluster, pursuit, and expansion planning;
- campaign and meaningful-experience design;
- measurement and learning systems;
- workshops, templates, facilitation guides, and capability transfer.

Client-specific evidence and deliverables remain in separate client or company workspaces. Only approved, generalized learning may return to the canonical ABM system.

### 4.6 Authority

Public authority outputs are derived from governed knowledge; they are not direct copies of the private vault. Their purpose is to make Rolf's reasoning, evidence discipline, strategic architecture, and practical execution capability visible.

### 4.7 Learning

Every meaningful application should capture what changed, what did not, which assumptions failed, what transferred, and what should be reconsidered. Learning re-enters the system as evidence and must pass the same promotion process as external material.

## 5. Knowledge-promotion governance

### Promotion pipeline

```text
Raw evidence
    ↓
Extract tactical insight
    ↓
Map to Enterprise Growth System
    ↓
Challenge existing blueprint
    ↓
Review evidence and boundaries
    ↓
Confirm • Extend • Contradict • Contextualize • Reject
    ↓
Candidate pattern or principle
    ↓
Human-approved canonical promotion
```

### Promotion outcomes

- **Confirm** — adds support to an existing principle without changing its meaning.
- **Extend** — adds useful scope, mechanism, or execution detail.
- **Contradict** — records material conflict requiring investigation or revision.
- **Contextualize** — establishes where, when, or for whom a practice applies.
- **Reject** — preserves the source but prevents weak, misleading, or non-transferable material from entering the canonical model.

### Promotion standard

A candidate principle should normally have multiple independent evidence points, a plausible mechanism, clear assumptions, known boundaries, practical decision value, and no unresolved contradiction that would make the guidance unsafe or misleading. A single case may generate a useful hypothesis or playbook but does not automatically justify changing the strategic kernel.

Rolf retains the final decision right for canonical promotion.

## 6. Confidentiality and publication governance

Knowledge intake and external publication are separate processes. Absence of sensitive data does not imply permission to publish.

### Publication states

- **Private** — usable only inside the ABM Operating System.
- **Internal reusable** — may inform private analysis and delivery.
- **Sanitized reusable** — a genuinely generalized method may be prepared for external use.
- **Approval required** — external use requires Rolf's explicit decision.
- **Public** — approved for unrestricted publication in its reviewed form.

### Default rule

**Methods may be reusable by default; real cases are not.**

Generalized principles, original frameworks, synthetic examples, blank templates, diagnostics, decision trees, and sanitized operating tools may be prepared for external use when ownership and source-use rules permit. Any artifact involving a real company, client, account, person, internal observation, performance result, or recognizable situation remains approval-gated.

Rolf will flag known sensitive uploads. Codex must ask when personal data, confidential business information, unclear ownership, recognizability, or publication risk is apparent. No external publication or external sharing is authorized by this charter.

## 7. Public authority strategy

### Brand and market

- **Brand:** Rolf's personal brand
- **Language:** English-first
- **Market:** international
- **Primary reader:** VP or Head of ABM, Demand, or GTM
- **Secondary reader:** marketing leaders considering ABM

### Channel roles

- **Personal website or portfolio:** canonical home for the public framework, full case analyses, selected tools, and durable career evidence.
- **LinkedIn:** discovery, discussion, short-form derivatives, visuals, and distribution.

### Flagship series

**Testing the Enterprise Growth System** is a case-based theory-building series. It asks what each real ABM case teaches about coordinated enterprise growth rather than presenting a company's tactics as universally transferable.

The editorial voice combines:

- **constructive evidence-led critique** as the dominant voice; and
- **practical operator interpretation** as the secondary voice.

Each public case should explain what works, why it works in that context, what does not transfer, what the Enterprise Growth System learns, and what an ABM leader should do differently.

### Launch strategy

Use a paired launch:

1. A concise manifesto provisionally titled **Why ABM Needs an Enterprise Growth System—not Another Collection of Tactics**.
2. **Testing the Enterprise Growth System #1: What ServiceNow Teaches Us About Pursuit Marketing**.

The public framework should reveal the North Star, five Growth Motions, Growth Execution Loop, core distinctions, and evidence method. Detailed diagnostics, implementation procedures, templates, client tools, and the complete challenge database remain private consulting IP.

### Publishing cadence

- One substantial case analysis per month.
- Weekly LinkedIn derivatives from the monthly research cycle.
- Progressive disclosure of framework components through the cases.
- A consolidated Enterprise Growth System Public Edition and cross-case synthesis after the first six cases.

### First six-month success criteria

1. Six strong, methodologically consistent case analyses are published or publication-ready.
2. A coherent public edition of the Enterprise Growth System exists.
3. Respected ABM and GTM professionals provide substantive engagement, challenge, citation, sharing, or dialogue.

Impressions and follower counts are secondary signals. Consulting demand and career opportunities are desirable later outcomes, not the first research-cycle optimization target.

## 8. Relationship to the Marketing Agent

The ABM Operating System is the canonical owner of ABM research, principles, methods, and intellectual property. The existing No/Low-Code Marketing Agent may consume approved frameworks, diagnostics, and methods when working on a company context.

The integration must prevent duplication and semantic drift:

- canonical ABM components have one owner;
- the Marketing Agent references versioned, approved components;
- company workspaces retain their own evidence and deliverables;
- application learning returns only through an explicit review path; and
- neither project silently edits the other's canonical material.

The existing Enterprise Growth System and ABM case assets remain in their current locations until a reviewed implementation and migration plan establishes target ownership, links, and rollback-safe sequencing.

## 9. AI evolution

AI capability will mature only after the underlying reasoning and outputs have been validated.

1. **Strategic Copilot** — retrieve evidence, challenge assumptions, map cases, and improve Rolf's reasoning.
2. **Structured Designer** — produce reviewed diagnostics, program architectures, campaigns, and authority drafts.
3. **Workflow Engine** — execute repeatable, validated methods with explicit gates.
4. **Autonomous Specialists** — perform bounded research, challenge, promotion, program, campaign, or authority tasks under defined permissions and human review.

Automation must not outrun methodological maturity. Human judgment retains commercial priorities, framework promotion, relationship decisions, client commitments, and external publication rights.

## 10. AI work blueprint

### Goal

Build a durable ABM capability that compounds research into stronger theory, transforms theory into practical delivery, and converts validated knowledge into credible public authority.

### Spec

- **Audience:** Rolf first; later ABM/GTM leaders, marketing leaders considering ABM, employers, and consulting clients.
- **Context:** Enterprise and mid-market B2B with adaptations for resource maturity, commercial complexity, and company archetype.
- **Sources:** Governed public evidence, user experience, approved client learning, and canonical project knowledge.
- **Boundaries:** Preserve provenance; separate evidence from truth; protect sensitive and recognizable cases; do not automate unvalidated judgment.
- **Output:** A private operating system, an evolving Enterprise Growth System, reusable practice assets, and an English-first authority platform.
- **First checkpoint:** Approve this charter before designing the project structure or moving assets.
- **Decisions still to confirm:** Initial six-case portfolio, detailed editorial standard, public-edition boundary, project folder architecture, migration sequence, and consulting offer design.

### Verification

- **Criteria:** Goal fit, framework fidelity, evidence traceability, contextual applicability, practical usefulness, confidentiality, publication control, and maintainability.
- **Critic perspective:** An experienced enterprise ABM leader who is skeptical of generic frameworks, vendor determinism, campaign-only ABM, and unsupported success claims.
- **External signal:** Independent case evidence, real application results, substantive practitioner feedback, and documented exceptions.
- **Failure conditions:** The project becomes a clipping archive; the framework changes after isolated claims; public content outruns evidence; consulting tools cannot adapt to context; duplicated canonical knowledge diverges; automation bypasses human judgment; or activity metrics replace commercial and learning outcomes.

### Environment update

- **Save or update:** Project charter, later project instructions, evidence schema, Blueprint Challenge template, pattern register, framework versions, practice tools, publication register, and learning log.
- **Always do:** Preserve provenance, label uncertainty, record applicability boundaries, keep private and public layers distinct, and connect outputs back to evidence.
- **Ask first:** External publication, use of real-company cases, canonical framework promotion, source relocation, major project restructuring, and automation that changes decision rights.
- **Never do:** Treat vendor claims as neutral proof, publish recognizable cases by inference, equate activity with impact, copy tactics without context, or let AI independently make relationship, commercial-priority, or publication decisions.

## 12. Non-goals for the first phase

- Migrating every ABM source or existing wiki page.
- Building an autonomous ABM agent.
- Publishing confidential, recognizable, or unapproved cases.
- Producing a universal ABM maturity score before validating its variables.
- Treating the Enterprise Growth System as finished doctrine.
- Launching a separate public lab brand.
- Optimizing early publishing for volume, followers, or lead capture.
