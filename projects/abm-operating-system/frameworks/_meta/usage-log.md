## 2026-07-22 | Enterprise Growth System | ServiceNow Pursuit Marketing

- **Version:** 0.1.0.

- **Selected modules:** 1:1 account system, Customer Expansion, portfolio/capacity, buying groups and relationship capital, content/experience, measurement, and AI controls.
- **Inputs available:** Public practitioner transcript, retained standalone SOP, canonical Enterprise Growth System.
