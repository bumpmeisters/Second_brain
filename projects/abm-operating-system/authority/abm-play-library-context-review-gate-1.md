# ABM Play Library Context Project — Review Gate 1

## Decision: taxonomy working assumptions and external research agenda approved

**Status:** Approved by Rolf on 24 July 2026<br>

## Executive verdict

The 63-entry Library is broad enough to justify a dedicated workstream and internally coherent enough to proceed to external challenge. It should not yet be treated as complete or validated.

The internal crosscheck produces:

- 56 provisional `retain` recommendations;
- 2 `reclassify` candidates;
- 3 definition extensions;
- 2 editorial rename candidates;
- 11 overlap clusters with explicit working boundaries;
- 7 possible gap areas for external research;
- 12 evidence-matrix claims awaiting human review.

The strongest internal architecture is:

`commercial or customer state -> Situation Play -> optional Play Format -> tactics and channels`

System Plays sit underneath this chain as repeatable operating capabilities. This architecture is a useful working model, not yet an externally verified taxonomy.

## Gate 1 decisions

### D1 — Treat Find the Spark as a reclassification question

**Recommendation:** Approve.

Its current job is to turn signals into a hypothesis. That may be a bounded instance of System Play `Turn Signals Into Stories` or the Intelligence & Hypothesis loop phase rather than a standalone Situation Play.

### D2 — Treat Turn Interest Into a Meeting as a reclassification question

**Recommendation:** Approve.

A meeting is normally a progression event. External research should test whether the durable Play is actually context transfer or opportunity creation. Until then SP-18 remains in the baseline.

### D3 — Extend Plant the Flag with a motion-routing boundary

**Recommendation:** Approve.

It belongs in the ABM Library only when the managed unit includes a deliberately selected account population. Pure market entry remains Market Demand.

### D4 — Extend Target the Competitor's Clients with a displacement admission rule

**Recommendation:** Approve.

Competitor usage is a population attribute. The Play requires an evidenced unmet need, change, risk or transition condition.

### D5 — Test Tiger Team as a component versus peer format

**Recommendation:** Approve.

The internal evidence supports temporary cross-functional concentration, but does not yet show whether Tiger Team is meaningfully distinct from Pursuit Marketing.

### D6 — Research a less adversarial name for Win Over the Blocker

**Recommendation:** Approve.

The strategic job remains useful. The name should not encourage teams to manipulate or bypass legitimate buyer risk. `Earn the Skeptic` is the leading working alternative.

### D7 — Research a reciprocal name for Make Sales Sign the Contract

**Recommendation:** Approve.

The working-contract mechanism is well supported internally. `Contract Before Campaign` is the leading alternative because readiness obligations are mutual.

## Boundaries provisionally accepted

Approve these as research assumptions, not final taxonomy:

- account dormancy, stalled opportunity and closed-lost re-entry are different states;
- first account access and access to a known missing role are different jobs;
- buying-group infrastructure and an account-specific mapping intervention are different levels;
- Bring in the Boss and Partner the Path name situations; Executive-to-Executive and Partner-First name formats;
- opportunity creation and qualified-opportunity support are different jobs;
- adoption, bounded friction and full account recovery are different levels of customer-health intervention;
- renewal preparation, acute renewal rescue and healthy-base defense are different states;
- Lift and Shift may ultimately become a method inside the 1:few Cluster format.

## External research authorization after Gate 1

Firecrawl authentication remains a hard start condition. Normal web search is not an authorized fallback.
