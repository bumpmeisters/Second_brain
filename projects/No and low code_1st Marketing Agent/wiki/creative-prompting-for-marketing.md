# Creative Prompting for Marketing

**Summary**: Historical creative-prompting synthesis retained for provenance. Current content work routes from the Marketing ContextOps handoff to Strategic Creative Direction and Content Execution in the Content Operating System.

---

## ContextOps Purpose

This page no longer owns an execution method. This project produces the upstream Content Context Packet; the Content Operating System decides what should be said and how an approved direction is expressed.

The central principle is **human first, AI second, human last**. The human supplies observation, tension, point of view, taste, and final judgment; AI supports divergence, recombination, variation, and critique (source: [[creative-prompting-frameworks-research-summary-2026-06-23]]).

## When To Use It

Use the historical synthesis only to understand the provenance of the human-first, AI-assisted creative method. For a current run, create a Content Context Packet and route to the Content Operating System.

Do not use it for upstream market analysis, segmentation, audience research, or proof-led positioning. If the evidence or claim boundary is weak, route upstream before ideation (source: `workflows/contextops-handoff-contract.md`; source: [Human-Led Creative Marketing Loop](../frameworks/journey-and-gtm/human-led-creative-marketing-loop.md)).

## Why The Guardrails Matter

The parent research summary reports that early AI assistance can reduce originality and ownership in some ideation settings, while AI-assisted outputs can become more similar across users. The same report also treats recent research as partially verified and not as settled consensus (source: [[creative-prompting-frameworks-research-summary-2026-06-23]]).

For this project, the practical implication is simple: never let AI start from a blank page and never accept polished fluency as strategy. Seed it with human tension and evidence, force it through separated divergent lanes, then judge outputs against proof, audience truth, brand fit, originality, feasibility, and testability (source: historical Creative Prompting Merge Review, not recovered; backup provenance only).
