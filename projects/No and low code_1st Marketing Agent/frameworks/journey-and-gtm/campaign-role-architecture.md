# Campaign Role Architecture

## Framework Job

Assign distinct strategic jobs to coordinated campaign activity rather than producing an undifferentiated tactic list.

## Classification And Fidelity

Original project framework derived from B2B GTM and campaign/funnel materials.

## Use When

- Positioning, audience, journey, and proof are ready for campaign strategy.

## Do Not Use When

- The task is only upstream context or channels are being chosen first.

## Minimum Required Inputs

- Objectives, audience, journey, positioning, proof assets, channels, constraints, and measurement.

## Core Model

Define campaign roles such as brand trust, demand creation, lead generation, conversion, nurture, proof, retention, and referral; connect each to audience stage and metric.

## Question Engine

### Minimum Viable Questions

- What business outcome must change?
- Which audience and journey state matters?
- What strategic job must communication perform?
- What message and proof fit that job?
- Which asset is required?
- Which channel can deliver the job?
- What behavior signals success?
- How does this role connect to other campaign roles?

### Deepening Questions

- What happens before and after this campaign?
- Which role is overfunded or missing?
- What proof bottleneck limits conversion?

### Counter-Questions

- Is this a channel plan disguised as strategy?
- Are multiple objectives competing?
- Would doing less create a clearer system?

### Optional Modules

- ABM.
- Launch.
- Seasonal D2C.
- Lifecycle/retention.

## Branching And Stop Rules

If audience, positioning, or proof is weak, route upstream. Stop before creative production.

## Evidence Standard

Use strategy, journey, historical performance, channel, and proof evidence.

## Business-Model Adaptations

- B2B: align marketing and sales touches.
- D2C: connect acquisition, PDP, lifecycle, and repeat.
- B2B2C: separate partner and end-demand roles.

## Output Contract

Campaign role map, audience/stage, strategic job, message, proof, channel hypothesis, metric, dependencies.

## Failure Modes

Tactic lists, one campaign doing everything, vanity metrics, and unsupported message claims.

## ContextOps Handoff

Feeds campaign briefs, content production, media, sales, and measurement.

## Evaluation Notes

Encoded in the B2B GTM skill; needs broader D2C use.

## Evolution Triggers

Revise when role overlap or measurement ambiguity repeats.
