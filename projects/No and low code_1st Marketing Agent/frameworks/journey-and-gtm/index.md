## Routing Notes

- Use Enterprise Growth System when the job is to design or govern the end-to-end account-based growth operating model.
- Combine it with `icp-account-tiering` and `b2b-buying-committee-intelligence` for account selection and role/relationship depth.
- Load Campaign Role Architecture or Sales Enablement Translation only after the motion, thesis, evidence, and readiness gate are explicit.
- Strongest use: designing or governing a complex B2B account-based growth operating model across acquisition and expansion.
- Compatible: `icp-account-tiering`, `b2b-buying-committee-intelligence`, `campaign-role-architecture`, `sales-enablement-translation`, and evidence-led learning.
- Conflicts: static linear funnels, lead-only scoring, revenue-only evaluation of early motions, and automated outreach from unqualified intent.
