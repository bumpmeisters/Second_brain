# Nonlinear Journey Question-Proof Map

## Framework Job

Map how questions, behaviors, objections, proof needs, and visible alternatives change across a nonlinear decision and adoption journey.

## Classification And Fidelity

Composite of project journey templates, HP journey material, and nonlinear decision-journey traditions.

## Use When

- Audience and positioning need translation into stage-specific information and proof.

## Do Not Use When

- The audience or buying context is not yet known.

## Minimum Required Inputs

- Audience packet, buying roles, trigger, alternatives, positioning, and observed behavior.

## Core Model

Journey stages are hypotheses, not a fixed funnel. Map transitions, loops, expanding/contracting consideration, questions, friction, proof, and success signals.

## Question Engine

### Minimum Viable Questions

- What activates the journey?
- What question appears first?
- Which alternatives become visible?
- What behavior signals movement?
- What objection blocks progress?
- What proof reduces uncertainty?
- What event changes the consideration set?
- What indicates successful adoption or referral?

### Deepening Questions

- Where do people loop backward?
- Which role enters or exits?
- Which stage is inferred rather than observed?

### Counter-Questions

- Are stages imposed by the company?
- Does content exist without a real audience job?
- Is purchase being mistaken for journey completion?

### Optional Modules

- B2B buying group.
- D2C gifting/recipient.
- Onboarding/adoption.
- Retention/referral.

## Branching And Stop Rules

Adapt stage names to evidence. If behavior is unknown, mark validation questions rather than inventing a flow.

## Evidence Standard

Use interviews, analytics, CRM, sales, support, search, order, and product-use evidence.

## Business-Model Adaptations

- B2B: map parallel participant journeys and consensus.
- D2C: include PDP, checkout, delivery, use, review, repeat.
- B2B2C: map partner and end-customer journeys separately.

## Output Contract

Stages/transitions, questions, behavior, objections, proof, assets, success signals, loops, and gaps.

## Failure Modes

Linear funnel assumption, content-first mapping, missing post-purchase, and one journey for all roles.

## ContextOps Handoff

Feeds campaign architecture, content, sales enablement, lifecycle, and measurement.

## Evaluation Notes

Applied in MirrorSoft journey mapping.

## Evolution Triggers

Revise when analytics or interviews reveal missing loops or role-specific paths.
