# Sales Enablement Translation

## Framework Job

Translate audience, positioning, journey, and claim governance into usable sales questions, responses, proof, and follow-up.

## Classification And Fidelity

Original project framework.

## Use When

- Sales must act on marketing strategy in qualification, discovery, objection handling, proposal, or handoff.

## Do Not Use When

- Claims are not governed or buyer roles are generic.

## Minimum Required Inputs

- Role architecture, audience packet, positioning, approved claims, journey, and available proof assets.

## Core Model

Map each sales moment to buyer signal, diagnostic question, safe response, proof asset, claim status, and next action.

## Question Engine

### Minimum Viable Questions

- What buyer signal starts this sales moment?
- What must the seller learn?
- Which question reveals the underlying job or barrier?
- What response is safe and relevant?
- What proof is required?
- What claim status applies?
- What next action advances the buyer?
- What should trigger escalation or disqualification?

### Deepening Questions

- Is the objection a symptom or root concern?
- Which role owns the concern?
- What proof format fits the moment?

### Counter-Questions

- Are we scripting persuasion before discovery?
- Does the response overstate evidence?
- Would a different role need a different answer?

### Optional Modules

- Qualification.
- Discovery.
- Objection handling.
- Proposal and implementation handoff.

## Branching And Stop Rules

Escalate risky claims. Disqualify when fit or evidence is absent. Do not force a response where discovery is needed.

## Evidence Standard

Use approved claims, sales calls, win/loss, audience evidence, and proof assets.

## Business-Model Adaptations

- Enterprise: include committee and governance.
- Partner-led: enable partner seller and end-customer handoff.
- Regulated: include specialist escalation.

## Output Contract

Sales-moment map, diagnostic questions, safe responses, proof, objection map, escalation, and follow-up.

## Failure Modes

Generic scripts, unsupported objection answers, no proof asset, and no qualification boundary.

## ContextOps Handoff

Feeds playbooks, training, CRM guidance, proposals, and handoff processes.

## Evaluation Notes

Derived from B2B GTM templates; live sales evaluation is pending.

## Evolution Triggers

Revise from call evidence, objection outcomes, and seller feedback.
