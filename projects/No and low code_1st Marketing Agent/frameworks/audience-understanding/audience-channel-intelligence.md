# Audience Channel Intelligence

## Framework Job

Map where an audience pays attention and which sources, people, communities, formats, and topics influence it.

## Classification And Fidelity

Adapted from SparkToro-style audience research. Project adaptation separates attention, affinity, authority, trust, and intent.

## Use When

Content distribution, PR, partnerships, SEO/GEO, media, influencer, or community strategy will follow.

## Do Not Use When

Buying motivation is the primary unknown or broad traffic is being mistaken for audience fit.

## Minimum Required Inputs

Selected audience, geography/language, buying context, and referral/search/social/survey/community evidence.

## Core Model

Map attention sources, trust sources, audience affinity, people, publications, communities, topics, formats, and information environments.

## Question Engine

### Minimum Viable Questions

- What does the audience read, watch, listen to, follow, search, or discuss?
- Which sources are distinctive rather than merely large?
- Which niche sources show high affinity?
- Which people shape language and beliefs?
- Where are questions actively asked?
- Which topics recur?
- Which formats fit context and attention?
- Which sources influence confidence at different stages?

### Deepening Questions

- Does following imply trust or intent?
- Which offline/private channels are missing?
- Which source reduces risk rather than inspires?

### Counter-Questions

- Are visible digital users representative?
- Is the evidence current and geographic?
- Are adjacent audiences contaminating the map?

### Optional Modules

- Zero-click/AI answers.
- Influencer/partner.
- Search/topic.
- Community observation.

## Branching And Stop Rules

Keep attention separate from buying intent. Stop before media planning.

## Evidence Standard

Use audience tools, referral, survey, interview, CRM source, search, social, community, media, and sales evidence.

## Business-Model Adaptations

- B2B: analysts, peers, events, partners, professional media.
- D2C: creators, reviews, marketplaces, social, newsletters, search.
- B2B2C/marketplace: map each side separately.

## Output Contract

Attention, trust, people/publication/community, topic, format, affinity, evidence, and gaps.

## Failure Modes

Traffic as fit, attention as intent, digital-only maps, and generic channel lists.

## ContextOps Handoff

Feeds content, PR, partnerships, SEO/GEO, media, and distribution.

## Evaluation Notes

Canonical structure complete; tool-specific validation remains future work.

## Evolution Triggers

Revise when affinity, trust, and intent repeatedly blur in applications.
