# B2B Buying Committee Intelligence

## Framework Job

Understand how account context and multiple participants shape a B2B decision.

## Classification And Fidelity

Adapted from project HP ABM/persona materials. Project adaptation generalizes examples into role, influence, evidence, and stall-risk logic.

## Use When

Multiple roles, approvals, implementation responsibilities, procurement, or partner influence matter.

## Do Not Use When

The purchase is truly individual or job titles are being treated as actual decision roles.

## Minimum Required Inputs

Account/segment, use case, contacts/roles, stage, objections, events, constraints, and relationship evidence.

## Core Model

Map account priorities and constraints alongside participant roles, desired outcomes, risk, influence, questions, proof needs, and decision work.

## Question Engine

### Minimum Viable Questions

- What organizational objective activates the decision?
- Why now and why delayed?
- What status quo shapes the choice?
- Who initiates, champions, evaluates, approves, pays, procures, implements, uses, or blocks?
- Who holds formal and informal influence?
- What outcome does each participant need?
- What objection and proof need is role-specific?
- What could stall consensus?

### Deepening Questions

- Who benefits while another absorbs risk?
- What information must travel between roles?
- Which participant is missing?

### Counter-Questions

- Are titles standing in for roles?
- Is the visible champion representative?
- What event invalidates the map?

### Optional Modules

- Enterprise governance.
- Partner-led.
- Regulated authorities.
- Relationship/influence map.

## Branching And Stop Rules

For SMB, allow roles to collapse without inventing a committee. Label inferred influence as hypothesis.

## Evidence Standard

Use CRM, calls, interviews, organization data, opportunity history, account research, implementation, and decision interviews.

## Business-Model Adaptations

- Enterprise: security, legal, procurement, implementation, sponsor.
- Partner-led: partner seller/owner and end customer.
- B2B2C: business buyer and end-user layers.

## Output Contract

Account context, role/influence map, goals, objections, questions, proof, consensus risk, and gaps.

## Failure Modes

One persona per account, title equals influence, missing implementation risk, and premature personalized campaigns.

## ContextOps Handoff

Feeds ABM, role positioning, content journeys, sales enablement, and qualification.

## Evaluation Notes

Source-grounded in internal examples; needs fresh live account evaluation.

## Evolution Triggers

Revise when hidden roles or consensus dynamics recur.
