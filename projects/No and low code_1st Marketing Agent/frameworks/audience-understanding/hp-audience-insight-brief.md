# HP Audience Insight Brief

## Framework Job

Distill deep audience research into a concise target insight, desired shift, barrier, and proof requirement for downstream teams.

## Classification And Fidelity

Adapted from HP Get/To/By and To/What/How briefing materials. Project adaptation stops before downstream `By` strategy.

## Use When

Audience research needs a concise positioning, creative, campaign, media, or agency handoff.

## Do Not Use When

Research is incomplete or the brief would force premature creative strategy.

## Minimum Required Inputs

Selected segment, current state, desired response, tension, barriers, proof needs, and evidence.

## Core Model

Get identifies target/context; To defines desired shift; By is deferred until downstream strategy. Capture the audience truth and proof required to enable that shift.

## Question Engine

### Minimum Viable Questions

- Who is the audience in this situation?
- Which characteristic changes the decision?
- What do they think, feel, or do now?
- What shift is needed next?
- What barrier blocks movement?
- What tension connects hope and frustration?
- What audience truth is meaningful?
- What proof makes the shift believable?

### Deepening Questions

- Would the audience recognize the tension?
- Which role disagrees?
- Can the insight be expressed clearly without losing evidence?

### Counter-Questions

- Is this a disguised product claim?
- Is aspiration being confused with insight?
- Does the desired response belong downstream?

### Optional Modules

- Rational support.
- Role-specific briefs.
- Regulated proof constraints.

## Branching And Stop Rules

Create separate briefs when roles or audience layers have conflicting needs. Stop before finalizing communication strategy.

## Evidence Standard

Use audience, VOC, decision, CRM/sales/support, and approved proof evidence.

## Business-Model Adaptations

- B2B: role and account situation.
- D2C: buyer/user/recipient, occasion, emotion.
- B2B2C: separate partner and end customer.

## Output Contract

Target, current/desired state, tension, barrier, proof requirement, evidence, and unresolved briefing questions.

## Failure Modes

Generic insight, slogan substitution, one insight across roles, and ignored proof.

## ContextOps Handoff

Feeds positioning, value proposition, creative, campaign, media, and agency briefs.

## Evaluation Notes

Grounded in direct internal HP examples; applied conceptually in project briefs.

## Evolution Triggers

Revise when concise handoffs lose critical audience nuance.
