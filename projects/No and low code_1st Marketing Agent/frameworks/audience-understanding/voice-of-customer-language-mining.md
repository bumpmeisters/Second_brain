# Voice Of Customer Language Mining

## Framework Job

Extract and organize audience language for problems, outcomes, objections, alternatives, proof, and action.

## Classification And Fidelity

Adapted from Voice-of-Customer copy research traditions. Project adaptation adds traceability, contradiction checks, roles, and ContextOps boundaries.

## Use When

A language corpus exists and later positioning, SEO/GEO, landing pages, email, ads, PDP, content, or sales work needs audience wording.

## Do Not Use When

There is no selected context or a few vivid quotes would be mistaken for a pattern.

## Minimum Required Inputs

Selected segment/context, language corpus, and source metadata.

## Core Model

Tag problem, trigger, desired outcome, failed alternative, objection, comparison, proof, and commitment language; keep verbatim evidence separate from interpretation.

## Question Engine

### Minimum Viable Questions

- How is the problem described before category jargon?
- Which symptoms recur?
- What makes it urgent?
- What outcome is requested?
- Which alternatives failed?
- What language expresses risk or hesitation?
- What evidence changes confidence?
- Which phrases recur across independent sources?

### Deepening Questions

- Is the phrase frequent or merely vivid?
- Who said it and at what stage?
- Does it describe cause, symptom, solution, or identity?

### Counter-Questions

- Is company or competitor copy contaminating the corpus?
- What do non-buyers say differently?
- What contradictory language exists?

### Optional Modules

- Search-query language.
- Review mining.
- Sales/support corpus.
- Role-specific vocabulary.

## Branching And Stop Rules

Separate corpora by role/context when language changes meaningfully. Never fabricate representative quotes.

## Evidence Standard

Use interviews, calls, reviews, support, emails, communities, search, and open survey responses with traceability.

## Business-Model Adaptations

- B2B: separate executive, evaluator, procurement, champion, and user.
- D2C: separate pre-purchase and post-purchase.
- B2B2C: maintain partner and end-customer corpora.

## Output Contract

Theme map, verbatim bank, objection/outcome/proof vocabulary, frequency, contradictions, and gaps.

## Failure Modes

Invented quotes, positive-only sources, collapsed roles, and direct copywriting before claim checks.

## ContextOps Handoff

Feeds positioning, SEO/GEO, content, landing pages, campaigns, lifecycle, and sales.

## Evaluation Notes

Structure validated; corpus-quality evaluation remains case-specific.

## Evolution Triggers

Revise when tagging misses recurring language functions or source traceability fails.
