# Five Rings Of Buying Insight

## Framework Job

Reconstruct how real buyers moved from the status quo to a decision.

## Classification And Fidelity

Adapted from Buyer Persona Institute's Five Rings. Project adaptation adds ContextOps evidence, branching, and handoff.

## Use When

High-consideration, risky, complex, or infrequent decisions can be investigated through recent buyers and lost buyers.

## Do Not Use When

No decision participants are available or attention/channel behavior is the main unknown.

## Minimum Required Inputs

Defined decision, selected segment, recent decision participants, and interview records.

## Core Model

Priority Initiative, Success Factors, Perceived Barriers, Decision Criteria, and Buyer's Journey.

## Question Engine

### Minimum Viable Questions

- When did you first realize change might be necessary?
- What happened next?
- Why was the status quo tolerated before?
- What outcome defined success?
- What nearly stopped the decision?
- Which alternatives were considered?
- What criteria became non-negotiable?
- Who and what shaped the final choice?

### Deepening Questions

- What consequence of delay became unacceptable?
- Which criterion emerged late?
- What changed confidence?
- What did the winning option do differently?

### Counter-Questions

- Which explanation is hindsight?
- What would have preserved the status quo?
- How would another participant tell the story?

### Optional Modules

- Lost-buyer comparison.
- Buying-group role comparison.
- Verbatim language extraction.

## Branching And Stop Rules

Use real decision stories as primary evidence. If only synthetic answers exist, stop at an interview plan.

## Evidence Standard

Interview recent buyers, non-buyers, and lost buyers. Preserve chronology and verbatim language; separate patterns from outliers.

## Business-Model Adaptations

- B2B: interview multiple roles.
- B2C/D2C: include household, occasion, emotion, affordability, and outside-category alternatives.
- B2B2C: reconstruct partner and end-customer decisions separately.

## Output Contract

Five-ring evidence, decision timeline, participant map, language, patterns, outliers, and gaps.

## Failure Modes

Happy-customer-only samples, abstract attribute ratings, mixed decisions, and small-sample frequency presented as proof.

## ContextOps Handoff

Feeds audience understanding, positioning, journey, content, and sales enablement.

## Evaluation Notes

Canonical structure complete; next evaluation requires real buyer interviews.

## Evolution Triggers

Revise when interviews reveal missing causal steps or ring overlap.
