# Audience Understanding Frameworks

## Purpose

Use this library after a segmentation strategy has selected or hypothesized the segments that need deeper understanding.

The goal is not to create fictional personas. The goal is to explain what selected audiences are trying to accomplish, what activates or blocks decisions, what they ask and believe, whose input they trust, which language they use, and what evidence remains missing.

## Framework Router

| Framework | Primary contribution | Strongest use |
|---|---|---|
| [Crestodina Question-Driven Audience Analysis](crestodina-question-driven-audience-analysis.md) | Structured open questions plus audience/content gap analysis. | B2B services, websites, content, conversion, AI-assisted exploration. |
| [Five Rings Of Buying Insight](five-rings-buying-insight.md) | Causal reconstruction of real buying decisions. | B2B and high-consideration decisions. |
| [Voice Of Customer Language Mining](voice-of-customer-language-mining.md) | Exact audience language, objections, outcomes, comparisons, and proof phrases. | Landing pages, SEO/GEO, email, ads, PDPs, sales language. |
| [Audience Channel Intelligence](audience-channel-intelligence.md) | Sources, people, publications, communities, formats, and topics that already hold attention. | Content distribution, PR, partnerships, SEO/GEO, channel planning. |
| [HubSpot Persona And CRM Activation](hubspot-persona-crm-activation.md) | Connect qualitative audience insight to measurable behavioral and lifecycle signals. | CRM, inbound, lifecycle, email, sales handoff, D2C operations. |
| [HP Audience Insight Brief](hp-audience-insight-brief.md) | Distill audience understanding into target insight, barrier, desired response, and proof need. | Handoff to positioning, creative, campaigns, and agency briefs. |
| [B2B Buying Committee Intelligence](b2b-buying-committee-intelligence.md) | Account context, roles, influence, conflicts, objections, and decision work. | Enterprise, ABM, complex B2B, partner-led sales. |
| [JTBD Demand-Side Understanding](jtbd-demand-side-understanding.md) | Progress sought, circumstances, switching forces, job steps, and desired outcomes. | B2C, D2C, product/service switching, category alternatives. |

## Default Lens Tournament

Compare at least three candidates:

1. A causal lens: Five Rings or JTBD.
2. A language lens: Voice of Customer.
3. A context-specific lens: Crestodina, B2B Buying Committee, HubSpot/CRM, HP Brief, or Audience Channel Intelligence.

Score each candidate on:

- downstream leverage;
- fit to available evidence;
- audience specificity;
- business-model fit;
- validation path;
- risk of synthetic or demographic filler.

## Common Combinations

- B2B services: Crestodina + Five Rings + B2B Buying Committee + Voice of Customer.
- Enterprise ABM: Five Rings + B2B Buying Committee + HP Audience Insight Brief.
- D2C ecommerce: JTBD + Voice of Customer + HubSpot/CRM + Audience Channel Intelligence.
- B2B2C: B2B Buying Committee for the partner layer + JTBD/VOC for the end-customer layer.
- Content/SEO/GEO: Voice of Customer + Audience Channel Intelligence + Crestodina gap analysis.

## Guardrail

Frameworks produce lenses and questions. Evidence produces audience claims. If the evidence is weak, the output must remain a hypothesis and include a validation path.
