# JTBD Demand-Side Understanding

## Framework Job

Understand the progress people seek and the circumstances and switching forces that shape choice.

## Classification And Fidelity

Composite of Christensen-style circumstance/progress and demand-side switching/job-step traditions. Project adaptation keeps segmentation and downstream strategy separate.

## Use When

Category labels hide the real problem, switching matters, or functional/emotional/social progress shapes choice.

## Do Not Use When

Only channel affinity is needed or job statements would be invented without evidence.

## Minimum Required Inputs

Job executor, context, switching stories, current solutions, alternatives, and behavior/review evidence.

## Core Model

Map progress, circumstance, current workaround, push, pull, anxiety, habit, job steps, desired outcomes, and alternatives.

## Question Engine

### Minimum Viable Questions

- What progress is sought?
- Why does it matter now?
- How is the situation handled today?
- What pushes away from the current way?
- What pulls toward a new option?
- What anxiety resists change?
- What habit protects the status quo?
- What alternatives compete for the same progress?

### Deepening Questions

- What larger identity or outcome is supported?
- Where does the job struggle?
- What changed in circumstance?

### Counter-Questions

- Is a feature disguised as a job?
- Do buyer and user share the job?
- Which evidence comes from actual switching?

### Optional Modules

- Job steps/outcomes.
- Switching timeline.
- Buyer/user/recipient roles.
- Non-consumption.

## Branching And Stop Rules

Run separate modules when organizational, participant, user, partner, or recipient jobs differ. Synthetic jobs remain hypotheses.

## Evidence Standard

Prefer switching/contextual interviews, reviews, usage, support, search, and considered alternatives.

## Business-Model Adaptations

- B2B: organizational, participant, and user jobs.
- D2C: purchase, gifting, delivery, use, replenishment, return.
- B2B2C: partner and end-customer jobs separately.

## Output Contract

Job/circumstance, push-pull-anxiety-habit, alternatives, job steps, outcomes, evidence, and gaps.

## Failure Modes

"Buy product" jobs, generic aspirations, ignored substitutes, mixed roles, and elegant unvalidated statements.

## ContextOps Handoff

Feeds audience understanding, segmentation refinement, positioning, product, content, and lifecycle.

## Evaluation Notes

Canonical structure complete; applied conceptually in Das Familienbuch buying contexts.

## Evolution Triggers

Revise when job-step and switching-force modules produce conflicting interpretations.
