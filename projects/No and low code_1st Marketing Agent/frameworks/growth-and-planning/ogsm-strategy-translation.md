# OGSM Strategy Translation

## Framework Job

Translate completed analysis into one objective, quantified goals, strategic choices, and measures.

## Classification And Fidelity

Adapted from the OGSM planning tradition. ContextOps readiness and anti-filler rules are project additions.

Project adaptation: require completed upstream choices, evidence-backed goals, exclusions, and explicit return upstream when strategy is unresolved.

## Use When

- Upstream strategic choices are complete and alignment/execution is needed.

## Do Not Use When

- The form is being used to avoid segmentation, positioning, or strategic trade-offs.

## Minimum Required Inputs

- Evidence-backed strategy, priorities, constraints, economics, owners, and measurement capability.

## Core Model

Objective defines direction; Goals quantify outcomes; Strategies state choices; Measures track execution and results.

## Question Engine

### Minimum Viable Questions

- What single objective defines the desired future?
- Which quantified goals prove progress?
- What strategic choices will achieve them?
- What will not be pursued?
- Which measures indicate execution?
- Which measures indicate outcome?
- Who owns each measure?
- What time horizon applies?

### Deepening Questions

- Does each strategy explain how goals change?
- Are goals outcomes or activities?
- Which assumption could invalidate the plan?

### Counter-Questions

- Is the objective generic?
- Is a tactic mislabeled as strategy?
- Are measures available and controllable?

### Optional Modules

- Milestones.
- Initiatives.
- Dependencies and risks.

## Branching And Stop Rules

If strategic choices are unresolved, return upstream. Limit strategies to a manageable set.

## Evidence Standard

Goals require baselines and sources. Strategies require rationale and assumptions.

## Business-Model Adaptations

Adapt metrics and ownership to B2B, D2C, B2B2C, marketplace, or regulated operations.

## Output Contract

Objective, goals, strategies, measures, owners, assumptions, exclusions, and review cadence.

## Failure Modes

Generic objective, activity goals, tactic lists, too many strategies, and measures without baselines.

## ContextOps Handoff

Feeds operating plans, briefs, budgets, experiments, and reviews.

## Evaluation Notes

Draft; referenced in historical planning artifacts but not yet evaluated canonically.

## Evolution Triggers

Revise after planning-cycle use or repeated ambiguity between goals, strategies, and measures.
