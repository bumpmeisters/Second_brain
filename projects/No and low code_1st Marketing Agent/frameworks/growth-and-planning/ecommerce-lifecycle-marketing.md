# Ecommerce Lifecycle Marketing

## Framework Job

Map customer states and transitions so communication responds to behavior, value, and relationship stage over time.

## Classification And Fidelity

Adapted from ecommerce lifecycle and Klaviyo practice. Project additions emphasize context, evidence, and strategy before flow design.

Project adaptation: organize lifecycle work around evidence-backed customer states rather than a fixed automation checklist.

## Use When

- Customer, order, product, email/SMS, retention, or repeat-purchase data exists.

## Do Not Use When

- The task is a generic list of automations without customer-state evidence.

## Minimum Required Inputs

- Audience contexts, customer/order events, products, consent, economics, and current flows.

## Core Model

Define meaningful states, transition triggers, customer questions, value exchange, message role, and success signal.

## Question Engine

### Minimum Viable Questions

- What customer state exists now?
- What event moves the customer into it?
- What job or question is active?
- What barrier blocks the next state?
- What message role is appropriate?
- What proof or value is needed?
- What event marks transition?
- What metric shows durable value?

### Deepening Questions

- Does the state differ by product or buying context?
- Is frequency appropriate?
- What happens when no transition occurs?

### Counter-Questions

- Are flows organized around company schedules rather than customer states?
- Does discounting substitute for relevance?
- Are consent and privacy respected?

### Optional Modules

- Browse/cart.
- Post-purchase/onboarding.
- Review/referral.
- Repeat occasion/win-back.

## Branching And Stop Rules

If data is incomplete, design states and required signals before automations. Stop before copy production.

## Evidence Standard

Use customer/order, engagement, product, support, review, survey, and cohort data.

## Business-Model Adaptations

- D2C: product, recipient, occasion, replenishment, returns.
- B2B ecommerce: account/contact roles and sales handoff.
- Subscription: activation, usage, renewal, churn.

## Output Contract

Lifecycle states, triggers, questions, barriers, message roles, proof, transitions, metrics, and gaps.

## Failure Modes

Flow checklist, discount dependence, weak consent, no holdout/measurement, and no product/context differentiation.

## ContextOps Handoff

Feeds lifecycle strategy, automation briefs, content, and measurement.

## Evaluation Notes

Active as a reusable D2C framework; live data validation remains required.

## Evolution Triggers

Revise from cohort performance, complaints, deliverability, and state-transition evidence.
