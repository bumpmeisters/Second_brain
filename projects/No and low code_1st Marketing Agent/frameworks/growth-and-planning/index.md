# Growth And Planning Frameworks

| Framework | Primary contribution | Evidence requirement | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Growth Loop Analysis](growth-loop-analysis.md) | Models how outputs of one cycle become inputs to the next. | Observable loop events and conversion data. | Renaming a funnel as a loop. |
| [Ecommerce Lifecycle Marketing](ecommerce-lifecycle-marketing.md) | Maps customer states, transitions, messages, and signals over time. | Customer, order, engagement, and product data. | Generic automated flow lists. |
| [D2C Funnel Diagnosis](d2c-funnel-diagnosis.md) | Finds the highest-leverage commercial bottleneck before prescribing tactics. | Funnel, offer, traffic, PDP, checkout, and retention evidence. | Blaming acquisition for downstream friction. |
| [OGSM Strategy Translation](ogsm-strategy-translation.md) | Converts analysis into one objective, quantified goals, strategic choices, and measures. | Completed upstream strategy decisions. | Filling OGSM before making real choices. |
