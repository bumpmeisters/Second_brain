# Positioning Frameworks

| Framework | Primary contribution | Evidence requirement | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Proof-Led Positioning System](proof-led-positioning-system.md) | Connects audience, category, promise, proof pillars, and claim constraints. | Audience packet, claim governance, market alternatives. | Clever promise outrunning proof. |
| [Competitive Alternative Positioning](competitive-alternative-positioning.md) | Frames value relative to what customers would do without the offer. | Real alternatives, differentiated capabilities, value evidence. | Inventing uniqueness or choosing a category too early. |
