# Competitive Alternative Positioning

## Framework Job

Determine the market context in which differentiated capabilities create the greatest value for best-fit customers.

## Classification And Fidelity

Adapted from April Dunford's positioning approach. ContextOps evidence and claim gates are project additions.

Project adaptation: add explicit source confidence, claim governance, business-model branches, and an upstream stop rule.

## Use When

- Category, alternatives, uniqueness, value, or best-fit customer context is unclear.

## Do Not Use When

- Market alternatives or product capabilities are invented.

## Minimum Required Inputs

- Real competitive alternatives, capabilities, customer value evidence, and best-fit customer patterns.

## Core Model

Move from competitive alternatives to unique attributes, value, best-fit customers, and market category.

## Question Engine

### Minimum Viable Questions

- What would customers do if this offer did not exist?
- Which alternatives are actually considered?
- What capabilities are genuinely different?
- What value do those differences create?
- Who cares most about that value?
- What context makes the value obvious?
- Which category helps customers understand it?
- What evidence validates each link?

### Deepening Questions

- Is the alternative a competitor, internal process, DIY, or no action?
- Which capability is difficult to copy?
- What customer characteristic makes value acute?

### Counter-Questions

- Are we choosing the category before alternatives?
- Is uniqueness claimed without proof?
- Would the category create harmful expectations?

### Optional Modules

- Trend/context overlay.
- Sales narrative.
- Product portfolio differentiation.

## Branching And Stop Rules

Return to market/audience research if alternatives or best-fit patterns are weak. Stop before final messaging.

## Evidence Standard

Use win/loss, interviews, sales, product, competitor, and usage evidence.

## Business-Model Adaptations

- B2B: emphasize workflows, risk, integration, and committee value.
- D2C: include retail, marketplace, DIY, occasion, and emotional alternatives.
- B2B2C: position partner and end-user value separately.

## Output Contract

Alternatives, unique capabilities, value themes, best-fit context, category options, proof gaps.

## Failure Modes

Feature dumping, invented uniqueness, broad ICP, category aspiration, and ignored status quo.

## ContextOps Handoff

Feeds proof-led positioning and sales narrative.

## Evaluation Notes

Begrenzt angewendet; vollständiger kundenevidenzgestützter Test ausstehend. Draft bleibt bestehen; Quellenreview und Anwendungsvalidierung sind getrennt.

## Evolution Triggers

Revise after win/loss evidence or repeated category confusion.
