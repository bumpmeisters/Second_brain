# Segmentation Frameworks

| Framework | Primary contribution | Evidence requirement | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Segmentation Basis Selection And STP](segmentation-basis-selection-stp.md) | Chooses a meaningful segmentation basis before targeting. | Market and buying context. | Demographic labels without causal value. |
| [Nested B2B Segmentation](nested-b2b-segmentation.md) | Moves from account structure to buying situation and people. | Account, use-case, procurement, and role evidence. | Excessive nesting and false precision. |
| [Outcome-Driven Segmentation](outcome-driven-segmentation.md) | Groups customers by important, poorly satisfied outcomes. | Structured outcome research. | Invented importance/satisfaction scores. |
| [Category Entry Point Demand Situations](category-entry-point-demand-situations.md) | Maps situations and cues that bring a category to mind. | Search, usage, memory, or occasion evidence. | Mistaking demand situations for target segments. |
| [Hybrid Activation Segmentation](hybrid-activation-segmentation.md) | Combines strategic and operational bases without creating unusable microsegments. | Strategic basis plus observable signals. | Overfitting. |
| [ICP And Account Tiering](icp-account-tiering.md) | Separates account fit, potential, readiness, and service level. | Firmographic, use-case, value, and sales evidence. | Treating ICP as full customer understanding. |
| [Buying-Context Segmentation](buying-context-segmentation.md) | Groups demand by role, trigger, job, barrier, proof need, and signal. | Buying-context packet and validation signals. | Prioritizing unvalidated contexts. |
