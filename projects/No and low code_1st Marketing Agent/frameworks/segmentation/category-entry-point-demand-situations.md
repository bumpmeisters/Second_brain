# Category Entry Point Demand Situations

## Framework Job

Map the situations, needs, cues, and occasions that bring a category to mind.

## Classification And Fidelity

Adapted from Ehrenberg-Bass category-entry-point thinking. Using CEPs as a segmentation candidate and ContextOps handoff is a project adaptation.

## Use When

- Mental availability, occasions, content prompts, or broad demand situations matter.

## Do Not Use When

- Narrow ICP selection or account economics is the primary job.

## Minimum Required Inputs

- Category boundary, buyer situations, search/occasion evidence, and current associations.

## Core Model

Identify when, where, with whom, while doing what, and for what need buyers enter the category; prioritize useful and under-owned cues.

## Question Engine

### Minimum Viable Questions

- When does the category become relevant?
- Where does the situation occur?
- Who is involved?
- What happened immediately before?
- What need or emotion activates recall?
- Which alternatives also come to mind?
- Which cues are frequent or strategically valuable?
- Which cues are weakly associated with the brand?

### Deepening Questions

- Does the situation precede active search?
- Is the cue category-level or brand-created?
- Which cue differs by buyer, user, or recipient?

### Counter-Questions

- Are we confusing campaigns with natural demand situations?
- Does a visible occasion represent enough demand?
- Would broad reach matter more than segment exclusion?

### Optional Modules

- Occasion calendar.
- Search-query mapping.
- Brand association audit.

## Branching And Stop Rules

If the task is target selection, combine with another segmentation basis. Stop before campaign creation.

## Evidence Standard

Use interviews, search, usage, occasion, sales, and memory-association evidence.

## Business-Model Adaptations

- B2C/D2C: emphasize occasions, emotions, gifting, and routines.
- B2B: use problem situations, deadlines, regulatory events, and operational triggers.
- B2B2C: separate partner and end-demand entry points.

## Output Contract

CEP map, evidence, frequency/value hypotheses, brand-association gaps, and downstream questions.

## Failure Modes

Generic occasion lists, campaign-created cues presented as demand, and treating CEPs as full segments.

## ContextOps Handoff

Feeds segmentation comparison, audience questions, content, and mental-availability strategy.

## Evaluation Notes

## Evolution Triggers

Revise after first association study or when B2B event adaptation is tested.
