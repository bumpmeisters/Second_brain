# ICP And Account Tiering

## Framework Job

Separate account fit, potential value, readiness, and required go-to-market investment.

## Classification And Fidelity

Adapted from common B2B ICP, ABM, and account-tiering practice, plus project ABM materials.

## Use When

- Sales capacity, ABM investment, partner routing, or account prioritization matters.

## Do Not Use When

- Firmographics are being mistaken for buyer needs or audience understanding.

## Minimum Required Inputs

- Account universe, use cases, value potential, product fit, readiness, sales motion, and capacity.

## Core Model

Assess fit, value, readiness, and cost-to-serve separately, then define tiers with distinct treatment.

## Question Engine

### Minimum Viable Questions

- Which account attributes predict product fit?
- Which use cases create meaningful value?
- What disqualifies an account?
- What is the potential economic value?
- What evidence indicates readiness?
- Which buying-group coverage is required?
- What sales/marketing effort does the account justify?
- What treatment differs by tier?

### Deepening Questions

- Is historical win data biased?
- Does fit differ by product or region?
- Which readiness signal is temporary?

### Counter-Questions

- Are large accounts being overvalued?
- Is an easy-to-find firmographic proxy hiding use-case fit?
- Can the team actually deliver tier differentiation?

### Optional Modules

- Partner fit.
- Expansion potential.
- Strategic logo value.
- Risk/compliance.

## Branching And Stop Rules

If tier treatment does not differ, merge tiers. Route audience and buying-role questions to Audience Understanding.

## Evidence Standard

Use wins/losses, pipeline, usage, implementation, revenue, margin, account, and buying-group evidence.

## Business-Model Adaptations

- Enterprise: include complexity, governance, and implementation capacity.
- SMB: emphasize repeatability and low service cost.
- Partner-led: include partner capability and incentive.

## Output Contract

ICP criteria, exclusions, fit/value/readiness scores with rationale, tiers, treatment, and validation.

## Failure Modes

Revenue-only tiers, arbitrary scores, firmographic persona substitution, and tiers without operating consequences.

## ContextOps Handoff

Feeds account selection, B2B audience understanding, ABM, and sales planning.

## Evaluation Notes

Grounded in project ABM examples; external source fidelity remains medium.

## Evolution Triggers

Revise when tier performance, cost-to-serve, or readiness signals contradict assumptions.
