# Outcome-Driven Segmentation

## Framework Job

Segment customers by clusters of important outcomes that are poorly satisfied rather than by profiles alone.

## Classification And Fidelity

Adapted from Anthony Ulwick's Outcome-Driven Innovation. ContextOps boundaries and simplified output are project additions.

## Use When

- Product innovation, premium value, unmet needs, or outcome trade-offs drive strategy.

## Do Not Use When

- Outcome importance and satisfaction cannot be measured or credibly researched.

## Minimum Required Inputs

- Job executor, job steps, desired-outcome statements, importance and satisfaction evidence.

## Core Model

Define stable desired outcomes, measure importance and satisfaction, identify underserved outcomes, and cluster respondents by different unmet-outcome patterns.

## Question Engine

### Minimum Viable Questions

- Who executes the job?
- What job is being completed?
- What steps make up the job?
- What measurable outcome matters at each step?
- How important is each outcome?
- How satisfied are customers with current solutions?
- Which outcomes are underserved?
- Which respondents share similar unmet-outcome patterns?

### Deepening Questions

- Is the outcome solution-independent?
- Is it measurable and unambiguous?
- Which trade-offs distinguish clusters?

### Counter-Questions

- Are scores invented or sample-biased?
- Is a feature disguised as an outcome?
- Does the segment persist across current products?

### Optional Modules

- Opportunity scoring.
- Segment attractiveness.
- Innovation concept fit.

## Branching And Stop Rules

If outcome data is qualitative only, produce hypotheses and a research design, not numeric opportunity segments.

## Evidence Standard

Structured interviews and quantitative outcome research. Do not guess importance or satisfaction.

## Business-Model Adaptations

- B2B: distinguish organizational and user outcomes.
- B2C/D2C: include functional, emotional, and process outcomes.
- B2B2C: measure partner and end-customer outcomes separately.

## Output Contract

Outcome map, underserved outcomes, candidate clusters, evidence quality, and research gaps.

## Failure Modes

Feature statements, invented scores, average-only analysis, and clusters with no reachable signals.

## ContextOps Handoff

Feeds segmentation strategy, product/value proposition, and audience research.

## Evaluation Notes

Draft until applied with genuine outcome data.

## Evolution Triggers

Revise after first quantitative application or repeated ambiguity in outcome wording.
