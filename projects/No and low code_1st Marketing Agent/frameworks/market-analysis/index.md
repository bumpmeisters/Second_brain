# Market Analysis Frameworks

| Framework | Primary contribution | Evidence requirement | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Market Boundary And Alternative-Set Analysis](market-boundary-alternative-set-analysis.md) | Defines the market without losing substitutes or overreaching into strategy. | Company evidence plus category and alternative sources. | Choosing the market definition that flatters the offer. |
| [Porter Five Forces](porter-five-forces.md) | Explains structural pressure on industry profitability. | Clearly bounded industry and force-level evidence. | Mechanical checklist or misuse for customer psychology. |

Use Market Boundary first. Use Five Forces only when structural economics matter to the current handoff.
