# Das Familienbuch Funnel Strategy Rebuild Workflow v0.1

**Summary**: Working plan for rebuilding the marketing strategy for an underperforming ecommerce shop, step by step, from media acquisition through activation, engagement, and conversion. The workflow is designed to minimize AI assumptions by using explicit user decision gates and verification after every major output.

---

## Strategy Reset

The current task is not more evidence collection for its own sake. The goal is to rebuild the marketing strategy for Das Familienbuch as a complete ecommerce funnel:

1. Media acquisition: who sees which promise in which channel and why they should care.
2. User activation: what first action or mental shift turns a visitor into an interested buyer.
3. User engagement: how the shop, content, email, retargeting, and product education keep intent alive.
4. Conversion: how the offer, proof, product pages, bundles, urgency, trust, and checkout reduce hesitation.
5. Post-purchase and repeat/referral: how gift outcomes, reminders, reviews, and cross-sells compound.

The shop is treated as not yet successful enough. Existing website evidence is an input, not a strategy endorsement.

## Operating Rules

- Work in small, decision-backed steps.
- Do not jump from scraped information to campaign recommendations without a funnel diagnosis.
- Separate evidence, interpretation, hypothesis, and recommendation.
- Ask Rolf before locking important strategic choices.
- Every output must include a verification plan.
- Prefer workflow artifacts that create context for the next step.
- Use frameworks only when they help generate or structure context for a decision.

## Decision Gates

| Gate | Decision Rolf must make | Why it matters | Output unlocked |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Strategic success target | What does "successful ecommerce shop" mean for the next phase: revenue, CAC, ROAS, conversion rate, AOV, list growth, repeat purchase, or learning velocity? | Without this, optimization can point in different directions. | Funnel diagnosis scorecard. |
| 2. Primary growth constraint | Is the suspected bottleneck traffic quality, offer clarity, trust, product-market fit, conversion UX, retention, or data infrastructure? | Defines where the first deep dive starts. | Bottleneck hypothesis map. |
| 3. Primary buyer/job | Which buyer situation matters first: adult child buying for parent/grandparent, partner gift, gratitude gift, new parent, legacy/health-history buyer, or multilingual family? | Controls messaging, media targeting, landing page structure, and offer architecture. | Positioning and persona hypothesis. |
| 4. Funnel depth | Should the first strategy pass optimize the current shop or redesign the funnel from acquisition to post-purchase? | Controls scope and how disruptive the recommendations can be. | Funnel strategy blueprint. |
| 5. Verification source | Which proof source should be treated as strongest: Shopify/Klaviyo analytics, ad account data, customer interviews, reviews/support tickets, heatmaps/session recordings, or user judgment? | Determines which hypotheses can become decisions. | Validation plan and test backlog. |

## Funnel Workstreams

### 1. Media Acquisition

**Context to build**

- Current channels, spend, creative angles, targeting assumptions, landing destinations, and observed performance.
- Current promise shown before the click.
- Difference between warm demand capture and cold demand creation.

**Useful frameworks**

- Jobs-to-be-done trigger map.
- Category entry points.
- Awareness ladder: unaware, problem-aware, solution-aware, product-aware, most-aware.
- Message-market fit by channel.

**Verification**

- Ad account metrics if available: CPM, CTR, CPC, thumb-stop/hold rate, outbound click rate, landing page view rate, CAC/CPA, ROAS.
- Creative review: does each ad make a clear buying situation visible?
- User review: Rolf confirms which acquisition channels are actually in use.

**User decision needed**

- Which channel should be treated as the primary acquisition system first: Meta, Google Search/Shopping, TikTok, Pinterest, email/list, organic/social, affiliates, or other?

### 2. User Activation

**Context to build**

- What the visitor must understand within the first 5-10 seconds.
- Which mental switch activates buying intent: "I need to preserve this before it is gone", "this is a meaningful gift", "this is easy enough", "this feels trustworthy", or "this is better than a normal book".
- Whether the product page currently creates that switch.

**Useful frameworks**

- First-screen clarity audit.
- Problem-promise-proof-CTA.
- Friction vs motivation.
- Offer comprehension test.

**Verification**

- Five-second test with human review or screenshots.
- Landing-page checklist: category, audience, emotional trigger, product mechanism, proof, next action.
- Analytics if available: landing page bounce, scroll depth, add-to-cart rate.

**User decision needed**

- Which activation promise should be the first strategic bet: memory preservation, meaningful gift, voice preservation, easy guided writing, family legacy, or health/family history?

### 3. User Engagement

**Context to build**

- How visitors are educated after first interest but before purchase.
- How email, retargeting, product comparison, reviews, FAQs, bundles, and content handle hesitation.
- Which objections matter most: effort, trust, price, quality, "will they fill it out", delivery timing, data/privacy for audio, or emotional discomfort.

**Useful frameworks**

- Objection map.
- Nurture ladder.
- Proof ladder.
- Consideration content matrix.

**Verification**

- Klaviyo flow audit if available: opt-in rate, welcome flow revenue, abandonment recovery, click rates, conversion by flow.
- Retargeting content audit: does each asset answer a distinct objection?
- Customer evidence: reviews/support tickets/interviews mapped to objections.

**User decision needed**

- Which engagement system matters first: email capture/welcome, browse/cart abandonment, retargeting, reviews/social proof, product education, or gift-reminder lifecycle?

### 4. Conversion

**Context to build**

- Product page structure, offer hierarchy, bundles, price anchoring, proof, guarantee, urgency, trust, checkout path, and payment/delivery confidence.
- Current conversion bottleneck: product page view to add-to-cart, add-to-cart to checkout, checkout to purchase, or post-click quality.

**Useful frameworks**

- Conversion clarity audit.
- Offer stack.
- Proof hierarchy.
- Risk reversal.
- Bundle architecture.

**Verification**

- Shopify analytics if available: product page conversion, add-to-cart, checkout start, purchase conversion, AOV, bundle attach rate.
- Manual UX audit against claim matrix and trust gaps.
- User review of commercial constraints: margin, stock, shipping promise, legal limits.

**User decision needed**

- Should conversion strategy prioritize clearer product pages, stronger bundles, trust repair, price/offer testing, checkout friction, or proof/reviews?

### 5. Post-Purchase, Retention, Referral

**Context to build**

- What happens after purchase: delivery, gifting moment, filling experience, review request, cross-sell, reminder, seasonal repurchase.
- Whether the brand can create repeat purchase through recipient variants, gratitude books, audio add-ons, baby products, or seasonal gift cycles.

**Useful frameworks**

- Moment-of-value map.
- Gift lifecycle.
- Cross-sell adjacency map.
- Review/referral loop.

**Verification**

- Repeat purchase rate, review generation rate, email revenue, support issues, product attach rate.
- Customer feedback on whether the recipient actually fills the book.
- User confirmation of operational feasibility.

**User decision needed**

- Should the first post-purchase strategy focus on reviews, referrals, cross-sell, recipient engagement, gift reminders, or reducing support/returns?

## First Recommended Step

Do not build the full strategy yet. First create a **Funnel Diagnosis Scorecard v0.1** with one row per funnel stage:

- current evidence
- likely bottleneck
- missing data
- risk of assumption
- recommended next diagnostic
- decision required from Rolf
- verification method

This scorecard becomes the control panel for the rest of the project.

## Minimum Data Request For Next Step

Ask Rolf for only the highest-leverage inputs first:

1. Current primary paid channels and whether there is active spend.
2. Rough monthly revenue or order volume range.
3. Current known pain: traffic too expensive, visitors do not buy, cart abandonment, low AOV, low repeat purchase, unclear positioning, or weak email flow.
4. Analytics access or exported snapshots available: Shopify, Klaviyo, Meta/Google, heatmaps, reviews/support.

If data is unavailable, create a hypothesis-based scorecard and mark it clearly as unvalidated.

## Verification Gate

The next output is accepted only if:

- it does not assume the main bottleneck without evidence or Rolf confirmation;
- every recommendation is labeled as data-backed, website-evidence-backed, user-confirmed, or hypothesis;
- it produces one clear next diagnostic rather than a generic marketing plan;
- Rolf makes the first strategic-success-target decision before full strategy drafting.
