## Comparison Takeaways

- Product architecture is now stronger evidence than before: the product-link overview and website snapshot agree on 18 listed product URLs.
- The market analysis is valuable as a map of hypotheses, but most competitor, SEO, market-growth, and legal claims remain unverified.
- The most actionable strategic lanes are core memory preservation, Voice Memory as differentiation, gratitude gifting as an adjacent offer, and trust-building around production/company identity.
- The most sensitive lane is health-history positioning. It may differentiate, but it also raises claim-risk and should be governed tightly.
