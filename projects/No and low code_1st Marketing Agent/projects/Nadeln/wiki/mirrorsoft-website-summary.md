# MirrorSoft Website Summary

**Summary**: Source summary for the official MirrorSoft product website and related PromaMedical / PromaInject pages.

---

## What The Source Is

`https://mirrorsoft.de/` is the official product website for Mirror Soft blunt atraumatic cannulas. It links to PromaInject as the product purchase destination and contains the visible embedded data sheet content that was saved locally as HTML and extracted text (source: https://mirrorsoft.de/; source: [[mirrorsoft-datenblatt-und-belege-auswertung]]).

`https://promamedical.de/` provides parent/company context for PromaMedical GmbH and its PromaDerm and PromaInject divisions (source: https://promamedical.de/).

`https://promainject.com/Mirror-Soft-Stumpfe-Kanuelen` is the linked shop/category page for Mirror Soft blunt cannulas (source: https://promainject.com/Mirror-Soft-Stumpfe-Kanuelen).

## Key Claims From The Website

- Mirror Soft is presented as blunt atraumatic cannulas for biostimulators and dermal fillers (source: https://mirrorsoft.de/).
- The website claims that an extra-wide exit opening and polished inner tube support flow for suspension-based biostimulators and dermal fillers and reduce the risk of clogging and cannula changes during injection procedures (source: https://mirrorsoft.de/).
- The website claims that the pilot needle and cannula are supplied together in one sterile blister pack, reducing additional handling steps and supporting patient safety (source: https://mirrorsoft.de/).
- The website positions the product around fewer cannula changes, safety/practicality, patient comfort, and cost efficiency (source: https://mirrorsoft.de/).
- PromaMedical presents itself as a wholesale partner for professionals in aesthetic dermatology, headquartered in the Munich metropolitan area, with PromaDerm and PromaInject divisions (source: https://promamedical.de/).

## Product Facts Reported By The Site

- The MirrorSoft website lists gauges from 17G to 30G, with outer diameters from 1.40 mm to 0.31 mm and lengths from 25 mm to 70 mm depending on gauge (source: https://mirrorsoft.de/).
- The PromaInject shop page lists 17 Mirror Soft blunt cannula variants and shows prices of 4.50 EUR per unit excluding VAT for listed variants, with minimum purchase and purchase interval of 10 units shown on product cards (source: https://promainject.com/Mirror-Soft-Stumpfe-Kanuelen).
- The shop page places Mirror Soft under the `Kanuelen` category and also shows adjacent categories such as injection systems, injection needles, syringes, multi-injectors, and catheters (source: https://promainject.com/Mirror-Soft-Stumpfe-Kanuelen).

## Caveats

- The MirrorSoft website says information is for general guidance, may change, and users must ensure suitability and follow manufacturer instructions (source: https://mirrorsoft.de/).
- PromaInject products are described as single-use and not for reuse or resterilization (source: https://mirrorsoft.de/; source: https://promamedical.de/).
- Claims about safety, infection risk, clogging, patient comfort, cost efficiency, and regulatory or quality standards are website claims. They should be verified against datasheets, manufacturer documentation, regulatory documents, and, where relevant, clinical or technical evidence before use in final strategy or sales material.
