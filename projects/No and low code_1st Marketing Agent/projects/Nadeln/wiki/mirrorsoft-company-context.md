# MirrorSoft Company Context

**Summary**: First evidence-backed context pass for MirrorSoft / atraumatic premium cannulas.

---

## What MirrorSoft Offers

Mirror Soft is presented as blunt atraumatic cannulas for biostimulators and dermal fillers (source: https://mirrorsoft.de/).

The product appears to sit inside PromaInject's injection and application systems portfolio, with purchase routed through the PromaInject shop (source: https://mirrorsoft.de/; source: https://promainject.com/Mirror-Soft-Stumpfe-Kanuelen).

## Positioning Signals

The official website emphasizes fewer cannula changes, patient comfort, safety/practicality, and cost efficiency (source: https://mirrorsoft.de/).

The core product argument appears to be: extra-wide exit opening plus polished inner tube should support flow for suspension-based biostimulators and dermal fillers, reducing clogging and cannula-change interruptions during injection procedures (source: https://mirrorsoft.de/).

The packaging argument is: pilot needle and atraumatic cannula are supplied together in one sterile blister pack, which the website links to fewer handling steps and patient safety (source: https://mirrorsoft.de/).

After data sheet review, the strongest current evidence supports a cautious technical story around wide exit opening, smooth edge, polished or obstruction-free lumen, and suitability for suspension-based filler workflows. Claims about patient safety, infection-risk reduction, comfort improvement, and price superiority remain higher-risk and need review before external use (source: [[mirrorsoft-datenblatt-und-belege-auswertung]]).

## Company / Channel Context

PromaMedical describes itself as a wholesale partner for professionals in aesthetic dermatology, headquartered in the Munich metropolitan area (source: https://promamedical.de/).

PromaMedical describes two divisions: PromaDerm for premium injectables such as hyaluronic acid fillers and biostimulators, and PromaInject for precise injection and application systems (source: https://promamedical.de/).

The Mirror Soft product is sold through the PromaInject shop page for blunt cannulas, where 17 variants are listed (source: https://promainject.com/Mirror-Soft-Stumpfe-Kanuelen).

## Product Range

The official website lists gauges from 17G through 30G, with outer diameters from 1.40 mm to 0.31 mm and lengths from 25 mm to 70 mm depending on gauge (source: https://mirrorsoft.de/).

The shop page shows Mirror Soft blunt cannula variants priced at 4.50 EUR per unit excluding VAT, with minimum purchase and purchase interval of 10 units shown on product cards (source: https://promainject.com/Mirror-Soft-Stumpfe-Kanuelen).

## Early Customer Hypotheses

- Primary user hypothesis: physicians or trained professionals performing aesthetic dermatology procedures with dermal fillers or biostimulators (source: https://mirrorsoft.de/; source: https://promamedical.de/; analysis).
- Buyer hypothesis: practices, clinics, aesthetic dermatology professionals, purchasing staff, or distributors who care about workflow reliability, safety perception, product availability, and unit economics (source: https://promamedical.de/; analysis; needs verification).
- Decision criteria hypothesis: flow behavior with suspension-based products, reduced clogging, sterile handling, range of sizes, price, compatibility with existing injection workflows, and trust in supplier/manufacturer (source: https://mirrorsoft.de/; analysis; needs verification).
