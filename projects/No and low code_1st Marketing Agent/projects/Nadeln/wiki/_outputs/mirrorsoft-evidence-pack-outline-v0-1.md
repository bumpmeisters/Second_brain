# MirrorSoft Evidence Pack Outline v0.1

**Summary**: Working outline for the evidence and documentation pack MirrorSoft needs before stronger sales, distributor, training, or campaign work.

---

## Status

This is an evidence planning document. It does not certify that claims are approved, complete, or externally usable.

The goal is to define what evidence exists, what evidence is missing, and which materials should be built next for trustworthy marketing and sales enablement.

## Core Principle

MirrorSoft should grow from a proof-led story:

1. Say only what the evidence supports.
2. Separate product facts, technical evidence, practitioner feedback, and commercial claims.
3. Keep clinical, safety, superiority, infection, pain, tissue-injury, regulatory, and price-leadership claims blocked until specifically supported and reviewed.
4. Make gaps visible instead of smoothing them over.

## Evidence Pack Users

| User | What they need from the pack |
|---|---|
| Aesthetic injector / practitioner | Product rationale, size range, handling logic, evidence summary, trial guidance. |
| Practice owner / clinic lead | Differentiation, documentation basics, cost and standardization rationale, risk boundaries. |
| Purchasing / operations | SKU/REF/GTIN, pack size, price, supplier details, IFU, certificates, reorder clarity. |
| Distributor / reseller | Safe claims, sales story, FAQ, competitor boundaries, training material. |
| Trainer / KOL | Full evidence caveats, publication details, study limitations, transparent scientific framing. |

9. Any internal test data on flow, clogging, or product handling.
10. Any approved legal/regulatory claim guidance already used by the company.
