Mirror Soft is presented as a blunt atraumatic cannula product for biostimulators and dermal fillers (source: https://mirrorsoft.de/).

## 4. Product Range Snapshot

The official website lists gauges from 17G to 30G, with outer diameters from 1.40 mm to 0.31 mm and lengths from 25 mm to 70 mm depending on gauge (source: https://mirrorsoft.de/).

The PromaInject shop page lists 17 Mirror Soft blunt cannula variants and shows 4.50 EUR per unit excluding VAT for listed variants, with minimum purchase and purchase interval of 10 units shown on product cards (source: https://promainject.com/Mirror-Soft-Stumpfe-Kanuelen).

This suggests a range strategy: broad size availability plus accessible unit pricing. That is an analysis hypothesis and needs comparison against alternatives before being used as a competitive claim.
