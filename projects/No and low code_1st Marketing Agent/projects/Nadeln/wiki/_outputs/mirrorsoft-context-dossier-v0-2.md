## Evidence-Backed Product Understanding

MirrorSoft is best understood, at this stage, as a blunt atraumatic cannula proposition for aesthetic practitioners working with suspension-based biostimulators and dermal fillers, especially PLLA and CaHA contexts (source: ../mirrorsoft-datenblatt-und-belege-auswertung.md).

The product story has three pillars:

1. Flow and anti-clogging: extrawide exit opening and polished lumen.
2. Tissue / comfort hypothesis: smooth inner edge of the exit opening.
3. Handling and workflow: pilot needle plus cannula in a sterile blister pack.
