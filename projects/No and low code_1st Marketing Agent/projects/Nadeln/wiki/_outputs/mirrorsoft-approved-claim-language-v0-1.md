## Status And Use

This document translates the claim matrix into usable language tiers:

1. `Internal only`: useful for strategy and planning, not for external copy.
2. `Careful external draft`: conservative language that may be used in draft marketing concepts, still requiring final review.
3. `Blocked pending review`: do not use externally until stronger evidence, regulatory review, or claim approval exists.

All external-facing wording should be checked against current IFU, declaration of conformity, manufacturer details, product labels, packaging, and applicable legal/regulatory guidance before publication.

## Core Safe Territory

MirrorSoft can currently be discussed most safely around:

- Product category: atraumatic blunt cannulas for aesthetic filler and biostimulator workflows.
- Design features: wide exit opening, smooth edge, polished or obstruction-aware lumen.
- Workflow relevance: designed to address flow and clogging-related workflow challenges.
- Packaging: pilot needle and blunt cannula in sterile blister packaging, if current packaging/IFU confirms it.
- Evidence: a scoped 2025 electron-microscopy letter and 2026 reply, with caveats.

## Recommended Message House Draft

This is internal working language.

### Category

Atraumatic blunt cannulas for aesthetic filler and biostimulator workflows.

### Problem

Suspension-based fillers and biostimulators can create flow and clogging-related workflow challenges.

### Product Answer

MirrorSoft is designed around a wide exit opening, smooth edge, and polished flow path to support practical injection workflows.

### Proof

The current evidence includes official product information and a scoped electron-microscopy comparison published as a Journal of Cosmetic Dermatology letter, plus a related reply discussing cannula design criteria.

### Caveat

Claims about safety, infection risk, tissue injury, comfort, clinical superiority, regulatory standards, and market price superiority require further review before external use.
