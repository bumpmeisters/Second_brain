## Positioning Hypothesis

MirrorSoft is a premium practical blunt cannula solution for aesthetic filler and biostimulator workflows, designed around smooth flow, a wide exit opening, and practical sterile handling.

This positioning is deliberately design-led and workflow-led. It does not claim clinical superiority, infection reduction, guaranteed no-clogging, or improved patient outcomes.

## Message House

### Audience

Aesthetic practitioners, practice owners, clinic operators, purchasing roles, distributors, and trainer / KOL roles involved in filler and biostimulator workflows.

Status: hypothesis. Needs customer validation.

### Category

Atraumatic blunt cannulas for aesthetic filler and biostimulator workflows.

### Core Problem

Suspension-based fillers and biostimulators can create flow and clogging-related workflow challenges. These challenges may create friction for practitioners during treatment planning, product handling, and procedure flow.

### Product Answer

MirrorSoft is designed around a wide exit opening, smooth edge, and polished or obstruction-aware flow path to support practical injection workflows.

### Core Promise

Support smoother, more practical filler and biostimulator workflows through design-focused cannula features.

## What We Should Not Say

Do not use:

- Clinically proven safer.
- Guaranteed clog-free.
- Prevents infection.
- Reduces infection risk.
- Increases patient safety.
- Reduces tissue injury.
- Improves patient comfort.
- Complies with highest EMA standards.
- Significantly below market average.
- Superior to TSK / SoftFil / DermaSculpt.

## Strategic Tension

MirrorSoft has a potentially strong product-design story, but the current evidence base is narrower than the strongest possible marketing claims.

This creates the central strategic discipline:

- Lead with design and workflow relevance.
- Use study references carefully and narrowly.
- Avoid clinical-outcome or regulatory overreach.
- Build trust through transparency and evidence hierarchy.
