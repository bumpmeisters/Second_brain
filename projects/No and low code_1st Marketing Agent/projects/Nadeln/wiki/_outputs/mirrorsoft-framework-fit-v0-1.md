## Starting Point

MirrorSoft now has a basic evidence base: product context, data sheet/evidence review, context dossier v0.2, and a claim matrix (source: mirrorsoft-context-dossier-v0-2.md; source: mirrorsoft-claim-matrix-v0-1.md).

The key constraint is claim safety. The strongest current territory is product design and workflow logic: wide exit opening, smooth edge, polished or obstruction-free lumen, and suspension-filler flow. Higher-risk territories include patient safety, infection-risk reduction, comfort improvement, clinical superiority, EMA standards, price superiority, and direct competitor claims (source: mirrorsoft-claim-matrix-v0-1.md).

This means the first strategy work should be evidence-led positioning and segmentation, not campaign copy.

## Framework-by-Framework Fit Notes

### 1. Claim Matrix / Evidence Validation

Role for MirrorSoft:

- Acts as the gate before messaging, segmentation, and sales material.
- Prevents sensitive claims from leaking into external copy.
- Forces every claim into evidence/risk/use categories.

Current fit:

- Essential, already started with `mirrorsoft-claim-matrix-v0-1.md`.
- Should become a reusable workflow for any medical, technical, or regulated product.

Next output:

- `mirrorsoft-approved-claim-language-v0-1.md`, with three levels: internal only, careful public wording, blocked pending review.

### 2. Messaging Frameworks + Brand System

Role for MirrorSoft:

- Turns product facts and safe claims into a message hierarchy.
- Separates category, value proposition, proof points, differentiators, and caveats.

Fit:

- Very strong because MirrorSoft's value depends on precise claim language.
- Should use proof-led messaging rather than broad emotional claims.

Possible structure:

- Category: blunt atraumatic cannulas for aesthetic filler / biostimulator workflows.
- Problem: flow interruptions and clogging-related workflow friction.
- Design answer: wide opening, smooth edge, polished/obstruction-aware lumen.
- Proof: scoped JCD microscopy evidence plus packaging facts.
- Caveat: not a claim of clinical superiority or guaranteed outcome.

### 3. B2B Persona Development

Role for MirrorSoft:

- Distinguishes users, buyers, influencers, and channel partners.
- Prevents one generic "doctor" audience.

Likely starting personas:

- Aesthetic physician / injector.
- Practice owner or clinic lead.
- Purchasing / operations role.
- Distributor / reseller.
- Trainer / KOL / scientific influencer.

Fit:

- High, but still hypothesis-driven because no customer voice has been ingested.

Next output:

- `mirrorsoft-persona-hypotheses-v0-1.md`.

### 4. Customer Journey Mapping

Role for MirrorSoft:

- Maps which evidence is needed at each stage.
- Helps avoid asking early-stage prospects to believe high-risk claims too soon.

MirrorSoft journey hypothesis:

- Passive awareness: "there is a cannula design issue in suspension-filler workflows."
- Active awareness: "wide opening and smooth-edge design may matter."
- Consideration: "how does MirrorSoft compare to my current cannula?"
- Active evaluation: "which sizes, price, packaging, documentation, and evidence support adoption?"
- Purchase: "how do I order, trial, train, and use responsibly?"

Fit:

- High, especially for content and sales enablement planning.

### 5. Campaign Types And Funnel Stages

Role for MirrorSoft:

- Converts evidence and personas into campaign roles.
- Keeps brand, demand generation, lead generation, conversion, and nurture separate.

Likely initial campaign mix:

- Demand generation: education around suspension-filler flow and cannula design criteria.
- Lead generation: downloadable technical summary / sample request / distributor inquiry.
- Conversion: PromaInject shop or sales contact.
- Nurture: evidence updates, application guidance, product range education.

Fit:

- Medium-high, but should follow messaging/persona work.

## Recommended Sequence

1. Finish claim governance:
   Create approved/blocked wording variants from the claim matrix.

2. Build positioning:
   Create a proof-led message house using Messaging Frameworks + Brand System.

3. Build personas:
   Create 4-5 persona hypotheses with needs, triggers, objections, and evidence requirements.

4. Map journey:
   Place each persona across awareness, consideration, evaluation, and purchase.

5. Build campaign architecture:
   Translate the journey into brand / demand-gen / lead-gen / conversion / nurture roles.
