# MirrorSoft Customer Journey Map v0.1

**Summary**: First journey map for MirrorSoft across awareness, consideration, evaluation, purchase, and adoption. This is a hypothesis map, not validated customer research.

---

## Status

This journey map is based on the current MirrorSoft persona hypotheses, message house, approved claim language, evidence review, and benchmark synthesis.

It should guide interview planning, content planning, sales enablement, and evidence collection. It should not yet be treated as a final go-to-market plan.

## Journey Logic

MirrorSoft is not primarily a broad consumer-style awareness problem. The likely adoption path is evidence-sensitive and role-based:

1. A practitioner or trainer notices a practical handling problem or technical product story.
2. The product must be made credible without overstating clinical or safety claims.
3. The clinic, purchasing role, or distributor needs documentation and SKU clarity.
4. Trial or adoption depends on trust, usability, price/availability, and claim-safe explanation.
5. Long-term growth depends on education, practitioner confidence, and repeatable sales material.

## Stage 1: Awareness

### Likely Customer State

Potential buyers or influencers first become aware of MirrorSoft through the official website, distributor/shop listing, peer recommendation, trainer mention, scientific discussion, or content about suspension-filler workflows.

### Main Questions

- What is MirrorSoft?
- Is it a cannula for fillers, biostimulators, or both?
- What makes it different from other blunt cannulas?
- Is this a credible professional product or just another accessory claim?

### Role-Specific Triggers

| Persona | Trigger |
|---|---|
| Aesthetic injector / practitioner | Sees a design story related to flow, wide exit opening, or biostimulator handling. |
| Practice owner / clinic lead | Hears that practitioners want to test a premium cannula option. |
| Purchasing / operations | Receives a product request or sees a new supplier/shop listing. |
| Distributor / reseller | Notices a potentially differentiated cannula SKU with channel potential. |
| Trainer / KOL | Encounters the JCD publication angle or technical design discussion. |

### Needed Assets

- Clear one-page product introduction.
- Short visual explanation of the wide exit opening and smooth flow path.
- Claim-safe category language.
- Link to official product page and shop page.
- Evidence caveat statement for professional audiences.

### Safe Core Message

MirrorSoft is a design-led blunt cannula for aesthetic filler and biostimulator workflows, with a wide exit opening and smooth flow path intended to support practical handling.

### Avoid

- "Clinically safer."
- "No clogging."
- "Less pain."
- "Less tissue damage."
- "Best cannula on the market."

## Stage 2: Consideration

### Likely Customer State

The prospect is interested but comparing MirrorSoft with familiar cannula brands or current practice habits.

### Main Questions

- Why should I consider switching or testing?
- Which procedures and products is it relevant for?
- What evidence exists, and what does it actually prove?
- Is the price reasonable compared with alternatives?
- Can I explain the product responsibly to colleagues or customers?

### Role-Specific Needs

| Persona | Consideration Need |
|---|---|
| Aesthetic injector / practitioner | Practical handling rationale, sizes, product compatibility boundaries, trial confidence. |
| Practice owner / clinic lead | Evidence-aware differentiation, team adoption logic, price and supplier clarity. |
| Purchasing / operations | SKU, pack size, price, documentation, supplier reliability. |
| Distributor / reseller | Approved claim language, objection handling, competitor comparison boundaries. |
| Trainer / KOL | Full evidence caveats, study scope, conflict disclosures, responsible teaching frame. |

### Needed Assets

- Claim matrix summary for commercial teams.
- Evidence summary with plain-language limitations.
- Product variant table.
- Competitor benchmark working table.
- FAQ for safe answers to common objections.

### Safe Core Message

MirrorSoft can be considered when a practice wants a premium blunt cannula option with a technical design story around flow-path geometry, especially in workflows where suspension-product handling matters.

### Avoid

- Direct superiority claims against named competitors without controlled benchmark evidence.
- Broad generalization from one studied size to the full product range.
- Treating early technical evidence as clinical outcome evidence.

## Stage 3: Evaluation

### Likely Customer State

The prospect is deciding whether MirrorSoft is credible enough to trial, stock, recommend, or list.

### Main Questions

- Are the documentation basics complete?
- What exactly is the intended use?
- Which variants are available?
- What is included in the sterile package?
- What can we safely say in internal and external communication?
- What evidence gaps remain?

### Evaluation Checklist

| Evidence / documentation | Current status | Importance |

| Label / REF / GTIN master data | Partial / needs confirmation | High |

| Customer or practitioner feedback | Missing | Medium |
| Controlled comparative flow data | Missing | Medium |

### Role-Specific Decision Criteria

| Persona | Decision Criteria |
|---|---|
| Aesthetic injector / practitioner | Handling confidence, size fit, trusted recommendation, trial experience. |
| Practice owner / clinic lead | Evidence quality, documentation, price/availability, team acceptance. |
| Purchasing / operations | Clean product data, ordering simplicity, documentation completeness. |
| Distributor / reseller | Margin/listing viability, safe claims, training assets, supply reliability. |
| Trainer / KOL | Evidence integrity, transparent limitations, technical plausibility. |

### Needed Assets

- Evidence pack.
- Documentation checklist.
- Trial protocol or structured feedback form.
- Internal sales FAQ.
- Approved language sheet.

### Safe Core Message

MirrorSoft has a credible technical design narrative and early publication-linked evidence, but external clinical, safety, and superiority claims should remain constrained until stronger documentation and validation are available.

### Avoid

- Hiding missing documentation.
- Using unresolved PDF links as secured evidence.
- Overclaiming the JCD publications.

## Stage 4: Purchase / Trial

### Likely Customer State

The buyer or influencer is ready to obtain samples, place a small order, or run a structured trial.

### Main Questions

- Which SKU should we buy first?
- How many units do we need for a meaningful trial?
- What procedures should we test in?
- How do we capture feedback?
- Who signs off if we want to standardize?

### Trial Design Hypothesis

A strong first commercial motion is not "buy everything." It is a small, structured trial with evidence-aware feedback.

Suggested trial dimensions:

- Procedure/product context.
- Cannula size used.
- Current alternative brand or baseline.
- Handling perception.
- Flow resistance perception.
- Interruption/clogging observations.
- Packaging and setup feedback.
- Willingness to reorder.
- Open objections.

### Needed Assets

- Trial starter recommendation.
- Feedback form.
- Product variant cheat sheet.
- Purchasing-ready SKU/price table.
- Responsible internal explanation for clinic teams.

### Safe Core Message

Test MirrorSoft in defined filler or biostimulator workflows and collect structured practitioner feedback before standardizing across the practice or channel.

### Avoid

- Promising trial outcomes.
- Suggesting universal replacement of existing cannulas before feedback.
- Using anecdotal trial feedback as broad proof.

## Stage 5: Adoption / Expansion

### Likely Customer State

The customer has tested MirrorSoft and is deciding whether to reorder, standardize, recommend, distribute, or teach with it.

### Main Questions

- Did it perform reliably enough in real use?
- Which procedures and practitioners fit best?
- Can we train the team consistently?
- Can we order it easily and document it cleanly?
- Can we communicate the rationale responsibly?

### Adoption Levers

| Persona | Adoption Lever |
|---|---|
| Aesthetic injector / practitioner | Positive handling experience and confidence in relevant workflows. |
| Practice owner / clinic lead | Standardization logic, team feedback, evidence-aware positioning. |
| Purchasing / operations | Reliable reorder process and complete documentation. |
| Distributor / reseller | Repeatable sales story, objection handling, margin/listing clarity. |
| Trainer / KOL | Educational credibility and transparent limitation handling. |

### Needed Assets

- Reorder and standardization checklist.
- Internal team training note.
- Distributor sales enablement pack.
- KOL / trainer evidence brief.
- Updated claim matrix based on any new validated evidence.

### Safe Core Message

MirrorSoft adoption should be built from documented product use, responsible evidence framing, and role-specific enablement, not from broad clinical or superiority claims.

### Avoid

- Turning internal feedback into unqualified external claims.
- Removing caveats from evidence summaries.
- Expanding into patient-safety language without review.
