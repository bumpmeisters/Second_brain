## Allowed Messaging Territories

Usable for internal strategy and possibly careful external wording after review:

- Product category: atraumatic blunt cannulas for aesthetic filler / biostimulator workflows.
- Design logic: wide exit opening, smooth edge, polished or obstruction-free lumen.
- Workflow logic: designed to address flow and clogging-related interruptions.
- Packaging fact: pilot needle plus cannula in sterile blister pack, if current packaging/IFU confirms it.
- Evidence-backed technical angle: microscopy-based comparison, carefully scoped to the published study.

## Red-Flag Territories

Do not use externally without claim review:

- Patient safety improvement.
- Infection-risk reduction.
- Comfort improvement during/after treatment.
- Tissue-injury reduction.
- Clinical superiority.
- EMA / regulatory standards.
- Price superiority versus market average.
- Comparative claims against named competitors.
