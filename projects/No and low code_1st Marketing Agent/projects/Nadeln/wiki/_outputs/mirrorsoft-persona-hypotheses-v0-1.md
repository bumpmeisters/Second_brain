# MirrorSoft Persona Hypotheses v0.1

**Summary**: First persona and segmentation hypotheses for MirrorSoft. These are working assumptions, not validated customer facts.

---

## Status

This document is hypothesis-driven. No customer interviews, sales notes, CRM data, distributor feedback, training feedback, or market research have been ingested yet.

Use these personas to guide validation, customer interviews, journey mapping, content planning, and sales enablement. Do not treat them as final segmentation.

## Segmentation Logic

MirrorSoft should not start with demographic segmentation. The more useful first segmentation is based on role in the buying/adoption system:

1. Clinical/practical user.
2. Practice or clinic decision maker.
3. Purchasing / operations gatekeeper.
4. Distributor / reseller.
5. Trainer / KOL / scientific influencer.

This fits the current Message House because the product story depends on workflow, evidence, claim safety, and practical adoption.

## Persona 1: Aesthetic Injector / Practitioner

### Role

Physician, aesthetic doctor, dermatologist, or qualified injector who directly uses cannulas in filler and biostimulator workflows.

### Likely Jobs

- Select the right cannula for filler or biostimulator application.
- Maintain smooth treatment workflow.
- Reduce avoidable interruptions and product-handling friction.
- Feel confident about product behavior during injection.
- Explain tool choices responsibly to patients when needed.

### Likely Triggers

- Frustration with flow resistance or clogging-related interruptions.
- Adoption of suspension-based products such as PLLA or CaHA.
- Need for a broader or more practical cannula range.
- Interest in technical product design and handling.
- Recommendation by distributor, trainer, or peer.

### Likely Objections

- "Is this actually better than my current cannula?"
- "Does it work across the products I use?"
- "Is the claim evidence strong enough?"
- "Will this change my handling technique?"
- "Is there a risk in switching from a known brand?"

### Evidence Needed

- IFU / intended use.
- Product range by gauge/length.
- Technical explanation of wide opening, smooth edge, and flow path.
- Narrowly scoped JCD evidence summary.
- Sample/trial or practitioner feedback, if available later.

### Safe Message Angle

MirrorSoft is designed to support practical filler and biostimulator workflows through wide-opening geometry and a smooth flow path.

### Avoid

- "Safer injections."
- "No clogging."
- "Less pain."
- "Less tissue injury."
- Any clinical outcome claim.

## Persona 2: Practice Owner / Clinic Lead

### Role

Owner, medical director, or clinic lead responsible for treatment quality, product selection, economics, staff consistency, and reputation.

### Likely Jobs

- Choose reliable tools for the practice.
- Balance practitioner confidence, cost, and supplier trust.
- Standardize products across practitioners.
- Reduce friction in purchasing and treatment setup.
- Avoid reputational or compliance risk from overclaimed products.

### Likely Triggers

- Need to standardize cannula selection.
- Rising consumable cost pressure.
- New filler/biostimulator service lines.
- Practitioner requests for better handling options.
- Distributor or supplier recommendation.

### Likely Objections

- "Is there enough evidence to justify switching?"
- "Will my team adopt it?"
- "Are documentation and regulatory basics in order?"
- "Is the pricing stable and supply reliable?"
- "Does this fit our premium positioning?"

### Evidence Needed

- Current price and pack-size clarity.
- Supplier and manufacturer relationship.
- IFU, certificate, declaration of conformity, label / packaging details.
- Practical sales sheet with safe claims.
- Benchmark versus current cannula brands.

### Safe Message Angle

MirrorSoft offers a design-led cannula option for practices that want practical handling and evidence-aware product selection in filler and biostimulator workflows.

### Avoid

- Price-superiority claims without benchmark.
- Clinical superiority.
- Safety claims beyond documentation.

## Persona 3: Purchasing / Operations Gatekeeper

### Role

Practice manager, purchasing coordinator, clinic operations role, or procurement-adjacent decision influencer.

### Likely Jobs

- Ensure product availability.
- Compare SKUs, price, minimum order, delivery, and supplier reliability.
- Maintain documentation for purchased medical / aesthetic consumables.
- Reduce ordering complexity.
- Avoid products with unclear manufacturer or compliance information.

### Likely Triggers

- Request from practitioner or clinic lead.
- Need for replacement or alternative cannula supplier.
- Price review.
- Stockout or delivery issue.
- New product trial.

### Likely Objections

- "Where are the REF / SKU / GTIN details?"
- "Who is the manufacturer?"
- "What is included in the package?"
- "What is the minimum order and unit price?"
- "Can we document this cleanly?"

### Evidence Needed

- Product variant master list.
- REF / GTIN / pack size.
- Current shop page and price.
- Manufacturer/importer/distributor details.
- IFU, packaging label, certificates.

### Safe Message Angle

MirrorSoft is available through PromaInject with listed variants and unit pricing; further documentation should be obtained before procurement standardization.

### Avoid

- "Best price."
- "Market-leading value."
- Claims unsupported by procurement-ready documents.

## Persona 4: Distributor / Reseller

### Role

Channel partner who may need to understand, explain, and sell MirrorSoft to practices, clinics, or professionals.

### Likely Jobs

- Understand the product story quickly.
- Know which claims are safe.
- Compare MirrorSoft with known cannula alternatives.
- Train sales reps on evidence and objections.
- Avoid risky claim language.

### Likely Triggers

- New product listing or distributor opportunity.
- Need for differentiation against TSK, SoftFil, DermaSculpt, or other cannulas.
- Price / margin opportunity.
- Customer questions about fillers and biostimulators.
- Demand for technical proof.

### Likely Objections

- "What can I safely claim?"
- "How does this compare to TSK or SoftFil?"
- "Is the evidence independent enough?"
- "Who manufactures it?"
- "Do we have approved sales material?"

### Evidence Needed

- Approved claim language.
- Manufacturer / brand relationship.
- Technical product sheet.
- Benchmark table.
- Sales enablement FAQ.
- Evidence summary with caveats.

### Safe Message Angle

MirrorSoft gives distributors a design-led cannula story centered on flow-path geometry, wide exit opening, and practical sterile handling, with claim-safe proof points.

### Avoid

- Unreviewed competitor superiority.
- Unreviewed medical outcome claims.
- Overstating the JCD publications.

## Persona 5: Trainer / KOL / Scientific Influencer

### Role

Trainer, educator, experienced injector, medical-scientific voice, or peer influencer who may shape trust and adoption.

### Likely Jobs

- Evaluate whether the technical story is credible.
- Translate product design into responsible education.
- Avoid weak or overreaching claims.
- Help practitioners understand when and how the product may fit.
- Challenge evidence quality and bias.

### Likely Triggers

- Interest in suspension-filler injection technique.
- Need for technical education content.
- Study publication or discussion.
- Product demonstration.
- Peer questions about clogging and cannula design.

### Likely Objections

- "Is the evidence too narrow?"
- "Was the study sponsored?"
- "Can findings from 22G/50mm apply to other sizes?"
- "Where are flow dynamics or post-use data?"
- "What exactly can be said responsibly?"

### Evidence Needed

- Full literature review.
- Study scope and limitation summary.
- Disclosure of sponsorship / conflicts.
- Product documentation and IFU.
- Demo assets that avoid clinical overclaiming.

### Safe Message Angle

MirrorSoft can be discussed as a design-led cannula with early technical evidence around exit-opening geometry and lumen/edge characteristics, while acknowledging that further research is needed.

### Avoid

- Treating the JCD publications as broad clinical proof.
- Ignoring sponsorship/conflict caveats.
- Generalizing one size comparison to the full product range.

## Validation Questions

Use these in interviews, sales calls, or internal review:

1. Which cannula brands are currently used, and why?
2. In which procedures do flow resistance or clogging concerns actually matter?
3. Which product sizes are most important?
4. Who decides cannula selection: injector, clinic owner, purchasing, distributor, or trainer?
5. What proof would make a practitioner trial MirrorSoft?
6. What documentation is mandatory before a clinic or distributor adopts it?
7. How sensitive is price compared with evidence, availability, brand trust, and handling?
8. Which claims would customers find credible, and which would feel overstated?
