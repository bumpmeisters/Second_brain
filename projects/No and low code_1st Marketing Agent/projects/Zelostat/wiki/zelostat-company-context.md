# Zelostat Company And Product Context

**Summary**: Zelostat is presented as a brand/product family of single-use low-dead-space injection needles distributed in Germany through PromaMedical/PromaInject. The legal manufacturer role appears to involve ASTI Corporation but is not yet verified from regulatory documentation.

---

## What Is Sourced

- The official site foregrounds a 35G / 4 mm LDS needle for botulinum toxin micro-injections (source: zelostat.com).
- The official range also includes 31G and 34G variants (source: zelostat.com).
- PromaInject is a division of PromaMedical GmbH focused on aesthetic injection application systems (source: zelostat.com/promainject-details).
- The PromaInject shop identifies ASTI Corporation as manufacturer for visible Zelostat items (source: promainject.com).
- Products are described as single-use (source: zelostat.com).

## What The Sources Claim

- Lower residual volume and reduced product waste.
- Comparable handling to a conventional 34G despite a thinner 35G outer diameter.
- Improved comfort, less bruising, and fewer adverse events.

These are manufacturer/distributor claims and require independent technical or clinical evidence before stronger external use.

## What Is Inferred

- Zelostat appears to compete primarily as a professional B2B medical consumable for aesthetic practitioners and clinics. This is an inference from the product application, professional shop context, net pricing, and PromaInject portfolio.
- The likely commercial model is manufacturer/brand plus specialized distributor, but the exact ASTI–PromaMedical relationship needs verification.

## Evidence Gaps

- Legal manufacturer, brand owner, authorized representative, and distributor roles.
- Intended use and regulatory class/status.
- Technical substantiation for residual volume and flow.
- Clinical substantiation for comfort, bruising, and adverse-event language.
- Current geography, channels, customer base, and procurement path.
- Resolution of the official-page versus shop catalogue discrepancy.
