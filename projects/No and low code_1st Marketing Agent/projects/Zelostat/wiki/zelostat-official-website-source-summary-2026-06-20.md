# Source Summary: Zelostat Official Website

**Summary**: The official site presents Zelostat as a single-use, fine-gauge low-dead-space needle family for aesthetic injection procedures, with the 35G / 4 mm product emphasized for botulinum toxin micro-injections.

---

## What This Source Is

An official product and portfolio website connected to PromaMedical/PromaInject. It is authoritative for the public offer and manufacturer/distributor claims, but not independent proof of performance or patient outcomes.

## Source Provenance

- Owner/operator: not explicit on the product page; PromaMedical/PromaInject context is visible.
- Capture date: 2026-06-20.
- URL: `https://zelostat.com/`
- Source type: official company/product source.
- Trust status: summarized; performance and outcome claims need verification.

## Useful Facts

- Visible official range: 31G, 34G, and 35G; lengths of 4 mm, 9 mm, and 13 mm.
- Visible packaging: 50 or 100 pieces.
- The product sits within a broader PromaInject injection-system portfolio.

## Caveats And Contradictions

- The PromaInject shop lists a 25G / 25 mm Zelostat item that is absent from the official product overview.
- The legal manufacturer, brand owner, website operator, and distributor roles are not fully disentangled by the product page.
- No IFU, declaration of conformity, technical datasheet, bench-test method, or clinical evidence was available from the pages reviewed.
