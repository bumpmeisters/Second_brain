# Zelostat Post-PASS Handoff Readiness

## Verdict

The market-context packet is sufficient input for a later Buying Context analysis and for comparing segmentation strategies. Neither downstream analysis was executed.

## Buying-Context Readiness

Ready inputs:

- Bounded market and geography.
- Direct, standard, system, adjacent, and status-quo alternatives.
- Observable product-comparison dimensions.
- Explicit hypotheses about possible procurement and proof requirements.
- Role, trigger, comparison-unit, proof, and observable-signal questions.

Evidence that must remain unresolved rather than invented:

- Actual buyer, user, approver, reorder, and influencer roles.
- Real switching triggers and barriers.
- Relative importance of savings, comfort, bruising, flow, availability, price, and documentation.
- Procurement path and trial behavior.

## Segmentation-Strategy Readiness

Ready inputs:

- Multiple candidate segmentation bases are named without selecting one.
- Germany is separated from exploratory European scope.
- Product/system/status-quo alternatives are preserved.
- Evidence limitations and false-precision risks are visible.

Evidence that must be added before strong segment hypotheses:

- Customer, sales, distributor, order, or CRM evidence.
- Account and practice-type structure.
- Procedure/use-case frequency and product economics.
- Observable signals for current system, switching, evidence need, and procurement path.

## Stop Rule

Do not advance sensitive Zelostat savings, flow, comfort, bruising, or adverse-event claims into positioning or execution unless the claim-risk overlay is upgraded with suitable evidence.
