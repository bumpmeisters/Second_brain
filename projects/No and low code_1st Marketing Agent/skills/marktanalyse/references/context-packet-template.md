## ContextOps Purpose

This market analysis is not the marketing strategy. Its job is to answer:

```text
In which market and alternative space does [company/product] currently operate, and what market context must later analysis steps preserve?
```

## Scope Boundary

### In Scope

- Minimal market definition.
- Category and adjacent-category boundaries.
- Competitor and substitute clusters.
- Current evidence status.
- Open questions and data needs for the next iteration.

### Out Of Scope

- Segment priority.
- Persona development.
- Positioning recommendation.
- Strategic options.
- Campaign or channel strategy.
- Content pillars.
- SEO/GEO strategy.
- PDP recommendations.
- Sales enablement.
- Lifecycle, retention, or growth-loop recommendations.
