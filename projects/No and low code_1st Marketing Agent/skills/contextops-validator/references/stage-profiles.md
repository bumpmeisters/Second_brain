# Stage Validation Profiles

Choose one primary profile. Add overlays only when the artifact requires them.

## Evidence Intake

Required:

- Source inventory, type, owner, date, location, and status.
- Raw, research, and generated-output boundaries preserved.
- AI-generated research labeled separately.
- Source summaries distinguish company claims from independent proof.
- Evidence gaps and contradictions are explicit.

Block when primary source access is required but unavailable. Revise when registration or labeling is incomplete.

## Market Context

Required:

- Working market definition.
- Core, adjacent, extended, and excluded boundaries.
- Competitor and substitute clusters.
- Minimal market dynamics and evidence status.
- Handoff questions for buying contexts or segmentation.

Reject segment priority, positioning recommendations, content pillars, SEO/GEO, campaign plans, sales enablement, lifecycle, or growth-loop recommendations as scope leakage.

## Buying Contexts

Required:

- Buyer, user, recipient, beneficiary, influencer, decision maker, and partner distinctions when relevant.
- Trigger, job, barrier, proof need, observable signal, and evidence status per context.
- Hypothesis labels for unsupported contexts.
- Handoff to segmentation or audience work.

Reject fictional biographies, target priority, positioning, campaign angles, or lifecycle recommendations.

## Segmentation Strategy

Required:

- At least three credible segmentation strategies compared.
- Explicit business-model lens.
- Evidence requirements and false-precision risks.
- Scoring against downstream leverage and operational usefulness.
- Winner, fallback, initial hypotheses, and validation signals.

Reject a renamed persona list, unsupported precision, or downstream positioning and campaign execution.

## Audience Understanding

Required:

- Selected segments inherited rather than silently reselected.
- At least three candidate lenses considered and a focused framework stack chosen.
- Questions, language, objections, motivations, proof needs, trusted sources, and behavior.
- Evidence ledger, synthetic-hypothesis ledger, and validation plan.
- B2B roles or B2B2C layers kept distinct where material.

Reject synthetic persona output presented as customer truth or premature downstream execution.

## Framework Fit

Required:

- Framework selection follows company context and current stage.
- Canonical local framework documents are preferred.
- Selected stack is small enough to execute.
- Each framework has a reason, required input, and next artifact.
- Deferred frameworks have a clear reason.

Reject frameworks treated as company evidence or selected before sufficient context exists.

## Claim Governance

Required:

- Compound statements split into atomic claims.
- Exact wording, source, evidence strength, risk, and allowed use.
- Safer wording does not hide material uncertainty.
- Comparative and outcome claims include fair scope and caveats.
- Evidence requests are concrete.

Block unsupported high-risk external claims. Apply this profile as an overlay whenever another artifact contains sensitive claims.

## Proof-Led Positioning

Required:

- Category, audience, context, problem, promise, proof, and caveats align.
- Claims trace to the evidence base and claim-governance constraints.
- Proof pillars are distinct and useful.
- External wording is more conservative than internal hypotheses.
- Do-not-say guidance and evidence gaps are present.

Reject positioning that depends on invented audience truth or unsupported superiority.

## Journey And GTM

Required:

- Roles, stages, questions, objections, proof, conversion work, and handoffs align.
- Campaign roles map to journey jobs rather than a channel list.
- Sales enablement uses approved claims.
- Persona and journey assumptions are labeled.
- Missing validation evidence is explicit.

Reject generic funnel stages that ignore the actual purchase or adoption context.

## Framework Document

Required:

- Framework type and provenance are explicit.
- Expert-owned content is separated from project adaptation.
- Question engine, branching, counter-questions, evidence standard, failure modes, and handoff exist.
- Business-model adaptations materially change reasoning where needed.
- Structural lint passes and evaluation status is honest.

Use the Framework Builder evaluation rubric for semantic depth.

## Recursive Learning

Required:

- Completed artifacts and feedback support the lessons.
- Case-specific facts are separated from transferable patterns.
- Proposed changes identify evidence, conditions, risk, and next test.
- Skill-wide changes require cross-case support or severe failure.

Reject broad self-modification based on one unexamined impression.
