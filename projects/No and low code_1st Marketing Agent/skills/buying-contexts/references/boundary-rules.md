# Buying Context Boundary Rules

Use these rules before writing a buying-context artifact.

## Core Rule

A buying-context packet describes purchase situations and validation hooks. It does not choose the target segment or write the strategy.

## Allowed

- Buyer, user, recipient, decision maker, influencer, channel partner, and beneficiary role maps.
- Candidate buying contexts.
- Triggers and occasions.
- Jobs to be done.
- Barriers, anxieties, objections, and friction.
- Proof needs and tone guardrails.
- Observable signals in CRM, ecommerce, analytics, reviews, support, sales notes, surveys, or ad data.
- Evidence status, open questions, and handoff prompts.

## Not Allowed Unless Explicitly Requested

- Segment priority or ICP decision.
- Full persona biographies.
- Positioning recommendation.
- Value proposition, message house, or copy.
- Content pillars or SEO/GEO clusters.
- Campaign angles, media plan, or creative routes.
- Sales enablement assets.
- Lifecycle, retention, referral, or growth-loop strategy.

## Leakage Test

Before finalizing, ask:

1. Is this a context field or a decision?
2. Could a later segmentation or positioning step reasonably disagree?
3. Is this statement useful even if the final strategy chooses a different target?

If yes, keep it as context. If no, move it to handoff questions or remove it.

## Persona Theater Test

Avoid invented names, lifestyles, backstories, and psychological detail unless sourced. Prefer:

```text
Buyer role + recipient/user role + trigger + job + barrier + proof need + observable signal
```

over:

```text
Fictional persona biography + demographic guesses + generic needs
```
