## ContextOps Purpose

This packet is not a final segmentation, persona, positioning, campaign, or strategy artifact. It answers:

```text
Which buying situations, roles, triggers, jobs, barriers, proof needs, and observable signals must later analysis steps preserve?
```

## Scope Boundary

### In Scope

- Candidate buying contexts.
- Role distinctions.
- Triggers and jobs.
- Barriers and proof needs.
- Observable validation signals.
- Evidence status and open questions.

### Out Of Scope

- Segment priority.
- Persona biographies.
- Positioning recommendation.
- Copy, campaigns, content pillars, SEO/GEO, sales enablement, or lifecycle strategy.
