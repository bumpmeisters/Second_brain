# Business Case Adaptations

Use this reference when a buying-context packet needs a specific market logic. Keep the generic structure unless the evidence requires a variation.

## D2C / Ecommerce

Add or emphasize:

- Buyer versus recipient/user when gifts are involved.
- Product-line and variant signals.
- Viewed product, add to cart, checkout started, placed order, discount use, bundle attach, repeat order.
- Review language, support questions, email/SMS behavior, and post-purchase survey answers.
- Barriers such as effort, trust, delivery, price, product comprehension, gift suitability, and whether the recipient will use it.

Avoid:

- Turning product view data into segment priority without revenue/conversion validation.
- Writing PDP or lifecycle recommendations inside the context packet.

## B2C

Add or emphasize:

- Occasion, emotion, life stage, retail/marketplace alternatives, and household roles.
- Cultural or seasonal triggers when relevant.
- Social proof, trust, convenience, and category familiarity.

Avoid:

- Inventing demographic personas without evidence.

## B2B

Add or emphasize:

- Buying committee roles: economic buyer, technical buyer, user, influencer, blocker, procurement, legal/compliance.
- Buying jobs: problem recognition, solution exploration, requirements, supplier selection, validation, consensus.
- Account-level observable signals: firmographics, CRM stage, intent, sales notes, objections, meeting notes, renewal/expansion signals.
- Risk and proof: ROI, implementation, integration, security, compliance, procurement, internal consensus.

Avoid:

- Treating one persona as the buyer when decisions are committee-based.
- Writing sales enablement before role evidence and objections are mapped.

## B2B2C / Partner-Led

Add or emphasize:

- Paying buyer, channel partner, recommender, operator, end user, and beneficiary as separate roles.
- Partner incentives and end-user value may differ.
- Observable signals across partner pipeline, partner activation, end-user usage, support, and sell-through.

Avoid:

- Optimizing only for end-user appeal when the channel partner controls access.

## Marketplace / Retail

Add or emphasize:

- Platform shopper versus brand-site shopper.
- Retail buyer, category manager, marketplace algorithm, reviews, price, shipping, availability, and trust.
- Signals: search rank, conversion, reviews, return reasons, retail sell-through, stockouts.

Avoid:

- Assuming brand-site messaging carries unchanged into retailer contexts.

## Regulated Or Sensitive Categories

Add or emphasize:

- Claim risk, approval paths, allowed language, proof requirements, and do-not-say guidance.
- Route high-risk claims to `claim-governance`.

Avoid:

- Turning emotional, medical, financial, sustainability, performance, or safety claims into copy without evidence review.
