# Claim Risk Model

Score every claim by evidence strength and risk.

## Evidence Strength

| Score | Label | Meaning |
|---:|---|---|
| 0 | None | No source found. |
| 1 | Weak | Source asserts claim but gives no proof, or evidence is anecdotal/AI-generated. |
| 2 | Medium-low | Plausible support, indirect evidence, narrow study, or internal source only. |
| 3 | Medium | Official source plus some corroboration, but limited scope. |
| 4 | Medium-high | Primary source, current documentation, or scoped empirical evidence. |
| 5 | High | Strong primary evidence, independent corroboration, or official authoritative source. |

## Risk

| Risk | Claim type |
|---|---|
| Low | Descriptive facts, category labels, source-attributed statements. |
| Medium | Differentiation, product fit, workflow benefits, pricing facts, customer fit. |
| High | Performance, ROI, safety, health, compliance, superiority, named competitors. |
| Very high | Clinical outcomes, guaranteed results, infection/safety prevention, legal/regulatory approval, financial guarantees. |

## Allowed Use

| Allowed use | Rule |
|---|---|
| Internal strategy | Useful for analysis but not external copy. |
| Careful external draft | Conservative wording may be drafted, pending review. |
| Claim review required | Do not publish before legal/regulatory/technical review. |
| Do not use externally yet | Unsupported or too risky. |
