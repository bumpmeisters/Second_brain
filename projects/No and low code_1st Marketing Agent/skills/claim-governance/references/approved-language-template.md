# Approved Language Template

Create three language tiers.

```markdown
# Approved Claim Language

## Status And Use

This is not legal, regulatory, or medical approval. It is a conservative language guide for strategy and draft communication.

## Core Safe Territory

- ...

## Tier 1: Internal Only

| Topic | Internal wording | Why internal only |
|---|---|---|

## Tier 2: Careful External Draft

| Topic | Careful wording | Required caveat/source |
|---|---|---|

## Tier 3: Blocked Pending Review

| Blocked wording | Why blocked | Safer direction |
|---|---|---|

## Required Evidence To Upgrade Claims

| Claim territory | Needed evidence | Owner/follow-up |
|---|---|---|
```

## Wording Rules

- Prefer "designed to", "intended to", "may support", and "positioned for" when evidence is indirect.
- Avoid "proven", "guaranteed", "prevents", "best", "only", and unqualified "safe" unless strongly supported and reviewed.
- Scope studies by date, sample, method, geography, and limitation.
- Attribute claims to the source when evidence is self-reported.
