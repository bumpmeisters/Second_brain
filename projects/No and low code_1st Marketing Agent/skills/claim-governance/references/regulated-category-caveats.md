# Regulated Category Caveats

Use these guardrails when the company/product touches sensitive categories.

## Medical / Health

Block or require review for:

- Treats, cures, prevents, diagnoses.
- Safer, reduces infection, reduces injury, improves comfort, improves outcomes.
- Clinically proven, doctor recommended, patient-safe.
- Claims beyond IFU, label, cleared indication, or professional guidance.

## Financial / Insurance

Block or require review for:

- Guaranteed returns, savings, risk reduction, approval, compliance.
- Financial advice, investment outcomes, credit decisions.
- Claims based on outdated rates, prices, or regulations.

## Legal / Compliance

Block or require review for:

- Legally compliant, certified, approved, fully protected.
- Jurisdiction-specific guidance without current legal source.

## Sustainability / ESG

Block or require review for:

- Carbon neutral, net zero, sustainable, eco-friendly, ethical.
- Impact claims without methodology, scope, and date.

## Cybersecurity / Privacy

Block or require review for:

- Secure, unhackable, compliant, prevents breaches.
- Privacy/legal compliance without current evidence.
