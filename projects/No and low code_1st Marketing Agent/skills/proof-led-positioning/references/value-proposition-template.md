## One-Sentence Formula

For `[audience]` who `[problem or moment]`, `[company/product]` is a `[category]` that `[main promise]`, supported by `[proof]`.

## Validation Questions

- Is the audience supported by evidence or still a hypothesis?
- Is the category clear enough for buyers?
- Does the promise outrun the proof?
- What would a skeptical buyer ask next?
