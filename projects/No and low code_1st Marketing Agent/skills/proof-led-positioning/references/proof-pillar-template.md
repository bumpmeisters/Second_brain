## Pillar Rules

- Use 3-5 pillars.
- Each pillar must connect to source evidence or be marked as a hypothesis.
- Do not mix proof and aspiration in the same pillar.
- For external copy, prefer cautious wording when evidence is company-owned, indirect, or dynamic.
- For high-risk domains, route unsupported claims to claim review rather than softening them into marketing copy.
