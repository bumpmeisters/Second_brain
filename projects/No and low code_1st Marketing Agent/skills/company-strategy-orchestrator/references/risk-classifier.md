# Risk Classifier

Classify company/product cases before strategy work.

## Industry Risk

| Risk | Examples | Default handling |
|---|---|---|
| Low | Local services, simple B2B services, internal tools, non-sensitive consumer goods. | Evidence intake first; claim governance optional. |
| Medium | B2B SaaS, industrial products, logistics, education, HR, productivity, agencies. | Claim governance for performance, ROI, competitor, security, or compliance claims. |
| High | Medical, health, finance, insurance, legal, safety equipment, cybersecurity, regulated devices, sustainability/ESG. | Run claim governance before positioning or external copy. |

## Claim Sensitivity

Escalate to high claim sensitivity when the source includes:

- Health, safety, patient, diagnosis, treatment, pain, injury, infection, or clinical claims.
- ROI, savings, guaranteed performance, yield, productivity, or financial outcome claims.
- Legal, regulatory, compliance, certification, official standard, or approval claims.
- Sustainability, carbon, environmental, ethical sourcing, or impact claims.
- Security, privacy, data protection, or risk reduction claims.
- "Best", "only", "leading", "proven", "guaranteed", "superior", "certified", or named competitor claims.

## Output Implications

- Low risk: strategy can proceed after basic evidence intake.
- Medium risk: use claim governance for claim-heavy sections.
- High risk: do not create external-facing wording before claim governance.
