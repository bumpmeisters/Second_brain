# Editorial Positioning

**Summary**: A provisional definition of the territory Rolf wants to own publicly, the value readers should receive, and the boundaries that keep the work distinctive and credible.

---

## Positioning hypothesis

Rolf makes complex B2B growth and marketing systems easier to reason about and operate. He connects strategy, evidence, and execution; distinguishes durable principles from context-dependent practice; and turns research into decision-useful frameworks, diagnostics, and examples.

This is a working hypothesis to calibrate, not a finalized personal-brand claim.

## Editorial promise

Readers should leave with at least one of the following:

- a clearer way to diagnose a strategic or operating problem;
- a useful distinction they can apply to a decision;
- a framework with explicit conditions and limits;
- a practical translation from strategy into execution;
- a more honest view of what the evidence does and does not support.

## Current territories

### 1. Account-based growth

ABM as an adaptive enterprise growth system rather than an isolated campaign type. Current canonical custody: `projects/abm-operating-system/`.

### 2. Content as a strategic operating capability

Content strategy, thought leadership, content quality, reuse, distribution, and learning as a coherent system rather than disconnected production. Canonical operating custody: `projects/content-operating-system/`.

### Connecting lens

How evidence, context, operating models, and human judgment turn marketing concepts into repeatable practice.

The connecting lens is not a third topic pillar. It is the recurring way the two current territories are examined.

## Audience architecture

### Primary

CMOs and Heads of Marketing in complex B2B organizations who need to design, improve, or govern strategic marketing and growth systems.

### Secondary

- ABM, DemandGen, and GTM leaders who need operational depth on programs, buying groups, campaigns, and orchestration;
- marketing and AI practitioners when the content concerns agentic systems, ContentOps, or AI-enabled marketing work;
- prospective advisory clients encountering a concrete strategic or operating problem addressed by Rolf's methods.

This is a selected editorial focus, not a validated market conclusion. Every Creative Direction still chooses one primary reader and one decision situation. The architecture remains subject to calibration through relevant conversations, existing-network evidence, and published-content feedback.

The operational audience context is [[personal-content-audience]]. It treats role, skepticism, proof needs, language, and channel behavior as versioned hypotheses. Future client work must use a client or source-project audience profile and may not silently inherit this personal profile.

## Recognizable point of view

The editorial system should repeatedly express these working convictions:

- Start with the commercial and organizational situation, not a fashionable tactic.
- Separate evidence, interpretation, recommendation, and advocacy.
- Treat frameworks as decision tools, not universal recipes.
- Connect strategic architecture to operating reality.
- Make applicability conditions, tradeoffs, and uncertainty visible.
- Use public content to teach reasoning, not merely announce conclusions.

## Deliberate exclusions

Avoid building the public identity around:

- generic trend summaries without an original interpretation;
- certainty that exceeds the evidence;
- unqualified performance or attribution claims;
- high-volume commentary created only for cadence;
- borrowed frameworks presented as original;
- private or client-derived detail without explicit rights and approval;
- channel conventions that erase Rolf's own reasoning style.

## Editorial test

Before developing a topic, answer:

1. Is this inside a current territory or clearly connected to it?
2. What decision, diagnosis, or understanding will improve for the reader?
3. What is Rolf's contribution beyond summarizing available information?
4. Which parts are evidence, analysis, experience, hypothesis, or opinion?
5. Why is Rolf a credible and useful person to make this argument?

## Open calibration questions

- Should the umbrella identity emphasize `B2B growth systems`, `marketing operating systems`, or another phrase?
- Is the primary audience senior leaders, hands-on strategists, or a deliberate combination?
- How personal should the public narrative become?
- Should German and English content share one positioning or use different entry points?
- Which topics should remain useful supporting lenses rather than visible editorial territories?
