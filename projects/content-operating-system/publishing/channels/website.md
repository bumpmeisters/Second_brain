# Website Channel Profile

**Channel job**: Provide a durable, owned expression of Rolf's thinking that can support deeper understanding, professional credibility, and later reuse.

## Calibration status

`not-directly-calibrated`: no verified public website article by Rolf was available in the 2026-07-27 review. This profile inherits the shared evidence for system-level reframing, useful metaphor, and operating implications, but it does not infer website-specific preferences from LinkedIn.

Carry across:

- thesis-first reasoning;
- system interpretation followed by operating consequence;
- vivid analogy when it is unpacked and bounded.

Do not carry across by default:

- platform-style hooks, hashtags, emoji, or engagement prompts;
- predictive intensity that a durable article cannot support;
- LinkedIn paragraph rhythm or length.

## Best-fit content

- complete framework or point-of-view essays;
- evidence-bounded case analyses;
- practical playbooks and diagnostics;
- portfolio or capability explanations;
- canonical public versions from which shorter derivatives can be created.

## Transformation rules

- Give the piece a durable question, title, and argument rather than a platform-dependent hook.
- State the answer or thesis early, then build the reasoning.
- Include context, definitions, applicability conditions, tradeoffs, and evidence boundaries.
- Make fit and non-fit conditions explicit where useful.
- Cite sources in a reader-usable form and separate external evidence from Rolf's analysis.
- Use headings, summaries, diagrams, tables, and examples only when they improve understanding.
- Add a version or updated date when the argument may evolve.
- Link to related public material rather than repeating it.
- Design the page to remain useful even when discovery channels or algorithms change.

## Recommended shape

```text
Reader question or problem
-> concise answer
-> why the problem exists
-> model, interpretation, or framework
-> evidence and limitations
-> operating implications
-> where it fits and where it does not
-> related material or next step
```

## Tone defaults

- authoritative but bounded;
- clear and generous;
- more complete than LinkedIn;
- personal where authorship or experience strengthens the argument.

## Channel quality gate

- Does the page answer a durable reader question?
- Is Rolf's original contribution explicit?
- Can a reader distinguish evidence, interpretation, and recommendation?
- Are important limitations and applicability conditions visible?
- Is the page useful without surrounding campaign context?
- Can future channel derivatives trace back to this version?

## Publication boundary

A website-ready Markdown file is still a private artifact. Deployment, upload, or external sharing requires direct instruction and must be recorded separately from approval.
