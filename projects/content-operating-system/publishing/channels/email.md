# Email Channel Profile

**Channel job**: Build continuity and trust with a known audience through contextual, useful communication that earns a reply, a decision, or a return visit.

This version covers personal editorial email and a possible future newsletter. Campaign, nurture, and lifecycle email require a separate profile when that use case becomes active.

## Calibration status

`not-directly-calibrated`: no verified published editorial email or newsletter by Rolf was available in the 2026-07-27 review. The profile inherits the shared system-thinking and operational usefulness rules, but relationship tone, warmth, subject-line style, cadence, and preferred length remain hypotheses.

Carry across:

- one central system insight;
- a clear practical consequence;
- a metaphor only when it helps this known audience understand the issue.

Do not infer from LinkedIn:

- a high-drama opening;
- hashtag, emoji, or platform formatting;
- a broadcast voice where recipient context should lead.

## Best-fit content

- a concise editorial note around one useful idea;
- a personal interpretation of a new article or framework;
- a curated set of related insights with an explicit editorial reason;
- a practical follow-up to an ongoing conversation;
- a periodic learning note with a clear audience promise.

## Transformation rules

- Write for the relationship and context the recipient has with Rolf.
- Make the subject line accurate and specific.
- Open with why this matters now to this audience.
- Deliver useful substance inside the email; do not make every message a link teaser.
- Use a more personal connective voice than the website while preserving evidence boundaries.
- Keep one primary communication job and one primary next action.
- Prefer a reply-worthy question or relevant next resource over a generic promotional call to action.
- Do not import a LinkedIn post unchanged.

## Recommended shape

```text
Specific subject
-> personal or situational context
-> one central insight
-> evidence, example, or practical implication
-> one relevant next step or reply question
```

## Tone defaults

- personal but purposeful;
- concise and generous;
- conversational without forced familiarity;
- clear about why the recipient is receiving the message.

## Channel quality gate

- Does the subject accurately set the expectation?
- Is the message useful even if the recipient does not click?
- Does it respect the relationship, audience context, and consent boundary?
- Is there only one primary action?
- Would a reply create a meaningful conversation?
- Does the text sound written to a person rather than distributed to a list?

## Publication boundary

Drafting an email never authorizes sending it. Audience selection, recipient data, sending platform, scheduling, and dispatch require a direct instruction and any applicable consent or privacy review.
