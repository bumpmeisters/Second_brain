# Content Operating System Artifact Inventory

**Summary**: Decision-level inventory of the vault's content artifacts, mapped to the provisional Content Operating System with overlaps, variants, gaps, and a canonical register.

---

## Inventory method and boundary

The inventory separates:

1. **Decision artifacts** — curated wiki concepts, workflows, checklists, playbooks, contracts, and experiments that can affect a Content OS decision.
2. **Reference artifacts** — source summaries and AI-research clusters that route to evidence but are not executable methods.
3. **Source-near candidates and variants** — binary-source sidecars and working-file extractions that preserve content but may duplicate, contradict, or represent generated variants.

The primary mapping contains two existing project frameworks, 32 pre-existing curated wiki decision or reference artifacts, and the new draft meta-framework. A path-based source-near scan also found 385 potentially content-relevant extraction files. That count is an inventory signal, not 385 distinct frameworks: it includes duplicates, presentation/PDF pairs, scripts, applied examples, AI-generated variants, and adjacent content material.

| Overlap | Risk | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| Content marketing strategy, niche fame, and strategic thought leadership | Three loops appear to compete as “the content strategy.” | Use [[content-marketing-strategy]] as the general portfolio loop; route to niche fame or thought leadership only when their narrower triggers apply. |
| Brand system, messaging frameworks, buying-situation calibration, and AI-assisted calibration | Strategy authority, message choice, and production critique are mixed. | Brand and messaging remain upstream authority; buying-situation calibration selects emphasis; AI calibration critiques bounded expression. |
| Strategic pillars, hubs, topic clusters, content models, and journeys | Different architecture concepts are treated as synonyms. | Pillar = enduring territory; hub = organizing asset or destination; cluster = related question/topic set; content model = repeatable structural schema; journey = use context over time. Build one future routing method across them. |
| Briefing system, content briefs, and creative briefs | A general handoff system is confused with format-specific templates. | Keep [[briefing-system]] as the cross-system authority; reconstruct one canonical content-brief schema as a specialized handoff. |
| Storytelling, hooks, writing guidelines, and content quality | All are treated as “writing frameworks.” | Storytelling structures the narrative; hooks structure the opening; writing guidelines govern expression; content quality evaluates the resulting asset. |
| Maintained distribution, niche sponsorship, and AI-mediated discovery | Channel tactics are mixed with asset lifecycle. | Maintained distribution owns the asset lifecycle; sponsorship is a bounded channel experiment; AI readiness is a discovery-surface check. |
| Campaign reporting and AI-search measurement | Broad operations and one measurement surface overlap. | Campaign reporting remains the decision-service authority; AI-search measurement remains a candidate specialist module until further verification and testing. |
| Source summaries and executable practices | Inventory pages can be mistaken for methods. | `source-summary` and `ai-research-summary` pages remain reference-only unless a separate reasoning artifact is created and evaluated. |

## Material gaps

1. **Content architecture routing** — no canonical method connects territory, pillar, buyer question, topic cluster, content model, journey role, format, and asset.
2. **Portfolio prioritization** — opportunity signals and prioritization criteria exist in deferred backlog item `PS-E01`, but no active backlog, calendar, or portfolio object is authorized.
3. **Narrative selection** — storytelling sources exist, but there is no evidence-aware router for choosing among narrative mechanisms inside Direction or Execution.
4. **Hook method** — the source image has not been visually analyzed, so detailed hook categories remain unavailable.
5. **Editorial writing system** — durable editorial rules, shared voice, channel rules, copy principles, and model-specific prompt tactics are not fully separated or calibrated.
6. **Measurement decision tree** — a minimal Performance Record exists, but no content-specific model connects asset purpose to quality, consumption, audience response, contribution evidence, and lifecycle action across a useful sample.
7. **Framework provenance packets** — large source families have not been reconstructed against primary or official sources.
8. **System evaluation** — neither the two canonical project frameworks nor the meta-router have completed the first two real end-to-end runs.

## Recommendation

Use the [Content Operating System Meta-Framework](../meta/content-operating-system-meta-framework.md) as a `draft` router. Retain Strategic Creative Direction and Content Execution as the only canonical project frameworks, also still `draft`. The next candidate framework-engineering project should be **Content Architecture**, because it resolves the largest overlap and determines how strategic pillars connect to buyer questions, topic clusters, content models, journeys, formats, and assets. Do not promote the meta-router or either canonical framework to `active` before the required real end-to-end runs are reviewed.
