# Strategic Creative Direction

## Framework Job

Turn approved business, audience, positioning, proof, claim, and campaign-role context into a channel-neutral decision about what should be said.

## Classification And Fidelity

Composite Content Operating System framework derived from the former Human-Led Creative Marketing Loop, the existing Publishing System Master Brief, `wiki/content-marketing-strategy.md`, and the user-approved Content Operating System architecture.

Project adaptation: creative exploration is separated from execution. This framework owns thesis, angle, narrative logic, evidence boundary, and optional Personal Take, but it does not write channel assets.

## Use When

- Approved context exists and a reusable content core, argument, or creative direction is needed.
- One strategic idea should support several channels, formats, or variants.
- Content risks becoming generic because critical decisions are otherwise repeated during drafting.

## Do Not Use When

- Audience, positioning, claims, proof, or campaign role are materially missing.
- The request is only to execute an already approved Creative Direction.
- A high-risk claim has not passed its upstream governance gate.

## Minimum Required Inputs

- Source project and exact upstream context references.
- Business and communication objective.
- Primary audience, situation, tension, and desired response.
- Positioning, approved claims, blocked claims, and proof.
- Campaign role or other strategic communication job.
- Brand, rights, confidentiality, timing, and production constraints.
- Human seed, judgment, observation, or explicit decision to proceed without a Personal Take.

## Core Model

1. Check intake readiness and route missing decisions upstream.
2. Distill one channel-neutral thesis and desired audience change.
3. Separate source-reported fact, verified fact, analysis, hypothesis, opinion, and excluded claims.
4. Explore genuinely different angles, narrative routes, and counterarguments.
5. Select one direction against evidence, audience truth, distinctiveness, usefulness, feasibility, and rights.
6. Run the optional Personal Take Checkpoint.
7. Record invariants, fit conditions, non-fit conditions, risks, and the decisions execution may not reopen.

## Question Engine

### Minimum Viable Questions

- What exact audience decision, belief, question, or behavior should change?
- What is the one-sentence thesis?
- Which evidence and proof can support it?
- Which claims or implications are blocked?
- What is Rolf's contribution beyond source summary?
- Which narrative tension makes the thesis useful?
- What must remain true across every execution variant?
- What would a skeptical but fair reader challenge?

### Deepening Questions

- Which category convention or false belief should the direction contrast with?
- Which counterargument improves the direction rather than merely weakening it?
- Which part is evidence, analysis, judgment, hypothesis, or experience?
- Can the direction survive without a channel-specific hook?
- What makes this direction recognizable and difficult to copy generically?

### Counter-Questions

- Is this a writing idea disguised as a strategic direction?
- Is unsupported psychology being attributed to the audience?
- Is personal experience being inferred rather than confirmed?
- Would a different upstream decision materially change the thesis?
- Is distinctiveness being confused with theatrical contrarianism?

### Optional Modules

- Personal Take Checkpoint.
- Category-cliche escape.
- Counterargument and pre-mortem.
- Conceptual collision for alternative angles.
- Narrative route comparison.
- Rights and confidentiality review.

## Branching And Stop Rules

- Route missing audience context to the upstream audience owner.
- Route missing or unsafe claims to claim governance.
- Route missing positioning or campaign role to Marketing ContextOps.
- Hold the direction when rights, confidentiality, or experience cannot be confirmed.
- Open a substantive dialogue with Rolf when a take conflicts with evidence or an earlier position, overstates certainty or experience, or relies on unrelated or sensitive Vault material; do not silently edit away the conflict.
- Stop when one direction is explicit enough that execution does not need to remake strategic decisions.

## Evidence Standard

Every material claim is sourced or labeled. Frameworks are methods, not company evidence. Human judgment and Personal Takes remain distinct from facts. Approved wording does not erase uncertainty or applicability limits.

## Business-Model Adaptations

- B2B: preserve buying roles, account context, long decision cycles, and proof needs.
- B2C/D2C: preserve buying situation, product economics, trust, lifecycle, and behavior evidence.
- B2B2C: separate partner and end-audience directions when their jobs conflict.
- Regulated or sensitive work: require claim, rights, and specialist review before approval.

## Output Contract

Produce one versioned Creative Direction with `content_id`, `direction_id`, source project, upstream references, audience, communication job, thesis, desired response, narrative logic, proof, evidence boundary, counterargument, Personal Take decision, fit/non-fit conditions, invariants, exclusions, open questions, status, and approval record.

## Failure Modes

Generic summaries, channel-first hooks, several competing theses, duplicated upstream context, unsupported audience psychology, invented personal experience, proof omitted from the narrative, and directions so broad that execution must make the strategy again.

## ContextOps Handoff

Consumes a referenced Content Context Packet. Produces the exact strategic decision that one or more variant-bundle briefs must preserve. Execution may change expression but not audience, thesis, proof boundary, approved Personal Take meaning, or desired response.

## Evaluation Notes

Initial validation will use the HP DemandGen Box Model, Enterprise Growth System LinkedIn Test Pack, and ServiceNow Authority Pilot migrations. These are structural migration cases, not evidence that the framework improves new content.

## Evolution Triggers

Review after two real end-to-end runs. Revise when briefs repeatedly need to reopen strategy, directions remain too generic, users skip meaningful human judgment, or the object creates more duplication than decision reuse.
