# Content Brief: Was Claude mit Tschaikowski macht

## Execution purpose

Create a light German LinkedIn article that lets a non-technical reader understand why the Tchaikovsky-to-Beethoven intervention is more revealing than finding an unspoken word. The reader should be able to retell both the experiment and its bounded meaning.

## Controlling direction

- **Creative Direction**: projects/ai/authority/directions/llm-thinking-emergence-direction-v5.md.
- **Approved version**: direction-ai-llm-thinking-emergence-01-v5, version 5.0.0.
- **Core takeaway**: The model forms an internal representation that can be changed and then used by later processing.
- **Retellable sentence**: Forschende tauschen in Claude Tschaikowski gegen Beethoven aus. Das Modell arbeitet mit dem neuen Zwischenkonzept weiter, und aus russisch wird deutsch.

## Execution contract

- **Voice**: curious, direct, conversational, and precise.
- **Point of view**: express Rolf's judgment through selection, weighting, argument, and conclusion. Do not add first-person markers merely to signal personality.
- **Reader relationship**: share one discovery and the moment its significance becomes clear; do not teach a paper.
- **Opening**: lead with the answer flip and the intervention.
- **Body**:
  1. Reconstruct the multi-hop task in ordinary language.
  2. Explain why changing the intermediate representation provides causal evidence.
  3. Use the France-to-China experiment briefly to show flexible reuse across operations, while noting mixed success.
  4. Bound the “concept,” “variable,” and “workspace” analogies.
  5. Complete the next-token mental model.
  6. End with this experiment as a concrete example of an emergent learned solution.
- **Length**: use only the space the argument earns; target roughly 600 to 800 German words excluding sources.
- **Structure**: short paragraphs and no more than three body headings. Lists are allowed only if they materially improve understanding.
- **Technical detail excluded unless essential**: Jacobian mathematics, activation variance, layer ranges, ablation battery, interpretability history, consciousness theory, prompting advice, marketing operations, and governance prescriptions.
- **Accuracy boundary**: Do not claim a readable human thought, human understanding, universal model behavior, or a refutation of next-token prediction.
- **Editorial boundary**: Do not retain Sentiment Neuron or Golden Gate Claude merely because earlier versions required them.
