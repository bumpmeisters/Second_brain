# Content Brief: Eine weitererzählbare Geschichte über emergente Eigenschaften

## Execution purpose

Turn Direction v4 into a light German LinkedIn article that gives the reader enough understanding to retell the example confidently. The article should feel like Rolf sharing one surprising discovery, not teaching a class.

## Controlling direction

- **Creative Direction**: projects/ai/authority/directions/llm-thinking-emergence-direction-v4.md.
- **Approved version**: direction-ai-llm-thinking-emergence-01-v4, version 4.0.0.
- **Core takeaway**: Beim Training von KI-Modellen entstehen Fähigkeiten und interne Strukturen, die niemand einzeln programmiert hat.
- **Retellable sentence**: Wir programmieren bei KI nicht jede Fähigkeit und interne Struktur. Wir schaffen die Bedingungen, unter denen sie beim Training entstehen.

## Execution contract

- **Voice**: loose, curious, personal, and precise enough to trust.
- **Reader relationship**: share a discovery with the reader; do not lecture or over-explain.
- **Opening**: tell the Tchaikovsky-to-Beethoven swap immediately.
- **Body**:
  1. Explain the experiment in everyday language.
  2. Give J-Space one short working description.
  3. Use Sentiment Neuron and Golden Gate Claude as two compact supporting examples.
  4. Define emergent properties.
  5. End with the retellable sentence.
- **Length**: approximately 500 to 750 German words excluding sources.
- **Scannability**: no more than three body headings; varied paragraphs; no list in the publication copy.
- **Technical detail excluded**: Jacobian lens, template-lens nuance, activation variance, ablation battery, layer ranges, mechanistic-interpretability history, consciousness research, prompting, and detailed epistemology.
- **Tone exclusions**: academic summary voice, artificial enthusiasm, constant direct address, generic AI phrases, em dash, en dash, semicolon, bold body markers, polished not-X-but-Y formulas, and generic CTA.
- **Accuracy boundary**: Say that the swap supports a role for the representation in this experiment. Do not imply arbitrary mind reading, universal model behavior, magic, or consciousness.
