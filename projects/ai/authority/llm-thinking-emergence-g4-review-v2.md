- **Verdict**: borderline pending human voice review.

### Strengths

- The opening earns curiosity with a concrete two-way intervention rather than a generic AI claim.
- “Eine Aktivierung ist noch keine Erklärung” names the article's actual knowledge delta.
- The three examples are framed as methodological reach, not a consciousness ladder.
- The close returns to exactly what can and cannot be inferred.
- Prompting relevance is fully absent from the article.

### Remaining risks

2. “Wachsende Reichweite” could be misread as a model-capability progression if the surrounding rejection of a ladder is removed in a derivative.
3. The exact rhythm, warmth, and title remain human taste decisions.
4. The article is intentionally conceptual; readers seeking an immediate operating tactic may not be the right audience.
