# Framework Fit: J-Space LinkedIn Article

## ContextOps purpose

- **In scope**: choose the storytelling structure and hook logic for a current, technically nuanced LinkedIn article about Anthropic's J-Space research.
- **Out of scope**: changing the evidence, adding a marketing application, certifying voice ownership, or authorizing publication.
- **Consumes**: the J-Space context packet, current article draft, LinkedIn channel profile, Anthropic's 6 July 2026 primary publication, and the local/parent storytelling framework inventory.
- **Produces**: selected narrative stack, deferred frameworks, article remapping, and hook contract.
- **Evidence status**: Anthropic date and research claims are primary-source reported. Framework fit is editorial judgment. Parent storytelling extractions are largely AI-generated syntheses and are treated as framework hypotheses, not performance evidence.

## Fit criteria

1. The hook must answer “Why now?” and “Why continue?” within the first screen.
2. The framework must carry a discovery, not manufacture a customer problem or sales resolution.
3. It must explain technical evidence without losing narrative momentum.
4. It must preserve uncertainty and the consciousness boundary.
5. It must suit a 900–1,300-word LinkedIn article.

## Recommended stack

### Primary framework: Three-Act Discovery Narrative

**Act 1 — The current discovery**

- Date the research: Anthropic published it on 6 July 2026.
- Start in medias res with Claude being told not to think about the Golden Gate Bridge.
- Reveal the stakes: researchers saw internal words that were absent from the output.
- Open the article question: what exactly did they find?

**Act 2 — The investigation**

- Explain J-Lens and J-Space in plain language.
- Distinguish priming from thought suppression through the white-elephant/white-bear analogy.
- Move from correlation to causal evidence: swaps, ablation, and internal reasoning.
- Keep the bridge experiment as the main thread; use arithmetic and automatic-versus-flexible processing as proof, not new stories.

**Act 3 — Meaning and boundary**

- State what changes in our mental model: output, internal activation, and internal working representation are distinguishable.
- State what does not follow: subjective experience or consciousness.
- Close on the scientific opening created by the discovery, not a forced business CTA.

## Hook contract

Use `In Medias Res + News Peg + Open Question`:

1. **News peg**: 6 July 2026, one month ago.
2. **Scene**: Claude is told not to think about the bridge.
3. **Unexpected observation**: the forbidden concept appears internally anyway, together with possible failure-monitoring terms.
4. **Promise**: the article explains the new J-Space discovery and why it reveals more than the model's output.

Do not lead with the reader visualization exercise alone. It creates participation but withholds the reason the article matters now.

## Deferred frameworks

- Do not use PAS, AIDA, StoryBrand, or Sparkline unless the communication job changes materially.
- Do not add more nested stories; the white-bear analogy is the only supporting loop required.
- Do not promote the parent AI-generated framework syntheses into a recurring canonical method from this single application.
