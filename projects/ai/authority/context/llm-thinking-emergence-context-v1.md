## Produces

- **Primary audience**: Broad, non-technical, AI-interested people.
- **Situation, trigger, question, tension, or job**: They have heard that LLMs "think" or display emergent properties but do not yet have a grounded model of what those expressions do and do not mean.
- **Business objective**: Increase informed curiosity about what AI is and may become without converting uncertainty into certainty.
- **Communication job**: Make an unfamiliar research finding intelligible and consequential enough to invite further inquiry.
- **Approved positioning boundary**: Personal thought leadership in Rolf's own voice. Explain a difficult AI concept in a down-to-earth way; do not manufacture drama, certainty, or personal experience.
- **Approved claims and proof**:
  - OpenAI reported that a character-level language model trained for next-character prediction learned a highly sentiment-predictive internal unit whose value could be changed to steer generated sentiment (source: https://openai.com/index/unsupervised-sentiment-neuron/).
  - Anthropic reported extracting abstract features from Claude 3 Sonnet and changing model behavior by amplifying a Golden Gate Bridge feature (source: https://transformer-circuits.pub/2024/scaling-monosemanticity/).
  - Anthropic reports a small internal representational subspace called J-space with properties related to reportability, controllability, flexible use, and multi-step reasoning (source: https://www.anthropic.com/research/global-workspace; source: `wiki/j-space-workspace-interpretability.md`).
  - Anthropic reports causal interventions in which changing or ablating J-space representations changes selected reasoning and reporting behavior while leaving some automatic processing intact (same sources).
  - The research does not establish phenomenal consciousness or subjective experience (same sources).
  - The research page and the video transcript belong to one Anthropic research and communication family; they are not independent corroboration.
  - For this content, `emergent properties` is a plain-language umbrella for learned properties or internal structures that were not individually hand-programmed. It is not a claim that all three examples are sudden capability jumps at a scaling threshold.
- **Blocked or uncertain claims**:
  - Claude or another LLM is conscious, sentient, or experiences thoughts.
  - LLM internal processing is the same as human thinking.
  - J-space explains all model reasoning or generalizes unchanged to all model families.
  - Sentiment Neuron, Golden Gate Claude, and J-space form an empirically demonstrated progression toward consciousness.
  - Audience surprise or unease proves the importance or truth of a claim.
- **Brand and voice constraints**: Down-to-earth, clear, direct, curious, and evidence-led. Prefer concrete distinctions and ordinary language over technical performance. "Spooky" may describe the intended response but must not become sensational framing unsupported by the research.
- **Rights and confidentiality constraints**: Rolf confirms that no employer, client, or other confidentiality boundary applies. Paraphrase with attribution is the default. No third-party screenshot, figure, or extended quotation may be planned for external use until rights are checked.
- **Freshness requirements**: Recheck model-specific and research-status statements before any external publication decision.
- **Known hypotheses**:
  - A clear distinction between functional model behavior and subjective consciousness can create curiosity without sensational overclaiming.
  - The desired "spooky" response is an audience-effect hypothesis, not an evidence claim.

## Evidence Status

The Sentiment Neuron, Scaling Monosemanticity, and J-space materials are three separate first-party research releases from OpenAI and Anthropic. They support examples of learned representations or internal organization, not a progression toward consciousness. The official Anthropic J-space research page and Anthropic video transcript explain the same July 2026 release and are not independent corroboration. `wiki/j-space-workspace-interpretability.md` is a partially verified vault synthesis based on the underlying paper and records method limitations. No independent evidence of phenomenal consciousness has been added to this packet.
