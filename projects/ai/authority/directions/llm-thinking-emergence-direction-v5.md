# Strategic Creative Direction: Claude arbeitet mit einem internen Zwischenschritt weiter

## Decision job

Give a non-technical reader one accurate and memorable correction to the autocomplete mental model: next-token prediction describes the objective and output, but not the full internal computation used to produce it.

## Audience and desired change

- **Primary audience**: curious CMOs, Heads of Marketing, and ambitious marketing practitioners without mechanistic-interpretability knowledge.
- **Situation**: they know generative AI mainly through prompts and outputs and want to understand what is newly visible inside a model.
- **Desired change**: Move from imagining a direct jump from input to answer to recognizing that a trained model can form an internal representation, reuse it as an intermediate input, and produce a causally different answer when that representation is changed.
- **Communication job**: Make the Tchaikovsky-to-Beethoven intervention easy to understand and retell without turning it into a technical tutorial or an instrumental marketing lesson.

## Direction

- **One-sentence thesis**: Anthropic did not merely find a hidden word inside Claude; it found and changed an internal representation that the model then used in further processing.
- **Strategic angle**: Let the causal intervention carry the article. Use the France-to-China experiment briefly to show why the representation behaves more like a reusable concept or variable than a one-off word association.
- **Rolf's contribution**: The meaningful discovery is not that a model processes unspoken information, but that researchers can now isolate, alter, and follow a functionally relevant intermediate step.
- **Narrative logic**:
  1. Show the answer flip immediately.
  2. Explain why the unspoken intermediate matters only because changing it redirects the result.
  3. Use one further experiment to establish reuse across operations.
  4. Bound the workspace and concept metaphors.
  5. Correct, rather than reject, the next-token description.
  6. End with a concrete, bounded meaning of emergence.
- **Retellable sentence**: Forschende tauschen in Claude Tschaikowski gegen Beethoven aus. Das Modell arbeitet mit dem neuen Zwischenkonzept weiter, und aus russisch wird deutsch.
- **Fit conditions**: Introductory personal-authority article for readers who want a credible mental model rather than technical completeness.
- **Non-fit conditions**: Prompting advice, marketing operations, governance guidance, consciousness claims, a history of interpretability, or a full paper summary.

## Evidence boundary

| Claim | State | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| A Tchaikovsky-to-Beethoven template swap at workspace layers flips Russian to German, and the reverse swap also works. | source-reported causal intervention | Main story; attribute to Anthropic. |
| Country representations can be reused across downstream operations such as capital, language, and continent, although many swaps fail. | source-reported, mixed success | One compact supporting example with an explicit non-universality boundary. |
| The representations function like concepts or variables. | bounded analogy and inference | Use “funktional” and state that this is not proof of human-like understanding. |
| Next-token prediction is false or obsolete. | excluded | It remains the objective/output description; it is incomplete as a description of internal computation. |
| The learned workspace structure is emergent in a broad sense. | supported synthesis | Define concretely as a useful internal solution that was not specified feature by feature. |
| J-Space establishes consciousness or readable thoughts. | excluded | State the boundary briefly. |

## Personal Take Checkpoint

- **Outcome**: approved interpretation.
- **Take**: Rolf is interested in the transition from observing model outputs to intervening in a meaningful internal step and seeing the remainder of the computation respond.
- **Evidence**: explicit user statements during the J-Space development that the important change is being able to observe internal structures, processes, and concepts rather than merely noting hidden words.
- **Boundary**: Do not extend this interpretation into a consciousness claim in this article.

## Direction invariants

- The causal swap is the protagonist.
- “Concept” is functional, not philosophical.
- The next-token mental model is completed, not caricatured.
- Historical supporting examples are removed because they dilute the main proof.
- Reader relevance comes from a better mental model of AI, not from a forced marketing application.
- The ending makes emergence concrete through this exact experiment.
