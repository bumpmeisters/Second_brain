# Strategic Creative Direction: Eine Entdeckung, die man weitererzählen kann

## Decision job

Give a non-technical reader just enough understanding to retell the Tchaikovsky-to-Beethoven intervention and recognize it as an example of an internal solution that emerged during training.

## Audience and desired change

- **Primary audience**: curious CMOs, Heads of Marketing, and ambitious marketing practitioners without mechanistic-interpretability knowledge.
- **Situation**: they want to understand one surprising AI finding without learning the paper or receiving a practical AI lesson.
- **Desired change**: Move from seeing the answer flip as a curious error to understanding that researchers altered a functionally relevant intermediate representation and the model continued processing with it.
- **Communication job**: Tell one memorable story, explain one causal relationship, name one meaning, and state one boundary.

## Direction

- **One-sentence thesis**: Beim Training können in KI-Modellen nützliche interne Lösungen entstehen, die niemand einzeln programmiert hat.
- **Strategic angle**: Begin with Claude answering German to a Tchaikovsky question, reveal the intervention, explain why the changed downstream answer matters, and stop at the bounded meaning of emergence.
- **Rolf's contribution**: The reader does not need the entire interpretability argument. The meaningful discovery is that a manipulated intermediate changes what the model subsequently does.
- **Narrative budget**:
  1. One event: Tchaikovsky is replaced with Beethoven.
  2. One explanation: the model uses the changed intermediate in later processing.
  3. One meaning: the useful internal solution arose through training rather than feature-by-feature programming.
  4. One boundary: this does not establish human understanding or consciousness.
- **Retellable sentence**: Forschende tauschen in Claude Tschaikowski gegen Beethoven aus. Das Modell arbeitet mit dem neuen Zwischeninhalt weiter, und aus russisch wird deutsch.
- **Fit conditions**: introductory, personal authority content optimized for comprehension and retelling.
- **Non-fit conditions**: paper summary, next-token explainer, prompting advice, marketing application, interpretability history, governance guidance, or consciousness argument.

## Evidence boundary

| Claim | State | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Swapping the Tchaikovsky template for Beethoven at workspace layers flips Russian to German, and the reverse swap also works. | source-reported causal intervention | Main and only proof story; attribute to Anthropic. |
| The changed representation is used by later processing. | source-reported and supported by the answer flip | Explain in ordinary language as a functionally relevant intermediate. |
| The internal solution emerged during training without being specified feature by feature. | bounded synthesis | Final meaning. |
| J-Space is readable thought, human understanding, or consciousness. | excluded | One short boundary sentence. |

Primary source: https://transformer-circuits.pub/2026/workspace/index.html.

## Personal Take Checkpoint

- **Outcome**: approved interpretation.
- **Take**: The important development is that researchers can intervene in a meaningful internal step and observe the rest of the computation respond.
- **Boundary**: Stop at emergence. Do not reopen prompting or consciousness.

## Direction invariants

- One event, one explanation, one meaning, one boundary.
- No France-to-China example, Sentiment Neuron, Golden Gate Claude, chess analogy, or next-token excursus.
- J-Space may be named once but does not receive its own explanation section.
