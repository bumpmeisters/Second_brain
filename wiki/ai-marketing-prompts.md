# AI Marketing Prompts

**Summary**: Research cluster for marketing prompt libraries, customer intelligence prompts, and AI marketing toolkits.

---

The AI marketing prompts folder contains six files, including spreadsheets and prompt-oriented documents for marketing workflows and customer intelligence (source: research/assets/07_AI_fuer_Marketing/AI_Marketing_Prompts; analysis: wiki/_outputs/ai-research-ingest-2026-06-03.json).

This cluster is a candidate source for reusable marketing prompt templates. Before adopting prompts, check whether they fit the vault's existing [[marketing-operating-system]], [[briefing-system]], and [[content-quality]] pages (source: research/assets/07_AI_fuer_Marketing/AI_Marketing_Prompts; claim requires verification).

## Useful Leads

- Group prompts by marketing job-to-be-done rather than by AI tool.
- For each useful prompt, preserve input data required, output format, validation step, and known limitations.
- Treat customer-intelligence prompts as hypothesis generators unless they are backed by real customer data.

## Open questions

- Which prompts should be converted into reusable templates?
- Which prompts rely on outdated tool behavior or weak customer assumptions?
