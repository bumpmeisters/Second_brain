# AI Marketing Priority Research Synthesis — July 2026

**Summary**: Curated content-level synthesis of 27 priority AI-marketing sources, plus one untranscribed audio source, covering brand consistency, research prompts, campaign strategy, creative evaluation, briefing, and writing style.

---

## What this bundle contributes

The selected sources converge on a practical marketing workflow: anchor work in audience and brand context, choose an explicit strategy or brief structure, generate alternatives, evaluate them against defined criteria, and document what was learned after execution. The material is strongest as a reusable workflow and template library. Claims about AI tools, market performance, or named frameworks remain unverified unless a separate page says otherwise (source bundle: research/assets/07_AI_fuer_Marketing).

## Durable synthesis

The bundle supports five reusable practices:

1. Encode brand and audience context before asking for copy.
2. Separate research, strategy, briefing, production, evaluation, and debriefing into explicit stages.
3. Use named frameworks as checklists, not as proof of effectiveness.
4. Describe writing style through observable attributes and examples.
5. Keep a human review gate for factual accuracy, brand judgment, and final accountability.

These practices are analysis across the selected sources. They do not validate the sources' vendor, model, or performance claims.
