# Account Based Marketing

**Summary**: Account-based marketing materials focus on named accounts, pipeline acceleration, buying committees, pain points, opportunity mapping, and sales/marketing alignment.

---

The HP ABM company campaign sample includes account overview, ARR potential, product areas, pain points, key individuals/personas, value proposition, sales stage, differentiators, objections, and opportunity overview (source: ABM Company Campagin Samples.pptx).

The pipeline acceleration worksheet focuses on moving target accounts between sales stages and includes leading indicators such as account engagement, coverage, meetings/demos, progression rate, and time to progress (source: Worksheet Pipeline Acceleration Campaign Planning.pdf).

The marketing strategy ABM file includes a "timeless account engagement model" and pipeline views for account engagement (source: HP_timeless Sales and Marketing framework_SS_Tminus_Apollo_summary.pdf).
