# Account Based Marketing

**Summary**: Account-based marketing materials focus on named accounts, pipeline acceleration, buying committees, pain points, opportunity mapping, and sales/marketing alignment.

---

The HP ABM company campaign sample includes account overview, ARR potential, product areas, pain points, key individuals/personas, value proposition, sales stage, differentiators, objections, and opportunity overview (source: ABM Company Campagin Samples.pptx).

The pipeline acceleration worksheet focuses on moving target accounts between sales stages and includes leading indicators such as account engagement, coverage, meetings/demos, progression rate, and time to progress (source: Worksheet Pipeline Acceleration Campaign Planning.pdf).

The marketing strategy ABM file includes a "timeless account engagement model" and pipeline views for account engagement (source: HP_timeless Sales and Marketing framework_SS_Tminus_Apollo_summary.pdf).

The July 2026 ABM clipping bundle adds a practitioner view that ABM is a fit when sales cycles are long, account values justify manual work, multiple buyers are involved, ICP/account qualification is clear, and sales, marketing, and executives agree on how progress will be measured (source: ABM strategy the step-by-step guide for B2B companies.md; analysis: [[abm-and-full-funnel-b2b-marketing-clippings-july-2026]]).

The newer ABM 3.0 sources argue for cluster-based targeting: accounts should be grouped by shared strategic challenge, use case, job-to-be-done, and evidence of need rather than only broad industry, territory, or revenue tiers (source: How to implement ABM strategy 3.0.md; source: 🕵🏻‍♂️ ABM strategy 3.0.md).

The same bundle describes ABM as a progression system rather than a one-shot campaign. Accounts can move through awareness, initial engagement, meaningful engagement, marketing-qualified account, sales-qualified account, opportunity, and re-engagement, and they can move backward when recent engagement disappears (source: EP 50 How to Run a Proper ABM Strategy with Mason Cosby @ Scrappy ABM.md).

The main caveat is evidentiary: the bundle contains useful practitioner patterns and case studies, but pipeline, revenue, reply-rate, and outcome figures are source claims that were not independently verified during ingest (source: [[abm-and-full-funnel-b2b-marketing-clippings-july-2026]]).

## Contested scope and academic boundary

One qualitative Theories-in-Use study based on interviews with ABM leaders in 22 firms defines ABM more narrowly than the vault's practitioner synthesis: an outbound digital marketing approach that identifies high-value account opportunities, develops deep account intelligence, and delivers personalized engagement in close sales collaboration. Its visible framework follows prospecting, sales negotiation, and relationship expansion, with prioritization changing according to opportunity complexity and value (source: Unpacking account-based marketing Conceptualization, key activities, and performance outcomes.md; sections “Introduction,” “Research approach,” “ABM process and its key activities,” and “Discussion”).

This does not invalidate the vault's broader operating-system model, which includes brand, reputation, customer adoption, retention, expansion, partners, and coordinated services. It establishes a definitional tension: the academic study operationalizes a bounded outbound-digital construct, while practitioner sources use ABM and ABX for a wider cross-functional lifecycle motion. State which definition a decision or evaluation uses rather than treating the label as self-explanatory.

The clipping does not include the full findings, coding evidence, figure, or limitations. The 22-firm qualitative design supports conceptual development, not prevalence or causal performance. Vendor budget, ROI, pipeline, conversion, engagement, and retention statistics quoted in its introduction remain excluded.

The post-July-7 operating-model bundle extends ABM from a targeting method into a cross-functional service model. Marketing, sales, executives, subject-matter experts, partners, and customer teams jointly select accounts, share insight, define the desired account change, and coordinate activation (source: “ABM Works 100% of the Time” - NTT Data’s Sloan Newman on Real Results (Episode 67).md; source: ABM vs RAM How Tanium’s Lindsay Baggett Kills Random Acts of Marketing (Episode 68).md; analysis: [[abm-operating-models-clippings-july-2026]]).

The [[ntt-data-embedded-account-management-playbook]] operationalizes NTT DATA’s “embedded reporter” model as a governed account role and converts “lift and shift” into an evidence- and confidentiality-controlled transfer from 1:1 learning to validated 1:few clusters. The dominant existing-footprint and share-of-wallet use case maps to Customer Expansion; strategic new-logo and deal-acceleration cases branch to 1:1 Strategic ABM (source: “ABM Works 100% of the Time” - NTT Data’s Sloan Newman on Real Results (Episode 67).md; analysis: [[ntt-data-embedded-account-management-playbook]]).

The [[enterprise-abm-scale-system-ten-operating-plays]] reconstructs a vendor list of ten scaling tactics as a governed Account-based Demand operating chain: dynamic portfolio identity, contextual signal interpretation, provisional buying-group mapping, maintained contact data, privacy-gated identity resolution, meaningful micro-experiences, selective channel activation, commercial alignment, bounded AI assistance, and decision-specific BI. It rejects the assumptions that a contact is automatically qualified, a click proves intent, more channels are always better, or correlated media and pipeline establish causality (source: 10 Hacks Great Teams Use to Scale ABM.md; analysis: [[enterprise-abm-scale-system-ten-operating-plays]]).

Account selection should include both strategic attractiveness and operating readiness. Fit and revenue potential are combined with relationship access, account-team commitment, engagement opportunity, executive relevance, and capacity to deliver the promised service level (source: From Pilot to Powerhouse How Pure Storage Rebuilt ABM to Scale (Episode 72).md; source: Channel-First ABM How Universal Robots Wins Expansion Deals (Episode 71).md).

The tier is best treated as a service contract: each tier specifies insight depth, personalization, channels, account-team participation, review rhythm, and measurement. Sources disagree on whether one-to-many qualifies as ABM, so the durable requirement is to define the service rather than rely on the label (source: From ABM to PBM How ServiceNow Runs Deal-Based Marketing at Sprint Speed (Episode 66).md; source: How SAP Built a Global ABM Playbook 138 Accounts, One Strategy (Episode 65).md).

The [[servicenow-pursuit-marketing-sprint-playbook]] operationalizes Pursuit Marketing as a time-bounded initiative inside a durable motion: primarily 1:1 Strategic ABM, or Customer Expansion when health, renewal, retention, or white space is the state to manage. It preserves strategic diagnosis, sales co-ownership, buying-group depth, explicit response rules, and handoff while preventing urgent requests from becoming an ungoverned content service (source: From ABM to PBM How ServiceNow Runs Deal-Based Marketing at Sprint Speed (Episode 66).md; analysis: [[servicenow-pursuit-marketing-sprint-playbook]]).

Prioritization has two time horizons. A strategic tier changes slowly according to fit and economics; current priority changes with stakeholder activity, relationship changes, topic progression, recency, and opportunity context (source: SaaS ABM at Scale How Buyer Behavior Tells You Where to Focus.md; analysis: [[buying-groups-and-account-prioritization]]).

The newer cases also broaden ABM across the customer lifecycle: reputation, relationship development, opportunity support, adoption, retention, and expansion can all be part of the same account system (source: Inside Kyndryl’s ABM Center of Excellence How a 90K-Person “Startup” Runs ABM (Episode 61).md; source: How SAP Built a Global ABM Playbook 138 Accounts, One Strategy (Episode 65).md).

For customer expansion, [[abm-customer-expansion-playbook]] turns that lifecycle principle into a gated account plan. Begin only after credible value, adoption, health, relationship access, and delivery capacity are visible; define the whitespace and adjacent buying-center outcome; instrument evidence and counter-evidence; discover role-specific needs; engage and prove incrementally; gate the proposal; then govern adoption, commercial progress, retention risk, and stop conditions through shared ownership. Usage and engagement remain observations rather than proof of purchase intent (source: What role does account-based marketing (ABM) play in expansion.md; sections “How ABM Powers Expansion,” “The ABM→Expansion Playbook,” and “Frequently Asked Questions about ABM for Expansion”; practitioner-promotional article; analysis: P27-C01).

The [[hp-enterprise-growth-system-source-summary|Enterprise Growth System]] turns these patterns into a single North Star while preserving important boundaries. It separates Market Demand, Account-based Demand, 1:few ABM, 1:1 Strategic ABM, and Customer Expansion; manages accounts as a dynamic portfolio; and routes initiatives through a gated Growth Execution Loop from commercial priority to measurement and learning (source: 20260718_Teil 1_HP_Enterprise_Growth_System_Strategischer_Blueprint_.docx; source: 20260718_Tei 2_HP_Enterprise_Growth_System_Modell_Legende_2.docx; source: 20260718_Teil 3_HP_Enterprise_Growth_System_Implementation_Playbook.docx).

The canonical inference framework is the [Enterprise Growth System](../projects/abm-operating-system/frameworks/enterprise-growth-system.md).

The canonical ABM Play Library v0.2.0 separates three operating levels plus a supporting catalog layer: Situation Plays name the material state to change, Play Formats configure delivery, System Plays provide reusable operating capability, and supporting entries name useful patterns, milestones, components, or methods beneath them. A thirty-search Firecrawl review found that external practice often uses “play” more loosely for signals, filters, campaigns, tactics, and workflows. Gate 2 therefore moved `Find the Spark`, `Turn Interest Into a Meeting`, `Tiger Team`, and `Lift and Shift` below the top-level Play layer while sharpening the boundaries for market entry, competitive displacement, buyer resistance, and Sales–Marketing collaboration (sources: [[abm-play-library-external-evidence-wave-1]]; analysis: `wiki/_outputs/abm-play-library-context/play-context-and-evidence-ledger-v0.2.csv`).

## Sales co-ownership and lean pilot cadence

Account-based sales is a shared operating role, not the recipient of a static marketing handoff. Sellers should help qualify accounts, research the buying group, maintain stakeholder and relationship context, progressively improve the account record, nurture relationships, and activate the next relevant conversation. The account record should retain what sales learns so marketing and other account participants can adapt the shared plan (source: Full-Funnel's Fundamentals.md; canonical title: *Sales in ABM: The good, the bad and the ugly*; practitioner transcript).

A lean pilot can attach this shared role to one existing strategic event or content priority. In a weekly review, marketing and sales inspect account movement and engagement, choose the next meaningful touch, assign ownership, and protect the capacity required to execute it. Existing relevant content should be reused before new assets are commissioned. The source's reported account movement, demonstrations, and opportunities are excluded because the case does not establish attribution (source: ABM Program to Engage and Land Enterprise Sales Opportunities Live Case Study.md; canonical title: *ABM to professional associations [Live Case Study]*; mixed practitioner transcript).

## Data and behavior gates

Before scoring or activation, verify that target accounts, contacts, roles, and fit criteria are accurate enough for the decision being made. Then add engagement evidence and audit downstream errors rather than allowing a composite score to hide weak source data (source: How to scale high-quality B2B campaigns with AI  OnBase podcast.md; canonical title: *Data-driven precision targeting: Workday's winning ABM formula*; mixed practitioner transcript; analysis: [[revenue-operations-ai-readiness]]).

Treat the website as an account-listening surface. Look for patterns across sessions, topics, assets, and inferred stakeholders; combine them with CRM context and third-party intent; and calibrate actionable triggers jointly with sales. These observations update a working account state but do not by themselves prove purchase intent (source: Your website is missing buying signals  Behavioral ABM with Radoj Glisik  OnBase podcast.md; mixed practitioner transcript; analysis: [[gtm-signals-and-contextual-intelligence]]).

## Four-block ICP architecture

Use four account-level evidence blocks as an ICP completeness check:

- **Company firmographics**: industry, geography, size, revenue, budget, and other attributes that affect economic or operational fit.
- **Buying-group or employee demographics**: whether relevant functions, authority roles, and decision responsibilities exist at the account.
- **Account technographics**: prerequisite, complementary, redundant, or maturity-signaling technologies.
- **Company psychographics**: maturity, commitment, values, aspirations, growth phase, internal constraints, and readiness to change.

Keep the ICP distinct from buyer personas. The ICP qualifies the account; personas explain the people participating in a decision. Selected employee attributes may improve account-readiness assessment, but they do not replace person-level research into needs, power, concerns, and behavior (source: 20260722_Defining_An_ICP_For_Manufacturers_HeinzMarketing.pdf).

Treat this four-block structure as a practical completeness heuristic rather than a universal scoring formula. The relevance and weight of each attribute depend on the offer, market, data quality, and service model. Validation and change governance belong in the [[b2b-persona-development#ICP evidence triangulation|ICP evidence triangulation]] loop.

## Use-case selection and account-specific evidence

When several shared-use-case clusters are plausible, choose the pilot cluster through explicit comparison rather than revenue alone. Classify historical customers and deals by use case, compare win rate, cycle length, contract value, available customer proof, strategic objective, and delivery capacity, then document one bounded selection and the alternatives deferred (source: B2B Demand Generation Playbook Webinar.md; transcript anchors 1:35:38-1:39:07; analysis: [[use-case-cluster-prioritization-workflow]]).

After an account is selected, commission bespoke content only when a visible initiative and verified context materially change the problem, value, proof, objection, decision, or experience. Reuse approved expertise and evidence first; isolate the account-specific insert; verify provenance and authorization; and deliver it through a relationship-appropriate route. This is deeper than inserting account attributes into a template and must not become mass pseudo-personalization (source: Account based content; source file: How to influence buyer journey with account based demand generation.md; transcript anchors 14:39-42:05; analysis: [[account-specific-evidence-brief-workflow]]).

## Awareness routing and progression baselines

For each priority solution, distinguish category maturity from brand familiarity before selecting the account-awareness job. An established category with an unfamiliar brand needs a different intervention from a new category associated with a familiar company; a new category and unfamiliar brand may require external credibility before direct product promotion (source: How to create awareness among strategic accounts 2.md; transcript anchors 36:03-38:59; analysis: [[category-brand-awareness-routing-matrix]]).

For long-cycle accounts, define observable states and map timestamped marketing and sales events with recency and uncertainty. Create a current baseline, inspect historical transitions and dwell time, and investigate stagnation. The state remains a working hypothesis: impressions do not prove awareness, event patterns do not prove intent, and transitions do not establish campaign attribution or a stable forecast (source: How ex-Google group marketing manager increased SQLs from 35% to 85% in one year.md; transcript anchors 23:16-28:40 and 39:07-46:39; analysis: [[account-progression-baseline-and-velocity-loop]]).

## Long-cycle playbook architecture

For a long, non-linear account journey, combine two operating layers. Maintain a planned cadence of useful, stage-appropriate touches that does not depend on an account producing a fresh signal, and add a signal-triggered layer that first reviews account history, relationship context, buying-group coverage, and current state before choosing the next action. A promotion, content interaction, funding event, website visit, or other observation is a prompt to investigate; it is not purchase intent or automatic permission to pitch (source: Untitled 7.md; canonical title: *How to Create an Account-Based Playbook for Long Sales Cycles and Multiple Decision-Makers*; practitioner webinar; analysis: P29-W4-C22).

Multithreading can extend beyond formal decision roles to power users, internal intermediaries, procurement, external advisers, associations, communities, and credible peers. Map why each role matters, who has an appropriate relationship, and what contact is authorized. Do not conceal commercial intent, request introductions without consent, imply endorsement, or treat the number of touches as attribution (same source; analysis: P29-W4-C23).
