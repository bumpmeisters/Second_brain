# Capture Process Surface

**Summary**: Capture fast, process occasionally, and surface automatically is the behavioral model behind the Obsidian vault setup.

**Sources**: raw/Clippings/The Obsidian Vault Setup That Replaced My $500 Per Month Software Stack.md.

**Last updated**: 2026-05-30

---

The source says capture should take less than 10 seconds because slow capture makes people avoid writing things down (source: The Obsidian Vault Setup That Replaced My $500 Per Month Software Stack.md).

The source says notes do not need to be organized immediately, but the system must tolerate later processing without collapsing (source: The Obsidian Vault Setup That Replaced My $500 Per Month Software Stack.md).

The source says useful ideas should surface later when they become relevant, which is part of why review and linking matter (source: The Obsidian Vault Setup That Replaced My $500 Per Month Software Stack.md).
