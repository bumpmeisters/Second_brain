## Retained contribution

The accessible practitioner article identifies risks that a simple output-per-person or headcount ratio can hide: loss of critical domain knowledge, weak absence and incident redundancy, insufficient on-call coverage, reduced slack, weaker mentoring and learning, higher workload, morale effects, legal and safety duties, and uncertain recovery when AI judgment fails.

The durable candidate is a pre-decision gate that asks whether representative accepted outcomes and the complete human-and-technical operating system remain viable under reduced capacity. It requires alternatives, safe employee input, accountable specialist review, preserved recovery capacity, and explicit rollback. It does not decide or authorize an employment action.
