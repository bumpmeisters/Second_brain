# GPT-5.6 limited preview

**Summary**: OpenAI confirms GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra, and Luna as a limited preview. The announcement is real, but practical access is restricted and several newsletter headlines overstated broad availability.

---

## Verified details

OpenAI describes Sol as the flagship model, Terra as a lower-cost option, and Luna as the fastest and least expensive tier. During the preview, approved organizations may receive API or Codex access; ChatGPT is excluded, there is no public waitlist, and no general-availability date has been announced (sources: [OpenAI launch](https://openai.com/index/previewing-gpt-5-6-sol/); [OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001325-a-preview-of-gpt-56-sol-terra-and-luna)).

The Help Center lists prices of $5/$30, $2.50/$15, and $1/$6 per million input/output tokens for Sol, Terra, and Luna respectively, plus explicit cache breakpoints and a 30-minute minimum cache life (source: [OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001325-a-preview-of-gpt-56-sol-terra-and-luna)).

## Assessment

- **Value**: Relevant product watch; low immediate action value without access and a defined workload.
- **Evidence status**: Official product details verified. Performance superlatives remain vendor claims unless independently benchmarked.
- **Main caveat**: “Out” or “dropped” in a headline can imply general availability, which is incorrect for this preview.

## Downstream use

Retain as a watch item. Run a controlled comparison only after access exists and a specific Second Brain workload makes the cost, caching, or capability differences material.
