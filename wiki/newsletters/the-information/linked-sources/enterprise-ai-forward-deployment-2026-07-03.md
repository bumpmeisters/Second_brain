# Enterprise AI forward deployment and consulting

**Summary**: Cursor and Microsoft signals suggest that enterprise AI adoption requires substantial workflow integration, customer enablement, and implementation capacity—creating a service layer around AI products.

---

## Source evidence

Cursor’s VP of Forward Deployed Engineering describes engineers working inside customer systems to customize agent deployments across workflows and the software lifecycle. She says adoption beyond individual enthusiasts requires leadership sponsorship, internal champions, and measurable returns (source: [Cursor FDE interview](https://www.latent.space/p/cursor-forward-deployed-engineers)).

The Information reports that Microsoft committed $2.5 billion and 6,000 employees to a new applied-AI customer implementation effort. This review did not locate a Microsoft primary announcement, so the figures remain attributed to The Information (source: [The Information briefing](https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/microsoft-commits-2-5-billion-new-applied-ai-consulting-effort)).

## Assessment

- **Value**: Strong product-strategy, ABM, and go-to-market relevance.
- **Evidence status**: Primary interview for Cursor’s stated strategy; reputable secondary report for Microsoft; broader market inference remains provisional.
- **Main caveat**: Vendor statements may emphasize implementation complexity to support their own service model.

## Downstream use

Add implementation burden and service intensity to AI-native product analysis. Test whether those dimensions improve account segmentation, positioning, and proof-of-value design.
