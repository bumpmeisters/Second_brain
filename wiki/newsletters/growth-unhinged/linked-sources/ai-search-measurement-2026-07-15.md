# Measuring AI-search impact beyond referral traffic

**Summary**: A practitioner framework measures AI-search influence with a stable prompt panel, weekly visibility and answer-quality checks, and monthly connection to buyer attribution, sales evidence, win rate, and influenced revenue.

## What the source contributes

The method separates leading indicators from business outcomes. It proposes tracking a stable set of high-intent prompts for several weeks, checking whether a company appears and how accurately it is represented, then connecting those observations to buyer and revenue evidence (source: [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/how-to-measure-the-impact-of-ai-search-the-right-way)).

This is more useful than treating AI referral traffic as the only measure: an AI answer may influence consideration without producing a directly attributable click. The framework therefore treats attribution as a panel of evidence rather than a single metric (source: Growth Unhinged; analysis).

## Assessment

- **Value**: A practical starting point for B2B AEO measurement.
- **Evidence status**: Practitioner framework; the measurement design is retained, not its unverified benchmarks.
- **Commercial caveat**: An AEO vendor supports the publication, so product and benchmark claims require independent checking.
