# Decision-first technology judgment

**Summary**: The accessible introduction proposes allocating research depth according to whether resolving an uncertainty could change a decision. Most of the promised method is paywalled, so this is retained as a practitioner hypothesis for a bounded trial rather than a validated research framework.

## What the source contributes

The visible sequence begins with decision questions: why the technology exists now, which constraint changed, who benefits, what may block adoption, and whether the answer changes an action. It then proposes forming an initial view, naming uncertainty, and going deeper where resolution could alter the decision (source: [Artificial Intelligence Made Simple](https://www.artificialintelligencemadesimple.com/p/how-to-build-judgement-about-a-new)).

The durable candidate is a prioritization gate for research effort. It does not justify skipping feasibility, safety, or primary-source checks.

## Assessment

- **Evidence status**: Unverified practitioner method; partial source coverage.
- **Commercial incentives**: The introduction promotes the author's paid community and authority.
- **Missing material**: The full procedure, worked examples, failure cases, and stopping criteria were not accessible.
- **Decision**: Test the gate on one research brief before considering wider adoption.
