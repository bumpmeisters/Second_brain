# AI business-value gate

**Summary**: A consulting essay usefully challenges stand-alone AI roadmaps and activity metrics, but its ROIC-minus-WACC framing is too narrow to serve as a universal value definition. The durable candidate is a broader decision gate that links an AI capability to an organizational objective, explicit value mechanism, baseline, full cost boundary, owner, and review horizon.

## Source contribution

The source argues that AI is a functional strategy that should serve corporate strategy rather than set company direction. It proposes asking whether an initiative widens or lengthens the spread between return on invested capital and cost of capital, while recognizing that some investments buy longer-term option value (source: [AI Strategy Is Not a Strategy](https://firmsconsulting.com/ai-strategy-vs-corporate-strategy/); consulting essay).

The transferable control is not the financial formula alone. It is the refusal to treat adoption, usage, output volume, or growth as sufficient without naming the objective, mechanism, baseline, investment boundary, time horizon, and accountable decision owner.

## Durable candidate

Before an AI capability enters training mode, record:

1. the company, functional, or project objective it serves;
2. the expected mechanism of value creation or protection;
3. the baseline and full investment boundary;
4. leading operational measures and lagging outcome measures;
5. the accountable owner and review decision; and
6. edge cases such as learning, option value, resilience, compliance, safety, accessibility, workforce effects, and other nonfinancial obligations.

## Evidence assessment

- **Evidence status**: Unverified practitioner/consulting heuristic; useful as a decision scaffold, not causal evidence.
- **Commercial incentive**: Direct consulting and authority-building incentive.
- **Main limitation**: No dataset, implementation comparison, attribution method, or treatment of public and workforce value sufficient for universal use.
- **Excluded claims**: No claim that ROIC minus WACC is the only valid objective, that adoption lacks all diagnostic value, or that the gate guarantees financial impact.
