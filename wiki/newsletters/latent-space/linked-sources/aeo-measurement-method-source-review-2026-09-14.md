# AEO Measurement Method Source Review

**Summary**: The publisher-operated tracker supports a configuration-complete AEO measurement record covering prompt variants, model and search configuration, extraction identity, scoring disclosure, failed coverage, sources, and self-preference. It does not validate rankings, business impact, or causal AEO effects.

## What the source is

Latent.Space describes a tracker that runs prompt variations across search-enabled models and categories, extracts recommendations and citations, and applies a proprietary score. Prompt-answer pairs are inspectable, but important sampling, scoring, extraction-validation, geography, and uncertainty details are not fully disclosed.

## Retained contribution

- Record prompt-variant identity, answer-model and search configuration, extraction-model identity, scoring rules, failed-run coverage, cited sources, and model self-preference.
- Interpret prompt stability only alongside answer quality and coverage.
- Keep visibility and tracker scores separate from buyer behavior, pipeline, revenue, and causal content effects.

## Evidence limits and contradictions

- The same publisher built the tracker, controls extraction and scoring, interprets the results, and invites commercial enquiries.
- Inspectable prompt-answer pairs coexist with a proprietary score that cannot be independently reconstructed from the page.
- Failed and excluded models leave the comparison panel incomplete.
- Low paraphrase sensitivity may indicate rigidity rather than confidence or recommendation quality.

## Explicitly excluded claims

No ranking, category winner, ROI, uplift, universal scoring threshold, markdown-negotiation effect, or causal AEO claim is retained.
