# Forward-Deployed Engineering Product-Learning Source Review

**Summary**: A vendor founder's firsthand account supports a narrow field-to-platform correction loop: capture customer semantics and exceptions, classify local fixes, and record what later deployments inherit. It does not establish universal superiority for an FDE organization.

## What the source is

The author distinguishes forward-deployed engineering from quota sales, consulting, sales engineering, and solutions architecture. Examples from his own work argue that embedded delivery becomes a product-learning function only when local corrections and operating knowledge flow upstream.

## Retained contribution

- Capture the customer's operational nouns, verbs, exceptions, corrections, and provenance.
- Classify each local fix as deliberately discarded, retained as bounded service work, or routed into the upstream product.
- Review recurrence, transferability, maintenance debt, delivery load, productization, and what subsequent deployments inherit.

## Evidence limits and contradictions

- This is a first-person vendor-founder essay, not an independent organizational comparison.
- The author's commercial position favors embedded technical delivery.
- A successful local fix can still create consulting and maintenance debt when it is neither discarded nor folded upstream.

## Explicitly excluded claims

No vendor, sector, deployment-success, speed, commercial-outcome, or universal FDE-superiority claim is retained.
