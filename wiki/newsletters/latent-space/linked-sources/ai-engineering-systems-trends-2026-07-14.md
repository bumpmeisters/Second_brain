# Five systems trends in AI engineering

**Summary**: A conference synthesis describes a shift from model-centric agent building toward harnesses, outer-loop control, enterprise implementation, coding-agent interfaces, and portable skills.

## What the source contributes

The durable synthesis is that agent quality depends on the surrounding system: workflow, context, permissions, persistent state, evaluation, observability, and human decision rights. It also frames enterprise deployment as integration and change work requiring internal champions, explicit automation boundaries, and measurable value (source: [Latent.Space](https://www.latent.space/p/aiewf26trends)).

The skills section adds an operating implication: skills behave like maintained procedures rather than timeless prompts. Smaller skills, periodic review, and adjustment after model or workflow changes reduce staleness and uncontrolled complexity (source: Latent.Space; analysis).

## Assessment

- **Value**: Strong synthesis for ContextOps, agent architecture, and skill maintenance.
- **Evidence status**: Conference and practitioner synthesis; useful direction, not controlled comparative evidence.
- **Main tension**: Increasing agent scale coexists with continued unreliability of fully autonomous loops.
