# Skill engineering and human steering

**Summary**: Impeccable creator Paul Bakaus describes skills as a way to compress expert vocabulary into operational guidance while preserving human steering and final judgment.

---

## What the source contributes

The Impeccable system gives design terms such as “bolder,” “quieter,” and “denser” more precise operational meanings. Bakaus argues that a useful skill supplies domain knowledge, context, and controllable steering rather than attempting one-shot generation (source: [Latent Space interview](https://www.latent.space/p/skill-engineering-design)).

The source also notes that skills may need internal routing and adaptation to different models and harnesses. Bakaus explicitly rejects a fully automatic design mode and places human judgment in the final, context-sensitive part of the work (source: [Latent Space interview](https://www.latent.space/p/skill-engineering-design)).

## Assessment

- **Value**: High relevance to local skill design and to converting Rolf’s domain expertise into reusable agent behavior.
- **Evidence status**: Primary evidence for the creator’s intent and system design; no comparative performance study was supplied.
- **Main caveat**: The “80/20” framing is a practitioner heuristic, not a measured general rule.
