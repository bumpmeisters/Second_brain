# Share Test and Onlyness Test for content review

**Summary**: Two compact review questions test whether content creates enough reader value to be shared and remains recognizably authored rather than generically AI-produced.

## What the source contributes

The **Share Test** asks why the ideal reader would forward the piece. The **Onlyness Test** asks whether the work would still be recognizably the author's without the byline. Used together, they shift AI from final writer to a coach that asks questions before publication (source: [Kieran Flanagan](https://open.substack.com/pub/kieranflanagan/p/i-built-an-ai-skill-to-sharpen-my)).

## Assessment

- **Value**: Memorable review gates for thought leadership and professional positioning.
- **Evidence status**: Practitioner method without comparative validation.
- **Main caveat**: The tests support judgment but do not prove content performance.

## Downstream use

Use the two questions as optional qualitative gates in [[content-quality]], alongside audience relevance, proof, brand voice, and performance evidence.
