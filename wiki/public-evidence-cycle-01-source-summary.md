## What The Sources Contribute

- PE1 supplies adversarial evidence about weak ABM readiness, unrealistic account coverage, missing resources, data, and cross-functional capacity.
- PE2 supplies buyer-side evidence about channel choice and nonlinear interaction, but its local custody gap means future re-checking depends on the live origin until a lawful original is supplied.
- PE3 challenges short-horizon demand capture with the 95-5 heuristic while explicitly warning against treating the ratio as universal.
- PE4a and PE4b provide different forms of partner-route evidence: a broader senior-marketer benchmark and a directional channel-practitioner report.
- PE5 tests 1:few terminology, resource constraints, AI outcome skepticism, and measurement problems across a current practitioner sample.

The sources support a framework challenge, not causal validation. Survey findings remain self-reported and bounded by the disclosed sampling methods. The Demand Gen Report surveys do not disclose enough sample detail to support population-level generalization.

## Caveats

- The archive is intentionally recorded as **five of six**, not complete.
- No synthetic or access-denied document was retained as a source.
- Local custody preserves what the publishers made publicly available; it does not improve the evidence quality or independence of the underlying claims.
- The McKinsey article should be admitted later only from a lawful original or user-provided export, without overwriting any existing source.
