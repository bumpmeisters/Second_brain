# Governed AI GTM Handoff Checklist

**Summary**: Prepare an AI-assisted GTM handoff by preserving shared account context while making ownership, provenance, permissions, review points, and proof explicit.

---

## When to use

Use before work moves between strategy, product marketing, content, media, account teams, sales, or customer success.

## Checklist

- **Problem**: Is the client or account problem stated in current language?
- **Context**: Are approved research, meeting notes, briefs, decisions, and findings attached?
- **Provenance**: Can each important claim be traced to a source?
- **Ownership**: Is one person accountable for the next decision and output?
- **Access**: Are private, team, and customer data boundaries explicit?
- **Process**: Are the next action, review point, and exception path named?
- **Platform**: Is the AI or tool trial tied to a testable workflow hypothesis?
- **People**: Do operators have the fluency and support needed to review the result?
- **Proof**: Is success measured through the job outcome rather than AI activity?

## Kampagnenübergabe vor Start mit Vertriebsreaktion verbinden

Vor Kampagnenstart den empfangenden Vertrieb über Zielgruppe, Akquisitionsweg, Inhalt, vermutete und tatsächlich erklärte Absicht sowie empfohlene Anschlussreaktion briefen. Demo-Anfrage, Content-Download, Eventteilnahme, No-show und zeitlich verschobener Bedarf brauchen passende, benannte Folgewege. Nach Übergabe Rückmeldungen zur Qualität und Antwortzeit an Kampagnen- und Content-Verantwortliche zurückgeben.

Ein Download ist kein Demo-Wunsch. Keine universelle Folgefrequenz, erfundene persönliche Relevanz oder Übernahme von Kanal-Erfolgsquoten. Kontaktregeln und menschliche Verantwortung bleiben bestehen.

## Guardrails

This is an operating checklist, not a validated maturity model. Participation, efficiency, and commercial outcome claims from the source remain anecdotal (source: Stop bolting on AI Rebuild your go-to-market from the foundation up  OnBase podcast.md; practitioner transcript; analysis: P11-C02).

## Handoff from promised to realized value

For a sales-to-delivery or customer-success handoff, add these fields to the existing packet:

- Buyer and actual user/usage center, including any difference between them.
- Expected value in use and the evidence that makes the promise feasible.
- Delivery owner, prerequisites, customer contribution, and a way to observe value in use.
- Observed realization, gaps from the promise, and who reviews the evidence.
- Corrections to delivery and future promises, with provenance and an accountable owner.

The conceptual synthesis distinguishes value-based selling around expected value, customer success around experienced value, and key-account management around relationship value across transactions. These are activity distinctions; they do not require three departments or establish revenue uplift. Keep account coordination and trust across the handoff while testing whether the promised value is actually realized.
