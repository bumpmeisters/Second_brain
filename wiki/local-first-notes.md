# Local First Notes

**Summary**: Local-first notes are plain files stored on the user's machine, making the vault portable and tool-independent.

**Sources**: raw/Clippings/The Obsidian Vault Setup That Replaced My $500 Per Month Software Stack.md.

**Last updated**: 2026-05-30

---

The Obsidian setup source argues that local Markdown files let users own notes, open them in text editors, back them up, and switch tools more easily (source: The Obsidian Vault Setup That Replaced My $500 Per Month Software Stack.md).

The source contrasts local Markdown files with productivity apps that depend on servers, proprietary formats, monthly payments, or changing product decisions (source: The Obsidian Vault Setup That Replaced My $500 Per Month Software Stack.md).

Local-first notes support [[obsidian-vault-design]] because Obsidian acts as a lens over files the user owns (source: The Obsidian Vault Setup That Replaced My $500 Per Month Software Stack.md).
