# Value-based selling, customer success, and key-account management

**Review scope:** PDF pp. 1, 7–10; conceptual framework and implications, not all 13 pages.

Wengler, Kleinaltenkamp, Heirati, and Prohl-Schwenke offer a conceptual literature synthesis. Value-based selling emphasizes expected value in use around purchase; customer success emphasizes experienced value in use after purchase; key-account management emphasizes relationship value across transactions. These are activity distinctions, not necessarily three separate departments or job titles. (source: Untangling-value-based-customer-management-approaches-in-b_2026_Industrial-Mark.pdf, pp. 1, 7–10; DOI 10.1016/j.indmarman.2026.01.009)

Buying and usage centers may differ. A credible promise needs delivery feasibility; realized-use feedback should inform future promises. VBS identifies opportunities and crafts/communicates value; CSM supports realization, monitoring, and enhancement; KAM coordinates account planning, trust, alignment, and relationship health. The paper does not experimentally establish revenue uplift from organizational redesign. (source: same PDF, pp. 7–10)
