# Brand System

**Summary**: The brand system combines business-minded brand strategy, identity rules, messaging frameworks, writing guidance, and PowerPoint design standards.

---

The brand library contains strategy, attributes, design guidelines, PowerPoint master samples, messaging frameworks, writing guidelines, and HP brand-analysis references (source: raw/assets/03_Brand).

The brand attributes PDF argues that a strong B2B brand has direct business impact, citing B2B brand effects on company value, market share, recommendations, repurchase, and premium prices (source: 032024_Golbal Marketing and Transformation Communication_Brand Attributes.pdf).

The DB Schenker design guide covers unique imagery, logo usage, creative flexibility, color, typography, the Pulse, icons, layout rules, and email/newsletter guidance (source: 210319_DB Schenker Design Guide_2.0_NEW.pdf).

The 2025 brand training deck covers basic design guide highlights, social media, company logo, typographic styles, the Pulse, icons, layout rules, brand checklist, and brandHUB (source: 250429_DBS basic brand_training_social-media.pptx).
