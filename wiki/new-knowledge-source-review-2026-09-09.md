## Contradictions and excluded claims

- Demand Bites interviews describe engagement-based scoring; B2B Effectiveness challenges its interpretation as buying readiness. Heinz offers a practical observed-trigger / stated-need / active-evaluation distinction. Preserve these differences; no universal scoring formula follows.
- Five episodes from the same two speakers are an overlapping argument stream, not five independent confirmations. Blanket anti-vendor and anti-model statements are not adopted.
- Sales acceptance, precision, false-positive rate and accuracy have different denominators. Acceptance is not ground truth; selection, missing follow-up and observation time matter.
- The blanket 95:5 framing in the Natasha Tibbetts interview conflicts with Episode 4's contextual qualifications. Neither establishes a universal budget ratio or a current market-wide in-market rate.
- Overall market share, one exceptional logo among won deals, or revenue growth cannot be substituted for an individual account's win probability. Relevant denominators and comparison populations are missing.
- Vendor AI visions and creator demonstrations supply hypotheses, not verified performance, cost, safety or product-superiority evidence.
- Hiring/holiday material supports bounded team methods. It does not authorize contacting candidates, changing HR processes, sending messages or deleting inbox items.
- Promotional statistics, affiliate/sponsor pitches, forecasts, private asides and image-only claims are excluded. No AI-generated research report was newly selected.
