The post lists nine [[web-scraping-tools]] for crawling, scraping, browser automation, AI extraction, reconnaissance, or no-code scraping (source: Post by @cyrilXBT on X.md).

The listed tools are Crawl4AI, Firecrawl, Scrapy, Crawlee, Playwright, ScrapeGraph AI, Browser Use, Katana, and Maxun (source: Post by @cyrilXBT on X.md).

The post frames Crawl4AI and Firecrawl as tools for LLM or RAG-oriented data extraction, which makes them relevant to [[ai-scraping-pipelines]] (source: Post by @cyrilXBT on X.md).

The comments add an important [[anti-blocking-limits]] caveat: rendering and automation tools do not by themselves solve IP reputation, proxy rotation, TLS fingerprinting, or Cloudflare-style checks (source: Post by @cyrilXBT on X.md).
