# Marketing Operating System

**Summary**: The ingested folders form a practical marketing operating system: strategy, audiences, brand, briefs, content, execution, reporting, and example libraries.

---

The library suggests a marketing operating system made of seven connected workstreams: strategy, briefing, brand, audience/personas, content, reporting/ops, and applied examples (source: raw/assets).

The expanded raw assets split this operating system into a reusable reference layer, [[frameworks-templates-reference-library]], and an active work layer, [[marketing-working-library]] (source: raw/assets/A_frameworks_templates; source: raw/assets/B_MKT_working_library).

[[marketing-strategy-library]] provides planning frameworks, campaign flow logic, OGSM, marketing orchestration, and country marketing planning (source: raw/assets/01_Marketing_Strategie).

[[persona-and-audience-library]] provides audience and journey material that can inform targeting and campaign insight (source: raw/assets/04_Persona_und_Audience).

[[brand-library]] provides brand identity, messaging, writing, and design rules that constrain how campaign outputs should look and sound (source: raw/assets/03_Brand).

[[briefing-template-library]] converts strategy, audience insight, and brand guidance into execution-ready briefs for content, creative, media, initiatives, events, and email (source: raw/assets/02_Briefing_Templates).

[[content-library]] provides quality, hooks, storytelling frameworks, and thought-leadership patterns that can shape campaign assets (source: raw/assets/05_Content).

[[reporting-and-ops-library]] closes the loop through campaign targets, delivery tracking, Eloqua quality checks, reporting templates, and project timelines (source: raw/assets/08_Reporting_und_Ops).

[[hp-examples-library]] supplies applied reference examples for how these ideas show up in real HP marketing artifacts (source: raw/assets/09_Beispiele_HP).

[[marketing-working-library]] adds active workstreams for strategy/planning, brand/company, content/social/PR, campaigns/missions/projects, marketing automation/analytics/ops, sales and buyer enablement, and presentations/meeting alignment (source: raw/assets/B_MKT_working_library).

AI-labeled or model-labeled working files in the reference and working libraries should be treated as secondary synthesis until verified against primary sources or current product/platform documentation (source: raw/assets/A_frameworks_templates; source: raw/assets/B_MKT_working_library; source: AGENTS.md).

[[logistics-market-intelligence-library]] adds 597 AI-generated deep-research reports and corresponding prompts for business units, vertical markets, personas, buyer journeys, products, trends, content pillars, and Connect spot booking. It is a source of hypotheses, research leads, and workflow inputs—not verified market evidence—until cited primary sources are checked (source: user clarification, 2026-06-20; source: research/assets/11_Markets).

The agentic-marketing sources add a compounding intelligence loop around the operating system: human judgment selects what matters; the owned knowledge layer stores customer, market, positioning, and performance evidence; AI executes against that context; and reviewed learning returns to the system (source: Stop outsourcing your marketing intelligence to AI. Do this instead.md; source: How to Rebuild Your Marketing Team for the Agentic Age. Full Breakdown.md).
