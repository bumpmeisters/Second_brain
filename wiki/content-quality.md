# Content Quality

**Summary**: Content quality combines audience relevance, user-centric value, clear structure, proof, brand voice, and performance-oriented review.

---

The content audit folder contains a content quality audit and presentation version including the prompt used for analysis (source: raw/assets/05_Content/Content_Audit).

The DB Schenker website copywriter briefing says copy should take a user-centric view where the emphasis is less on what the company does and more on what the user gets (source: DBS_Website_Content_CopywriterBriefing_2022.08.04 (1).pdf).

Content quality in this vault connects [[content-briefs]], [[storytelling-frameworks]], [[hook-frameworks]], [[brand-system]], and [[writing-guidelines]].
