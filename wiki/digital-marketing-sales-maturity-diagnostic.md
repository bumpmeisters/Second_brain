# Digital Marketing and Sales Maturity Diagnostic

**Summary:** Diagnose mismatches across marketing/sales capabilities, processes, instruments, and performance.

## Trigger

Use when a marketing/sales transformation is reduced to tool choice or stakeholders disagree about operating readiness. Start with a specific customer or business outcome.

## Required inputs

- Business goals, decision owner, and customer-journey evidence.
- Capability/skill evidence, process map, and tool/data inventory.
- Outcome measures, their definitions and limitations, and relevant stakeholder perspectives.

## Procedure

1. Bound the workflow and outcome. Identify the stakeholders whose views or evidence may disagree.
2. Examine **capabilities**: skills, ownership, and ability to use and improve the workflow. Separate demonstrated ability from training attendance.
3. Examine **processes**: the actual customer journey, handoffs, dependencies, and exceptions. Record differences between the stated process and observed work.
4. Examine **instruments**: tools and data used in those processes, integration/access constraints, and evidence of practical use.
5. Examine **performance**: available customer/business outcomes, definitions, measurement gaps, and alternative explanations. Do not substitute tool adoption for results.
6. Compare the four dimensions. Make contradictions and missing evidence explicit instead of averaging them into an invented score.
7. Select one improvement hypothesis with an owner, expected outcome, prerequisite, observable test, and review point. Record reasons to defer action where evidence or prerequisites are missing.

## Inspectable output

Create a gap map with dimension, observation, cited evidence/date, uncertainty, related dependency, and candidate response. Attach the selected improvement, owner, test, and review decision. Preserve stakeholder disagreement where unresolved.

## Reuse boundaries

The 2020/2021/2023 studies are historical repeated surveys, not an established stable longitudinal panel. Profitability associations do not establish causation; stronger profits can fund investment. Do not create a numeric ranking, causal ROI promise, or current AI benchmark from this adaptation. Small subgroups and perspective differences limit generalization. This assesses an organization's operating capability; customer progression remains a distinct question in [[customer-maturity-progression-contract]].
