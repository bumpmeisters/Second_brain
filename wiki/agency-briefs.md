# Agency Briefs

**Summary**: Agency briefs define what external partners need to understand, create, and deliver against a focused marketing objective.

---

The HP agency briefing template says a good brief clearly defines success, conveys passion, is singularly focused, defines desired audience response, is insight-led, and balances inspiration with information (source: Agency_Briefing Template.pptx).

The HP briefs summary explains that a brief defines the request, while the briefing is the meeting where the brief is discussed with the organization that will deliver the work (source: Briefs Summary.pptx).

The same summary says campaigns and initiatives can use input briefs to agencies, while NPIs use product value proposition, GTM value proposition, and strategic brief structures (source: Briefs Summary.pptx).
