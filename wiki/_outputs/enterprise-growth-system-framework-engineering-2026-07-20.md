# Enterprise Growth System — Framework Engineering Result

## Framework Job

Turn Rolf's three-part ABM North Star into a project-local, inference-ready framework that routes work across demand, account development, and customer expansion; enforces evidence, readiness, and capacity decisions; and hands clean operating contracts to execution, measurement, technology, and learning.

Target users are enterprise growth leaders, account leaders, ABM and demand teams, customer success/delivery, RevOps/analytics, experts, executives, and transformation leads. The framework may produce operating-model and orchestration requirements, but it must not authorize external outreach, relationship scoring, opportunity-stage changes, or resource allocation without accountable human decisions.

## Classification

`original`. The three documents are the primary internal source set for this original method. The migration adds modular questions and ContextOps routing but does not attribute those adaptations to HP or any external expert.

### Selection

Candidate B wins because it preserves the source's distinctive control mechanism: a durable motion decision and an initiative-specific execution loop are different but connected decisions. Optional modules load only when a case needs them.

Candidate A remains the three-part reference. Candidate C remains the implementation module. Candidate D is rejected as canonical because it removes Market Demand and Customer Expansion and narrows the North Star into an acquisition-only ABM model.

## Selected Reasoning Architecture

1. Establish the commercial state to change and unit of management.
2. Route the case using fit, value, trigger, thesis, buying-group movement, relationships, customer health, internal readiness, delivery readiness, and capacity.
3. Open the relevant Growth Execution Loop gate.
4. Separate fact, signal, interpretation, hypothesis, unknown, and disproved belief.
5. Coordinate ownership and federated contributions through a Commercial Alignment Contract.
6. Design an action around one observable progression.
7. Measure activity, response, progression, commercial outcome, customer value, and execution effectiveness separately.
8. Close the loop with an explicit portfolio, motion, initiative, or reuse decision.

### Realistic Synthetic Case

An enterprise provider starts with 200 selected accounts, a seven-account transformation cluster, three strategic accounts, and existing customers with varying health. The framework keeps the broad population in Account-based Demand; admits the cluster to 1:few only if one change and value hypothesis holds; admits strategic accounts to 1:1 only with thesis, access, commitment, delivery readiness, and capacity; routes healthy customers to expansion and unhealthy customers to recovery; rejects automatic sales outreach from isolated intent; and requires motion-specific outcomes.

This demonstrates internal consistency and inference leverage, not market effectiveness.
