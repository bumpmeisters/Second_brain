## Contradictions and caveats

- The strongest sources are practitioner or vendor material; none validates universal performance or commercial outcomes.
- Product, interface, platform-ranking, reach, follower, model, connector, pricing, and cost claims are time-sensitive and excluded.
- The Earley source treats security as a commercial enabler; the proposed vault wording keeps risk truth, remediation, and certification limits ahead of sales language.
- The Nate Jones source contains an unverified sabotage statistic and punitive employment language. Neither is promoted. The durable extension is transparency about credible workforce impact and protected human responsibility.
- The Bright Data test is explicitly not a benchmark. Its fixed break-even estimate, vendor comparison, and near-zero retrieval-cost framing are excluded.
- The Creator Magic tutorial recommends persistent login and an always-on local host. That is not promoted because it conflicts with stronger identity, secret, and least-privilege controls.
- The Mr. Paid Social sources contain an unsupported Meta/LLM analogy and an automation demo without adequate licensing, consent, privacy, security, or platform-policy evidence.
