## Approved durable extensions

### P34-W3-C01 — Coach judgment without creating clones

- **Rule**: Label feedback as observation versus preference, teach the evaluation test rather than a verdict, use peer critique before coach intervention, and treat learner ownership as graduation.
- **Source**: `2026-08-12--EYXs4h_cLMk.md`.
- **Target**: `wiki/observable-capability-teach-back-loop.md`.
- **Why it is additive**: It operationally sharpens the already approved protected-reps pattern; no new practice is needed.
- **Excluded**: historical analogies, effectiveness, scaling, career, and employment claims.

### P34-W3-C02 — Security proof as an enterprise AI buying layer

- **Rule**: Translate buyer security requirements into verifiable controls, assurance evidence, unresolved gaps, and owned remediation. A certification or trust page must not imply that risk is eliminated.
- **Source**: `2026-07-28--Y3ri8UdilF8.md`.
- **Target**: `wiki/ai-native-gtm-operating-model.md`.
- **Why it is additive**: Existing security pages govern the system, but the GTM page does not yet connect truthful assurance evidence and unresolved risk to enterprise deal readiness.
- **Excluded**: revenue, efficiency, market-access, questionnaire, certification, customer, and universal-framework claims.

### P34-W3-C03 — Build versus rent recurring web context

- **Rule**: Use external search or context services for variable ad-hoc work; reassess owned collection when the same entities and questions recur and freshness, coverage, repeated cost, permission, maintenance, or reliability become material.
- **Source**: `2026-08-14--Ot4OPrPH4xY.md`.
- **Target**: `wiki/ai-operating-system.md`.
- **Why it is additive**: The page distinguishes owned intelligence from rented models, but not the operating decision for live external context.
- **Excluded**: vendor scale, decay percentages, provider rankings, one-day build, zero-cost retrieval, and the fixed 15,000-query crossover.

### P34-W3-C04 — A credible people contract before AI rollout

- **Rule**: State a credible workforce-impact commitment and the human work that remains accountable; choose one bounded use case with a business or customer outcome; name team-level ownership; scale only after evidence, safeguards, and workflow changes are understood.
- **Sources**: `2026-08-09--JIGaCPv44QI.md`, corroborated by `2026-07-01--NHM6OgVjEmI.md`.
- **Target**: `wiki/ai-native-gtm-operating-model.md`.
- **Why it is additive**: The existing people-process-platform-proof checklist lacks an explicit workforce-impact and protected-human-work boundary.
- **Excluded**: sabotage percentages, employment threats, macro labor claims, company anecdotes, productivity, job forecasts, and causal adoption claims.

### P34-W3-C05 — Client LLM-wiki delivery starts with friction and ends with handoff

- **Rule**: Start from bounded business friction, connect durable context to a named business goal, test retrieval, and hand off an inspectable plain-file system before adding complex infrastructure.
- **Source**: `How to Build Second Brains for Clients The AI Business I’d Start Today.md`.
- **Target**: `wiki/llm-wiki.md`.
- **Why it is additive**: It adds a client-delivery application to the sparse canonical definition without creating a commercial methodology page.
- **Excluded**: treating the branded seven types as universal; course, price, ease, client-value, and business-opportunity claims.

### P34-W3-C06 — Competitor signals trigger review, not automatic repositioning

- **Rule**: Compare exact claim language, category scope, buyer relevance, and current proof; let an accountable human choose watch, clarify, update, or reject; record the decision with its source.
- **Source**: `2026-07-22--jUt6r248w1A.md`.
- **Target**: `wiki/messaging-frameworks.md`.
- **Why it is additive**: The current positioning workflow maps competitors, but does not state the ongoing signal-to-review maintenance decision.
- **Excluded**: current Canva and Figma facts, automatic competitor classification, product accuracy, semantic-search, and commercial-effect claims.
