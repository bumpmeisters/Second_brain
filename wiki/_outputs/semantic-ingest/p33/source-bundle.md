## Contradictions and caveats

- All W1 sources are practitioner or vendor material; no product effectiveness or universal safety claim is treated as independently validated.
- The second-brain source's compliance and effectiveness figures are self-reported and excluded.
- The Gadgets talk's broad claim that generated-code vulnerabilities cannot matter is narrowed to an architecture signal; connector, platform, and cross-boundary risks remain.
- The clinical AI interview supplies no evaluation dataset, error rates, calibration record, independent replication, or regulatory assessment.
- One manual source has an exact-content duplicate under a misleading filename. P33 preserves the exact user-approved `Your AI Second Brain Is Slowly Rotting` path as the canonical decision row and does not mutate either raw file.
- W2's “trust recession” is explicitly framed by its speaker as an unverified hypothesis. Its market forecasts, traffic claims, demographic anecdotes, and proposed verification shortcuts are excluded.
- W2's LinkedIn, Claude, and jobs roundup is secondary and time-sensitive. Its platform counts, product interpretation, employment figures, and anecdotal advice are not promoted.
- W2's fixed 24-month content-age rule is not accepted as a universal retirement policy; review timing remains risk- and volatility-based.
- W4's dependency-aware elicitation pattern is one practitioner method, not evidence that question batching improves speed or specification quality. Recommended answers can bias reviewers.
- W4 product availability, compatibility, model, adoption, legal, personnel, market, medical, and performance claims are excluded.
- W5's lighthouse/landgrab heuristic comes from an investor-produced practitioner discussion with portfolio conflicts. It is a stage-specific hypothesis, not an exhaustive taxonomy or validated growth strategy.
- W5 product, model, pricing, audience, campaign, revenue, traffic-attribution, productivity, hiring, and employment-culture claims are excluded.
- W6's Liquid Death interview is a practitioner account, not causal evidence for revenue, brand growth, platform effectiveness, fixed frequency, test timing, or gain thresholds. The method does not recommend copying the brand's comedy.
- W6's writing source is one promotional livestream, not evidence that voice mode improves quality, productivity, or voice fidelity. Its unfinished historical and technical claims are not promoted.
