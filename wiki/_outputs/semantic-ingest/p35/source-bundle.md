- Shared authentication and one persistent agent machine can enlarge the blast radius even when a source presents them as simplification.
- Named-model rankings, prices, performance, availability, product features, market shares, forecasts, and legal/compliance characterizations are excluded unless separately verified from current primary sources.
- The Jack Carr workflow is one experienced novelist's practice. It is reusable as a bounded option, not a universal law of writing or proof of commercial success.
- The Codex–Claude Code comparison is a single, confounded run and even reports inconsistent cost figures. It supports evaluation design only.
