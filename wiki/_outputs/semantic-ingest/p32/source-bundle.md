# P32 Semantic Ingest Source Bundle

**Summary**: Complete source bundle for all ten waves of the 31-source YouTube pilot. Sixteen qualified evidence patterns were promoted, fourteen fully reviewed sources add no durable delta, and one source is a duplicate representation. No source remains unread or undecided.

## Knowledge delta

- A revenue-intelligence review becomes useful when it converts customer conversations, seller activity, and market signals into questions, named actions, owners, and a recurring feedback cycle rather than stopping at a dashboard (source: 2026-07-28--g-g6E-qtCjA.md).
- AI-assisted campaign audio can follow a brief-test-iterate loop: specify audience, emotional tone, tempo, instruments, and exclusions; generate contrasting directions; listen and critique; then regenerate (source: 2026-07-21--VnVpfOTYP0E.md).
- The current Agent Plugins Working Draft separates a small portable package core from client-specific extensions and distribution mechanics (source: 2026-08-07--M3gHlwHKsBI.md; [Agent Plugins specification](https://agent-plugins.org/specification)).
- When the automation problem itself is unclear, a permission-bounded discovery preflight can generate independent, evidence-linked problem candidates for human comparison before any implementation is authorized (source: 2026-07-17--uCWKXIyvM_8.md).
- A coding-agent sandbox needs boundary-specific preflight checks across runtime, workspace, network, Docker-daemon access, credentials, and cross-sandbox integrations; clone-style isolation prevents host-repository writes but is not a confidentiality boundary (source: 2026-07-23--zb2LyMro77M.md; [Docker isolation layers](https://docs.docker.com/ai/sandboxes/security/isolation/)).
- A practitioner GTM architecture corroborates the existing separation of signals, buyer context, and actions, including distinct fit and intent inputs, an auditable policy layer, and feedback from drift and ignored alerts (source: 2026-07-20--ltv-L5oMPIs.md).
- Paid creator amplification can be tested as a staged motion from approved expert content to a defined audience, an observed-engagement cohort, and a later lead offer, with each transition measured separately and engagement kept distinct from intent (source: 2026-08-04--9GE5AMN964s.md).
- AI-assisted media repurposing needs separate rendering, per-asset acceptance, scheduling, and publication gates, including count and cost reconciliation plus visual checks for captions, occlusion, branding, and preserved meaning (source: 2026-07-31--4RPFO8ytYaY.md).
- Multi-agent verification should inspect the artifact rather than producer reports, apply to controller-authored changes as well as worker output, and retain a specification-grounded adjudication path for faulty verifier decisions (source: 2026-07-08--suY66oTDn0s.md).
- High-consequence document extraction should make every material field reviewable against the exact source passage and applicable document or amendment version, evaluate representative ordinary, ambiguous, amended, and outlier cases under an agreed interpretation, and route uncertain or novel cases to a qualified human (source: 2026-07-31--me78s3FxxSE.md).
- A cyber-capability evaluation is an adversarial end-to-end system, not one sandbox: independently test every permitted egress, secret, metadata, identity, and cross-system boundary; correlate distributed low-signal activity; and pre-stage a vetted in-boundary path for hostile-trace analysis while accountable humans retain incident authority (source: 2026-07-29--YPIojUaqs-Q.md; [OpenAI incident disclosure](https://openai.com/index/hugging-face-model-evaluation-security-incident/); [Hugging Face technical timeline](https://huggingface.co/blog/agent-intrusion-technical-timeline)).
- Rhetorical form is a purpose-fit editing layer after idea, audience, evidence, and genre are clear: use repetition, balance, imagery, surprise, rhythm, or intentional fragments only for a named communicative job, test consequential lines aloud, and retain form that reinforces meaning (source: 2026-08-05--6ZNCJH8jJD0.md).
- Revision should separate diagnosis from prescription: identify the draft's emerging patterns and reader effects, treat repeated discomfort as evidence about where it fails, and keep the accountable author responsible for choosing and rechecking the repair (source: 2026-08-12--0kkAl04_0og.md).
- A commercial explainer or Video Sales Letter should make its persuasive sequence inspectable by connecting the intended audience and problem to useful teaching, the offer, verifiable proof, material objections and alternatives, price or other trade-offs, fit and non-fit, and a low-friction next step without treating testimonials, engagement, or the sequence itself as causal proof (source: 2026-06-16--xGDgK1LpTwU.md).
- The boundary between agentic and deterministic execution is lifecycle-dependent: use bounded agentic or MCP-mediated interaction for exploration, read-only inspection, prototyping, and failure discovery, then graduate stable recurring paths into reviewed deterministic code or bounded tools while preserving scoped permissions, verification, and accountable approval for production writes (source: 2026-07-17--jb-ABR52jZ4.md; practitioner example).
- A promotional marketing-agent interview corroborates only the narrower runtime rule: keep stable scheduled execution and data movement in ordinary code and invoke a model at explicit interpretation or judgment points inside a bounded process (source: 2026-08-05--mD7JpNHLT70.md; practitioner corroboration, not system validation).

## Contradictions and caveats

- Two promoted sources are vendor demonstrations and one is a single practitioner comparison; none establishes commercial impact or causal effectiveness.
- Most pilot transcripts are automatic German captions, often translated from English. Terminology and named entities can be distorted, so durable technical claims need stronger corroboration.
- Agent Plugins v1.0.0 was a Working Draft when checked on 2026-08-15. Exact schema, client support, governance, and distribution behavior are time-sensitive.
- Product accuracy, content rights, royalty status, named-model superiority, growth, token, privacy, cost, and outcome claims were deliberately excluded.
- The sandbox source is a sponsored product demonstration. Current Docker documentation supports the boundary distinctions, but documented defaults are time-sensitive and do not establish universal safety.
- The buyer-aware GTM source corroborates architecture only. Its statistics, visitor-identification accuracy, deployment volume, automatic-outreach implications, and commercial outcomes were excluded.
- Wave 3 reviewed almost 100,000 estimated tokens without promoting a pattern. This is deliberate: two sources repeat stronger existing coverage, and the Super-App transcript is the same Latent.Space interview already analysed elsewhere in the vault.
- Wave 4 uses three practitioner demonstrations only for narrow operating controls. Platform formats, budgets, self-reported campaign metrics, tool scores, permission bypass, cost and speed claims, model superiority, accessibility, and commercial outcomes remain excluded.

- Wave 5 promotes only one evaluation extension from a vendor-practitioner contract-intelligence interview. Product accuracy, automation rates, token and cost figures, model comparisons, platform superiority, compliance, financial outcomes, and universal thresholds or schemas remain excluded. The two other videos are strongly promotional and add no durable evidence beyond stronger existing commercial and sales-context coverage.
- Wave 6 treats three videos as one security-incident family rather than three independent confirmations. The incident core is supported by first-party OpenAI and Hugging Face disclosures, but the two broad news roundups add no durable evidence. Excluded claims include model consciousness or deception, autonomous-defense necessity, ordinary-assistant equivalence, model and product comparisons, legislative outcomes, geopolitics, market timing, and vendor claims. The locally hosted forensic model is an incident-specific implementation, not a universal prescription.
- Wave 7 promotes two practitioner editing heuristics, not measured writing effects. Exact quotations and historical, literary, biblical, musical, etymological, award, sales, sponsor, and education claims are excluded, as are universal instructions to ornament prose, draft out of order, ignore editors, or expect memory, persuasion, retention, or conversion gains.
- Wave 8 uses a vendor's self-commentary only to expose an inspectable commercial-explainer sequence. Product, privacy, platform, targeting, testimonial, metric, price, setup, conversion, and commercial-outcome claims are excluded, and the sequence is not treated as causal proof.
- Wave 9 promotes one lifecycle extension and one corroborating practitioner citation, not either demonstrated system. Product setup, compatibility, privacy, `localhost` security sufficiency, scraping, personal-data enrichment, automated outreach or replies, legality, consent, autopublishing, platform, metric, cost, and outcome claims are excluded.
- Wave 10 is deliberately zero-yield. The parallel-agent livestream illustrates controls and failures already covered more rigorously in the vault, while the OpenCode founder interview repeats existing portability, runtime, evaluation, and adoption patterns. Product, model, provider, usage, growth, revenue, market, performance, security-sufficiency, and enterprise-readiness claims are excluded.
