# ABM Play Library Priority Research Backlog

**Summary**: Completed P10 research waves plus the prioritized residual research agenda after Gate 2. No normal-web fallback is authorized.

---

## Research rules

- Search ABM plus sales pursuit, buyer enablement, customer success, renewal, partner/channel and revenue-operations practice.
- Prefer customer/practitioner records, independent research and primary operating evidence over vendor lists.
- Use vendor Playbooks for discovery and terminology; qualify all outcomes.
- Add every credible lead to the Discovery Source Map.
- Archive only decision-relevant originals through the approved source inbox.
- Treat a source as evidence for a mechanism only within its documented context.
