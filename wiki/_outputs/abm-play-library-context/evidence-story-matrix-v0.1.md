# ABM Play Library - Evidence Story Matrix

**Summary**: A 63-row editorial research matrix that separates story potential from evidence readiness. It identifies the safest available evidence route, claim boundary, narrative, and next evidence request for every catalog entry.

## How to read the matrix

- **Story potential** rates editorial tension and usefulness, not proof strength.
- **Evidence readiness** carries the approved v0.2.0 confidence assessment.
- **Story mode** determines whether the post should lead with a mechanism, boundary, conditional case, reframing, category clarification, or explicit evidence gap.
- **Allowed use** is a drafting permission, not publication approval.
- A source can support a mechanism without proving that the Play caused a commercial outcome.

## Portfolio summary

- Rows: 63
- High story potential: 39
- High or medium-high evidence readiness: 18
- Low or medium-low evidence readiness: 11
- New web leads awaiting archive or promotion: 3

## Editorial and claim-governance rules

1. Lead with the operating tension, not with a performance promise.
2. Attribute vendor, consultancy, and practitioner claims to the source.
3. Do not convert source-reported outcomes into universal Play outcomes.
4. Gap-led stories may challenge assumptions or propose a hypothesis, but must not imply validation.
5. Competitor, privacy, compliance, AI, pipeline, revenue, retention, and ROI claims require an additional claim review before publication.
6. Publication remains separately approval-gated.
