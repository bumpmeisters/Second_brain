## Candidate gaps for external research

These are research questions, not additions:

1. **Commercial and procurement navigation** — determine whether security, legal, procurement and commercial consensus require a distinct Play or are already covered by Shape the RFP, Build the Case and Close the Confidence Gap.
2. **Customer whitespace discovery** — test whether discovering underpenetrated value is a separate state-changing Play or merely the intelligence step preceding Plant the Next Use Case or organizational expansion.
3. **Buying-group mobilization** — test whether helping a group coordinate internal work is sufficiently covered by Make a Champion, Build the Case and Break the No-Decision.
4. **M&A integration and divestiture** — test whether these states require more than Survive the Reorg and Cross the Border.
5. **Partner recruitment and partner-account growth** — clarify whether the Library governs only end-customer accounts or also partner accounts as managed commercial units.
6. **Community and peer-network interventions** — determine whether these are formats/tactics or can represent a distinct relationship state.
7. **Executive mandate creation** — test whether category-level or transformation sponsorship is covered by Teach the Problem, Bring in the Boss and Create the Opportunity.

## Contradictions and limits

- The Library uses the word “Play” for three different analytical levels. This is memorable but requires visible labels to prevent category errors.
- Several entries are strongly derived from framework logic but have little direct case evidence.
- The internal cases support mechanisms more strongly than outcomes.
- Public evidence challenges strengthen buyer-access and partner-route questions but do not establish a complete Play taxonomy.
- External research must look for disconfirming evidence and missing states, not merely matching terminology.
