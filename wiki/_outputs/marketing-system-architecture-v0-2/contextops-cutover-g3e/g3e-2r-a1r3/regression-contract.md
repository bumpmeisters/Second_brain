The values are forwarded through every runtime-binding boundary to A1. A1 requires canonical absolute literal regular-file paths without reparse traversal, exact static SHA-256 and version matches, x64 PE architecture, and launches each version probe only through the verified absolute path. Ambient `Get-Command rg` and `Get-Command git` resolution is forbidden. All four runtime bindings remain compared byte-for-byte with the seal or capability-probe authority before stateful execution.
