## Durable synthesis

Intent observations should move an account between explicit working states rather than trigger outreach by themselves. A practical distinction is fit without demonstrated awareness, future potential with incomplete need evidence, and active focus with stronger awareness, need, relationship, or opportunity context. Marketing and sales review these states together, then choose the next meaningful value-adding touch based on known history, role, and buying-group coverage. Anonymous visits or third-party topic activity are inputs, not proof of purchase intent.

ABM adoption behaves like an operating change, not a campaign launch. Begin with a bounded pilot, a specific situation and account set, leadership sponsorship, sales co-ownership, a review cadence, and measures of process adoption, engagement quality, relationship development, and account movement. Revenue may lag substantially behind early operating evidence.

## Caveats

Both discussions include vendors and self-reported practitioner examples. Named outcomes, recommended pilot durations, and claims about the only “proper” form of ABM are not universal evidence. The proposed account states are a useful operating vocabulary, not a validated scoring model.
