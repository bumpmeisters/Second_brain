# Why Enterprise ABM Falls Short and How to Fix It

## Core thesis

The interview presents enterprise ABM as a coordinated operating system spanning brand, demand, nurture, sales, data, buying groups, tiering, content, and AI-assisted personalization. Its practical recommendation is to repair foundations, align global and local teams, begin with a small program, and connect signals to relevant next actions. Those ideas are useful, but the vault already contains more specific and executable versions of each mechanism.

## Structured outline

1. Treat ABM as an operating model rather than an isolated campaign.
2. Align sales, marketing, global, and local ownership.
3. Repair account, contact, and buying-group data.
4. Tier accounts according to context and feasible relevance.
5. Start small, observe response, and adjust the motion.
6. Connect content and engagement signals to account states.
7. Consolidate campaigns and use AI selectively for personalization.

## Reusable mechanisms

- Establish data and ownership foundations before orchestration.
- Match account effort to tier and buying-group context.
- Use a bounded pilot rather than launching a broad transformation.
- Interpret signals cautiously and choose contextual next actions.
