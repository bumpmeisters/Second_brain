# Sara Whitwer on Leadership, Culture, and Marketing Agility

## Core thesis

The interview’s most useful operating example is a decision-oriented channel review: clarify an event’s intended role, inspect funnel and closed-won evidence without ignoring brand and multi-touch limits, and test a bounded alternative for named target personas instead of either defending or eliminating the whole channel.

## Structured outline

1. Identify the business problem and the intended role of the event or channel.
2. Inspect direct response, funnel, account, seller, and closed-won evidence.
3. State what brand, relationship, and multi-touch effects cannot be inferred from the available data.
4. Decide whether to continue, stop, hold, narrow, or test an alternative.
5. Align the alternative with named accounts and target personas.
6. Reassign or develop the team capabilities needed for the new format.
7. Review the bounded test before wider budget reallocation.

## Reusable mechanisms

- Refuse both lead-count-only evaluation and unmeasurable brand defenses.
- Convert a channel disagreement into a bounded portfolio decision.
- Preserve the difference between no observed closed-won outcome and proof of no effect.
- Adapt existing team capabilities rather than treating a channel change as purely a budget move.

## Lean analysis

The example is sensible but does not add durable knowledge beyond the vault. [[campaign-types-and-funnel-stages]] already requires an explicit event role; [[campaign-reporting-and-operations]] connects evidence to stop, continue, test, and fund decisions while separating attribution; and existing ABM and sponsorship practices cover named-account alternatives. The interview provides no thresholds, minimum data, cadence, or complete reuse boundary. It is therefore `registered-only`, not a new artifact or promotional corroborating row.

## Caveats

This is a Demandbase-hosted practitioner interview and a selective success narrative. Zero closed-won outcomes do not prove a trade show had no value, and the source itself notes brand and multi-touch limits. Exclude claims that the pivot caused better outcomes, that culture causes strategy execution, award references as independent proof, average CMO-tenure claims, and general career or strengths anecdotes.
