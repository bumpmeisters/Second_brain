# How to Implement ABM Strategy 3.0

## Core thesis

The transcript presents ABM as a cluster-based, long-horizon operating system spanning account research, buying groups, staged activation, content reuse, weekly account review, progression measures, and process-first AI support. Its body is already present in the vault under the correct title and video identity, so this file is a covered variant rather than new or corroborating evidence.

## Structured outline

1. Group accounts by a shared challenge or use case instead of broad industry alone.
2. Adapt existing activities to the chosen cluster and buying roles.
3. Agree success criteria and ownership with sales before launch.
4. Run a small pilot, retrospect, improve, and test again before scaling.
5. Research accounts and separate evidence from working hypotheses.
6. Match governed content and next actions to account and buyer context.
7. Add AI only after the manual process, evidence sources, benchmark, and review are understood.
8. Review account progression, blockers, actions, and leading observations weekly.

## Reusable mechanisms

- Pilot, retrospect, repeat, and gate scaling.
- Convert account research into a source-bounded evidence brief.
- Organize sales content around use-case clusters and buyer context.
- Use a quality benchmark and human review before expanding AI support.
