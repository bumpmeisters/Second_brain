# Global ABM and AI-SDR transcript bundle

**Summary**: Two sources address scale at different levels: a global-regional ABM organization and a staged AI-SDR rollout. Both rely on shared operating objects, bounded automation, continuing human ownership, and comparable outcome review.

## Sources

- [How to build a global, AI-native ABM function](../../../../../raw/Clippings/How%20to%20build%20a%20global%2C%20AI-native%20ABM%20function%202.md)
- [AI SDRs explained](../../../../../raw/Clippings/AI%20SDRs%20explained%20How%20AI%20agents%20are%20redefining%20inbound%20pipeline%20%20OnBase%20podcast.md)

## Durable synthesis

Global ABM needs a shared core without separating regional teams from their sales counterparts. Standardize the account portfolio, qualification and state model, cadence, measures, and reporting. Embed regional ABM owners in sales territories so they share responsibility for the same account movement and pipeline questions. Comparable account “swim lanes” then make it possible to diagnose whether a region’s constraint is enablement, capacity, operating discipline, or culture rather than inventing a different model for every territory (anchors: 8:52-13:26 and 16:51-21:41 in the global-ABM transcript).

AI can remove research, synthesis, and context-assembly work, but human owners should still decide the account bucket, apply judgment to the proposed play, and conduct consequential relationship work. This is a practical boundary between scaled preparation and human engagement (anchors: 51:15-55:39).

For an AI SDR, begin with one visible inbound bottleneck rather than replacing a role wholesale. Stage the workload, define when the agent hands off, coach against approved product and brand knowledge, and compare accepted outcomes with the current process. Adoption also depends on incentives: sellers should not lose quota credit or compensation because an agent assisted the pipeline, or they will rationally resist the workflow (anchors: 6:23-10:33, 14:44-20:18, and 21:07-22:18 in the AI-SDR transcript).

## Caveats

Both sources contain strong promotional claims. Reported cost, speed, conversion, productivity, pipeline, customer, and workforce outcomes are excluded. The global model is one company’s operating design; the AI-SDR model is vendor guidance. Both require local risk, data, labor, brand, and buyer-experience review.
