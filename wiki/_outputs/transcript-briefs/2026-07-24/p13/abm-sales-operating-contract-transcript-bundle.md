# ABM sales operating-contract transcript bundle

**Summary**: Three practitioner sources converge on ABM as shared account work rather than a marketing program followed by a sales handoff. Their strongest additions are an alignment preflight, a seller role contract, and a weekly signal-response pilot loop.

## Durable synthesis

Alignment must be made observable before a pilot starts. Sales and marketing should compare what they mean by account stages and qualification, which outcomes they own, the time horizon in which they work, how seller compensation reacts to the proposed motion, and how much capacity each role can actually commit. A simple visual should show account states, activities, owners, inputs, and measures. Agreement with the words “ABM” and “revenue” is insufficient (anchors: 6:49-8:04, 15:30-24:22, and 50:26-52:36 in the alignment transcript).

The corresponding seller role extends across the account lifecycle. Sellers help define fit and value criteria, map buying roles, contribute private account knowledge, progressively profile needs, nurture relationships, and co-create activation. Their observations must return to the common account record so marketing can improve content and account plays. This replaces the static lead handoff with shared account learning (anchors: 14:46-18:59, 34:55-37:07, and 45:42-52:23 in the sales transcript).

A lean pilot can then run as a weekly signal-response loop. Integrate it with one existing strategic event or content priority, review account movement and engagement together, choose a next meaningful touch, reserve execution time, and reuse relevant content. ABM should not become an extra campaign silo competing with the existing plan (anchors: 11:24-21:55, 36:55-38:24, and 42:51-52:51 in the association case).

## Caveats

The sources promote practitioner frameworks or consulting work. Reported account movement, opportunities, meetings, conversion, touch counts, time allocations, and budget ratios are excluded. The durable contribution is the operating contract, not a claim that the featured playbooks guarantee pipeline.
