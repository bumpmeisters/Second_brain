# Data and behavioral-signals transcript bundle

**Summary**: Two mixed practitioner interviews add complementary controls around account signals: validate source data before scoring, then ground third-party intent in timely first-party behavior and CRM context.

## Durable synthesis

Account activation needs a data-admission gate. Before scoring, validate that the account belongs in the addressable market, the contact is associated with the correct organization, the role is relevant, and the fields are accurate enough for the intended decision. Do not scale lead generation or scoring over data that is known to be unreliable. Once admitted, trace how observations move through the CRM and engagement systems and periodically audit errors that appear downstream (anchors: 0:00-1:27, 9:47-12:07, and 17:30-19:17 in the precision-targeting transcript).

The website can provide a second control by acting as an account-listening surface. A single visit or topic signal is weak. A compressed pattern across repeated sessions, multiple visitors, reference content, people pages, legal or procurement material, and related high-intent paths may indicate active evaluation. Join those observations with CRM state and third-party intent before changing account priority (anchors: 7:51-9:34 and 17:37-18:34 in the behavioral-ABM transcript).

Composite triggers should be calibrated with sales in a proof of concept. The goal is not to generate more MQAs, but to surface a small number of account-state changes that add context the seller did not already know. Record why the trigger fired and which behavior supported it (anchors: 19:31-24:12).

## Caveats

Both interviews include vendors, customers, or promoted services. Named-platform performance, pipeline shares, deal speed, conversion percentages, and client anecdotes are excluded. Behavioral patterns may have benign explanations and should initiate review or a bounded next action, not automatically authorize seller outreach.
