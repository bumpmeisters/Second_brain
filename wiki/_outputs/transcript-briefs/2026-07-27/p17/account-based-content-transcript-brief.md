# Account-Based Content

## Core thesis

For a high-value account with a visible project or strategic initiative, build a bounded evidence asset from verified account context, owned expertise, comparable cases, approved market evidence, and authorized first-party input. Tailor only where the account context materially changes the problem, value, proof, objection, decision, or experience.

## Structured outline

1. Select a high-value account and a visible initiative worth deeper research.
2. Clarify the economic or operational decision behind the initiative.
3. Gather owned expertise, comparable cases, market evidence, and first-party input where available.
4. Separate reusable proof from account-specific elements that change substance.
5. Create the evidence asset and deliver it through a relationship-appropriate channel.
6. Record the response and decide whether deeper engagement is justified.

## Reusable mechanisms

- Convert existing expertise and comparable cases into project-relevant evidence.
- Use a light research-led reason to engage rather than an immediate pitch.
- Connect the buyer's economic decision to authorized first-party and analogous proof.
- Let AI organize owned inputs while preserving human judgment and source control.

## Caveats

This is a vendor-hosted client case discussion. Project figures, rent and cash-flow effects, opportunities, expected deals, and claims that content or LinkedIn caused pipeline or trust are excluded. Passive-consumption and channel-preference statements are not universal. Tailoring must not invent account facts, expose restricted data, or become mass pseudo-personalization.
