# Zero-click SEO: How AI Is Disrupting the B2B Buyer’s Journey

## Core thesis

The interview argues that AI-mediated answers increasingly sit between published information and buyers. Marketers should therefore make approved buyer answers accessible, structured, current, and easy to verify, while retaining the website as a useful destination.

## Structured outline

1. Search and website traffic are changing, but the source overstates the scale and certainty of that change.
2. Buyer questions, roles, themes, value propositions, and related topics should structure discoverable content.
3. Technical accessibility should be inspected before assuming retrieval systems can reach intended public material.
4. Brands should review how they are represented and correct stale or contradictory public information.
5. Measurement should combine representation accuracy, visibility, referrals, engagement, and downstream outcomes.

## Reusable mechanisms

- Map real buyer questions and roles to approved source-backed answers.
- Audit whether intended public content is technically reachable.
- Review the externally visible historical footprint and assign refresh or retirement actions.
- Monitor a bounded set of brand and competitor representations for accuracy.

## Caveats

The interview supplies no linked research for its traffic, click-through, prompt-length, buyer-behavior, crawler-frequency, trust, or market forecasts. Anonymous client examples, tool recommendations, Gartner predictions, and future advertising or platform claims are excluded. Visibility or referral does not establish pipeline or revenue causality.
