# Brian Gladstein on Tufin’s Data-Led GTM Turnaround

## Core thesis

The useful contribution is a weekly diagnostic loop that moves from observable funnel discrepancy to deal-level inspection, a bounded process test, and joint review of the intended measure and adjacent guardrails before scaling.

## Structured outline

1. Repair data only to the level required for the next decision and make funnel steps observable.
2. Separate new-logo, expansion, and renewal funnels before diagnosing performance.
3. Scan weekly for regional, historical, segment, or target-versus-actual gaps.
4. Inspect the underlying deals to distinguish local anomalies from systemic causes.
5. Test one narrow process change with limited exposure.
6. Review the target measure and adjacent numbers for unintended effects before expanding.

## Reusable mechanisms

- Use discrepancy detection to choose where analysis begins.
- “Walk the mile” by inspecting individual deals rather than accepting aggregate explanations.
- Convert the localized cause into a falsifiable process hypothesis.
- Bound the remediation to a small team, segment, or time window.
- Monitor guardrail metrics so improvement in one number does not hide damage elsewhere.

## Lean analysis

The vault already covers recurring orchestration remediation and decision-purpose reporting, but not this complete sequence from weekly gap scan through deal-level localization and guarded experiment. The pattern therefore extends [[marketing-orchestration]] and [[campaign-reporting-and-operations]]. It also passes the standalone-artifact test because it has a clear trigger, required inputs, executable sequence, inspectable output, and explicit limits.

## Caveats

Tufin’s profitability, growth, digital pipeline, savings, cloud revenue, customer-advisory-board, expansion, and efficiency outcomes are self-reported and excluded. The three-stage private-equity turnaround, Gartner references, attribution examples, and AI touchpoint ideas do not provide independent evidence. A local discrepancy is not causal proof and should not be generalized without repeated tests.

## Decision rule

Open a diagnosis only when a trusted comparison reveals a meaningful gap. Narrow the gap to a funnel, segment, region, or period; inspect the underlying cases; and test the smallest plausible correction. Scale only when repeated evidence improves the target measure without unacceptable movement in adjacent guardrails. Otherwise revise the hypothesis or stop.
