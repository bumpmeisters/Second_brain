# Amanda Dyson on Nurturing and Growing Authentic Leadership

## Core thesis

Leadership expectations become more usable when converted from personal values into a reciprocal team working agreement. The agreement should make communication, meetings, focus time, feedback, delegation, escalation, and contribution credit explicit, then be reviewed against actual team experience rather than treated as a leader’s unilateral “user manual.”

## Structured outline

1. State leadership values together with confidentiality and disclosure boundaries.
2. Describe preferred communication, meeting, participation, and focus-time practices.
3. Ask the team to challenge and complete the draft with reciprocal expectations.
4. Establish frequent, multi-directional feedback and escalation routes.
5. Make expertise boundaries visible and delegate to the people closest to the work.
6. Record contributions and ensure credit reaches the people who created the result.
7. Review workload, friction, exceptions, and whether the agreement is actually used.

## Reusable mechanisms

- Translate “authenticity” into observable working rules rather than personality claims.
- Protect focused work by making meeting purpose, agenda, attendance, and optionality explicit.
- Separate leadership accountability from the fiction that a leader must be the strongest domain expert.
- Use feedback and contribution visibility as operating mechanisms, not occasional gestures.
- Make the agreement reciprocal so that power asymmetry does not turn preferences into hidden mandates.

## Caveats

The source is a practitioner interview and reports personal experience, not independently measured organizational effects. Do not claim that transparency, 360-degree feedback, protected focus time, recognition, or “radical candor” causes performance, retention, wellbeing, or psychological safety. Authenticity does not require complete disclosure. The practice cannot replace employment law, HR processes, performance management, accessibility accommodations, or company policy. Career, family, reading, and impostor-syndrome discussion is excluded from durable promotion.
