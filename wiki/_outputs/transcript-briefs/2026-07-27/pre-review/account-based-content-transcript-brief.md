# Account-Based Content

## Core thesis

A high-value named account or tight cluster may justify an evidence-backed content asset built around a visible initiative, project, or decision problem. The asset should combine reusable proof with only those account-specific elements that materially change the problem, value, objection, decision, or experience.

## Structured outline

1. Start from a known project or strategic initiative rather than generic personalization.
2. Research the account and decision context and obtain first-party input when possible.
3. Select comparable cases, approved market evidence, and reusable core content.
4. Create a tailored evidence asset only where account context changes substance.
5. Validate claims, permissions, confidentiality, and data use.
6. Deliver through the relationship-appropriate channel and record the response.

## Reusable mechanisms

- Convert expertise and comparable evidence into project-relevant content.
- Connect the buyer’s economic decision to authorized first-party and analogous evidence.
- Use a light, research-led reason to engage rather than an immediate pitch.
- Let AI organize owned inputs but not invent account facts or replace proprietary evidence and judgment.

Reported opportunities, expected deals, project and company figures, rent or cash-flow effects, sales-cycle duration, passive LinkedIn consumption, and universal channel preferences are excluded. Tailoring must not invent facts, expose restricted data, bypass authorization, or become mass pseudo-personalization.
