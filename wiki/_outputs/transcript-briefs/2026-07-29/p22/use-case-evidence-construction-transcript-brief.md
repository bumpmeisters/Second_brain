# How to Target Accounts in 2026

## Core thesis

The source extends use-case cluster prioritization at the step before comparison: construct consistent, traceable historical evidence from positive and negative deal paths. When comparable history is unavailable, do not pretend the normal scoring model applies; enter a lower-claim exploratory mode that tests explicit hypotheses until evidence supports cluster comparison or a stop decision.

## Structured outline

1. Define the business decision, period, and meaning of a use case.
2. Classify won, lost, open, and disqualified evidence with provenance and confidence.
3. Compare clusters using economics, cycle, proof, and the actual strategic objective.
4. Inspect largest, fastest, lost, and contradictory cases.
5. Extract roles, triggers, questions, touchpoints, fit, differentiation, and exclusions.
6. Select or defer clusters jointly and retain the evidence record.
7. If history is weak, declare an exploratory hypothesis branch.
8. Gather confirming and disconfirming evidence before returning to normal prioritization.

## Reusable mechanisms

- Use one explicit job-and-outcome definition for use-case labels.
- Preserve cohort, period, source, definition, and confidence.
- Analyze negative cases and exclusions, not only successful qualified deals.
- Weight criteria according to the decision objective rather than the largest deal.
- Separate hypothesis validation from pipeline or revenue commitments.

## Caveats

This is a commercial Fullfunnel methodology, not independent research. Historical deals are selected organizational records rather than a market sample; qualified-only data creates survivorship bias. ACV, LTV, win rate, cycle, and retention require stable definitions, cohorts, currencies, periods, and censoring rules. Descriptive differences are not causal. Titles, meeting attendance, email inclusion, and signatures do not prove buying role or authority. Engagement and discovery do not validate product-market fit, intent, pipeline, or revenue. AI may suggest labels only with permitted data, provenance, human review, and retained negative cases. The “2026” title does not establish current-market validity.
