# How Modern ABM Works: From Account Selection to Execution

## Core thesis

The interview frames mature ABM as a people-and-process operating model rather than a digital channel or tool deployment. It emphasizes cross-functional governance, account selection, lifecycle uses beyond acquisition, cautious signal interpretation, human-reviewed AI, and a small pilot. Those mechanisms are useful, but already exist in more executable and better-governed vault practices.

## Structured outline

1. Build ABM maturity through people, process, technology, and iterative pilots.
2. Involve sales, RevOps, marketing operations, content, and design early.
3. Use a cross-functional group to manage account and priority requests.
4. Apply ABM to acquisition, renewal, retention, expansion, or advocacy.
5. Combine fit, business context, relationships, and observable engagement.
6. Treat AI and intent signals as fallible inputs requiring explanation and review.
7. Position ABM as an operating model rather than a digital channel.
8. Start with a manually scored bounded account pilot.

## Reusable mechanisms

- Protect portfolio decisions from the loudest internal request.
- Combine account fit with strategic purpose, data quality, and committed capacity.
- Require a human explanation for signal-driven next-action recommendations.
- Begin manually and document learning before scaling tools or automation.
