# The ABM Team Structure That Actually Works

## Core thesis

This Fullfunnel and Dealfront session recommends a small, cooperative ABM pilot pod: begin manually with a marketer and willing sales counterpart, agree responsibilities and effort, inspect signals and fit together, add specialist support as required, and review accounts and next actions weekly. The vault already contains a more complete version of that operating contract.

## Structured outline

1. Start with one bounded pilot and a cooperative sales counterpart.
2. Choose participants with domain knowledge and willingness to learn.
3. Inspect account fit and observations jointly in real time.
4. Keep initial market, account, and team scope small.
5. Agree effort, roles, and mutual accountability.
6. Use an ABM lead and sales counterpart as the core pod.
7. Add content, product, design, executive, and RevOps support when needed.
8. Review signals, account insight, next action, owner, and blockers weekly.
9. Scale only after manual work reveals repeatable responsibilities.

## Reusable mechanisms

- Select pilot participants for cooperation and contextual judgment, not job title alone.
- Confirm actual capacity before assigning core or support roles.
- Treat manual operation as the source of the future playbook.
- Keep account observations and next-action ownership visible in a joint review.
