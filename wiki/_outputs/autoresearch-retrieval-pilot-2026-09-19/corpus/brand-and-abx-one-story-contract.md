# Brand and ABX One-Story Contract

**Summary**: Align brand building and account activation around one promise while keeping their roles and evidence layers distinct.

---

## When to use

Use before an account-based campaign, activation play, or sales program enters production.

## Required inputs

- The current approved brand promise and its supporting evidence.
- Customer-choice evidence: why current customers say they selected the company, including contradictions and sample limits.
- The relevant buying-group roles, decision contexts, use cases, and proof needs.
- Named Brand, Demand, Sales, and customer owners with authority to resolve conflicts.
- The signals permitted for adaptation and a reviewer for AI-generated variants.

## Contract

Brand, marketing, and sales jointly record:

- the one evidence-backed brand truth or promise the buying group should recognize;
- which customer-choice evidence supports or challenges that promise;
- the durable evidence supporting that promise;
- the account context that makes it relevant now;
- which elements must remain consistent and which proof, use cases, detail, or expression may adapt by buying-group role;
- which observed signals permit an adaptation and who reviews the resulting variant;
- the roles of brand, activation, sales, and customer teams;
- the measures used at each evidence layer.

## Preflight

1. Compare the brand story, campaign message, seller narrative, and customer proof.
2. Resolve contradictions before production.
3. Separate long-term recognition and receptivity from current account relevance.
4. Define linked evidence layers: brand signals, account reach and engagement, then commercial outcomes.
5. Compare each role-specific variant with the stable promise and approved proof before release.
6. Review the evidence layers together without treating their correlation as causal attribution.

## Output

A signed-off promise, proof set, adaptation boundary, role map, and layered measurement plan.

## Guardrails

The contract coordinates evidence; it does not prove that brand activity caused account engagement or revenue. Adaptation must not change the core claim, invent customer motives, or turn an activity signal into intent. AI personalization requires explainable inputs and accountable review. Buying-cycle, buying-group, audience-overlap, revenue, reach, and engagement figures from the interviews are excluded (source: Inside Roku’s programmatic playbook daily rigor, culture fit and the future of CTV  OnBase podcast.md; mixed practitioner transcript; analysis: P12-C04).
