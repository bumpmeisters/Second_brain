# Frameworks Templates Reference Library

**Summary**: Source summary for `raw/assets/A_frameworks_templates`, a reorganized reference library for marketing frameworks, briefing templates, brand material, persona/audience work, content frameworks, AI prompting, AI-for-marketing material, reporting/ops, and HP examples.

---

This bundle contains 359 cataloged files across the reference library and archive folders (source: raw/assets/A_frameworks_templates; analysis: wiki/_outputs/raw-assets-new-marketing-inventory-2026-06-05.md).

The active reference folders mirror the existing [[marketing-operating-system]] taxonomy: strategy, briefing, brand, personas/audience, content, AI prompting, AI for marketing, reporting/ops, and HP examples (source: raw/assets/A_frameworks_templates).

The bundle overlaps with earlier ingested folders such as [[marketing-strategy-library]], [[briefing-template-library]], [[brand-library]], [[persona-and-audience-library]], [[content-library]], [[reporting-and-ops-library]], and [[hp-examples-library]], but it also includes AI prompting and AI-for-marketing sections that were previously represented in `research/assets` (source: raw/assets/A_frameworks_templates; source: research/assets).

AI-labeled files in `06_AI_Prompting`, `07_AI_fuer_Marketing`, and `_Archiv_AI_veraltet` should be treated as secondary synthesis or prompt material unless independently verified (source: raw/assets/A_frameworks_templates; source: AGENTS.md).

## Top-Level Areas

- `01_Marketing_Strategie` - ABM, campaign planning, campaign types and flows, marketing plan templates, strategy blueprints, OGSM, and online strategy files (source: raw/assets/A_frameworks_templates/01_Marketing_Strategie).
- `02_Briefing_Templates` - content, creative, email, event, initiative, and media brief templates (source: raw/assets/A_frameworks_templates/02_Briefing_Templates).
- `03_Brand` - brand strategy, design guidelines, HP brand analysis, messaging frameworks, and writing guidelines (source: raw/assets/A_frameworks_templates/03_Brand).
- `04_Persona_und_Audience` - customer journey, persona analysis, and persona template assets (source: raw/assets/A_frameworks_templates/04_Persona_und_Audience).
- `05_Content` - content audits, hook frameworks, and storytelling frameworks (source: raw/assets/A_frameworks_templates/05_Content).
- `06_AI_Prompting` - prompting fundamentals, context engineering, deep research, meta prompting, creative/video prompting, agentic prompting, and reasoning model material (source: raw/assets/A_frameworks_templates/06_AI_Prompting).
- `07_AI_fuer_Marketing` - AI brand/persona, AI briefing templates, marketing prompts, AI strategy frameworks, and AI writing/style material (source: raw/assets/A_frameworks_templates/07_AI_fuer_Marketing).
- `08_Reporting_und_Ops` - Eloqua email, mission templates, presentation templates, and project management files (source: raw/assets/A_frameworks_templates/08_Reporting_und_Ops).
- `09_Beispiele_HP` and `_Archiv_HP_Legacy` - HP examples and legacy reference material for ABM, briefs, media, strategy, project management, and RFP/SOW patterns (source: raw/assets/A_frameworks_templates/09_Beispiele_HP; source: raw/assets/A_frameworks_templates/_Archiv_HP_Legacy).

## Caveats

- This pass cataloged the folder at inventory level; not every Office, PDF, image, audio, video, notebook, archive, or web-download file was deeply extracted (analysis: wiki/_outputs/raw-assets-new-marketing-inventory-2026-06-05.md).
- Some AI and model-labeled files are useful leads, not verified facts (source: raw/assets/A_frameworks_templates; source: AGENTS.md).
- One empty `New folder` and empty `_Archiv_Duplikate` folder are present in the source bundle (source: raw/assets/A_frameworks_templates).
