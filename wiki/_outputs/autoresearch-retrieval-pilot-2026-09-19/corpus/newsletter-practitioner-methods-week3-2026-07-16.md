# Newsletter Practitioner Methods, Week 3

**Summary**: Four explicitly approved practitioner sources add compact methods for agent-facing infrastructure, permissioned knowledge scopes, skill vocabulary, and effortful thinking with AI. They are useful design evidence, not controlled proof of universal outcomes.

---

## Durable methods

- **Agent experience**: Agents benefit from typed, compact interfaces, rapid feedback, persistent state and machine-accessible observability. Deterministic permission boundaries must remain outside model discretion (source: [Modal CTO interview](https://www.latent.space/p/modal2026); vendor interview).
- **Permissioned knowledge scopes**: Personal, team and company knowledge can share one governed layer while remaining separate scopes; cross-boundary promotion should be explicit rather than automatic (source: [The Rise of the AI Brains](https://www.kieranflanagan.io/p/the-rise-of-the-ai-brains-and-why); practitioner synthesis and analysis).
- **Vocabulary transfer through skills**: Skills can operationalize domain terms, examples and constraints so an agent does not interpret expert language generically. Humans retain final judgment where taste and context matter (source: [Skill engineering](https://www.latent.space/p/skill-engineering-design); practitioner interview).
- **Thinking with AI**: Adversarial challenge, structured thinking aloud, source-conscious research and active learning use AI to extend effortful thought instead of replacing it (source: [A Guide to Thinking With AI](https://wondertools.substack.com/p/5-ways-i-use-ai-to-think-better); practitioner method).

## Caveats

- The Modal source is a vendor interview with commercial incentives and unverified scale claims.
- The AI-brain examples are anecdotal and do not establish productivity effects.
- The skill and thinking methods are practitioner heuristics, not controlled evaluations.
- Product recommendations were not promoted as durable knowledge.
