# Minimal Evaluation And Observability Framework For AI Marketing Workflows

**Summary**: Source summary for an AI-generated research document proposing a Markdown-native evaluation and observability layer for bounded AI marketing workflows.

---

This source argues that the main gap for applied AI marketing workflows is not more prompting technique, but a lightweight assurance layer that records evidence lineage, run traces, evaluation outcomes, freshness status, and human approvals before AI outputs are reused or promoted into durable knowledge (source: Minimal Evaluation and Observability Framework for AI Marketing Workflows.docx).

The report frames creative brief assistants, persona research assistants, and brand consistency reviewers as bounded workflows with optional agentic steps rather than fully autonomous agents. It recommends governing them through explicit inputs, evidence checks, quality rubrics, and human approval gates (source: Minimal Evaluation and Observability Framework for AI Marketing Workflows.docx).
