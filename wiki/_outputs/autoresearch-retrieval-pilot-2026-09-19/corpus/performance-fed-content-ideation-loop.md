# Performance-Fed Content Ideation Loop

**Summary**: Use qualified audience and performance evidence to propose better content candidates while preserving human ownership of the idea, interpretation, selection, and final expression.

---

## Trigger

Use when a recurring channel or editorial program needs a replenished idea queue and the team can compare its own past content without violating platform rules or mistaking engagement for causal audience truth.

## Required inputs

- A dated content-audience profile with relevant roles, needs, language, and exclusions.
- Safely exported or owned content history and platform-specific performance fields.
- A definition of what counts as useful performance for the current objective.
- Current research sources, active themes, brand boundaries, and approved claims.
- A human editor who owns selection and can reject attractive but generic patterns.

## Procedure

1. **Set the audience and decision boundary.** Record who the content is for, what job it should perform, and which topics or claims are out of bounds.
2. **Normalize the owned history.** Compare like with like where possible: channel, format, audience, timing, distribution conditions, and the chosen performance signals.
3. **Extract candidate patterns.** Identify recurring hooks, questions, formats, topics, and distribution conditions associated with qualified response. Keep observations separate from explanations.
4. **Add current research.** Gather source-traceable developments and unanswered audience questions; mark freshness, uncertainty, and source quality.
5. **Generate building blocks.** Combine audience needs, qualified patterns, research, owned experience, and brand territory into candidate theses, not finished generic posts.
6. **Apply human selection.** Accept, revise, merge, defer, or reject each candidate based on distinctiveness, evidence, strategic relevance, and the editor's actual point of view.
7. **Queue bounded tests.** For selected ideas, record channel, format, hypothesis, evidence, owner, timing, and the signals that will inform the next decision.
8. **Refresh periodically.** Re-import only permitted evidence, compare new observations with the prior period, and update the queue without rewriting history.

This loop retains the sources' useful combination of audience profile, platform patterns, current research, human selection, queue management, and periodic refresh while rejecting autonomous scraping and virality claims (sources: How I Use This Claude Skill to Grow My LinkedIn Audience.md; Never Run Out of Content Ideas With This AI System.md; Use AI to Craft ‘Slop’ Free Content 1.md).

## Inspectable output

A dated idea queue with source links, audience job, candidate thesis, evidence strength, human disposition, test boundary, owner, and next refresh date, plus a short record of patterns retained or rejected.

## Reuse boundaries

Use only data the team is permitted to access and process. The LinkedIn source explicitly withdrew browser scraping because of platform terms; screenshot or approved export is the retained safe example. Past performance, reach, or engagement does not prove that a topic or format caused results or will repeat them. AI may organize and challenge ideas but does not supply lived experience, original judgment, or publication authority.
