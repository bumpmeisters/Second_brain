# Creative Briefs

**Summary**: Creative briefs translate business and audience insight into creative direction, messaging, proof, deliverables, and desired action.

---

The DBS creative brief template includes fields for date, version, key benefit, insight, and list-style brief inputs (source: DBS_Creative Brief_template.docx).

The customs creative brief examples apply the template to customs-clearance services, post-Brexit compliance, regulations, target decision-makers, logistics solutions, and trade context (source: DBS_Creative Brief_DBS customs.docx).

The HP creative brief examples provide applied creative direction references in `09_Beispiele_HP` (source: raw/assets/09_Beispiele_HP/Creative brief).
