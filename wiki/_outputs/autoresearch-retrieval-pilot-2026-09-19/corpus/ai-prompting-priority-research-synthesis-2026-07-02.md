# AI Prompting Priority Research Synthesis — July 2026

**Summary**: Curated content-level synthesis of 46 priority prompting sources, plus one technically unreadable source, across prompting fundamentals, context engineering, deep research, meta-prompting, creative media, agent skills, and reasoning models.

---

## What this bundle contributes

Across the selected sources, the most reusable pattern is a staged prompt architecture: define the task and audience, supply bounded context, choose an explicit output contract, decompose difficult work, and add a review or verification step. The sources repeatedly present frameworks as scaffolding rather than guarantees; model-specific performance claims remain `needs verification` (source bundle: research/assets/06_AI_Prompting).

The sources also distinguish three related design problems. Prompt engineering shapes a single interaction, context engineering assembles the information environment around the interaction, and agentic or meta-prompting coordinates repeated steps, tools, and evaluation. This distinction is analysis across the bundle and is reflected in [[prompt-engineering-research]], [[context-engineering]], [[meta-prompting]], and [[agentic-prompting]].
