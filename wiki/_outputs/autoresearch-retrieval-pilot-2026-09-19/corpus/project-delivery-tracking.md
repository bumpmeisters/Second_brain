# Project Delivery Tracking

**Summary**: Project delivery tracking uses trackers, timelines, Gantt views, milestones, tasks, and team context to manage campaign or marketing execution.

---

The project-management folder contains delivery trackers, timeline templates, milestone/task spreadsheets, and one XLSB project plan that was cataloged but not deeply extracted (source: raw/assets/08_Reporting_und_Ops/Project_Management).

The delivery tracker files include project team and Gantt/timeline structures (source: Project_delivery_tracker.xlsx; source: Project_delivery_tracker_.xlsx).

The timeline templates provide milestone and task views for planning project schedules (source: Milestone and task project timeline.xlsx; source: Project timeline with milestones.xlsx).

## Capacity-bound multi-sprint programs

For an authorized program with delayed outcomes, augment the static timeline with a versioned sprint contract and cumulative evidence view. Each sprint should record one learning or delivery decision, capacity by role, scope, milestones, owners, dependencies, controllable activities, early observations, commercial outcomes, guardrails, and escalation conditions. Close the sprint with a retrospective and an explicit `continue`, `revise`, `stop`, or `rescope` disposition before committing the next stage (source: Account based content.md; canonical title: *How to plan long term marketing and sales programs*; transcript anchors 3:31-14:10 and 24:42-50:33; analysis: [[long-horizon-marketing-program-sprint-loop]]).

Keep the original baseline and later revisions distinct. Activity completion and intermediate engagement are delivery evidence, not proof of commercial impact or attribution.
