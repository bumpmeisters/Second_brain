# Niche Sponsorship Portfolio Experiment

**Summary**: Test podcast and thought-leader sponsorships as a bounded portfolio using verified audience fit, authentic host use, triangulated evidence, and a fixed renewal decision.

---

## When to use

Use when a narrow audience is distributed across trusted shows or expert communities and direct click attribution will capture only part of the influence path.

## Procedure

1. Define the audience, problem, offer, test period, budget boundary, and decision date.
2. Admit only channels with verified audience fit and credible evidence of authentic host or creator use.
3. Diversify across several relevant channels rather than betting the test on one placement.
4. Provide a valid landing path, tagged links, and an offer that will remain available after publication.
5. Collect UTM and landing activity, self-reported discovery, known-account context, and qualitative sales or participant evidence.
6. Review the portfolio after the fixed period.
7. Stop, replace, repeat, or deepen each relationship based on the combined evidence.

## Output

A channel portfolio, admission rationale, measurement bundle, renewal decision, and evergreen-link maintenance owner.

## Guardrails

The evidence can indicate influence but does not prove pipeline causality. Download benchmarks, prices, lead counts, returns, and named-vendor outcomes are excluded (source: How to plan long term marketing and sales programs.md; mixed practitioner transcript; analysis: P14-C03).
