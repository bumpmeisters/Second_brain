# Global-Core Regional ABM Operating Model

**Summary**: Scale ABM through a shared global core and explicit regional execution patches rather than either full centralization or fragmented local programs.

---

## When to use

Use when several regions run the same account-based motion but differ in relationship context, language, capacity, or market practice.

## Shared core

Standardize:

- account portfolio and qualification rules;
- account-state model and buying-group representation;
- operating cadence and decision rights;
- core measures, reporting, and evidence definitions;
- reusable research, content, and activation patterns.

## Regional patch

1. Embed a regional ABM owner with the relevant sales territory.
2. Record justified local variations in language, relationships, channels, capacity, and culture.
3. Preserve the shared state and evidence model when adapting execution.
4. Compare equivalent account swim lanes across regions.
5. Diagnose whether variation comes from enablement, capacity, process, data, or market context.
6. Update either the global core or the local patch based on repeated evidence.

## Enablement spine

Publish model purpose, inputs, definitions, limitations, owners, and allowed actions. Teach each role its decisions and feedback duties through concise global modules; assign regional office hours for context and exceptions; maintain one versioned resource hub; and deliver recurring evidence-bearing reports inside normal workflows. Feed questions, rejected recommendations, errors, and useful local adaptations into model and playbook review. Evaluate demonstrated use and correction quality rather than training completion alone (source: The early bird wins the sale Achievers elevates timely outreach, collaboration, and pipeline visibility through Demandbase insights.md; body title: *CyberArk achieves 4x higher close rates with an ABX approach powered by Demandbase*; section “Enablement”; vendor case; analysis: P26-C02).

## Output

A common operating backbone, explicit regional exceptions, comparable reporting, and a learning path between global and local teams.

## Guardrails

AI may assist bounded research and preparation but does not replace regional relationship judgment. Cost, speed, conversion, and product claims are excluded (source: How to build a global, AI-native ABM function 2.md; mixed practitioner transcript; analysis: P13-C06).
