# Media Briefs

**Summary**: Media briefs define campaign media objectives, audiences, markets, channels, performance targets, and execution context.

---

The DBS media brief templates and examples provide reusable structures for media planning and campaign context (source: raw/assets/02_Briefing_Templates/Media_Briefs).

The HP media brief folder contains spreadsheet templates, document templates, media education material, and strategic media references (source: raw/assets/09_Beispiele_HP/Media brief HP).

The HP 3M Strategy RTB and MEDIA 101 PDFs are likely useful source material for media strategy and planning education, but their details should be mined separately before making precise claims (source: raw/assets/09_Beispiele_HP/Media brief HP/HP 3M Strategy RTB - 11.03.19.pdf; source: raw/assets/09_Beispiele_HP/Media brief HP/MEDIA 101 Session_CE_18_5.pdf).

## Extraction Caveat

The legacy `.doc` media brief was cataloged but not deeply extracted in this pass (source: MediaBrief_2019_08_Lead_Gen20_V2.doc).
