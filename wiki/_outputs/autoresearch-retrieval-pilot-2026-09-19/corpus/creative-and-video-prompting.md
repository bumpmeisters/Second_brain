# Creative And Video Prompting

**Summary**: Research cluster for creative prompting, image generation, video generation, onboarding guides, and marketing creative playbooks.

---

The creative/video folder contains seven files focused on creative prompting, image generation onboarding, video prompting, and creative marketing playbooks (source: research/assets/06_AI_Prompting/05_Creative_und_Video; analysis: wiki/_outputs/ai-research-ingest-2026-06-03.json).

This material is highly time-sensitive because image and video model behavior, product interfaces, and prompting conventions can change quickly. Any operational guidance should be checked against current tool documentation and local tests before use (source: research/assets/06_AI_Prompting/05_Creative_und_Video; analysis: [[ai-research-validation]]).

## Useful Leads

- Capture creative intent, audience, brand constraints, composition, reference style, and format requirements.
- Save strong tested prompts as examples only after recording the model/tool and date.
- Link creative prompting back to [[brand-system]], [[messaging-frameworks]], and [[storytelling-frameworks]].

## Video-led microsite commissioning

When one approved video should become the narrative spine of a commercial web experience, separate concept ideation, media creation, and site implementation. Lock the media and rights, derive chapters and interaction from observable film stages, state accessibility and technical acceptance criteria, preserve a reconstruction brief, and verify the production build. Use [[video-led-microsite-build-contract]] for the complete reusable method (source: 1. All files - Recently Uploaded · Maker Zero Claude Code, AI.md; sections Prompt 0, Prompt 1, and Prompt 2).

The source is an executable prompt, not evidence of creative quality, accessibility, performance, security, or commercial effectiveness. Its named models, libraries, and browser techniques require current validation.

## Multi-scene animation continuity gate

For a multi-scene animation, make continuity inspectable before spending on animation and again after composition. The accountable human first chooses the audience problem, message angle, and script concept. Break the script into scene beats, review a storyboard grid, and define consistent start and end keyframes for each scene before animating between those reviewed states. Leave enough timing space for the intended narration, then assemble voiceover, clips, and captions and inspect the finished sequence as one artifact (source: 2026-07-21--ZJwchfbRfvE.md; vendor-practitioner tutorial; analysis: P35-W5-C04).

This is a production-control sequence, not evidence that the featured tool, model, or technique produces viral reach, conversion, superior quality, faster work, or lower cost. The presenter builds the recommended product, so current product mechanics and comparative claims require independent verification.

## Agent-assisted video post-production

Keep large raw media local and expose transcript- and timestamp-aware proxies for review. Separate editorial selection from aesthetic revision, collect timestamped human comments, and require explicit approval plus verification of the external publication state before release. This is a bounded post-production control pattern, not evidence for the source's product performance, security, cost, labor-replacement, one-shot-quality, or autonomous-publication claims (source: 2026-08-07--YnmNX3W-zoo.md; practitioner demonstration; analysis: P36-W6R3-C01).

## AI-assisted VFX over live footage

Pre-plan gaze, empty space, timing, posture, and replaceable props before generating VFX over live footage. Provide separate role-labeled appearance references and annotated anchor frames for composition and timing; iterate at reduced cost, inspect continuity and unintended changes, and render production quality only after the direction is accepted. Current tool behavior, pricing, quality, and media rights require separate validation (source: 2026-07-26--BiXWH3QUX1Q.md; practitioner tutorial; analysis: P38-W6R3-C05).
