# Customer Maturity Progression Contract

**Summary**: Translate observed customer capability into shared discovery questions, customer-visible next steps, realistic packaging, and role-specific enablement across the lifecycle.

---

## When to use

Use when a commercial team needs one progression model across SDR, sales, implementation, and customer success without reducing maturity to company size or seller funnel stage.

## Build the contract

1. Define stages through observable technology, process, people, skill, and change-readiness evidence.
2. Gather perspectives from several customer stakeholders.
3. For each stage, record:
   - discovery questions;
   - evidence required to assign the stage;
   - the next customer-visible capability or practice;
   - relevant education and support;
   - realistic packaging and budget horizon;
   - reasons not to advance.
4. Create role-specific guidance for SDR, sales, and customer success while preserving one progression model.
5. Update the stage after adoption evidence, not merely after purchase.
6. Review disagreements and ambiguous assignments with the customer.

## Output

A customer-facing progression map, stage evidence, next-step plan, packaging boundary, and cross-functional enablement guide.

## Guardrails

The model should describe useful progress, not justify larger product packages. Company performance, retention, NPS, and valuation assertions are excluded (source: Bryan Law on Brains, Data, and Distinction.md; practitioner transcript; analysis: P14-C02).
