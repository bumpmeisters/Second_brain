# Reporting and Ops Library

**Summary**: Source summary for `raw/assets/08_Reporting_und_Ops`, covering campaign reporting, Eloqua email QA, mission controlling, C-level presentations, project trackers, and timelines.

---

This folder contains 11 files across email reporting, Eloqua checklists, mission controlling, presentation templates, project tracking, and timeline templates (source: raw/assets/08_Reporting_und_Ops).

The folder supports [[campaign-reporting-and-operations]] through templates for campaign targets, benchmarks, email KPIs, social-media mission control, project delivery, and milestone tracking (source: raw/assets/08_Reporting_und_Ops).

The email report template uses campaign targets such as objective, target audience, expected sends, setup, timing, and KPIs including delivery rate, unique opening rate, clickthrough rate, bookings, event registrations, and form submissions (source: raw/assets/08_Reporting_und_Ops/Eloqua_Email/2023_Email_Report_template.pptx).

The Eloqua checklist summarizes pre-activation checks intended to support deliverability and email statistics (source: raw/assets/08_Reporting_und_Ops/Eloqua_Email/Eloqua Checklist_combined version1.docx).

The C-level presentation template emphasizes audience understanding, clear opening/middle/ending structure, visual simplification, substantiated claims, authenticity, and reducing presentation chaos (source: raw/assets/08_Reporting_und_Ops/Presentation_Templates/20241209_C-Level Presentation template.pdf).

The project-management spreadsheets and timeline templates provide practical operating artifacts for delivery tracking, Gantt/timeline views, milestones, tasks, and project-team context (source: raw/assets/08_Reporting_und_Ops/Project_Management/Project_delivery_tracker.xlsx; source: raw/assets/08_Reporting_und_Ops/Project_Management/Timeplan/Milestone and task project timeline.xlsx).
