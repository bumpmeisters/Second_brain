# Marketing Campaigns Missions and Projects Library

**Summary**: Source summary for the campaigns, missions, and projects section of the marketing working library, covering campaign frameworks, RAG, blog strategy, RevTech, future marketing, competitor analysis, content generation, and campaign execution material.

---

This workstream contains 422 cataloged files under `04_Campaigns_Missions_and_Projects`, making it the largest part of the working library (source: raw/assets/B_MKT_working_library/04_Campaigns_Missions_and_Projects; analysis: wiki/_outputs/raw-assets-new-marketing-inventory-2026-06-05.md).

The folder contains active mission-style project work rather than only reusable templates: AI in marketing, blog strategy, campaign planning, competitor analysis, content generation, country campaigns, future marketing, future publishers, campaign framework work, RAG, RevTech, and Unleash material (source: raw/assets/B_MKT_working_library/04_Campaigns_Missions_and_Projects).

The `Marketing Campaign Framework` area includes campaign-planning issue files, demand generation/lead generation work, social-media analysis spreadsheets, prompt files, transcripts, presentations, and broken-funnel or AI-optimized campaign-funnel material (source: raw/assets/B_MKT_working_library/04_Campaigns_Missions_and_Projects/Missions/Marketing Campaign Framework).

The `RAG` area includes Marketing RAG MVP presentations, final-output folders, O3/Gemini/Genspark variants, and explanatory material on what RAG is and how it could apply to marketing (source: raw/assets/B_MKT_working_library/04_Campaigns_Missions_and_Projects/Missions/RAG).

## Notable Clusters

- Blog strategy and content-authority work, including Gemini, O3, Genspark, Brain Traffic, Prophet, School of Thought, pillar/hub/topic-cluster, and content architecture files (source: raw/assets/B_MKT_working_library/04_Campaigns_Missions_and_Projects/Missions/Blog Stragtegy).
- RevTech work, including modern RevTech stack files, Gemini/O3/Genspark variants, prompts, and web-download assets (source: raw/assets/B_MKT_working_library/04_Campaigns_Missions_and_Projects/Missions/RevTech).
- Future-of-marketing and future-of-publisher research, including AI, zero-click/search-adjacent, media-company, publisher strategy, and community-versus-AI materials (source: raw/assets/B_MKT_working_library/04_Campaigns_Missions_and_Projects/Missions/Future Marketing_Media Company; source: raw/assets/B_MKT_working_library/04_Campaigns_Missions_and_Projects/Missions/Future of publishers).
- Competitor analysis material with planning and example subfolders (source: raw/assets/B_MKT_working_library/04_Campaigns_Missions_and_Projects/Missions/Competitor Analysis).

## Caveats

- Many project files are model-labeled, prompt-labeled, draft/final variants, generated PDFs, or web-download assets; treat them as a project trail until a specific claim is verified (source: raw/assets/B_MKT_working_library/04_Campaigns_Missions_and_Projects; analysis: wiki/_outputs/raw-assets-new-marketing-file-inventory-2026-06-05.csv).
- Audio, video, `.msg`, `.zip`, `.html`, `.download`, and extensionless web assets were cataloged but not deeply extracted in this pass (analysis: wiki/_outputs/raw-assets-new-marketing-inventory-2026-06-05.md).
