# Content Briefs

**Summary**: Content briefs define topic, audience, purpose, structure, and guidance for producing written or web content.

---

The DB Schenker website copywriter briefing emphasizes a user-centric communication stance: less what the company does, more what the user gets (source: DBS_Website_Content_CopywriterBriefing_2022.08.04 (1).pdf).

The Superpath content brief template includes fields such as working title or target keyword, chapter URL, questions, and answers (source: Superpath_Content Brief Template.docx).
