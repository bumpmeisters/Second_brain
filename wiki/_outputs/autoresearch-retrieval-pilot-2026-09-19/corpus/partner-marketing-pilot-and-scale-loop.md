# Partner-Marketing Pilot and Scale Loop

**Summary**: Treat partner marketing as a small, evidence-gated portfolio that progresses from partner enablement to joint activation and partner-led reach, with explicit continuation and exit decisions.

---

## Trigger

Use this loop when a niche or smaller company needs complementary credibility, reach, access, or capability, or when an existing partner portfolio consumes effort without reciprocal evidence.

## Required inputs

- A bounded set of partner candidates.
- A joint customer problem and value hypothesis.
- Marketing, business-development, and sales owners.
- Approved proof, enablement assets, and activation capacity.
- A measurement window and portfolio review cadence.
- Applicable incentive, contract, privacy, and data-sharing policies.

## Loop

1. **Select a few partners.** Compare strategic complementarity, customer overlap, credibility, reach, commitment, and operating fit.
2. **Define joint value and proof.** State the shared customer problem, complementary value, evidence, and memorable activation idea.
3. **Agree the operating charter.** Name owners, roles, service level, incentives, measures, conflict rules, and review date.
4. **Enable to-partner.** Supply concise positioning, proof, qualification guidance, approved assets, and an easy participation path.
5. **Activate with-partner.** Run a bounded joint campaign, event, content, referral, or co-selling test.
6. **Scale through-partner carefully.** Expand partner-led reach only after the partner can represent the joint value accurately and reciprocal evidence exists.
7. **Score the relationship.** Review participation, referrals, partner-sourced and partner-influenced activity, co-sold outcomes, effort, quality, and reciprocity without claiming causal attribution.
8. **Decide.** Scale, remediate, hold, or exit. Retain the evidence and reasons.

## Inspectable output

The loop produces a partner charter, ready-to-use enablement kit, joint activation plan, scorecard, and recorded portfolio decision.

## Reuse boundaries

The interview supports focused selection, to/with/through progression, cross-functional alignment, easy participation, incentives, and evidence-based continuation. Legal, tax, contractual, channel-conflict, privacy, and data-sharing controls are vault governance additions. Partner types have different economics; attendance, revenue, awards, impact, and causal ROI claims are excluded (source: Anne Baker on Partner Marketing in Niche Industries; source file: Ellina Shinnick on How to be a Fearless Leader.md; transcript anchors 16:37-34:09).
