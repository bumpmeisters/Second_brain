# AI Briefing Templates Research

**Summary**: Research cluster for AI-adjacent briefing templates and reusable brief structures.

---

The AI briefing templates folder contains two files: an adapted media brief and a creative brief template (source: research/assets/07_AI_fuer_Marketing/AI_Briefing_Templates; analysis: wiki/_outputs/ai-research-ingest-2026-06-03.json).

These sources should be compared against the existing [[briefing-template-library]] and [[briefing-system]] before being treated as a separate AI briefing standard (source: research/assets/07_AI_fuer_Marketing/AI_Briefing_Templates; claim requires verification).

## Useful Leads

- Use briefing templates as structured context for AI work.
- Keep source evidence, audience, objective, constraints, and output expectations explicit.
- Avoid mixing unverified AI suggestions with approved briefing standards.
