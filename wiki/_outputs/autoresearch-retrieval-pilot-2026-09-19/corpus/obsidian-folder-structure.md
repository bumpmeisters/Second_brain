# Obsidian Folder Structure

**Summary**: The sources recommend simple folder structures, with one workflow separating raw/wiki material and another separating inbox, notes, projects, and resources.

---

The LLM wiki source recommends `raw/`, `wiki/`, and optionally `templates/` for an AI-maintained wiki (source: Karpathy's LLM Wiki - Full Beginner Setup Guide.md).

The Obsidian vault setup source recommends four folders: `00 - Inbox`, `01 - Notes`, `02 - Projects`, and `03 - Resources` (source: The Obsidian Vault Setup That Replaced My $500 Per Month Software Stack.md).

In the Obsidian vault setup, `00 - Inbox` is where unprocessed ideas, tasks, links, quotes, and thoughts land first (source: The Obsidian Vault Setup That Replaced My $500 Per Month Software Stack.md).

In that setup, `01 - Notes` holds processed evergreen ideas, `02 - Projects` holds active projects, and `03 - Resources` holds reference material from other people (source: The Obsidian Vault Setup That Replaced My $500 Per Month Software Stack.md).
