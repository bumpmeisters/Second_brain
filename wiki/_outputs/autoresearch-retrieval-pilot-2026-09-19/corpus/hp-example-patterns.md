# HP Example Patterns

**Summary**: HP example patterns provide applied references for ABM, agency briefs, country strategy, media briefs, creative briefs, strategic briefs, asset tracking, and project management.

---

The HP examples folder is a practical reference library for applied marketing artifacts rather than a single topic source (source: raw/assets/09_Beispiele_HP).

The folder includes ABM examples, agency brief decks, asset tracking, country strategy examples, creative briefs, initiative briefs, media briefs, optimization guides, project management examples, an RFP/SOW, and strategic brief templates (source: raw/assets/09_Beispiele_HP).

These examples should be used as pattern references and compared against DB Schenker-specific brand, audience, and execution needs before direct reuse.
