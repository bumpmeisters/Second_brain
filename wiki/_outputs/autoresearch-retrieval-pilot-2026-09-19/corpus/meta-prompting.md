# Meta Prompting

**Summary**: Research cluster for prompts that create, critique, refactor, or standardize other prompts.

---

The meta prompting folder contains 23 files, including customer analysis meta-prompts, prompt-analysis materials, and system-prompt-inspired prompt design (source: research/assets/06_AI_Prompting/04_Meta_Prompting; analysis: wiki/_outputs/ai-research-ingest-2026-06-03.json).

Meta prompting is useful for turning scattered prompt examples into reusable operating patterns. The validation pass still treats these sources as unverified or partially verifiable until their claims and examples are tested (source: research/assets/06_AI_Prompting/04_Meta_Prompting; analysis: [[ai-research-validation]]).

## Useful Leads

- Use meta prompts to audit unclear prompts for goal, context, constraints, output format, evidence, and evaluation criteria.
- Keep generated prompts close to the source task and expected user workflow.
- Connect prompt design to [[context-engineering]] and [[content-quality]] rather than treating it as standalone wording.

## Open questions
