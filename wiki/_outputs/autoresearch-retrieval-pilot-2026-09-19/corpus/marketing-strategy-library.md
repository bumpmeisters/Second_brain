# Marketing Strategy Library

**Summary**: Source summary for `raw/assets/01_Marketing_Strategie`, a folder of marketing strategy frameworks, campaign flow materials, OGSM templates, ABM material, and country marketing planning assets.

---

This folder contains 16 files: PDFs, PowerPoints, Word documents, and one skipped temporary Office lock file (source: raw/assets/01_Marketing_Strategie).

The strongest recurring themes are [[marketing-orchestration]], [[campaign-types-and-funnel-stages]], [[ogsm]], [[country-marketing-planning]], and account-based marketing (source: raw/assets/01_Marketing_Strategie).

The Heinz Marketing CMO guide frames marketing orchestration as a disciplined approach to how marketing work gets done from planning through execution (source: raw/assets/01_Marketing_Strategie/Campaign_Planning_und_Orchestration/Heinz Marketing/20250724_CMO guide to marketing orchestration.pdf).

The campaign-type materials distinguish brand, demand generation, lead generation, conversion, and nurture campaign roles, including objectives, tactics, metrics, and funnel logic (source: raw/assets/01_Marketing_Strategie/Campaign_Types_und_Flows/2025_campaign types_brnd_DemandDen_LeadGen_Conversion.pptx).

The country marketing process materials position marketing as moving from a service-provider role toward a business-driver role with stronger planning, customer strategy, demand generation, storytelling, and execution alignment (source: raw/assets/01_Marketing_Strategie/Marketing_Strategy_Blueprint/County Marketing Planning Process_Playbook_Presentation 02.01.18111.._.pptx).

The OGSM materials provide a planning structure around objectives, goals, strategies, and measures (source: raw/assets/01_Marketing_Strategie/OGSM/20240408_OGSM_explained.pptx).
