# Focused Talent Recruiting

**Summary**: For scarce, high-impact roles, replace high-volume funnel optimization with a small, relationship-led process grounded in specific evidence and realistic two-way evaluation.

---

## Trigger

Use this workflow when the role materially affects outcomes, the credible candidate universe is small, and transferable capability matters more than keyword or employer matching.

## Required inputs

- Named hiring manager and decision owner.
- Observable success outcomes and transferable capabilities.
- Lawful sourcing, privacy, fairness, accessibility, and record-retention rules.
- Capacity to cultivate relationships before urgency.
- A role-shaped work sample that candidates can evaluate as well as complete.

## Procedure

1. Define success in observable outcomes, working conditions, and transferable capabilities.
2. Map a deliberately small candidate universe using specific evidence-bearing referrals rather than generic endorsements.
3. Record why each person may fit and which evidence remains missing.
4. Build a useful relationship before immediate hiring pressure where timing permits.
5. Run a realistic two-way work sample with explicit evaluation criteria, reasonable effort, and accessibility adjustments.
6. Keep the hiring manager accountable for judgment, relationship quality, and timely decisions.
7. Manage offer acceptance through start explicitly, including unresolved constraints, communication, and withdrawal risk.
8. Record the decision and later compare on-the-job evidence with the original success criteria.

## AI-Kompetenz in Recruiting über überprüfbare Urteile prüfen

Für Marketing-Führungsrollen vorhandene, freigebbare Arbeitsbeispiele und einen begrenzten gemeinsamen Fall nutzen. Nach Entscheidungen, verworfenen Optionen, Ergebnissen und Fehlern fragen. AI-Kompetenz daran prüfen, welche Teile delegiert wurden, welche Fehler erkannt wurden und wie der Kandidat die Qualität beurteilte. Erfolgskriterien, verfügbare Unterstützung und realistische Anlaufzeit gegenseitig klären.

Keine vollständige unbezahlte Jahresstrategie verlangen; Vertraulichkeit, faire Kriterien und angemessener Aufwand gelten weiter. Weder Ähnlichkeit noch rhetorische Sicherheit als Leistungsnachweis verwenden.

## Inspectable output

A bounded candidate map, referral evidence, contact and consent record, two-way work-sample assessment, documented decision, and acceptance-to-start plan.

## Reuse boundaries

This workflow does not replace employment law, HR policy, compensation governance, diversity and fairness review, accessibility, privacy, or qualified recruiting judgment. Do not infer ability from pedigree, similarity, protected characteristics, personal networks, or AI scoring. The source's compensation, conversion, named-company superiority, and universal talent-density claims are excluded (source: 2026-08-09--zegYJ6dhIg4.md; practitioner interview; analysis: P37-W6R3-C03).
