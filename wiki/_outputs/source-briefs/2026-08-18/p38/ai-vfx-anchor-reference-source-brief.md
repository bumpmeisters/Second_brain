# AI VFX Anchor Reference Source Brief

**Summary**: A VFX practitioner demonstrates how source footage, role-labeled references, annotated anchor frames, and planned physical blocking can constrain AI-generated effects.

## Durable pattern

Plan the live footage for the intended transformation: leave space, gaze, timing, posture, or a replaceable prop where the effect belongs. Supply separate visual references for identity, environment, or asset appearance, label each role explicitly, and add one or more annotated anchor frames for composition and timing. Iterate cheaply at reduced resolution, inspect continuity and unintended changes, then render only accepted directions at production quality.
