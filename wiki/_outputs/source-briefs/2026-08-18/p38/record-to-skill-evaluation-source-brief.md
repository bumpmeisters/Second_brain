# Record-to-Skill and Evaluation Source Brief

**Summary**: A practitioner tutorial uses narrated screen demonstrations to derive task skills and evaluator criteria, while warning against automating unstable or unexplained work.

## Durable patterns

Demonstrations should capture why a person acts or declines to act, not merely cursor movement. Use positive and negative examples, decompose the process at stable input/output boundaries, and keep an orchestrator separate from reusable atomic skills. Before scheduling, require a stable and explainable process, manually test the skill on fresh cases, and verify functional behavior rather than appearance alone.

For evaluator construction, translate expert reactions into checkable criteria and require evidence for each verdict. A recorded walkthrough is input to a draft skill or rubric, not proof that the resulting automation is correct.
