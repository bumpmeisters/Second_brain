# Governed Team Skill Sharing Source Brief

**Summary**: A Zapier workshop proposes a simple team lifecycle for candidate, approved-optional, and required skills, backed by a shared editable source rather than isolated personal copies.

## Durable pattern

Separate skill proposal from team authorization; distinguish approved optional practices from required standards; name the reviewer or approving group; keep the maintained instruction in a shared, permissioned source; and let client-specific wrappers point to that source so updates do not depend on one employee's private copy.

## Recommendation

`extended-claim` for `wiki/agent-skill-design.md`. This adds an explicit team adoption and maintenance lifecycle. Google Drive, Claude, Zapier MCP, connector counts, road-map claims, and the exact pointer mechanism remain product-specific and unverified.
