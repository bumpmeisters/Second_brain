# Focused Talent Recruiting Source Brief

**Summary**: Cursor's head of talent presents a narrow, relationship-led recruiting system for roles where a small number of unusually strong candidates matter more than funnel volume.

## Durable pattern

Define success and transferable capabilities before sourcing; map a small candidate universe through specific peer referrals and evidence-bearing company histories; cultivate relationships before a role becomes urgent; use role-shaped work samples for two-way signal; keep the hiring manager accountable for the decision; and treat acceptance-to-start as an explicit pre-boarding phase.

## Recommendation

`new-claim` and proposed reusable practice `wiki/focused-talent-recruiting.md`. It has a recognizable trigger, required inputs, an executable sequence, an inspectable shortlist and decision, and clear limits. Exclude compensation anecdotes, named-company superiority, hiring-volume, conversion, and universal talent-density claims.
