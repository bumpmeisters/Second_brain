# Intelligence Cost Economics Source Brief

**Summary**: A strategy essay argues that model economics should be compared at the level of useful intelligence or completed work rather than list price per token alone.

## Durable pattern

When models differ in correctness, reasoning length, tool use, latency, and serving requirements, token price is not a comparable unit. Evaluate total cost per accepted task outcome under aligned conditions, alongside failure and operational risk.
