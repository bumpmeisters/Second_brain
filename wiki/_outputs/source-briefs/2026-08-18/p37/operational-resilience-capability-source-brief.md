# Operational Resilience and Capability Source Brief

**Summary**: A practitioner case about manufacturing a specialty product connects supplier concentration, tacit process knowledge, dedicated tooling, selective vertical integration, and long-term production resilience.

## Durable pattern

Treat a critical process as a capability, not only a unit-cost purchase. Map single-buyer and single-supplier dependencies, preserve specialist know-how through apprenticeship and documentation, maintain qualified alternatives, and internalize a process only when volume, quality control, continuity, and operating competence justify the fixed commitment.
