# Instruction-Layer Ablation Source Brief

**Summary**: A practitioner tests representative tasks with and without an established instruction layer after a model change.

## Durable pattern

Ablate always-loaded instructions, compare both output quality and adherence to organization-specific conventions, then restore only rules that measurably improve attention, safety, or workflow. Treat on-demand skills and subagents separately.

## Recommendation

`extended-claim` for `wiki/agent-skill-design.md`. Do not infer that all instruction files or capability layers should be deleted.
