# Reverse Benchmarking Marketing Source Brief

**Summary**: Rory Sutherland presents differentiation as finding an important category dimension competitors routinely neglect and making it conspicuously strong.

## Recommendation

`new-claim`, staged as proposed [[reverse-benchmarking-differentiation-workflow]]. The method inventories category norms, identifies an emotionally or experientially important neglected dimension for a defined use case, makes the trade-off explicit, and tests one causal variable or a genuinely different complete concept. Anecdotes, placebo claims, universal effectiveness, and numerical outcomes are excluded.
