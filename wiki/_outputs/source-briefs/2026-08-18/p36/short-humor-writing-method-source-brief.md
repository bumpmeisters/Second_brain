# Short Humor Writing Method Source Brief

**Summary**: An experienced humor editor describes a practical creation sequence that separates observation, divergent generation, escalation, and editing.

## Durable pattern

Start from a specific real observation or opinion, generate many candidate takes, select one precise idea, escalate it, and separate an uninhibited drafting pass from later editorial judgment. For short-form delivery, design hook, context, and closer deliberately.
