# AI Revenue Loops Source Brief

**Summary**: A vendor masterclass describes recurring AI work using human triggers, schedules, events, and goal-directed loops, then connects those patterns to commercial services.

## Review

The taxonomy mixes activation mechanisms with control and termination semantics. `wiki/loop-engineering.md`, `wiki/agentic-systems.md`, and `wiki/agent-evaluation.md` already separate trigger, observable goal, feedback, verifier, state, bounds, escalation, and stop conditions more rigorously.

## Recommendation
