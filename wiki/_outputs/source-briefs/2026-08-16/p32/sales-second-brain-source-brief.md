# Sales Second Brain — Source Brief

**Summary**: A heavily promotional tutorial demonstrates a file-based sales context layer connected to live systems, scheduled routines, skills, and a dashboard. The architecture is already covered more rigorously by the vault's AI operating-system, GTM, and sales-context work and adds no durable knowledge delta.

## What the source covers

- The system separates raw sales software, an AI filtering layer, a file-based sales second brain, a personalized dashboard, and skills or routines that act from the stored context (01:03–03:15).
- The proposed memory includes calls, core commercial documents, deal history, periodic intelligence, campaigns, skills, and templates (05:27–07:17).
- It distinguishes reusable skills, event-triggered routines, scheduled updates, and one-off work performed against the context folder (07:26–10:13).
- It connects CRM, meeting transcripts, proposals, communications, research, and outreach systems; maintains relatively stable commercial context separately from changing deal data; and proposes daily refresh, CRM reconciliation, call scoring, and periodic reporting routines (12:11–20:36).
- A generated dashboard surfaces current calls, priorities, pipeline, documents, and reports and is rebuilt from the context layer (20:37–23:10).

## Critical assessment

The durable architecture repeats `wiki/ai-operating-system.md`, `wiki/personal-ai-cowork-system.md`, and `wiki/ai-native-gtm-operating-model.md`. The vault's existing `second-brain-enabled-agentic-sales-enablement-strategiepapier-2026-08-03.md` is substantially stronger: it separates the commercial core, account and interaction memory, patterns, methods, and evaluation; keeps facts, inference, recommendations, and decisions distinct; limits the first pilot to a Prepare–Debrief–Update loop; and blocks autonomous outreach and unreviewed CRM mutation.

## Caveats and exclusions

- The video repeatedly promotes the creator's accelerator, templates, consulting, setup service, and affiliate-linked tools.
- Demonstrated metrics are explicitly fictional, while workflow and outcome claims are self-reported and unevaluated.
- The automatic German captions appear translated from English and frequently distort product names.
- Exclude productivity, revenue, maintenance, personalization, reliability, completeness, tool-necessity, and commercial-effect claims.
- Broad CRM, email, calendar, transcript, LinkedIn, and web access requires purpose limitation, authorization, privacy, retention, identity, audit, and human-action controls absent from the tutorial.
- A live dashboard must not expose sensitive sales context through an inadequately protected URL or become an ungoverned shadow CRM.

## Proposed disposition

`registered-only`. The source was fully reviewed, but its architecture and routines are redundant with stronger existing coverage and its missing governance makes it unsuitable as a reusable pattern.
