## Approved knowledge delta

`wiki/buyer-question-coverage-checklist.md` now includes this optional audit for commercial explainers or VSLs:

> Make the persuasive sequence inspectable. Explicitly connect the intended audience and problem to useful teaching, the offer, verifiable proof, material objections and alternatives, price or other trade-offs, fit and non-fit, and a low-friction next step. Keep education and selling legible, and do not treat testimonials, engagement, or the sequence itself as causal proof.

This is an `extended-claim`, not a new framework. The existing checklist already governs cost, risk, comparison, proof, fit, balanced review, and next-step coverage; the source adds only a compact ordering and audit lens for the VSL format.
