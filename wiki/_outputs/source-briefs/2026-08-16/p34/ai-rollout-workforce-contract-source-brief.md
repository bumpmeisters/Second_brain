# AI Rollout Workforce Contract — Source Brief

**Summary**: A practitioner argues that AI adoption fails when leaders avoid credible discussion of job impact, begin with an organization-wide mandate, or measure usage rather than changed customer or business outcomes.

## Useful contribution

The proposed durable pattern is a transparent, credible workforce-impact commitment; one bounded use case tied to an outcome; a supportive team-level owner; explicit success and failure criteria; safeguarded systems; and clarity about the human work that remains accountable (source: 2026-08-09--JIGaCPv44QI.md).
