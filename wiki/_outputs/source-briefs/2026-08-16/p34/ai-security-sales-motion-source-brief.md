# AI Security as a Sales Motion — Source Brief

**Summary**: A vendor-practitioner interview argues that security and AI governance can reduce buyer friction when they are represented as verifiable assurance rather than fear or generic compliance language.

## Useful contribution

The useful operating pattern joins buyer security requirements, evidence such as standards and controls, owned remediation, third-party risk, and executive reporting.
