# Sales Skills Automation Source Brief

**Summary**: A promotional walkthrough of eight reusable sales skills spanning call preparation, pipeline review, follow-up, proposals, personalized offer pages, lead generation, social-engager mining, call coaching, and dormant-opportunity review. The workflow inventory is familiar; the source adds no durable control or evidence beyond stronger existing GTM and account-data practices.

## Content reviewed

- Combines CRM, calendar, email, meeting transcripts, public-web research, social scraping, proposal tools, payment links, Slack, and hosted dashboards into reusable skills and scheduled routines.
- Uses account history to prepare calls and prioritize pipeline, creates draft follow-ups and proposals, reviews seller calls, and mines dormant CRM records.
- Builds prospect lists from databases, websites, directories, LinkedIn profiles, and post engagement, then enriches contacts and proposes personalized outreach.

## Caveats

- The presenter sells the skills, courses, consulting, and several affiliated tools. Reported revenue, usage, close, qualification, and productivity claims are self-reported and unevaluated.
- Scraping people who engaged with posts, locating emails, inferring intent, and preparing outreach require lawful data access, purpose limitation, contact policy, consent or legal basis, suppression, and accountable approval. Engagement does not prove purchase intent.
- Automated CRM mutation, deal scoring, proposals, payment links, live pages, and scheduled routines require source lineage, permissions, conflict checks, deterministic validation, rollback, and human authority. The demo does not establish them.
