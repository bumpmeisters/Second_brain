# Influencer Campaign AI Guide Source Brief

**Summary**: A HubSpot promotional walkthrough for finding, contacting, costing, and scoring a creator partnership. Its useful elements—audience fit, a bounded test, first-party performance questions, joint creative shaping, and portfolio learning—already exist in the vault's niche-sponsorship experiment with stronger measurement and attribution boundaries.

## Content reviewed

- Starts with direct platform exploration, then uses an AI assistant to locate contact information, estimate a price range, and draft outreach.
- Suggests checking expected economics against lead value and asking the creator for prior campaign reach, clicks, and offer completion.
- Scores candidates on audience size, fit, and growth, prefers a high-fit growing creator with usable scale, and recommends letting the creator shape the offer and creative for the audience.

## Caveats

- The source promotes HubSpot's book and creator program. Its paid-channel comparisons, Unilever count, price estimates, lead math, audience-size preferences, program spend, and effectiveness claims are not independently established.
- AI-inferred contact details, prices, audience fit, and expected performance require direct verification. Outreach still requires an appropriate contact basis and policy.
- Audience growth and engagement are not proxies for buyer fit, trust, business impact, or causal attribution. A one-creator live search is not a portfolio evaluation.

## Decision recommendation

`registered-only`. [[niche-sponsorship-portfolio-experiment]] already requires verified fit, authentic creator use, a bounded budget and decision window, triangulated evidence, evergreen-link ownership, and an explicit stop/replace/repeat/deepen decision. The source's scorecard does not add enough evidence to change that artifact.
