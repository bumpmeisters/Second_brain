# Enterprise Sales Motion Selection Source Brief

**Summary**: An a16z practitioner discussion proposes a useful initial sales-motion decision: favor a small set of credible lighthouse customers when buyer exposure is high and reference proof travels; favor broader landgrab execution when exposure is lower, an existing budget or workflow makes value easier to demonstrate, and many buyers can adopt independently.

## Durable decision signal

- The core axes are buyer exposure—the operational, regulatory, reputational, or customer-facing downside of a bad purchase—and whether proof from one buyer materially reduces uncertainty for similar buyers (approximately 02:50–04:30).
- High exposure plus portable proof favors a lighthouse hypothesis: work deeply with a small number of credible accounts and use the resulting reference evidence carefully. Existing budget, a replacement workflow, demonstrable economics, and weaker dependence on marquee proof favor a landgrab hypothesis (approximately 11:30–16:30).
- The speakers treat the initial choice as stage-specific. Customer conversations and real willingness to buy should test the hypothesis; a company may later add the other motion as the market, product, vertical, or organization changes (approximately 27:00–32:30 and 37:45–39:30).
- When a proof of concept is needed, constrain it with a fixed end date, agreed scope, and success criteria defined before work begins. Separate product performance from customer configuration and adoption so the trial does not become an open-ended science project (approximately 22:20–25:20).

## Caveats

- The source is an investor-produced discussion featuring portfolio-company examples and explicit investment conflicts. The examples are practitioner anecdotes, not a comparative study.
- “Lighthouse” and “landgrab” are hypotheses, not permanent company categories or guaranteed growth strategies. Mixed and sequential motions are expected.
- Buyer exposure and proof portability must be evidenced for a defined market; a famous logo is not automatically a useful reference.
- Exact account-value thresholds, quota practices, adoption timing, AI-market forecasts, company outcomes, and broad claims about current enterprise demand are excluded.
