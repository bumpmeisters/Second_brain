# Liquid Death Full-Funnel Media Source Brief

**Summary**: Practitioner interview describing a media operating system that maps each funnel stage to one audience job, controllable channel levers, a focused creative role, and a decision-relevant measure. This is more specific than the vault's current campaign-stage and reporting guidance and supports one qualified reusable artifact.

## Durable operating pattern

- Audit spend by funnel stage before reallocating it, and treat platform-reported return as an efficiency signal rather than proof of incremental sales (00:01:58–00:04:55).
- Use new-to-brand share only as a fast triage proxy; when causal effect matters, define a comparison such as a geographic holdout and inspect whether the treated market changes relative to the untreated market (00:04:04–00:08:39).
- Choose a channel for the job and the levers it exposes: awareness requires controllable reach or repetition, consideration requires a path toward a product page, and conversion may happen on a retailer or marketplace rather than the brand site (00:14:48–00:17:16).
- Give each creative one primary job. Awareness creative should make the brand memorable; consideration creative should connect the brand to product benefit and availability; retargeting and lapse messaging should address the next unresolved barrier rather than repeat the same ad (00:18:45–00:23:00).
- Preserve portfolio-level strategic control when a platform optimizer sees only one fragment of the journey, while accepting less channel control where distribution scale and purchase actually occur (00:29:05–00:31:05).

## Caveats

- The interview is a practitioner account hosted by HubSpot and includes promotion for the host's book and products. It does not independently establish that Liquid Death's media system caused revenue or brand growth.
- ROAS, new-to-brand, click-through rate, cost per click, and product-page visits are context-dependent observations. None alone proves incrementality, customer quality, brand memory, or future performance.
- The source's fixed frequency, gain, timing, channel, and early-stage prescriptions are not universal rules. Test design, sample adequacy, seasonality, geographic comparability, retailer effects, and spillover can invalidate a simple holdout.
- The video's title and framing emphasize “weird ads,” but the durable contribution is the stage-role-control-evidence map, not a recommendation to imitate Liquid Death's comedy or creative style.
