# Gemini Spark Sponsored Agent Demo Source Brief

**Summary**: A Google-sponsored demonstration of a current consumer agent across Gmail, Calendar, Docs, Sheets, travel, monitoring, and writing tasks. It illustrates familiar use cases but adds no durable architecture, evidence, or governance beyond existing vault coverage.

## Content reviewed

- Shows scheduled inbox triage, meeting preparation, travel planning, price monitoring, spreadsheet tracking, writing-style instructions, and multi-perspective prompts.
- Recommends checking important results, but otherwise treats broad connected-account access and scheduled actions mainly as convenience features.
- The demonstrated behavior depends on current product capabilities and one creator-run session.

## Caveats

- Sponsorship, current feature availability, integration behavior, prices, and performance are time-sensitive.
- Broad access to private mail, calendar, documents, travel, and health-related context requires purpose limitation, least privilege, reviewable actions, and accountable approval. The demo does not establish those controls.
- A polished demonstration is not outcome evidence and does not authorize recurring execution.

`registered-only`. [[applied-ai-use-cases]], [[agent-security]], [[ai-work-blueprint]], and the vault's automation gates already cover the reusable substance more rigorously.
