# Animated-Ad Production Continuity Source Brief

**Summary**: A vendor-practitioner demonstration of an AI-assisted animated-ad pipeline. Its useful delta is not the named tools or performance claims, but an explicit visual-continuity gate between script and animation.

## Durable workflow signal

- Research the audience/problem and generate several message angles before a human selects the concept.
- Turn the chosen script into scene beats and a storyboard grid so the whole sequence can be judged before animation spend.
- Extract consistent keyframes and animate between reviewed start/end frames, leaving timing room for assembly and voiceover.
- Assemble, caption, and inspect the finished sequence as a separate stage.
