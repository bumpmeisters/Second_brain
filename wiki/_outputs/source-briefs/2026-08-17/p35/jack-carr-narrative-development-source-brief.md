# Jack Carr Narrative Development Source Brief

**Summary**: A long practitioner interview describing a repeatable long-form fiction workflow: premise test, thematic spine, evolving outline, deliberate drafting, continuity control, source-rich observation, specialist review, and separation of writing from business operations.

## Durable workflow signal

- Draft a one-page jacket-style summary and ask whether the project merits the creator's time and the reader's attention.
- Use a concise theme as a guide, not a rule that every passage must state directly.
- Expand into an outline while marking gaps that can be solved during drafting rather than blocking the entire project.
- Preserve series continuity through a character/story bible as complexity grows.
- Collect specific sensory and contextual observations from fieldwork, maps, photographs, period sources, and qualified readers.
- Finish a complete draft before perfection work; then use differentiated beta readers and an accountable editor.
