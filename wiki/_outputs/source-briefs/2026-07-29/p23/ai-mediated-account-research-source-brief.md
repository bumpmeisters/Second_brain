# AI-Mediated Account Research

## Core thesis

A conversational layer can reduce the mechanical burden of assembling account intelligence when it works over authorized internal data, exposes supporting sources, preserves prior research conversations, and translates findings into an inspectable stakeholder brief. It remains a synthesis interface, not an autonomous source of account truth.

## Structured outline

1. Account research required navigating several reports and manually combining intent, engagement, and account information.
2. Repeated stakeholder requests made this analyst-mediated process hard to scale.
3. The AI interface accepts plain-language questions and returns summaries, tables, visualizations, recommendations, and supporting data.
4. Conversations can be saved and revisited for recurring questions.
5. The intended output is understandable business context for stakeholder discussion.

## Reusable mechanisms

- Start from a named account question and decision rather than open-ended chat.
- Restrict retrieval to permitted, current account sources.
- Require each material statement and recommendation to expose its source lineage.
- Preserve the question, response, evidence set, corrections, and decision as a reusable research thread.
- Separate observed facts, inferred account state, recommendation, and unresolved evidence gap.
- Have an accountable person verify the brief before consequential targeting or outreach.

## Lean analysis

The vault already requires verified context, provenance, a stable evidence core, tailored inserts, and human review in the account-specific evidence-brief workflow. The source materially sharpens the research stage by describing a persistent conversational workbench: source-visible responses and saved research history make recurring questions easier to inspect and continue. This should extend the existing artifact rather than create a product-specific workflow.

## Caveats

The customer is anonymous and the story is authored by Demandbase. No evaluation of factual accuracy, completeness, recommendation quality, permissions, hallucination, or comparison methodology is reported. Intent and engagement data do not prove a buying decision. Time-saving claims, stakeholder reactions, opportunity discovery, and business-impact claims are excluded.
