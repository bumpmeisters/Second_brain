# Randstad ABM Evolution Case

## Core thesis

Despite its filename, the clipping contains a short Demandbase description of Randstad Enterprise's multi-year move from lead-focused marketing toward an account-based model supported by integrated data, dynamic targeting, engagement scoring, shared objectives, stakeholder buy-in, and test-and-learn iteration.

## Structured outline

1. Randstad began redefining its ABM approach eight years before the case description.
2. Early challenges included leaving lead-centric practices and aligning sales and marketing.
3. Demandbase is credited with helping address data integration and campaign scalability.
4. The article names intent data, dynamic targeting, engagement scoring, Salesforce integration, and shared objectives.
5. It characterizes Demandbase as both platform and strategic partner.
6. Long-term success is attributed generally to data-driven decisions, internal buy-in, flexibility, and test-and-learn behavior.

## Reusable mechanisms

The named mechanisms are directionally sensible: align teams around shared goals, integrate account data into existing systems, begin with bounded tests, and adapt the operating model over time. However, the capture does not explain account selection, data admission, cadence, roles, decision rights, experiment design, outputs, or review thresholds.

## Caveats

This is Demandbase-authored marketing copy rather than an auditable customer evaluation. “Improved campaign performance,” efficiency, scalability, proactive campaigns, innovation, and long-term success are asserted without definitions, denominators, timelines, comparison groups, or contrary evidence. Intent data is not purchase intent, engagement scores are not buyer truth, and alignment cannot be attributed to a platform from this description.
