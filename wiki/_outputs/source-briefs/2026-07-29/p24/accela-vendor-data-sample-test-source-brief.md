# Accela Vendor-Data Sample Test

## Core thesis

Despite its WIKA filename, the clipping describes Accela's Demandbase selection. The durable mechanism is a pre-purchase data test: give candidate providers a sample of known accounts, compare returned intelligence with CRM and account-team knowledge, inspect discrepancies and genuinely novel information, and judge whether the output supports a bounded sales decision.

## Structured outline

1. Accela wanted earlier account intelligence in public-sector buying cycles.
2. Its field-marketing leader ran a months-long vendor search.
3. Each vendor received a sample set of Accela accounts.
4. Returned intelligence was compared with historical CRM knowledge.
5. The reviewer checked both consistency with known facts and novel actionable insight.
6. Availability, delay, support, and integration into Salesforce, Marketo, and Outreach also influenced selection.
7. The remainder presents usage anecdotes and pipeline, reach, webinar, and relationship outcomes.

## Reusable mechanisms

- Define the intended downstream decision and acceptable error before the test.
- Use a representative sample containing known positives, negatives, ambiguous records, and important edge cases.
- Freeze the sample and request comparable outputs from each provider.
- Compare identity, hierarchy, attributes, freshness, provenance, confidence, missingness, and contradictions with trusted internal evidence.
- Separate “matches current knowledge” from “adds novel, verifiable, actionable information.”
- Record false positives, false negatives, unresolved cases, restrictions, owner, and retest conditions.
- Evaluate workflow delivery and support separately from data validity.

## Lean analysis

[[account-data-admission-gate]] already requires testing a sample against human account knowledge. The Accela case supplies concrete corroborating evidence for that gate and sharpens what the sample review should inspect. It does not warrant a new artifact; the safest promotion is a qualified implementation example plus clearer sample-design language on the existing gate.

## Caveats

The case is authored by the selected vendor and does not name losing providers, sample composition, error rates, scoring rubric, test independence, or contrary findings. Internal CRM knowledge may itself be incomplete or stale. Reported pipeline, reach, registration, relationship, competitive-intent, adoption, and “first to the conversation” claims remain excluded.
