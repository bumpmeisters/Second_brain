# PageUp ABM Intelligence-Layer Case

## Core thesis

Demandbase presents PageUp's implementation as a central intelligence layer for global ABM: executive sponsorship, shared data, explainable account qualification, configurable workflows, regional activation, seller alerts, dashboards, advertising, and multi-stakeholder coverage.

## Structured outline

1. PageUp sought global expansion across markets with different awareness levels.
2. Senior leadership positioned ABM as a company-wide growth strategy.
3. The company compared Demandbase and 6sense on transparency, configurability, and partnership.
4. Sellers were meant to see why an account qualified and how signals were derived.
5. Advertising and sales intelligence were rolled out across regions.
6. Slack, email, and Salesforce delivered account context into daily workflows.
7. The article attributes adoption, opportunities, pipeline, deals, engagement, and benchmark performance to the platform.

## Reusable mechanisms

The useful mechanisms are explainability at the point of action, shared account context, global-core and local execution, workflow delivery, leadership sponsorship, and buying-group coverage. Each is already represented in [[gtm-signals-and-contextual-intelligence]], [[contextual-next-best-action-loop]], [[global-core-regional-abm-operating-model]], [[abm-sales-marketing-operating-contract]], and [[account-data-admission-gate]].

## Lean analysis

The case adds no independently inspectable method beyond those existing practices. Its most promising idea—show users why an account was qualified—is already a durable vault requirement: a priority output should expose observations, roles, topics, recency, breadth, uncertainty, and why a recommended action fits. Additional vendor testimony would not materially strengthen that rule.

Its “central source of truth” language also needs qualification. A shared interface can coordinate work, but it does not automatically reconcile source conflicts, permissions, historical definitions, model changes, or local knowledge. Those responsibilities remain with the operating model and named data owners.

## Caveats

Demandbase authored the case, is the selected provider, and reports the results. The article does not expose model logic, error rates, alternative explanations, campaign cohorts, benchmark sources, incremental lift, seller-quality outcomes, privacy review, or contrary cases. Impression volume does not prove influence; engagement does not establish intent; opportunities and pipeline do not establish attribution. All numerical outcomes, award references, product superiority, rapid-onboarding, ROI, JourneyIQ, and causal claims remain excluded.
