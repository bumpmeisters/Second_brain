# Verified Multi-Agent Build Orchestration — Source Brief

**Summary**: A practitioner reports a website build with a lead model, worker agents, and separate verifier jobs. The durable delta is role-independent, artifact-grounded verification plus a specification-based path for challenging a faulty verifier—not the source's cost or model-superiority claims.

## Useful evidence

- Workers were trialled on a bounded task before broader assignment, and implementation was decomposed into checked tasks (03:31–05:14).
- Verifiers ignored producer reports and inspected actual artifacts, catching quotation errors and harmful shortcuts (07:11–09:58).
- A controller-authored defect was caught, while an incorrect verifier rejection was challenged against the underlying specification and reversed (09:59–11:24).
- Browser checks, protected source passages, accessibility criteria, and final human review illustrate several acceptance surfaces (12:45–15:47).
