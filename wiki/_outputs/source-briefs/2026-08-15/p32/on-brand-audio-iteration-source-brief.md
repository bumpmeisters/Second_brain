# On-Brand Audio Iteration — Source Brief

**Summary**: A Google Workspace tutorial demonstrates a structured route from a plain-language creative brief to several audio directions, generation, critique, and iterative regeneration.

## Useful evidence

- Begin with project, audience, emotional tone, tempo, and instruments rather than an underspecified “make music” request (00:42–01:42).
- Ask for several deliberately different directions and state what to avoid (01:42–02:02).
- Select one direction, generate it, then review tone and the audience impact of the opening seconds (02:04–02:38).
- Feed larger directional changes back into the brief and make narrower revisions in the generation tool; explicit negative prompts can remove unwanted elements (02:38–03:43).

## Caveats

- The source is a product tutorial, not evidence that the workflow improves campaign performance or brand distinctiveness.
- “High-quality” and royalty-free usage claims were not independently verified.
- Tool names and capabilities are time-sensitive.
