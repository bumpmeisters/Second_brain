# Account-State Service Levels for Sales

## Core thesis

Despite its filename, the capture is a short promotional description rather than a transcript. It distinguishes an “Active Focus” queue from “Future Pipeline” and assigns different work and measures to each: active accounts receive research, buying-group mapping, engagement, content, and offers; future accounts receive blind-spot research, progressive profiling, relationship building, and awareness work.

## Structured outline

1. The author rejects high-volume, automated SDR outreach measured by leads and appointments.
2. A proposed account-based sales role works jointly with marketing and subject-matter experts.
3. Qualified accounts are divided into Active Focus and Future Pipeline.
4. Active Focus emphasizes current account research, committee coverage, engagement, content, and activation.
5. Future Pipeline emphasizes information gaps, progressive profiling, relationships, and awareness.
6. Measures differ by state; the article then presents unsupported outcome and inevitability claims.

## Reusable mechanisms

- Define account states with observable entry, exit, regression, and unknown conditions.
- Assign a service level to each state: required research, buying-group coverage, allowed actions, owner, cadence, and capacity.
- Use commercial progression measures only where opportunity evidence justifies them.
- For future accounts, measure evidence quality, relationship and role coverage, learning, and useful engagement without pretending they are sales-ready.
- Review transitions jointly and preserve the evidence behind a state change.
- Keep automation inside approved actions and prevent premature sales sequences.

## Lean analysis

The three-state logic already exists in [[gtm-signals-and-contextual-intelligence]] and the July ABM bundle. This clipping nevertheless adds a useful operational extension: every account state should carry an explicit service level and state-appropriate measurement contract, preventing Future Pipeline from being managed like an active opportunity. The change fits the existing operating contract; a second standalone artifact would duplicate it.

## Caveats

The capture contains no transcript or timestamps and provides no definitions for qualification, relationship, engagement, or pipeline. It is practitioner-promotional material linked to courses and community offerings. The old-SDR diagnosis, automation and layoff predictions, account-to-pipeline percentages, universal role redesign, and causal claims remain excluded. Progressive profiling must respect consent, privacy, necessity, retention, and truthful identity.
