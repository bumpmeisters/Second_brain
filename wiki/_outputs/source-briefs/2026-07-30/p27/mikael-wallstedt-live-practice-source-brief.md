# Mikael Wällstedt Live-Practice Testimonial

## Core thesis

The three-minute testimonial contrasts passive knowledge acquisition with live, action-oriented practice. Mikael Wällstedt says a scheduled bootcamp helped him repeatedly apply a LinkedIn and ABM framework, including posting, commenting, target definition, and consistent participation. He attributes awareness, trust, leads, and faster learning to the program.

## Structured outline

1. The participant had used LinkedIn only at a basic level and wanted a practical start.
2. He distinguishes reading or recorded courses from training an ability.
3. Live sessions, a Monday/Thursday rhythm, and work between sessions made the program feasible.
4. Repeated practice allegedly improved speed and consistency.
5. He says clearer targeting and commenting complemented posting.
6. The testimonial reports opportunities, a likely deal, and an inbound lead.
