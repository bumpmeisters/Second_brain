# SEI Decentralized ABM Rollout Case

## Core thesis

Despite its GoCardless filename, the clipping describes SEI Services preparing an ABM rollout across geographically independent locations without a centralized sales team. A vendor-led discovery, maturity assessment, prioritized roadmap, and joint workshop with an agency partner produced a proposed standardized pilot.

## Structured outline

1. SEI locations operated independently with multiple revenue-generating teams.
2. The organization wanted a coherent ABM approach but lacked a common rollout path.
3. Demandbase Professional Services conducted discovery into the local structure.
4. A maturity assessment prioritized recommendations and produced a roadmap.
5. SEI, Demandbase, and an agency partner held a collaborative workshop.
6. The article describes a pilot framework and standardization direction, not observed rollout evidence.

## Reusable mechanisms

- Diagnose local structures and decision rights before standardizing.
- Map capability, data, ownership, and operating constraints.
- Prioritize recommendations rather than launching a full redesign.
- Bring internal and external delivery partners into one decision workshop.
- Test a shared playbook through a bounded pilot before broader rollout.
- Preserve justified local differences and review them as evidence.
