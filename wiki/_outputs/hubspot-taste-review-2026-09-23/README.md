# Loop Marketing Book Plugin

Official AI companion to **Loop: Outlearn. Outmarket. Outgrow.** by Kipp Bodnar and Kieran Flanagan.

This plugin turns the book's core frameworks into live, personalized AI tools. Each skill starts by learning your business — then puts the Loop to work for you specifically.

## Skills Included

| Skill | What it does | Book stage |
|-------|-------------|------------|
| **Taste Profile** | Builds your Customer Taste + Brand Taste context document | Express |
| **Buyer Sim** | Creates an AI simulation of your ideal customer | Express |
| **Tailor** | Multiplies one piece of content across channels | Tailor |
| **Prompt Coach** | Fixes prompts that aren't producing what you want | Universal |
| **4 Rs Scorecard** | Builds your personalized Loop Marketing dashboard | Evolve |

## How to use

Each skill opens with a few quick questions about your business — answer them and the skill personalizes everything it produces to your specific situation. You can pick from options or type your own answer at any point.
