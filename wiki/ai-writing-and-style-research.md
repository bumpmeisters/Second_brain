# AI Writing And Style Research

**Summary**: AI writing guidance should separate durable editorial judgment from model-specific prompting advice and review drafts against the target genre, audience, and communicative function.

---

The research cluster contains AI writing guides, audience style guidance, LLM writing frameworks, and related PDF/DOCX materials. Much of that material is AI-generated or time-sensitive, so it remains a source of leads rather than verified rules (source: research/assets/07_AI_fuer_Marketing/AI_Writing_und_Style; analysis: [[ai-research-validation]]).

## Genre alignment is a review requirement

A 2025 PNAS study compared human text with continuations produced by GPT-4o, GPT-4o Mini, and base and instruction-tuned Llama 3 variants across several genres. The instruction-tuned models showed systematic grammatical and rhetorical differences, including greater use of nominalizations, present-participial clauses, phrasal coordination, and recurring vocabulary. The authors characterize the resulting tendency as informationally dense, noun-heavy, and less responsive to human genre variation (source: Do LLMs write like humans Variation in grammatical and rhetorical styles.md).

The operational implication is narrower than AI detection: review a draft against the actual genre, audience, purpose, and situational conventions. A grammatically correct answer can still be awkward, confusing, unpersuasive, or inauthentic for the intended document. Expert users can revise against genre knowledge; less experienced writers may need explicit examples and guided review so automation does not replace the learning required to recognize an effective report, summary, argument, or message (source: Do LLMs write like humans Variation in grammatical and rhetorical styles.md; analysis: P29-W2-C10).

## Practical review questions

- Does the draft behave like the intended genre rather than like a generic answer?
- Does its information density fit the reader and decision context?
- Are abstractions and nominalizations obscuring actors, actions, or responsibility?
- Does it reproduce conspicuous stock vocabulary or sentence patterns across unrelated sections?
- Are stance, uncertainty, interaction, and persuasion appropriate to the purpose?
- Can a qualified human explain why the final form works for this audience?

These questions complement [[content-quality]] and [[writing-guidelines]]. They are not a classifier and should not be used to infer authorship from isolated stylistic traits.

## Evidence limits

The primary study covers a bounded set of English-language corpora and model versions. Its within-corpus classifiers performed well, but generalization to another model and register was weaker. The findings do not establish a timeless signature of AI writing, apply automatically to current models or other languages, or prove that instruction tuning always reduces writing quality. Model-specific advice in the broader research folder still requires current verification.

## Open questions

- Which target genres in the vault have enough qualified human examples for comparative review?
- Which linguistic checks improve revision without turning style into a mechanical detector?
- Which existing AI-writing guides remain valid after model and workflow changes?
