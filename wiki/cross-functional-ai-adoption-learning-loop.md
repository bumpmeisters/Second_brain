# Cross-Functional AI Adoption Learning Loop

**Summary**: Govern AI adoption as a continuing learning system involving operators, leaders, legal, security, data, and workflow owners.

---

## When to use

Use when AI experimentation is spreading across functions and policy, practical support, and shared learning are becoming disconnected.

## Procedure

1. Convene interested operators with accountable legal, security, data, and workflow owners.
2. Define usable policy for data, tools, permissions, disclosure, review, and prohibited actions.
3. Ask leaders to participate in real bounded workflows rather than only sponsor training.
4. Select small experiments tied to specific jobs and observable outcomes.
5. Record failures, exceptions, corrections, and lessons in a shared learning register.
6. Reward useful disclosure of failed experiments and safe stopping decisions.
7. Update policy, support, workflow design, and training from the accumulated evidence.

## Output

A governed experiment portfolio, usable policy, named owners, learning register, and recurring review cadence.

## Guardrails

This practice does not establish legal compliance or a universal return on investment. Regional, political, workforce, and commercial claims from the source remain commentary (source: Why generative AI demands new thinking, not just new tools  OnBase podcast.md; mixed practitioner transcript; analysis: P12-C06).
