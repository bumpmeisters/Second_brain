# Cross-Functional AI Adoption Learning Loop

**Summary**: Govern AI adoption as a continuing learning system involving operators, leaders, legal, security, data, and workflow owners.

---

## When to use

Use when AI experimentation is spreading across functions and policy, practical support, and shared learning are becoming disconnected.

## Procedure

1. Choose one consequential domain and define the future operating choice before launching broad training.
2. Install structural anchors in budgets, governance, incentives, ownership, and recurring review.
3. Convene interested operators with accountable legal, security, data, and workflow owners.
4. Define usable policy for data, tools, permissions, disclosure, review, and prohibited actions.
5. Ask leaders to participate in real bounded workflows rather than only sponsor training.
6. Select small experiments tied to specific jobs and observable outcomes.
7. Record failures, exceptions, corrections, and lessons in a shared learning register.
8. Reward useful disclosure of failed experiments and safe stopping decisions.
9. Scale onboarding, examples, training, and behavioral support only after the structural anchors can absorb the learning.
10. Update policy, support, workflow design, and training from the accumulated evidence.

## AI-Nutzen und zusätzliche Mitarbeiterarbeit gemeinsam prüfen

Im Experimentregister neben dem Kundenergebnis auch zusätzliche Arbeit der beteiligten Menschen dokumentieren: Kontrolle, Nacharbeit, Ausnahmen, Eskalationen und Koordination. Bei scheinbar schnellerem Output prüfen, wohin Arbeit verlagert wurde. Betroffene Operatoren in die Neugestaltung einbeziehen und das tatsächliche Arbeitsdesign statt nur Toolnutzung bewerten.

Bovas Beitrag ist ein fachlicher Standpunkt; die erwähnte Forschung wurde hier nicht primär geprüft. Keine behaupteten Effizienzgewinne oder allgemeinen Aussagen über Beschäftigung übernehmen.

## Output

A governed experiment portfolio, usable policy, named owners, learning register, and recurring review cadence.

## Guardrails

This practice does not establish legal compliance or a universal return on investment. Regional, political, workforce, commercial, framework-superiority, market, forecast, and transformation-outcome claims from the sources remain excluded (source: Why generative AI demands new thinking, not just new tools  OnBase podcast.md; mixed practitioner transcript; analysis: P12-C06; source: 2026-07-29--UarCP7KcKJU.md; vendor webinar; analysis: P40-W6R3-C01).
