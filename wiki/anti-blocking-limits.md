# Anti Blocking Limits

**Summary**: The comments on the scraping tools post warn that scraper libraries do not fully solve blocking because production blocking also depends on network and fingerprint factors.

**Sources**: raw/Clippings/Post by @cyrilXBT on X.md.

**Last updated**: 2026-05-30

---

One comment says Crawl4AI, Firecrawl, and Crawlee are strong for AI pipelines but serious volume still needs residential proxies and smart rotation (source: Post by @cyrilXBT on X.md).

Another comment says the listed tools solve the rendering layer rather than the network layer, and that production bans can depend on IP reputation and TLS fingerprinting (source: Post by @cyrilXBT on X.md).

A third comment says scrapers are not what determines blocking and argues that IPs are central to whether requests get blocked (source: Post by @cyrilXBT on X.md).

These comments contradict or qualify the post's headline claim that the repositories let users scrape any website without getting blocked (source: Post by @cyrilXBT on X.md).
