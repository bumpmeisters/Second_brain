# Digital maturity in marketing and sales: 2020, 2021, and 2023 studies

**Review scope:** PDF pages: 2020: 10–14, 46–48, 51; 2021: 6–8, 18, 23, 27; 2023: 4, 6, 8, 20–22, 29. Page inventories inspected for all three; selected-page content review, not exhaustive reading. Chart layout/visuals not independently verified.

## Findings and provenance

The series organizes marketing/sales digital maturity around capabilities, processes, instruments, and performance. Its practical value is connecting tools to process, customer needs, organizational ability, and measured outcomes rather than counting software. (source: 2020elstedigiindexmarketingvertrieb.pdf, pp. 10–14; Digitalisierungsindex_2021_final.pdf, pp. 6–8; Studie_Digitalisierungsindex_2023_BF_sec.pdf, pp. 6–8)

The 2020 report uses 2019 data: 136 marketing/sales responses, 15 owner interviews, and 25 usable procurement questionnaires. The 2021 study describes summer 2021 collection and reports a chart sample of 177. The 2023 report uses autumn 2022 fieldwork and reports 349 responses. These are historical repeated surveys; a stable longitudinal panel is not established. The series reports overall indices of .49, .53, and .57, but changing samples prevent a clean causal trend claim. (source: 2020elstedigiindexmarketingvertrieb.pdf, pp. 46–48, 51; Digitalisierungsindex_2021_final.pdf, pp. 18, 27; Studie_Digitalisierungsindex_2023_BF_sec.pdf, pp. 4, 8, 29)

The 2023 discussion explicitly permits reverse causality: stronger profits can fund digital investment. Its five success-factor themes include customer journey, skills, goals, change, and individual customer needs. Association is not proof that a higher index causes profitability. Small procurement samples and supplier/customer perspective differences further limit generalization. (source: Studie_Digitalisierungsindex_2023_BF_sec.pdf, pp. 20–22; 2020elstedigiindexmarketingvertrieb.pdf, pp. 46–48)

## Proposed use

P58-C07 proposes a **digital marketing and sales maturity diagnostic**: gather evidence in the four dimensions, expose mismatches, and choose an owned improvement with a test. This is a qualitative adaptation, not a validated new score, company ranking, or current AI benchmark. The existing [[customer-maturity-progression-contract]] addresses customer progression; this diagnostic addresses an organization's marketing/sales operating capability. No canonical diagnostic has been created yet.
