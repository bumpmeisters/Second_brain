# Buyer Question Coverage Checklist

**Summary**: Review buyer-facing content against the difficult questions needed for an informed decision, including reasons not to buy.

---

## When to use

Use during content planning, audit, briefing, or pre-publication review for consequential B2B decisions.

## Coverage check

- **Cost**: Are cost drivers, effort, and likely trade-offs explained?
- **Risks**: Are failure modes, dependencies, and implementation risks visible?
- **Comparisons**: Can buyers understand meaningful alternatives and decision criteria?
- **Balanced review**: Are strengths and limitations described without disguised promotion?
- **Top options**: Does the content help the buyer build or narrow a credible shortlist?
- **Fit**: Are the contexts in which the offer works well explicit?
- **Non-fit**: Are disqualifying conditions and better alternatives stated?
- **Proof**: Are factual claims connected to readable, current evidence?
- **Category value**: Can the buying group understand why this category or change matters before comparing vendors?
- **Internal criteria**: Are the criteria and trade-offs that the champion must explain internally explicit?
- **Role-specific validation**: Can finance, procurement, legal, users, and other validators answer their material questions?
- **Champion evidence**: Is there a concise, credible evidence set that can be reused in the buyer's internal decision?
- **Next step**: Can the buyer continue evaluation without unnecessary friction?

## Commercial explainer sequence

For a commercial explainer or Video Sales Letter, make the persuasive sequence inspectable. Explicitly connect the intended audience and problem to useful teaching, the offer, verifiable proof, material objections and alternatives, price or other trade-offs, fit and non-fit, and a low-friction next step. Keep education and selling legible, and do not treat testimonials, engagement, or the sequence itself as causal proof (source: 2026-06-16--xGDgK1LpTwU.md; vendor walkthrough; analysis: P32-W8-C01).

## Output

A documented coverage gap list that can become a content brief, refresh plan, comparison page, or sales-enablement task.

## Guardrails

The checklist does not imply that every buyer uses the same questions or that complete coverage causes commercial performance. Complex buying is an internal negotiation among roles whose questions, power, and evidence needs may change; more information can also reduce confidence when it is irrelevant or difficult to verify (source: Dave Gerhardt on Marketing Leadership and Team Building.md; practitioner transcript; analysis: P11-C04; source: How to create awareness among strategic accounts 2.md; transcript anchors 7:19-14:29 and 25:13-31:44; mixed panel).
