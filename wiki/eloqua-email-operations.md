# Eloqua Email Operations

**Summary**: Eloqua email operations include campaign setup, reporting templates, deliverability checks, and email-performance metrics.

---

The Eloqua folder contains reporting templates and an activation checklist for campaigns (source: raw/assets/08_Reporting_und_Ops/Eloqua_Email).

The checklist says it summarizes points to check before activating an Eloqua campaign to ensure good deliverability and email statistics (source: Eloqua Checklist_combined version1.docx).

The email report template includes benchmark and target fields for delivery rate, unique opening rate, clickthrough rate, bookings, event registrations, and form submissions (source: 2023_Email_Report_template.pptx).
