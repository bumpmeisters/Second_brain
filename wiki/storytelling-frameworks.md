## Customer story within a stable promise

Start the narrative from the customer's existing situation, tension, and desired progress rather than making the brand the hero. Calibrate the role of the story to decision mode: before active evaluation it can connect the stable promise to a recognizable situation; during comparison it should help make evidenced differences and decision criteria understandable. Vary examples and expression without silently replacing the core positioning (source: Bryan Law on Brains, Data, and Distinction; source file: Zarina Stanford on Mission-Driven Authentic Leadership.md; transcript anchors 11:15-21:05; analysis: [[buying-situation-message-calibration]]).

The source offers a practitioner heuristic. Its neuroscience, emotion-effect, memory, and buyer-percentage claims are not treated as established facts.
