# Creative Prompting for Marketing

**Summary**: A human-led method for using AI to expand, combine, evaluate, and execute marketing ideas without outsourcing strategic taste or converging on generic AI patterns.

---

The central principle is **human first, AI second, human last**. The human contributes observation, tension, point of view, taste, and final judgment. AI contributes rapid exploration, recombination, variation, and criticism (source: 2026-06-23-creative-prompting-frameworks-for-marketing.md).

## Creative Marketing Loop

1. **Human seed** — Record a real observation, audience tension, point of view, emotional target, objective, and three rough ideas.
2. **Expand the map** — Use SCAMPER, First Principles, Generic Parts, counterfactuals, semantic territories, and stakeholder perspectives without judging ideas.
3. **Force collisions** — Blend the problem with distant industries, metaphors, rituals, formats, or cultural domains.
4. **Select strategically** — Judge originality together with audience truth, objective fit, brand distinctiveness, proof, emotional power, feasibility, and extensibility.
5. **Shape the narrative** — Use PAS, BAB, SCQA, AIDA, ERA, STAR, Challenger-style reframing, or a hook pattern according to the communication job.
6. **Red-team and test** — Check facts, brand voice, clichés, emotional response, risk, and testability.

The framework is an original composite. It adapts the vault's creative playbook and marketing templates and incorporates current human–AI creativity research; it is not an official framework attributed to any single external author (source: 2026-06-23-creative-prompting-frameworks-for-marketing.md).

## Why sequencing matters

Using AI before independent human ideation may reduce originality, ownership, and creative self-efficacy. AI assistance can improve individual outputs while making different people's work more similar. Separate generation from evaluation and create independent semantic or persona lanes before merging results (source: [Qin et al., 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.06197); [Doshi & Hauser](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.00506); [Anderson et al., 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01536)).

## Brief-test-iterate loop for campaign audio

Treat AI-generated campaign audio as a creative iteration task. Start with a brief that names the project, audience, intended emotional tone, tempo, instruments, and explicit exclusions. Generate several deliberately different directions, choose one, listen for whether the opening seconds create the intended audience response, then feed broad changes back into the brief and narrow changes into the next generation step (source: 2026-07-21--VnVpfOTYP0E.md).

This is a vendor tutorial and a workflow example, not proof of audio quality, brand distinctiveness, campaign performance, or usage rights. Verify licensing and royalty conditions for the exact tool, account, output, and intended use before publication.
