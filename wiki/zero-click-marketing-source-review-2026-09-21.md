# Zero-click marketing: audience fit and measurement limits

**Review scope:** Description and selected substantive transcript passages across 1:03–32:15; not exhaustive transcript or video review.

## Findings

Amanda Natividad recommends moving from a channel choice to the specific source that influences an audience, then to a suitable tactic. Native content should deliver useful value on its own. This extends content planning with an explicit channel → influence source → tactic decision and an outcome hypothesis. (source: Zero-Click Marketing How to Win When Google Stops Sending Traffic.md)

The discussion contains a useful disagreement: the guest discounts lost low-quality traffic, while the host at about 12:56 says low-intent volume did generate demand. Preserve that tension; do not infer that traffic never matters or replace a useful search mix automatically. Influence, engagement, attributed clicks, and incremental business impact are different observations. The transcript's 68%, 232/1,000, and large traffic totals remain unchecked. (source: same clipping, selected passages above)
