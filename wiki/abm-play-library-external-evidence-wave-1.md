# ABM Play Library External Evidence — Wave 1

**Summary**: Four selectively archived external sources sharpen the boundary between a signal, a progression event, a campaign tactic, a delivery format, and a state-changing ABM Play. They support several taxonomy changes but do not validate outcomes or the completeness of the Library.

---

## What the source bundle is

The bundle contains three Firecrawl captures with both rendered raw HTML and readable Markdown, plus one original vendor PDF:

- INFUSE's guide to five B2B ABM plays;
- Fractorial's specialist account of intent signals in life-sciences ABM;
- Madison Logic's competitive-displacement blueprint;
- Inflexion's practitioner explanation of Pursuit Marketing, featuring a Fujitsu marketing-transformation leader.

The Firecrawl files are captures, not publisher originals. Their embedded metadata retains the source URL and capture context. The Madison Logic PDF is the downloaded original and has a validated searchable sidecar.

## Durable findings

### A signal is not yet a Play

Fractorial treats intent as the beginning of a process that still requires contextual interpretation, ICP fit, multiple evidence sources, owner judgment, and a route to action. INFUSE similarly combines signal thresholds with buying-group progression and coordinated activation. This supports treating `Find the Spark` as a hypothesis or intelligence mechanism rather than a standalone commercial state change. (sources: 2026-07-24-fractorial-intent-signals-firecrawl-capture.json; 2026-07-24-infuse-5-b2b-abm-plays-firecrawl-capture.json)

### A meeting is progression evidence

INFUSE describes qualified meetings as a possible result of intent activation. The state-changing job is better expressed as creating a credible commercial initiative or transferring enough context for a joint dialogue. This supports moving `Turn Interest Into a Meeting` below the top-level Situation Play layer. (source: 2026-07-24-infuse-5-b2b-abm-plays-firecrawl-capture.json)

### Competitive use is only a population attribute

Madison Logic distinguishes competitor customers by readiness to switch, incumbent pain, intent, engagement, buying-group roles, and prior customer history. This supports an admission rule for `Target the Competitor's Clients`: a competitor install alone is insufficient. The source is a commercial vendor blueprint, so performance and causal claims remain excluded. (source: 2024-07-madison-logic-competitive-edge-blueprint.pdf)

### Pursuit Marketing is a qualified delivery format

Inflexion's practitioner source emphasizes starting early, working with sales or the bid team, securing budget, using a phased approach, understanding the customer's business need, and making the decision easier. This corroborates Pursuit Marketing as a bounded, cross-functional delivery format around a qualified strategic decision. It does not establish `Tiger Team` as a separate peer format. (source: 2026-07-24-inflexion-what-is-pursuit-marketing-firecrawl-capture.json)
