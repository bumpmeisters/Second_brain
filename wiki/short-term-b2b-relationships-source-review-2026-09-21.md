# Strategic short-term B2B relationships through social media

**Review scope:** PDF pp. 1, 5, 11–12 and selected passages/tables on pp. 9–10; not all 17 pages.

McGrath, O'Toole, and Drummond study 33 Irish craft food/drink firms, more than 4,000 posts, and 326 relationship episodes. Their resource-dependence account differentiates 12 relationship types using cooperation/interdependence versus adversarial independence and personal-contact versus network-development orientations. Social media may create alternatives without changing a dominant partner's power. Stronger buyer relationships can still be an important growth source. (source: Swipe--like--connect--A-typology-for-strategic-short-term_2026_Industrial-Ma.pdf, pp. 1, 5, 9–12; DOI 10.1016/j.indmarman.2026.01.005)

Focal entrepreneur accounts and retrospective Facebook/X/Instagram material in a specific sector do not establish general causal channel effects. Do not translate the typology into a rule to avoid large buyers or equate more social connections with less dependence. (source: same PDF, pp. 11–12)
