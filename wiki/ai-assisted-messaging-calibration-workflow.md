# AI-Assisted Messaging Calibration Workflow

**Summary**: Critique and adapt messaging against approved research, brand voice, product context, demand context, and buyer evidence while keeping interpretation human-accountable.

---

## When to use

Use when several teams create messaging from different context sets or when conversation evidence should inform a controlled refresh.

## Inputs

- Approved customer and market research.
- Current brand voice and message architecture.
- Product-marketing and demand-generation context.
- Traceable buyer, sales, or customer conversation material.
- Named reviewers for evidence, brand, and commercial interpretation.

## Procedure

1. Define the message, audience, decision context, and intended use.
2. Assemble only approved and current context sources.
3. Ask AI to identify inconsistencies, missing proof, generic language, and possible alternative interpretations.
4. Trace every important suggestion back to its source material.
5. Treat inferred motives or emotional themes as hypotheses for human review.
6. Adapt the message while preserving approved claims and voice boundaries.
7. Record accepted changes, rejected suggestions, and remaining uncertainty.

## Output

A calibrated message, source map, review record, and list of unresolved evidence gaps.

## Guardrails

AI critique is not customer truth. Emotional inference, efficiency, cost, rebrand, and product outcome claims from the source are excluded (source: Bruno Bertini's advice on becoming  a modern CMO.md; practitioner transcript; analysis: P14-C01).
