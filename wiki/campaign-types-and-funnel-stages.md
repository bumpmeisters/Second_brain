# Campaign Types and Funnel Stages

**Summary**: The campaign-type materials distinguish brand, demand generation, lead generation, conversion, and nurture roles across the customer journey.

---

The campaign-type deck distinguishes brand, demand generation, lead generation, conversion, and nurture as different campaign roles with different objectives, metrics, tactics, and execution patterns (source: 2025_campaign types_brnd_DemandDen_LeadGen_Conversion.pptx).

Brand campaigns focus on establishing, reinforcing, and elevating identity, values, and market presence (source: 2025_campaign types_brnd_DemandDen_LeadGen_Conversion.pptx).

Demand generation campaigns educate the market, create interest, and connect solutions to market pain points (source: 2025_campaign types_brnd_DemandDen_LeadGen_Conversion.pptx).

Lead generation campaigns capture and nurture interested prospects into contacts or qualified leads for CRM or marketing automation (source: 2025_campaign types_brnd_DemandDen_LeadGen_Conversion.pptx).

Conversion campaigns reduce friction and encourage high-intent prospects to take a desired final action (source: 2025_campaign types_brnd_DemandDen_LeadGen_Conversion.pptx).

The related Word document describes an objection-driven campaign approach that addresses potential hesitations throughout the customer journey (source: 2025_campaign types_brand_DemandGen_LeadGEN_Conversion.docx).
