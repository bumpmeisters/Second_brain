# Account-Specific Evidence Brief Workflow

**Summary**: Create a bounded evidence asset for a high-value account only when verified account context materially changes the problem, value, proof, objection, decision, or experience.

---

## Trigger

Use this workflow when a high-value account has a visible project or strategic initiative, the likely decision problem can be researched, and an existing reusable asset is not sufficiently decision-specific. Do not use it to manufacture superficial personalization at scale.

## Required inputs

- Verified account and initiative context.
- A stated economic, operational, or strategic decision.
- A named research question and a permitted, current source set.
- Owned subject-matter expertise.
- Comparable customer cases and approved market evidence.
- Authorized first-party input when available.
- A relationship-appropriate delivery route and named owner.

## Workflow

1. **Qualify the use case.** Confirm that the account value, initiative visibility, evidence gap, and available capacity justify bespoke work.
2. **Research the decision.** Record the initiative, likely decision, affected stakeholders, constraints, known facts, unknowns, and sources. When using a conversational AI interface, restrict it to the permitted source set and preserve the question, response, supporting evidence, and corrections as a persistent research thread.
3. **Assemble reusable proof.** Gather approved expertise, comparable cases, market evidence, and existing assets before creating anything new.
4. **Separate core from insert.** Keep reusable claims and proof stable. Tailor only where account context changes substance.
5. **Draft the evidence brief.** Connect the account's decision to analogous evidence, implications, open questions, and a useful next step.
6. **Run the governance gate.** Verify claims, provenance, authorization, confidentiality, privacy, and permitted data use.
7. **Deliver appropriately.** Choose the channel and sender based on the existing relationship; lead with useful evidence rather than an immediate pitch.
8. **Record the response.** Capture delivery, feedback, corrections, evidence gaps, and whether deeper engagement is justified.

## Inspectable output

The completed brief contains the account initiative, decision context, verified facts, reusable core, account-specific insert, comparable proof, evidence limitations, open questions, delivery owner, channel, and next-step rule. AI-mediated research also retains the question history, source lineage, evidence snapshot, corrections, and separation between observed facts, inferred account state, recommendations, and unresolved gaps.

## Reuse boundaries

The transcript supports the research-to-evidence-asset sequence and the use of owned inputs; the governance gate is a vault control added for safe reuse. The vendor AI case supports source-visible synthesis and persistent conversation history, but it does not establish factual accuracy, completeness, permission safety, recommendation quality, or commercial effect. Do not invent account facts, expose restricted information, imply causality from a case, or treat passive engagement as purchase intent. Reported project figures, opportunities, pipeline, time savings, trust effects, and channel preferences are excluded (source: Account based content; source file: How to influence buyer journey with account based demand generation.md; transcript anchors 14:39-42:05; source: From hours to minutes How to accelerate account research with Demandbase's AI Chat.md; sections “The problem” and “The solution”; vendor case; analysis: P23-C02).
