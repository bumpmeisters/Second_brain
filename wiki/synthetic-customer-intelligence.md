# Synthetic Customer Intelligence

**Summary**: Synthetic customer intelligence uses AI-generated or AI-assisted persona research as a source of hypotheses, not as verified customer truth.

---

Several persona analysis files appear to be AI-generated or AI-assisted and should be used as hypothesis material until claims are verified (source: raw/assets/04_Persona_und_Audience/Persona_Analysis).

The B2B persona blueprint file describes an "AI-Augmented B2B Persona Blueprint" and should therefore be handled with the same uncertainty discipline defined for AI-generated research in `AGENTS.md` (source: 20250626_B2B Persona Blueprint Creation_Gemini.docx).

For durable wiki claims, synthetic persona findings should be checked against customer interviews, analytics, sales input, CRM data, or other primary sources before being treated as facts.
