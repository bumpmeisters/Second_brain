# B2B State of Martech and Revenue Operations 2026

**Summary**: Source summary of an LXA and LeanData survey on enterprise B2B revenue-operations maturity, AI adoption, lead management, and governance readiness.

---

## What this source is

The report combines a literature review, practitioner input, and an April 2026 survey of 201 marketing, sales, and revenue-operations leaders at B2B organizations with at least 2,500 employees across seven countries (source: 20260605_B2B-State-of-Martech-and-Revenue-Operations-2026.pdf).

It was produced by LXA in partnership with LeanData. LeanData also supplies the foreword, a customer case study, and product positioning, so commercial claims and case-study outcomes should not be treated as independent evidence (source: 20260605_B2B-State-of-Martech-and-Revenue-Operations-2026.pdf).

## Key findings

- Process and Operations was the lowest of the report's five maturity pillars at 3.66/5, while People and Teams scored 3.82 and Platform and Technology 3.81. The report interprets this as investment in talent and tools outpacing workflow and governance maturity (source: 20260605_B2B-State-of-Martech-and-Revenue-Operations-2026.pdf).
- Fifty-one percent cited technical integration as a barrier to operations maturity; 47% cited manual lead-management processes that cannot scale; and 42% cited poor marketing-sales alignment on lead qualification (source: 20260605_B2B-State-of-Martech-and-Revenue-Operations-2026.pdf).
- Thirty-nine percent used rules-based routing without SLA enforcement, 26% used rules with SLA enforcement, 17% relied on manual assignment, and 15% reported AI or agentic routing. Thirty-seven percent were not confident every lead reached the right person at the right time (source: 20260605_B2B-State-of-Martech-and-Revenue-Operations-2026.pdf).
- AI use was concentrated in comparatively recoverable activities: content and campaign creation led at 46%, while AI lead routing and assignment was lowest at 11% (source: 20260605_B2B-State-of-Martech-and-Revenue-Operations-2026.pdf).
- Eighty-two percent agreed that clean data, defined processes, and reliable routing are prerequisites for scaling AI, but only 50% were confident their organization had the governance and controls required to deploy AI safely at scale (source: 20260605_B2B-State-of-Martech-and-Revenue-Operations-2026.pdf).

## Caveats

- The sample is limited to large B2B organizations and is not necessarily representative of smaller firms.
- Prior-year comparisons are directional because earlier editions used a broader respondent mix.
- Survey responses measure reported practice and confidence, not independently observed operational performance.
- The report is commercially sponsored and includes sponsor-supplied case-study content.
