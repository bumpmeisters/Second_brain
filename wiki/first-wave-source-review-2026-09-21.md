## Two proposed reusable assessments

### P58-C07 — Digital marketing and sales maturity diagnostic

- **Trigger:** a marketing/sales transformation decision is being reduced to tool choice or departments disagree about readiness.
- **Inputs:** business goals, customer-journey evidence, capabilities/skills, process and tool inventory, outcome measures, and stakeholder perspectives.
- **Method:** examine capabilities, processes, instruments, and performance separately; identify contradictions and missing evidence; select one improvement with an owner, expected outcome, and observable test.
- **Inspectable output:** an evidence-backed gap map and prioritized improvement hypothesis, including unknowns and reasons not to act yet.
- **Boundaries:** no invented numeric score, ranking, causal profitability promise, or current AI benchmark; the studies use historical samples and do not establish a stable longitudinal panel.
- **Routing metadata:** `description`: Diagnose mismatches across marketing/sales capabilities, processes, instruments, and performance. `use_when`: A transformation decision needs an evidence-led capability and process diagnosis. `avoid_when`: A validated benchmark, causal ROI estimate, or current market statistic is required. `output`: A sourced four-dimension gap map with an owned improvement and test.

### P58-C09 — Outcome-based service scaling assessment

- **Trigger:** an outcome-based service pilot needs a scaling decision or an existing offer encounters repeatability/coordination constraints.
- **Inputs:** the promised outcome, current service configuration, customer control/access, measurement feasibility, costs, operating constraints, and strategic fit.
- **Method:** identify the current configuration; test scale/scope/learning/structure logics against its constraints; compare advance, stay, or redesign; document the evidence and a bounded next test.
- **Inspectable output:** a scaling decision record with the chosen logic, binding constraint, measurement plan, owner, and explicit stop/stay rationale.
- **Boundaries:** not a linear maturity ladder or guaranteed growth recipe; derived from nine industrial firms and supplier-side interviews. Cross-sector application must be tested.
- **Routing metadata:** `description`: Assess how an outcome-based service can scale within its configuration and constraints. `use_when`: A provider must choose a scaling path for an outcome-based offer. `avoid_when`: The offering lacks a defined outcome or the task requires a validated quantitative maturity score. `output`: An advance/stay/redesign decision with constraints, evidence, and next test.

If approved, both assessments will follow the reusable-practice template and be registered in the reusable-practices library and router. No skill promotion is proposed.
