# ABM and Full-Funnel B2B Marketing Clippings July 2026

**Summary**: Source summary for a large clipping batch on account-based marketing, full-funnel B2B marketing, account progression, sales alignment, measurement, events, LinkedIn, and ABM case studies.

---

This bundle contains a broad practitioner library, mostly from Fullfunnel/Scrappy ABM-style sources, about moving ABM away from lead handoffs and toward a cross-functional revenue motion around selected accounts (source: raw/Clippings/ABM strategy the step-by-step guide for B2B companies.md; source: raw/Clippings/EP 50 How to Run a Proper ABM Strategy with Mason Cosby @ Scrappy ABM.md).

## Key Claims

- ABM fit depends on long sales cycles, meaningful ACV or LTV, multiple buyers, clear ICP/account qualification criteria, tailored value propositions, and sales/executive agreement on progress measures (source: raw/Clippings/ABM strategy the step-by-step guide for B2B companies.md).
- The newer ABM 3.0 framing argues for cluster-based targeting: accounts grouped by shared strategic challenge, use case, job-to-be-done, and evidence of need rather than only industry, territory, or static revenue tier (source: raw/Clippings/How to implement ABM strategy 3.0.md; source: raw/Clippings/ABM strategy the step-by-step guide for B2B companies.md).
- Several sources reject treating ABM as paid air cover plus sales sequences; they frame effective ABM as account research, buying-committee mapping, account awareness, account development, and account activation over long sales cycles (source: raw/Clippings/ABM strategy the step-by-step guide for B2B companies.md; source: raw/Clippings/🕵🏻‍♂️ ABM strategy 3.0.md).
- The account-progression model separates awareness, initial engagement, meaningful engagement, marketing-qualified account, sales-qualified account, opportunity, and re-engagement, with accounts able to move backward when engagement decays (source: raw/Clippings/EP 50 How to Run a Proper ABM Strategy with Mason Cosby @ Scrappy ABM.md).
- Measurement emphasis shifts from clicks, MQLs, and isolated campaign metrics toward leading account indicators, account-to-pipeline movement, sales pipeline velocity, influence on opportunities, and revenue outcomes (source: raw/Clippings/🕵🏻‍♂️ ABM KPIs and reports.md; source: raw/Clippings/🕵🏻‍♂️ How to measure ABM (w report templates).md; source: raw/Clippings/EP 50 How to Run a Proper ABM Strategy with Mason Cosby @ Scrappy ABM.md).
- Case-study clippings claim pipeline and revenue outcomes, but those figures are source claims from commercial/practitioner material and were not independently verified during this ingest (source: raw/Clippings/ABM campaign that generated $300k in revenue step by step case study.md; source: raw/Clippings/Four ABM Programs that Drove $7 Mil Pipeline A Practical Guide to Launching and Adapting ABM to Your Business.md).

## Useful Facts

- Common ABM program components in the bundle include ICP definition, account qualification, account segmentation, buying-committee mapping, problem-aware messaging, sales plays, account warm-up, events, social selling, LinkedIn ads, custom content, expansion/upsell plays, and reporting (source: raw/Clippings/Ideal Customer Profile for ABM programs (wnew template).md; source: raw/Clippings/🕵🏻‍♂️ Field eventsconference playbook.md; source: raw/Clippings/LinkedIn Marketing How to generate sales opportunities with dream accounts.md).
- The ABM 3.0 sources define three dynamic account states: Cluster ICP for qualified but unaware accounts, Future Pipeline for engaged accounts with unclear timing or need, and Active Focus for accounts with stronger awareness, relationship, and product-need evidence (source: raw/Clippings/🕵🏻‍♂️ ABM strategy 3.0.md; source: raw/Clippings/How to implement ABM strategy 3.0.md).
- The 6A framework in the bundle covers account segmentation and prioritization, account research, account awareness, account development, account activation, and alignment/reporting; the precise naming varies across clippings but the operating logic is consistent (source: raw/Clippings/🕵🏻‍♂️ ABM strategy 3.0.md; source: raw/Clippings/How to implement ABM strategy 3.0.md).
- Several sources argue that marketing and sales need shared target accounts, weekly review rhythms, joint playbooks, and explicit ownership rather than a marketing-to-sales handoff (source: raw/Clippings/Full-Funnel B2B marketing function and cross-functional teams.md; source: raw/Clippings/🕵🏻‍♂️ Who owns ABM.md; source: raw/Clippings/🕵🏻‍♂️ The sales perspective on ABM.md).

## Caveats

- The sources are practitioner and commercial clippings, not neutral academic evidence. Pipeline, revenue, reply-rate, and performance figures should be treated as source claims until checked against primary customer evidence or controlled measurement records.
- Several files are duplicates by hash, including `ABM strategy the step-by-step guide for B2B companies.md` / `1`, `EP 50 How to Run a Proper ABM Strategy with Mason Cosby @ Scrappy ABM.md` / `1`, and `🕵🏻‍♂️ How to create ABM ads that enterprise buyers engage with.md` / `1` (analysis: wiki/_outputs/clippings-ingest-inventory-2026-07-07.md).
- The batch is large enough that this is a synthesis ingest, not a line-by-line extraction. Use the inventory output when deciding which individual source to revisit.
