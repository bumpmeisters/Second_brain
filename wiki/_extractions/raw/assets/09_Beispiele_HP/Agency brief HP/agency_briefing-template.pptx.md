## Slide 6

Evaluation of Concepts and Content

3 Questions to ask
What are we reinventing?
The only things worth reinventing are the things that really matter.  Are we capturing that?
What is the role of the product?
Does the product have a clearly understandable role in the ad.  Is our product solving for the tension and/or clearly delivering the benefit? Are we the hero without over taking credit?
Is “keep reinventing” the only logical way to close this thought?
As the ad draws to a close, is “keep reinventing” not only logical but the only real way this could end?
