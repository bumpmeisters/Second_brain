1. Know Your Audience
• Understand Who They Are:
o Identify decision-makers and influencers attending the presentation.
o Determine their interests, concerns, and potential objections.
• Action Steps:
o Gather intelligence by consulting colleagues or researching past presentations.
o Assess their attitude toward the topic (e.g., supportive or resistant).
• Why This Matters:
o Tailoring your approach ensures alignment with their priorities and increases
engagement.

2. Create Killer Content
• Structure the Presentation:
o Start with a clear and engaging opening:
▪ Explain why the audience is present and what value they’ll gain.
o Present a storyline aligned with the presentation’s objectives:
▪ Examples: Research findings, roadmap pitches, or updates.
o Conclude with a strong ending:
▪ Recap key points.
▪ Open discussions for clarification.
▪ Propose next steps and decision-making moments.
• Use Visuals:
o Convert complex ideas into visuals like timelines or frameworks.
o Replace heavy text with simplified, visually engaging formats.
• Timing Allocation:
o Opening: 5-10% of the time.
o Middle (content-heavy): 70% of the time.
o Ending: 20-25% of the time.

3. Balance High-Level Overview with Details
• Find the Right Depth:
o Avoid being too vague (leading to excessive questions).
o Avoid excessive detail (which risks losing attention).
• Actionable Advice:
o Tailor depth based on the audience’s preferences and presentation length.
o Always substantiate claims with data.
▪ Provide 2-3 references for key points, especially when requesting budget
or headcount increases.

4. Be Yourself
• Delivery Tips:
o Authenticity is key—don’t feel compelled to perform or tell jokes.
o Focus on clarity and direct answers to audience questions.
o Confidence comes from preparation:
▪ Practice repeatedly to refine delivery and increase confidence.
• Why This Matters:
o Genuine communication resonates better with C-Level executives.

5. Minimize Chaos
• Presentation Environment:
o Choose a quiet, distraction-free location.
o Ensure the background is tidy, blurred, or replaced with a virtual background.
o Use good lighting (e.g., ring lights) to illuminate your face.
o Eliminate distractions:
▪ Silence phones.
▪ Suppress background noise using platform features.
• Why This Matters:
o A clean, controlled environment keeps the audience focused and enhances your
professionalism.

Actionable Checklist for C-Level Presentations
1. Pre-Presentation Preparation:

o Create a structured flow with a strong opening, detailed middle, and decisive
ending.
o Develop visuals for complex ideas.
