# Briefing Template Library

**Summary**: Source summary for `raw/assets/02_Briefing_Templates`, a folder of content, creative, email, event, initiative, and media briefing templates.

---

This folder contains 15 files across content briefs, creative briefs, email briefs, event briefs, initiative briefs, and media briefs (source: raw/assets/02_Briefing_Templates).

The folder is primarily a reusable [[briefing-system]] library: it contains structures for defining target audience, objectives, insights, product/service claims, proof, deliverables, campaign flow, media requirements, and operational inputs (source: raw/assets/02_Briefing_Templates).

The website copywriter briefing emphasizes a user-centric voice: it says communication should be less about what DB Schenker does and more about what the user gets (source: raw/assets/02_Briefing_Templates/Content_Briefs/DBS_Website_Content_CopywriterBriefing_2022.08.04 (1).pdf).

The E2E briefing deck uses positioning and brief structures such as "To / What / How" and "Get / To / By" to connect target insight, desired behavior, communication strategy, and concise audience takeaway (source: raw/assets/02_Briefing_Templates/Creative_Briefs/E2E Briefing Documents.pptx).

The customs creative brief examples show how briefing templates can be filled with market analysis, customs-clearance propositions, post-Brexit compliance context, logistics services, and target decision-makers (source: raw/assets/02_Briefing_Templates/Creative_Briefs/Examples/DBS_Creative Brief_DBS customs.docx).

Spreadsheet-based briefs and workbooks appear to support landing pages, events, initiative briefs, and campaign email flows (source: raw/assets/02_Briefing_Templates/Email_Briefs/Copy of DBS Supplier Emails_Final_Content Workbook_16022022.xlsx; source: raw/assets/02_Briefing_Templates/Event_Briefs/Event standard workbook (2).xlsx; source: raw/assets/02_Briefing_Templates/Initiative_Briefs/Initiaitve Modular Initiative Brief.xlsx).
