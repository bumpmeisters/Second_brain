# Lean Weekly ABM Pilot Loop

**Summary**: Run a small ABM pilot through a protected weekly signal-response cadence connected to one existing strategic priority.

---

## When to use

Use when a team needs to learn how account-based work operates without creating a parallel campaign factory.

## Setup

- Choose a bounded account set and one strategic event or content priority.
- Name the sales, marketing, and account owners.
- Define observable account states and a small library of meaningful touches.
- Protect the weekly operating time and execution capacity.
- Separate the planned value cadence from the signal-triggered response layer, and define when either may be suppressed by consent, contact policy, account coordination, or capacity.

## Weekly loop

1. Review account movement, engagement, relationship evidence, and open questions.
2. Update the working account state without overstating intent.
3. Select the next meaningful touch jointly.
4. Assign one owner and deadline.
5. Reuse or adapt relevant existing content before creating new material.
6. Check whether capacity or dependencies prevent execution.
7. Record response, learning, and the next state for the following review.

For long journeys, the weekly review should inspect both tracks: whether the agreed stage-appropriate cadence was executed and whether new observations justify a different next action. A signal triggers context review, not an automatic pitch, and the absence of a response does not by itself prove lack of awareness or interest (source: Untitled 7.md; canonical title: *How to Create an Account-Based Playbook for Long Sales Cycles and Multiple Decision-Makers*; practitioner webinar; analysis: P29-W4-C22).

The Denodo case supplies another implementation example: marketing, sales, and BDR teams reviewed a shared account view in weekly snapshot meetings, discussed accounts in motion, and aligned on next actions. It supports the cadence and shared-action mechanism only; the vendor-authored case does not validate the score, intent inference, adoption effect, or reported commercial results (source: Denodo transforms its go-to-market with Demandbase.md; sections “Embedding Demandbase into daily workflows” and “Empowering sales adoption”).

## Output

A weekly account decision log, assigned actions, content reuse decisions, capacity issues, and accumulated pilot learning.

## Guardrails

The loop is a learning mechanism, not evidence that the pilot caused account movement, demos, or opportunities. Client cases corroborate the small cross-functional weekly cadence and show how the review can expose execution-capacity constraints, but they do not validate reported reach, account movement, pipeline, revenue, productivity, ROI, scoring accuracy, or intent (source: ABM Program to Engage and Land Enterprise Sales Opportunities Live Case Study.md; canonical title: *ABM to professional associations [Live Case Study]*; analysis: P13-C03; source: Misaligned Despite Our Best Intention.
