# Wiki Linting

**Summary**: Wiki linting is the practice of auditing the wiki for structural and factual problems.

---

The source describes linting the wiki as a way to check for contradictions, outdated claims, orphan pages, and concepts that lack their own pages (source: Karpathy's LLM Wiki - Full Beginner Setup Guide.md).

Linting is recommended because the AI can miscategorize or misconnect information during wiki maintenance (source: Karpathy's LLM Wiki - Full Beginner Setup Guide.md).

The tutorial compares wiki linting to code linting because both inspect a growing knowledge or codebase for problems (source: Karpathy's LLM Wiki - Full Beginner Setup Guide.md).
