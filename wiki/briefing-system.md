# Briefing System

**Summary**: The briefing system turns strategy, audience insight, brand guidance, and operating constraints into execution-ready direction for agencies, content, media, events, and initiatives.

---

The briefing template folder contains content, creative, email, event, initiative, and media brief templates (source: raw/assets/02_Briefing_Templates).

The HP agency briefing template says a good brief clearly defines success, is singularly focused, defines what people should think, feel, or do, is underpinned by insight, and balances inspiration with information (source: Agency_Briefing Template.pptx).

The E2E briefing documents use concise structures such as "To / What / How" and "Get / To / By" to connect target, desired behavior, strategy, and audience takeaway (source: E2E Briefing Documents.pptx).
