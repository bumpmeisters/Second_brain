# Observable Capability Teach-Back Loop

**Summary**: Evaluate enablement through demonstrated capability rather than course completion, using teach-back, realistic application, visible feedback, and another demonstration.

---

## When to use

Use for sales, marketing, customer, or operational skills that must transfer into real judgment and behavior.

## Procedure

1. Define one bounded capability and a visible evaluation rubric.
2. Teach the underlying method and provide approved reference material.
3. Ask the learner to explain the method in their own words.
4. Apply it in a realistic, low-consequence scenario with relevant constraints. When AI could produce a plausible answer, let the learner make the first judgment before showing model output or an expert solution.
5. Ask the reviewer to narrate the evidence and judgment behind corrections. Label each correction as an observable fact, a rubric-based interpretation, or a coach preference, and teach the evaluation test rather than only giving the verdict. Do not silently replace the learner's work with a senior or AI-generated answer; the explanation is part of the learning rep.
6. Compare the work with the rubric. Where consequences permit it, use peer critique before manager or coach intervention so the learner practices evaluating work rather than merely matching one senior person's answer.
7. Use AI only for approved retrieval, practice-case generation, or a first-pass comparison.
8. Ask the learner to demonstrate again and record what changed.
9. Inspect transfer into real work before declaring capability. Treat independent ownership of the judgment and its consequences—not imitation of the coach—as the graduation criterion (source: 2026-08-12--EYXs4h_cLMk.md; practitioner source; analysis: P34-W3-C01).

## Output

A teach-back record, scenario artifact, rubric-based feedback, second demonstration, and transfer evidence.

## Guardrails

AI is a coach and practice aid, not the sole evaluator or a surveillance mechanism. Fluent delivery alone does not prove competence. Retention, sales, churn, and onboarding outcome claims are excluded (source: How teach-back sales training, AI coaching and culture drive real sales performance  OnBase podcast.md; vendor interview; analysis: P15-C02).

Protecting a first-pass learner judgment does not authorize unchecked novice publication or imply that automation should be withheld from consequential work. The “formative debt” source is a practitioner argument, not outcome evidence; its industry-wide employment, career-pipeline, productivity, and universal expertise claims are excluded (source: 2026-08-04--hnVwF42gGMA.md; analysis: P33-W2-C01).

The judgment-training extension is also practitioner guidance, not evidence of historical continuity, training effectiveness, organizational scale, career progression, or employment outcomes. Keep the rubric, reviewer authority, and release controls appropriate to the consequence of the work (source: 2026-08-12--EYXs4h_cLMk.md; analysis: P34-W3-C01).
