# Marketing Orchestration

**Summary**: Marketing orchestration is the disciplined coordination of planning, teams, execution, workflows, and systems across campaign work.

---

The Heinz Marketing CMO guide describes marketing orchestration as a disciplined approach to how marketing work gets done from planning through execution (source: 20250724_CMO guide to marketing orchestration.pdf).

The guide frames orchestration as a response to siloed teams, poor visibility, duplicated effort, campaign planning complexity, and content quality issues (source: 20250724_CMO guide to marketing orchestration.pdf).
