# Approved Source Conversion Batch 2026-07-15

**Summary**: Reviewed conversion batch that made 25 selected binary sources searchable as Markdown sidecars while preserving their immutable originals.

---

## What this source bundle is

This batch contains 25 unique non-Markdown sources selected from iterative source bundles: 7 files from `raw/assets/` and 18 files from `research/assets/`. The formats are 12 DOCX, 6 PDF, 5 PPTX, and 2 XLSX files (source: `wiki/_outputs/source-conversions/approved-batch-2026-07-15/approved-batch-inventory.csv`).

The sidecars live under `wiki/_extractions/raw/assets/` and `wiki/_extractions/research/assets/`. They are searchable derivatives, not replacements for the originals.

## Conversion result

- 22 new Markdown sidecars were created; 3 existing sidecars were preserved and revalidated.
- The approved audit found 23 `ok`, 2 `review`, and 0 `poor` results.
- No conversion failed and no PDF required OCR deferral.
- DOCX used Pandoc where available; PPTX, XLSX, and PDF used the available built-in fallback (source: `wiki/_outputs/source-conversions/approved-batch-2026-07-15/approved-batch-summary.md`).

## Trust and scope

Files derived from `research/assets/` are AI-generated or secondary synthesis and remain `unverified` unless a separate source summary records independent verification. Claims from these files must not be promoted as facts without checking their cited primary sources.

The `research/assets/11_Markets/` material is searchable for provenance and downstream project work but remains outside the active Second Brain content scope under the 2026-07-02 user decision. This batch does not reverse that decision.

## Caveats

- Two PPTX extractions contain isolated image-only slides without embedded text; the surrounding deck text was extracted successfully.
- Sidecars do not reliably preserve layout, charts, images, formulas, comments, or speaker notes.
- The original binary remains authoritative for visual or high-stakes verification.
- Seven ambiguous source bundles were deferred for a later content-level decision.
