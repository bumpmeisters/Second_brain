# HP Examples Library

**Summary**: Source summary for `raw/assets/09_Beispiele_HP`, a folder of HP example assets for ABM, agency briefs, country strategy, media briefs, creative briefs, strategic briefs, project management, asset tracking, optimization, RFPs, and SOWs.

---

This folder contains 29 files and is the largest ingested source bundle in this pass (source: raw/assets/09_Beispiele_HP).

The folder acts as an applied [[hp-example-patterns]] reference library: it provides examples for ABM account planning, agency input briefs, country strategy planning, media brief structure, creative briefs, asset tracking, strategic briefs, project management, optimization, and production-services RFP/SOW material (source: raw/assets/09_Beispiele_HP).

The ABM sample deck shows company-level account planning with company overview, ARR potential, product areas, pain points, key individuals/personas, value proposition, sales stage, objections, differentiators, and opportunity overview (source: raw/assets/09_Beispiele_HP/ABM campaign/ABM Company Campagin Samples.pptx).

The HP agency briefing template says a good brief clearly defines success, conveys passion, is singularly focused, defines what people should think/feel/do, is underpinned by a penetrating insight, and balances inspiration with information (source: raw/assets/09_Beispiele_HP/Agency brief HP/Agency_Briefing Template.pptx).

The HP briefs summary distinguishes campaign/initiative briefs, NPI value-proposition/GTM briefs, strategic briefs, HP Studios/eTailer asset briefs, and the difference between a brief and a briefing (source: raw/assets/09_Beispiele_HP/Agency brief HP/Briefs Summary.pptx).

The country strategy examples include country marketing planning templates, an integrated communication planning media brief request, an insights deck for market and customer understanding, and writing guidelines for business-performance communication (source: raw/assets/09_Beispiele_HP/Country Strategy).

The HP media brief folder contains spreadsheet and document templates plus media education material and strategy examples, including an HP 3M Strategy RTB PDF and MEDIA 101 session PDF (source: raw/assets/09_Beispiele_HP/Media brief HP).
