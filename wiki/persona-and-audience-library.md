# Persona and Audience Library

**Summary**: Source summary for `raw/assets/04_Persona_und_Audience`, covering B2B persona analysis, persona templates, customer journeys, and audience mapping.

---

This folder contains 14 files: PowerPoint templates, Word/PDF persona analyses, customer journey materials, and one MP4 that was cataloged but not transcribed (source: raw/assets/04_Persona_und_Audience).

The folder centers on [[b2b-persona-development]] and [[customer-journey-mapping]]: identifying decision roles, needs, buying triggers, journey stages, and how customers move through expanding and contracting consideration sets (source: raw/assets/04_Persona_und_Audience).

The HP customer journey template describes a journey that is not a simple funnel but an expanding and contracting universe of visible solutions across passive awareness, active awareness/perception, consideration, active evaluation, and purchase (source: raw/assets/04_Persona_und_Audience/Customer_Journey/HP_Customer Journey Template.pptx).

The B2B persona blueprint files frame personas as a strategic architecture that can move from profile to predictive asset; these files appear to be AI-generated or AI-assisted and should be treated as `needs verification` until their cited sources are checked (source: raw/assets/04_Persona_und_Audience/Persona_Analysis/20250626_B2B Persona Blueprint Creation_Gemini.docx).

The persona template decks provide reusable structures for prospect journeys, personas, journey mapping, SMB personas, and commercial-services personas (source: raw/assets/04_Persona_und_Audience/Persona_Templates).
