# Brand Library

**Summary**: Source summary for `raw/assets/03_Brand`, covering DB Schenker brand strategy, design guidelines, brand attributes, messaging frameworks, writing guidance, and HP brand analysis examples.

---

This folder contains 17 files across brand strategy, design guidelines, PowerPoint master samples, HP brand analysis, messaging frameworks, and writing guidelines (source: raw/assets/03_Brand).

The folder supports a [[brand-system]] that combines business-minded marketing, visual identity, messaging architecture, writing guidance, and evidence for why B2B brand matters (source: raw/assets/03_Brand).

The DB Schenker brand strategy text opens with a business-minded marketing principle and frames marketing activities as connected to business outcomes (source: raw/assets/03_Brand/Brand_Strategy/202408_DB Schenker Brand Strategy Part 1_text.docx).

The brand attributes PDF states that a strong B2B brand has direct business impact and cites market share, recommendations, repurchase rate, and premium price effects from external studies (source: raw/assets/03_Brand/Brand_Strategy/Brand Attributes/032024_Golbal Marketing and Transformation Communication_Brand Attributes.pdf).

The design guide materials cover unique imagery, logo usage, color, typography, the Pulse, icons, layout rules, email/newsletter guidance, brand checklists, and brandHUB references (source: raw/assets/03_Brand/Design_Guidelines/210319_DB Schenker Design Guide_2.0_NEW.pdf; source: raw/assets/03_Brand/Design_Guidelines/2025/250429_DBS basic brand_training_social-media.pptx).

The messaging framework files include AI-generated or prompt-based messaging framework materials and should be treated as useful but potentially uncertain until verified against source citations or expert review (source: raw/assets/03_Brand/Messaging_Frameworks/20260203_PROMPT_GEN_AI Messaging Frameworks for DSV_all frameworks.docx).
