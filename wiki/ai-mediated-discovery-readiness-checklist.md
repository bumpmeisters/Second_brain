# AI-Mediated Discovery Readiness Checklist

**Summary**: Prepare approved evidence for human and machine-mediated discovery without replacing conventional channels or overstating attribution.

---

## When to use

Use when publishing or refreshing strategically important buyer information that may be retrieved, summarized, or cited by search and AI systems.

## Checklist

- Is the buyer question explicit?
- Is there a concise, qualified core answer before promotional elaboration?
- Does each important section open with a complete claim that a skimming reader can understand in context?
- Are important claims connected to readable, current evidence?
- Are entities, products, authors, dates, metrics, and source relationships concrete and unambiguous rather than artificially repeated?
- Are important ideas repeated only where a new section or retrieval context requires them?
- Can people and retrieval systems navigate the structure?
- Are internal-search discovery paths limited to curated category pages and bounded combinations rather than crawlable arbitrary result permutations?
- If a URL must stay out of search results, is deindexing handled explicitly rather than assumed from crawl blocking alone?
- Does the website provide verification detail and a low-friction next step?
- Are ownership, refresh date, and retirement conditions assigned?
- Do conventional search, social, publisher, sales, and direct channels remain supported?
- Are visibility, referrals, engagement, and conversion reported as separate trends?

## Output

A readiness review with evidence gaps, structural fixes, responsible owners, and a refresh decision.

## Guardrails

Machine readability does not guarantee inclusion in synthesized answers. Schema prescriptions and market behavior are time-sensitive and should be externally verified before becoming technical policy. Definitive wording must not erase genuine uncertainty. Artificial entity density, repetition, or front-loading does not guarantee retrieval or citation. Proprietary citation-uplift figures and claimed equivalence between human and transformer attention are excluded. Referral and conversion trends do not prove causality (sources: AI in B2B advertising Craig Miller (AWS) on what’s next for marketers  OnBase podcast.md; From SEO to GEO How AI is changing B2B visibility and buyer trust  OnBase podcast.md; analysis: P15-C01).

Exact `robots.txt`, `noindex`, canonicalization, and internal-search controls are site-specific; verify the deployed behavior rather than copying a generic directive (source: 2026-07-30--cfIQ_ksB61U.md; primary platform guidance; analysis: P44-C03).
