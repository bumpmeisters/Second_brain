## Scope

The bundle contains 597 files totaling about 1.72 GB: 307 DOCX, 190 PDF, 54 PPTX, 26 XLSX, 10 Markdown, four TXT, two ZIP, one PNG, one HTML, one legacy XLS, and one Windows shortcut (source: research/assets/11_Markets; analysis: [[new-sources-inventory-2026-06-20]]).

The user identified the collection as AI-generated deep research together with the prompts used to produce or guide that research. The collection was moved from `raw/assets/11_Markets` to the canonical AI-research location `research/assets/11_Markets` on 2026-06-20. It is secondary synthesis, not primary evidence (source: user clarification, 2026-06-20; source: AGENTS.md).

The five top-level areas are:

- `2025_market` — 337 files covering target audiences, trends, content pillars, market reports, Connect spot booking, products, presentations, and business-unit material.
- `Vertical market` — 117 files covering prompts, AI aggregation, and verticals such as automotive, healthcare, industrial, e-commerce, cloud, fashion and retail, perishables, aerospace and defense, aid and relief, and others.
- `Persona` — 78 files covering vertical-specific personas, aggregated inputs, and prompt templates.
- `Connect` — 47 files focused mainly on email blueprints, customer-journey analysis, and market analysis.
- `Business unit` — 18 files covering air, ocean, land, contract logistics, market analysis, and prompts (source: research/assets/11_Markets; analysis: [[new-sources-inventory-2026-06-20]]).

## Provenance and trust

The entire bundle is classified as AI-generated deep research plus corresponding prompts. Any embedded quotations, copied evidence, company material, or third-party references remain citations inside an AI-generated synthesis until checked directly. Every factual claim promoted from this collection should therefore be marked `needs verification` unless its underlying primary source has been opened and checked (source: user clarification, 2026-06-20; source: AGENTS.md).

Market claims are often time-sensitive. Files dated 2020–2025 may remain useful historically but should not be presented as current market conditions without external verification (source: research/assets/11_Markets).

ZIP files, the legacy XLS file, and the Windows shortcut were cataloged but not deeply extracted. The rest of the bundle was ingested at inventory and thematic level because a full file-by-file synthesis would require separate domain batches (source: research/assets/11_Markets; analysis: [[new-sources-inventory-2026-06-20]]).

Tool/model provenance, generation dates, prompts, and citation-check status should be captured during any later deep ingest of an individual report or report bundle. Current overall trust level: `unverified`.
