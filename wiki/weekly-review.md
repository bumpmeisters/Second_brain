# Weekly Review

**Summary**: The weekly review is the recurring habit that keeps the vault from becoming a passive archive.

**Sources**: raw/Clippings/The Obsidian Vault Setup That Replaced My $500 Per Month Software Stack.md.

**Last updated**: 2026-05-30

---

The source calls the weekly review the habit that makes or breaks the Obsidian system (source: The Obsidian Vault Setup That Replaced My $500 Per Month Software Stack.md).

The weekly review includes processing the inbox, closing out daily notes, updating project statuses, reviewing active projects, reviewing last week's priorities, and planning next week's outcomes (source: The Obsidian Vault Setup That Replaced My $500 Per Month Software Stack.md).

The source recommends opening three random evergreen notes during the weekly review to create resurfacing and new connections (source: The Obsidian Vault Setup That Replaced My $500 Per Month Software Stack.md).
