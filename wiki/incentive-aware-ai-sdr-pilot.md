# Incentive-Aware AI SDR Pilot

**Summary**: Introduce an AI SDR through a bounded inbound bottleneck while protecting buyer experience, human handoffs, comparable evaluation, and fair incentives.

---

## When to use

Use when an inbound workflow has a clear bottleneck and the organization can compare accepted outcomes without framing the pilot as workforce substitution.

## Procedure

1. Select one bounded inbound job and define what remains human-owned.
2. Establish buyer-experience, brand, contact-policy, and escalation standards.
3. Stage the workload and retain a rapid human handoff.
4. Coach the agent and human team from reviewed examples and failures.
5. Compare accepted outcomes on equivalent work rather than raw activity volume.
6. Define quota and compensation treatment before agent-assisted pipeline enters production.
7. Expand, revise, or stop based on quality, exceptions, buyer experience, and adoption evidence.

## Output

A pilot charter, workload boundary, handoff contract, review set, coaching log, incentive rule, and scale decision.

## Guardrails

The playbook does not assume product superiority or prove pipeline impact. Customer anecdotes, workforce substitution, and vendor outcome claims are excluded (source: AI SDRs explained How AI agents are redefining inbound pipeline  OnBase podcast.md; vendor interview; analysis: P13-C07).
