# ABM Sales-Marketing Operating Contract

**Summary**: Establish shared ABM language, ownership, account stages, time horizons, incentives, and feedback responsibilities before execution begins.

---

## When to use

Use before an ABM pilot or when sales and marketing appear aligned in principle but make different operating assumptions.

## Contract fields

- Shared definitions for target account, qualification, engagement, opportunity, progression, and success.
- Common goals and realistic time horizons.
- Capacity, compensation, quota, and priority constraints.
- Account stages with entry and exit evidence.
- Named owners for qualification, research, buying-group mapping, profiling, nurturing, activation, and review.
- A rule requiring buyer and seller learning to return to the shared account record.
- Review cadence, decision rights, and escalation path.

## Account-state service levels

For every account state, add a service-level row containing:

- entry, exit, regression, and `unknown` evidence;
- required research and buying-group coverage;
- allowed and prohibited actions;
- owner, review cadence, and capacity limit;
- the evidence that must travel with a handoff;
- state-appropriate measures and escalation conditions.

Active-focus accounts may justify opportunity-oriented actions when the evidence supports them. Future-pipeline accounts are not sales-ready by default: manage them through evidence quality, role and relationship coverage, learning, and useful engagement rather than forced sequences or premature pipeline measures. Marketing and sales jointly approve state transitions and preserve the observations and uncertainty behind the change (source: Account-based journeys how enterprise sales reps create multi-channel journeys to book more calls.md; body title: *Account-Based Sales: How to drive pipeline in 2025*; sections “Active Focus” and “Future pipeline”; practitioner-promotional description; analysis: P25-C03).

## Procedure

1. Surface conflicting definitions and incentives explicitly.
2. Visualize stages, activities, owners, handoffs, and measures.
3. Assign shared responsibilities rather than treating sales as the recipient of a static lead.
4. Test the contract on a bounded account set.
5. Review where ownership, time horizon, or capacity breaks down.
6. Revise the contract before scaling.

## Output

A visible operating agreement that sales, marketing, and RevOps can use to run and review the same account motion.

## Implementation evidence

When several teams share an account, route planned customer-facing activity through [[multi-team-account-contact-coordination-gate]] before activation. Name the primary coordinator, communication sequence, suppressions, exception authority, and notification rules (source: Accela propels sales to success by implementing Demandbase One.md; body title: *Hexagon takes the guessing game out of ABM with insights from Demandbase*; vendor case; analysis: P26-C01).

For global adoption, pair the contract with concise role training, regional office hours, a maintained resource hub, recurring contextual reports, and a feedback path from questions, rejected recommendations, errors, and local adaptations into model and playbook review (source: The early bird wins the sale Achievers elevates timely outreach, collaboration, and pipeline visibility through Demandbase insights.md; body title: *CyberArk achieves 4x higher close rates with an ABX approach powered by Demandbase*; section “Enablement”; vendor case; analysis: P26-C02).

A Demandbase-authored Denodo case provides a concrete, qualified example of this contract in operation: one shared account score was embedded in Salesforce and Marketo, marketing, sales, and BDR teams used a weekly snapshot review to discuss accounts in motion and assign next actions, and sales enablement covered interpretation of engagement and journey context. The case corroborates the shared-data, shared-cadence, and enablement pattern; it does not establish that the score was accurate or that the platform caused commercial outcomes (source: Denodo transforms its go-to-market with Demandbase.md; sections “Building a unified scoring model,” “Embedding Demandbase into daily workflows,” and “Empowering sales adoption”; vendor case).

## Guardrails

Benchmark percentages and universal budget ratios are excluded. The contract must be adapted to the actual commercial motion and incentive system. A composite score must not hide weak fit, identity, engagement, or intent evidence; review source quality and false-positive risk before it changes priority or outreach. Progressive profiling requires consent, necessity, privacy, access, and retention controls. The account-based-sales source's outcome percentages, automation and layoff predictions, universal role claims, and causal claims are excluded (sources: Sales in ABM The good, the bad and the ugly.md; Full-Funnel's Fundamentals.md; practitioner transcripts; analysis: P13-C01 and P13-C02; source: Denodo transforms its go-to-market with Demandbase.md; vendor outcomes excluded; source: Account-based journeys how enterprise sales reps create multi-channel journeys to book more calls.md; practitioner-promotional description).
