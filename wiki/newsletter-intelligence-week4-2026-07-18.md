## Approved methods

- Restart from a curated handoff when repeated corrections suggest a contaminated conversation trajectory; compact evidence and decisions between work phases.
- Retest and simplify harness scaffolding after model changes; use first-divergence checks when intermediate reference artifacts exist.
- Separate bounded execution from outer-loop priority, context, evaluation, permission and human-decision controls.
- Require observable progress, bounded work, persisted state, resource ceilings and escalation before using autonomous loops.
- Reduce the human review surface with visible evidence paths, claim mappings and targeted diffs.
- Build specialized harnesses only for stable repeated workflows; route volume and judgment work according to task needs.
- Use constrained primary-document searches and multi-system reverse-image checks as provenance methods.
- Apply a total-cost and strategic-differentiation gate before custom AI-tool development.

These are qualified operating patterns drawn from practitioner, conference and vendor sources. They are not universal performance claims (source: 2026-07-18-newsletter-intelligence-week4.md).

## No-change and held decisions

- The installed Content Taste skill already implements the reviewed Share, Onlyness, byline, steal, source and sparring tests. No skill edit was made.
- The claim that synthetic-content abundance increases the value of creator authenticity remains held because the substantive interview, evidence and counterarguments were inaccessible (source: 2026-07-18-newsletter-intelligence-week4-review-dispositions.md).

## Contradictions and caveats

- More context does not necessarily produce better working context.
- More repetition does not imply improvement without feedback and stop conditions.
- High nominal accuracy does not guarantee adoption when humans must re-check the whole artifact.
- Stronger models can make old harness scaffolding obsolete.
- Vendor, conference, hardware, cost, benchmark, adoption and productivity figures were excluded from durable claims.
