# DB Schenker Brand Guidelines

**Summary**: DB Schenker brand guideline materials cover design identity, logo use, typography, color, imagery, social-media use, PowerPoint samples, and layout rules.

---

The 2021 design guide states that old versions lose validity with the updated release 2.0 and then lays out the design system across imagery, logo usage, color, typography, the Pulse, icons, layout, and email/newsletter guidance (source: 210319_DB Schenker Design Guide_2.0_NEW.pdf).

The 2025 basic brand training materials summarize key brand design topics for social-media and basic design use (source: 250429_DBS basic brand_training_social-media.pdf).

The PowerPoint sample decks provide reusable presentation patterns under the DB Schenker brand system (source: raw/assets/03_Brand/Design_Guidelines/PowerPoint).
