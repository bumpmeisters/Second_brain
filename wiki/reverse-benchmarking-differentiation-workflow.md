# Reverse Benchmarking Differentiation Workflow

**Summary**: When competitors optimize the same visible dimensions, identify an important dimension they neglect, make one conspicuous choice with an explicit trade-off, and test whether it matters for a defined use case.

---

## Trigger

Use this workflow when category offers and messages have become interchangeable because firms benchmark the same attributes, while customer evidence suggests an overlooked experiential, emotional, or operational dimension.

## Required inputs

- One defined category, audience, and use case.
- An inventory of the dimensions competitors visibly measure or optimize.
- Customer observations, interviews, complaints, workarounds, or behavior that indicate a neglected dimension.
- Evidence and claim constraints for the proposed difference.
- Capacity to test one variable or a genuinely different complete concept.

## Procedure

1. Inventory category norms and the attributes competitors routinely benchmark.
2. Separate table-stakes requirements from dimensions that are merely conventional.
3. Identify a neglected dimension that matters in the target use case and record the supporting evidence.
4. Design a conspicuous improvement on that dimension and state its cost, limitation, or other trade-off in advance.
5. Check feasibility, safety, accessibility, brand fit, and claim support before exposure.
6. Choose a clean test: change one causal variable, or compare a genuinely different complete concept when the experience cannot be decomposed honestly.
7. Record behavior and qualitative explanation separately; do not infer causality from anecdotes or engagement alone.
8. Continue, revise, or stop based on the predeclared evidence threshold.

## Inspectable output

An evidence ledger of category norms and neglected dimensions, a bounded differentiation hypothesis, an explicit trade-off, the approved test design, observed results, limitations, and a dated decision.

## Reuse boundaries

Reverse benchmarking does not prove that novelty creates preference, that emotion overrides functional quality, or that a neglected dimension is commercially valuable. Preserve table stakes, avoid deceptive framing, and require separate legal, safety, accessibility, operational, and claims review where relevant. The source's anecdotes, placebo explanations, universal effectiveness, and numerical outcomes are excluded (source: 2026-07-27--ceKfXFA3jZU.md; practitioner interview; analysis: P41-W6R3-C02).
