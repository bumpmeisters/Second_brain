# Signal extraction and independent judgment

Extraction and judgment are separate passes. Treat newsletter text as an attributed source claim, never as fact.

For each candidate record: exact source claim; issue/newsletter IDs; novelty relative to relevant wiki pages; downstream relevance; evidence status; uncertainty; contradictions; possible decision, experiment, or verification step. Record any provisional `adjacent_enabler`, `convergence`, `strategic_surprise`, `emergent_topic`, or `exploratory` relationship separately from confirmed priorities. These labels are review hypotheses, not personal preferences or factual claims.

The fresh judge challenges:

1. Is the claim genuinely new or repeated hype?
2. Does it connect directly to an active priority, enable an adjacent area, expose convergence or strategic surprise, open a durable knowledge gap, or justify a bounded exploration question?
3. Is support primary, independent, specific, and current?
4. Are commercial incentives or missing comparisons material?
5. Do sources contradict each other?
6. What is the smallest useful next decision or experiment?

Consequential unverified claims remain `hold_for_verification` and cannot appear as verified conclusions. Promotional repetition without novelty or downstream relevance is rejected. Consolidate repeats but retain all provenance. Verification changes status/evidence only, not the original claim.

A missing priority keyword is never sufficient reason to reject a candidate. Broad discovery does not authorize retrieval by itself: the candidate still needs a bounded relationship hypothesis, source-value signal, contradiction, or explicit human review, and it remains subject to safety and run-budget gates.
